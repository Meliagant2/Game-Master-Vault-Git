<h3>Bonus Actions</h3>
<p><strong><em>Fey Step:</em></strong> The spirit magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. Then one of the following effects occurs, based on the spirit's chosen mood:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Fuming:</b> The spirit has <b>ADV</b> on the next attack roll it makes before the end of this turn.</li>
<li><b>Mirthful:</b> One creature the spirit can see within 10 feet of itself makes a <b>WIS Save</b>. <b><i>Failure:</i></b> The target is <a href="5e - Charmed.md" class="internal-link">☠️Charmed</a> by you and the spirit for 1 minute or until the target takes any damage.</li>
<li><b>Tricksy:</b> The spirit fills a 10-foot Cube within 5 feet of it with magical Darkness, which lasts until the end of its next turn.</li>
