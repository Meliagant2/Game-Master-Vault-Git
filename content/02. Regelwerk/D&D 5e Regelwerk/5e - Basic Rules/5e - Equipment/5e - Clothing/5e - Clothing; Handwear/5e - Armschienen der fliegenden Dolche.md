_This armband appears to have thin daggers strapped to it._

While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Attunement/5e - Attunement|🌟attuned]] to this item, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Hurl Daggers:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action, you can forgo one of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attacks]] to pull up to <u>two</u> magic [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Dagger|🗡️Daggers]] from the bracer and immediately hurl them, making a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] with each [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Dagger|🗡️Dagger]]. A [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Dagger|🗡️Dagger]] vanishes if you don't hurl it right away, and the daggers disappear right after they hit or miss. The bracer never runs out of daggers.
