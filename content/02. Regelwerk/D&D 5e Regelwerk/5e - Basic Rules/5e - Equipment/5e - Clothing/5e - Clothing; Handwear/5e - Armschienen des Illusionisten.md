_A powerful illusionist originally developed these bracers, which enabled her to create multiple minor illusions at once. The bracers' power, though, extends far beyond illusions._

While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Attunement/5e - Attunement|🌟attuned]] to these bracers, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Quickened Spell:</u>** Whenever you cast a cantrip, you can use a **Bonus Action** on the same turn to cast that cantrip a second time.
