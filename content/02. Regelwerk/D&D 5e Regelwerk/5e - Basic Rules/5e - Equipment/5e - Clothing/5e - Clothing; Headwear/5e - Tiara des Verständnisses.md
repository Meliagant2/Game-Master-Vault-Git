While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Attunement/5e - Attunement|🌟attuned]] to this item, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Investigator:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Investigation|💃Investigation]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]].

**<u>Spellcasting:</u>** The tiara has `10` charges and regains `1d6 +4` charges after finishing a **Long Rest**. While wearing the tiara, you can expend charges to cast one of the following spells (save DC 14) from it:

- [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] (2 charges).
- [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 3 Divination/5e - Speak with Plants (Spell)|☄️Speak with Plants]] (3 charges).
- [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 3 Divination/5e - Speak with Dead (Spell)|☄️Speak with Dead]] (4 charges).

**<u>Supreme Divination:</u>** When you are casting a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - School of Divination|☄️Divination]] spell while wearing the tiara, you can expend _charges_ instead of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] spell components, that would be consumed. You expend <u>one</u> charge for each level you cast the spell at.
