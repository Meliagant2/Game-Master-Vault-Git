# ⛑️5e - Weighty⛑️

While wearing **Weighty** armor, your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is reduced by <u>10 feet</u>, unless your <u>STR modifier</u> is equal to or higher than the number listed behind the **Weighty** property in parentheses.
