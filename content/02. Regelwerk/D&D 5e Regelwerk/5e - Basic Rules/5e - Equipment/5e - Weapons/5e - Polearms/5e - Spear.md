**<u>Reach:</u>** A Reach weapon can attack enemies further than <u>5 feet</u> from you. If a weapon has a longer reach, it is stated in parentheses after this property.

**<u>Versatile:</u>** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a <u>melee attack</u>.

**<u>Mastery: Sap:</u>** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] on its next attack roll before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
