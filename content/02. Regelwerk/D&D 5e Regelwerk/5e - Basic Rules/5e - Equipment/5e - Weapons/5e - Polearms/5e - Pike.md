**<u>Heavy:</u>** You have **DISADV** on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your <u>STR modifier</u> isn't at least `+1` or if it's a Ranged weapon and your <u>DEX modifier</u> isn't at least `+1`.

**<u>Reach:</u>** A Reach weapon can attack enemies further than <u>5 feet</u> from you. If a weapon has a longer reach, it is stated in parentheses after this property.

**<u>Two-Handed:</u>** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

**<u>Mastery: Push:</u>** If you _**hit**_ a creature with this weapon, you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] the creature up to <u>10 feet</u> straight away from yourself if it is _Large or smaller_.
