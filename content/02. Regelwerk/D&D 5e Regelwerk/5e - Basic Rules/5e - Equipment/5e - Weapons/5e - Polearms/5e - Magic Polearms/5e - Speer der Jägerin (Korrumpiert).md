While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Attunement/5e - Attunement|🌟attuned]] to this weapon, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Attack Bonus:</u>** You have a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Rolls]] made with this magic weapon.

**<u>Blood Spear:</u>** When you reduce a creature to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], using this spear, you gain `2d6` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]].

### Weapon Properties

**<u>Reach:</u>** A Reach weapon can attack enemies further than <u>5 feet</u> from you. If a weapon has a longer reach, it is stated in parentheses after this property.

**<u>Versatile:</u>** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a <u>melee attack</u>.

**<u>Mastery: Sap:</u>** If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] on its next attack roll before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
