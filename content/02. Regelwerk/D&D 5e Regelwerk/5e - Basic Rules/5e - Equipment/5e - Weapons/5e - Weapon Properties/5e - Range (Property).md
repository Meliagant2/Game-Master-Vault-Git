# 🗡️5e - Range🗡️

A Range weapon has a range in parentheses after the property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon's **normal range** in feet, and the second is the weapon's **long range**. When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have **DISADV** on the attack roll. You can't attack a target beyond the long range.
