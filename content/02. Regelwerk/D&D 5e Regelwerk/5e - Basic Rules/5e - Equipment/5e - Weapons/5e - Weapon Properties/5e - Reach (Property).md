# 🗡️5e - Reach🗡️

A **Reach** weapon can attack enemies further than <u>5 feet</u> from you. If a weapon has a longer reach, it is stated in parentheses after this property. You have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] against enemies within <u>5 feet</u> of you, when using a **Reach** weapon.

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Reach** property, _spells_ you cast with a range of **Touch** have a range of <u>10 feet</u> instead.
