# 🗡️5e - Heavy🗡️

You have **DISADV** on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your <u>STR modifier</u> isn't at least `+1` or if it's a Ranged weapon and your <u>DEX modifier</u> isn't at least `+1`.
