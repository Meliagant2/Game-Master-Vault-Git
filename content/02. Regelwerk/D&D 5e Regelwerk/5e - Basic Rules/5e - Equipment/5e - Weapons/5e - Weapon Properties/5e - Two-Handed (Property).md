# 🗡️5e - Two-Handed🗡️

A **Two-Handed** weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

A **Two-Handed** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] requires two hands for you to hold it while casting spells.
