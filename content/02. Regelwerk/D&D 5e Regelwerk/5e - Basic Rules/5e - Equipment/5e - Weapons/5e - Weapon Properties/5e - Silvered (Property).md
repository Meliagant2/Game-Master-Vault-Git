# 🗡️5e - Silvered🗡️

An alchemical process has bonded silver to this weapon. When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] with it against a creature that is shape-shifted, the weapon deals <u>one</u> additional die of damage.
