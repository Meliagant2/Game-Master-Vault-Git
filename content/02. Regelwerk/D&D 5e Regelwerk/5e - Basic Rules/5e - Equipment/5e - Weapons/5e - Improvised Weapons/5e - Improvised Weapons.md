# 🗡️5e - Improvised Weapons🗡️

An improvised weapon is an object wielded as a makeshift weapon, such as broken glass, a table leg, or a frying pan. A Simple or Martial weapon also counts as an improvised weapon if it's wielded in a way contrary to its design; if you use a Ranged weapon to make a melee attack or throw a Melee weapon that lacks the Thrown property, the weapon counts as an improvised weapon. An improvised weapon follows the rules below.

**<u>Proficiency:</u>** Don't add your Proficiency Bonus to attack rolls with an improvised weapon.

**<u>Damage:</u>** On a _**hit**_, the weapon deals `1d4` damage of a type the GM thinks is appropriate for the object.

**<u>Range:</u>** If you throw the weapon, it has a normal range of <u>20 feet</u> and a long range of <u>60 feet</u>.

**<u>Weapon Equivalents:</u>** If an improvised weapon resembles a Simple or Martial weapon, the GM may say it functions as that weapon and uses that weapon's rules. For example, the GM could treat a table leg as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Hammers/5e - Club|🗡️Club]].
