**<u>Finesse:</u>** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your <u>Strength</u> or <u>Dexterity</u> modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

**<u>Light:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action on your turn and attack with a <u>Light weapon</u>, you can make one <u>extra attack</u> as a **Bonus Action** later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a different Light weapon, and you don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative.

For example, you can attack with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Shortsword|🗡️Shortsword]] in one hand and a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Dagger|🗡️Dagger]] in the other using the **Attack Action** and a **Bonus Action**, but you don't add your <u>STR</u> or <u>DEX</u> modifier to the damage roll of the **Bonus Action** unless that modifier is negative.

**<u>Mastery: Vex:</u>** If you _hit_ a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against your next attack roll you make before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
