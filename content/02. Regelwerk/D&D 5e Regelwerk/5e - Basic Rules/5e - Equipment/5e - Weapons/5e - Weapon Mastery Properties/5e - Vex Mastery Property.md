# 🗡️5e - Vex Mastery🗡️

If you _hit_ a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against your next attack roll you make before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
