# 🗡️5e - Graze Mastery🗡️

If your <u>attack roll</u> with this weapon _**misses**_ a creature, you can deal damage to that creature equal to `the ability modifier` you used to make the attack roll. This damage is the same type dealt by the weapon, and the damage can be increased only by increasing the ability modifier.
