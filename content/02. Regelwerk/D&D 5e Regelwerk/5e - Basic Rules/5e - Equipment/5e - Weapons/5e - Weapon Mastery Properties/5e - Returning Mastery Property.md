# 🗡️5e - Returning Mastery🗡️

A **Returning** weapon allows you to hurl the weapon in such a way that it returns to you. When you make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] with a **Returning** weapon, the weapon returns to you immediately after _**hitting**_ or _**missing**_ the target. You can catch the weapon if you have a hand free to do so.
