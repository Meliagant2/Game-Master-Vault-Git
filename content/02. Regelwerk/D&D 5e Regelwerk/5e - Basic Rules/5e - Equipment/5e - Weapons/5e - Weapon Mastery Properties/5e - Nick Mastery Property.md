# 🗡️5e - Nick Mastery🗡️

When you make the extra attack of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Light (Property)|🗡️Light]] property, you can make it as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action instead of as a **Bonus Action**. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
