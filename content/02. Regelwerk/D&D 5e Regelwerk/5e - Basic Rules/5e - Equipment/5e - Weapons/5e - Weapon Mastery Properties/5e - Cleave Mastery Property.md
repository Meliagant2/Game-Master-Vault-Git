# 🗡️5e - Cleave Mastery🗡️

If you _**hit**_ a creature with a <u>melee attack roll</u> using this weapon, you can make a <u>melee attack roll</u> with the weapon against a second creature within <u>5 feet</u> of the first that is also within your reach. On a _**hit**_, the second creature takes the weapon's damage, but don't add your ability modifier to that damage unless that modifier is negative. You can make this extra attack only <u>once per turn</u>.
