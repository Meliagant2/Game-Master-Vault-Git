# 🗡️5e - Slow Mastery🗡️

If you _**hit**_ a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] by <u>10 feet</u> until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed <u>10 feet</u>.
