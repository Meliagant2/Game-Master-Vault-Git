**<u>Finesse:</u>** When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your <u>Strength</u> or <u>Dexterity</u> modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

**<u>Reach:</u>** A Reach weapon can attack enemies further than <u>5 feet</u> from you. If a weapon has a longer reach, it is stated in parentheses after this property.

**<u>Mastery: Topple:</u>** If you _**hit**_ a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a **CON Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].
