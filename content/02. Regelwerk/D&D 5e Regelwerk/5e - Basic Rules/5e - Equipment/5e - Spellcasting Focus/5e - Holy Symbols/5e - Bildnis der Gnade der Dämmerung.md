_The Icon is a 20 centimeter tall statuette made of the purest silver, weighing 5 kilograms. It depicts a cleric kneeling in supplication._

**Only a Cleric or a Paladin who follows or respects the Dawnfather may attune to this item.**

While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Attunement/5e - Attunement|🌟attuned]] to this item, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Augury (1/day):</u>** As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action you can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - Augury (Spell)|☄️Augury]] from the icon, without needing any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] components.

**<u>Bane of the Undead:</u>** When you use the icon as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Holy Symbols/5e - Holy Symbols|🎩Holy Symbol]] while using the **Abjure Foes** or **Turn Undead** feature, you increase the **save DC** of that feature by `2`.

**<u>Cure Wounds (1/day):</u>** As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action you can present the icon to heal one creature that you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you. The target regains `6d8 + 3` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], unless it is an _Undead_, a _Construct_, or a _Fiend_.

**<u>Protection from Evil (1/day):</u>** You can present the icon as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to grant yourself and each creature within <u>30 feet</u> of you the effect of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - Level 1 Abjuration/5e - Protection from Evil and Good (Spell)|☄️Protection from Evil and Good]] spell against _Fiends_ and _Undead_ until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Sentience:</u>** The Icon is a sentient magic item inhabited and empowered by a shard of the divine consciousness of the Deva Ithuriel. The icon has hearing and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Darkvision|👁️Darkvision]] out to <u>120 feet</u>. In addition, it can understand all languages and communicate telepathically with any character that carries or wields it.

### Spell Focus Properties

**<u>Mastery: Muffle:</u>** While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Muffle** property, [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]] components of _spells_ you cast can only be heard within <u>30 feet</u> of you.
