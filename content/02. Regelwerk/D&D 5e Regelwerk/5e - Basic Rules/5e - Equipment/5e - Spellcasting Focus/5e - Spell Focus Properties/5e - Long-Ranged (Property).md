# 🎩5e - Long-Ranged🎩

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Long-Ranged** property, spells you cast with a range greater than <u>5 feet</u> have their range increase by <u>30 feet</u>.
