# 🎩5e - Powerful🎩

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Powerful** property, _spells_ you cast deal an additional `+1` damage of the spell's damage type.
