# 🎩5e - Soothing🎩

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Soothing** property, _healing spells_ you cast _heal_ an additional `2` points of damage to the target.
