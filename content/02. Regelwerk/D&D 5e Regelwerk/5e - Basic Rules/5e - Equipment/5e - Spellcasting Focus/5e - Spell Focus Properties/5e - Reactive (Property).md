# 🎩5e - Reactive🎩

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Reactive** property, <u>once per turn</u>, when you roll dice for an effect of a _spell_ you cast, be it damage or healing, and you roll the maximum number on one of the dice, you may roll <u>one</u> more die and add it to the total of your spell.
