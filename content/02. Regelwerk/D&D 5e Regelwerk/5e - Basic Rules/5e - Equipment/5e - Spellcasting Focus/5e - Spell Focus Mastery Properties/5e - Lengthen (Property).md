# 🎩5e - Lengthen🎩

While holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] with the **Lengthen** property, _spells_ you cast with a _range_ greater than <u>5 feet</u>, that require you to make a <u>spell</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], have their range increase by <u>30 feet</u>, while _spells_ you cast with a _range_ of **Touch**, gain a _range_ of <u>10 feet</u>.
