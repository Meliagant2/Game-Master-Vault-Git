# ☠️5e - Grappled☠️

You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Immobilized|☠️Immobilized]].

- You can't move.
- You have **DISADV** on <u>DEX Saves</u>.
- You have **DISADV** on <u>attack rolls</u> against any target other than the grappler.

While you are grappling a creature, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]].

###### Ending Grappled

**<u>Contested Check:</u>** You can spend an **Action** to make an <u>Acrobatics</u> or <u>Athletics Check</u> against the Grappler's Maneuver Save DC. _**Success:**_ The Grapple immediately ends.

**<u>Falling:</u>** The Grappler is unable to carry your weight when you begin falling, provided they aren’t also falling with you.

**<u>Forced Movement:</u>** An effect forcibly moves you or the Grappler, causing you to move outside the Grappler’s reach. If the effect requires a Contested Check or Save, the Grappler makes the Check or Save instead of you. If the effect targets both you and the Grappler, the Grappler makes 1 Check or Save for both of you. _**Success:**_ The targets are not moved. **Failure:** The targets are moved.

**<u>Incapacitated:</u>** The Grappler becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]]
