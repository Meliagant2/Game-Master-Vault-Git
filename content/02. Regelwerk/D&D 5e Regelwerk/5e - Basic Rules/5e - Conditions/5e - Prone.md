# ☠️5e - Prone☠️

You’re subjected to the following effects:

- You have **DisADV** on Attacks, Ranged Attacks have **DisADV** against you, and Melee Attacks against you  have **ADV**.
- Until you stand up, your only movement option is to Crawl, which costs an extra 1 ft. of movement for every 1 ft. moved.

#### Prone in the Air

When you become Prone, you immediately enter an Uncontrolled Fall unless you’re supported by a solid surface.

**<u>Ending Prone</u>**
**Standing up:** You can end being Prone by spending half of your movement to stand up.
