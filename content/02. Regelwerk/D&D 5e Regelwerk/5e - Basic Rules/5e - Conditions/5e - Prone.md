# ☠️5e - Prone☠️

You’re subjected to the following effects:

- You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] (**DISADV** on Attack Rolls).
- <u>Ranged Attacks</u> have **DisADV** against you,
- You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] (Attacks against you have **ADV**) against <u>Melee Attacks</u>.
- Until you stand up, your only movement option is to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Crawling|🏃‍♀️Crawl]].

#### Prone in the Air

When you become Prone, you immediately enter an Uncontrolled [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏃‍♀️Fall]] unless you’re supported by a solid surface, or you hover magically.

###### Ending Prone

**<u>Standing up:</u>** You can end being Prone by spending half of your movement to stand up. If your Speed is `0`, you can't stand up.
