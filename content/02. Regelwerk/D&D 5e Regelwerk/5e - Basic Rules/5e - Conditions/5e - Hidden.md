# ☠️5e - Hidden☠️

You are Hidden from a Creature while you are both [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unseen|☠️Unseen]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unheard|☠️Unheard]] by them.

- Your location is unknown to creatures you’re Hidden from.
- You have **ADV** on Attacks against Creatures you are Hidden from.
- If you are Hidden when you roll Initiative, you have **ADV** on the roll.

**<u>Ending Hidden</u>**
You stop being Hidden, when either the conditions for your <u>Unseen</u> Condition or your <u>Unheard</u> Condition end. This can happen in multiple ways:
**Attack:** You make an attack roll.

**Being observed:** A Creature makes a <u>Perception Check</u> to spot you and succeeds on their Roll.

**Line of Sight:** You step into the line of sight of the Creature you are hidden from.

**Noise:** You make a sound louder than a whisper or you cast a spell with a Verbal component
