# ☠️5e - Fatigue☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You gain a penalty equal to `2 × X` on all <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, <u>CON</u> <u>d20</u> rolls you make.
- Your Speed is reduced by  a number of feet equal to `5 × your Fatigue level`.
- Reaching <u>3 Fatigue</u> gives you an <u>Injury</u>.
- Reaching <u>5 Fatigue</u> gives you a Permanent <u>Injury</u>.

###### Ending Exhaustion

**<u>Long Rest:</u>** Finishing a **Long Rest** removes `1` Stack of both <u>Fatigue</u> and <u>Strife</u>.
