# ☠️5e - Fatigue☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You gain a penalty equal to `X` on all <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, <u>CON</u> <u>d20</u> rolls you make.
- Reaching <u>5 Fatigue</u> gives you an <u>Injury</u>.
- Reaching <u>10 Fatigue</u> gives you a Permanent <u>Injury</u>.

**<u>Ending Exhaustion</u>**
**Long Rest:** Finishing a Long Rest removes 1 Stack of both <u>Fatigue</u> and <u>Strife</u>.
