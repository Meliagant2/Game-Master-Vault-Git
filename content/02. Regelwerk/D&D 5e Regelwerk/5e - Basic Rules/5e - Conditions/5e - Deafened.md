# ☠️5e - Flanking☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You can't Hear (see [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unheard|☠️Unheard]])
  - You automatically fail <u>ability checks</u> that require hearing.
  - Effects that require you to hear automatically fail.
  - Creatures that are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Tactical Positioning/5e - Flanking|🏃‍♀️Flanking]] you have **ADV** on Attack Rolls against you.
