# ☠️5e - Blinded☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You can't see (see [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unseen|☠️Unseen]])
  - You automatically fail ability checks that require sight.
  - You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] (Attacks against you have **ADV**).
  - You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] (You have **DISADV** on Attacks).
- All Terrain is considered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] for you unless you're guided by another Creature.
