# ☠️5e - Reverberation☠️

**Reverberation** is a _stackable_ condition. A creature can have a maximum of `3` stacks of **Reverberation**. Creatures <u>Immune</u> to <u>Thunder</u> damage are also <u>Immune</u> against the **Reverberation** condition.

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You have a `-1` penalty to <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, and <u>CON</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] per remaining turn.
- When you have `3` stacks of **Reverberation** and take any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]], you take `1d4` <u>Thunder</u> damage and make a **DC10 CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]]. _**Success or Failure:**_ All stacks of **Reverberation** are removed.

###### Ending Reverberation

**<u>End of Turn:</u>** At the end of your turn, you remove `1` stack of **Reverberation**.

**<u>Taking damage while having 3+ stacks:</u>** After making your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] for having `3` stacks of **Reverberation**, all stacks of the condition are removed.
