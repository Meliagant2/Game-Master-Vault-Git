# ☠️5e - Burning☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You take `1d8` <u>Fire</u> damage at the start of each of your turns.
- You emit [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>5-foot Radius Sphere</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for another <u>5 feet</u>.

###### Ending Burning

**<u>Extinguish:</u>** A creature can spend an **Action** to extinguish burning fire on themselves or another Creature within <u>5 feet</u>.

**<u>Repeated Save:</u>** At the end of your turn you make a **DEX Save** against the **DC of the effect that started the Condition** or, if there isn't any, against the attacker's **Maneuver Save DC**, or **12** if there is neither. _**Success:**_ The condition ends.

**<u>Water:</u>** The Condition ends when you’re doused by at least 1 gallon (4 liters) of water or fully immersed in water.
