# ☠️5e - Burning☠️

You take `1d8` <u>Fire</u> damage at the start of each of your turns.

**<u>Ending Burning</u>**

**Extinguish:** A creature can spend an **Action** to extinguish burning fire on themselves or another Creature within 5 ft.
**Repeated Save:** At the end of your turn you make a **DEX Save** against the DC of the effect that started the Condition or, if there isn't any, against the attacker's Maneuver Save DC, or 12 if there is neither.
**Water:** The Condition ends when you’re doused by at least 1 gallon (4 liters) of water or fully immersed in water.
