# ☠️5e - Petrified☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You are transformed, along with any nonmagical objects you are wearing and carrying, into a solid inanimate substance (usually stone). Your weight increases by a factor of ten, and you cease aging.
- You’re [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]].
  - You can’t speak.
  - You can’t take **Actions**, **Bonus Actions** or **Reactions**.
  - Your Concentration is broken.
  - You have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]].
- Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is `0` and can't increase.
- You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] (Attacks against you have **ADV**).
- You automatically fail <u>STR</u> and <u>DEX Saves</u>.
- You have <u>Resistance</u> to all damage.
- You have <u>Immunity</u> to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition.
