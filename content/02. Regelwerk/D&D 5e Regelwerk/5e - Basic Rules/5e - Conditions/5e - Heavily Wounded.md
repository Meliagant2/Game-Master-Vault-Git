# ☠️5e - Heavily Wounded☠️

You can't regain HP by any means.

**<u>Ending Heavily Wounded</u>**
**Healed:** You are subjected to an effect that restores HP equal to or higher than two times your Character Level.
**Medicine Action:** A creature can spend an Action to make a **DC20 Medicine Check** on themselves or another Creature within 5 ft. _**Success:**_ The Condition ends.
**True Rest:** For 5 days you take long rests without engaging a combat or doing hard work.
