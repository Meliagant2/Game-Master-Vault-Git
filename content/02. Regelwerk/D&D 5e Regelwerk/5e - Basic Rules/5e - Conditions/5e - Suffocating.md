# ☠️5e - Suffocating☠️

A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to `1 + CON` (minimum of 30 seconds). Once a creature is out of breath, it begins **suffocating** and gains 1 [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]] and 1 [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Strife|☠️Strife]] at <u>the end of each of its turns</u>. When a Creature can breathe again, it removes all levels of **Fatigue** and **Strife** gained from suffocating.

While suffocating, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Silenced|☠️Silenced]].
