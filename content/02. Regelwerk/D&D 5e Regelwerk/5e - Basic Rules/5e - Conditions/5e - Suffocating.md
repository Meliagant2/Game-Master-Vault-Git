# ☠️5e - Suffocating☠️

If a creature goes into a scenario where it would **suffocate**, it makes a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CON Skills/5e - Hold Breath|💃Hold Breath]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] to determine the time it can hold its breath. Depending on the situation (low stress, like diving for clams; high stress, like in combat), it can hold its breath for a number of minutes, or rounds:

| Hold Breath DC | Minutes (willingly) | Rounds (unwillingly) |
| :------------: | :-----------------: | :------------------: |
|      1-9       |         1/2         |          0           |
|     10-11      |          1          |          1           |
|     12-13      |          2          |          1           |
|     14-15      |          3          |          2           |
|     16-17      |          4          |          2           |
|     18-19      |          5          |          3           |
|     20-21      |          6          |          3           |
|     22-23      |          7          |          4           |
|     24-25      |          8          |          4           |
|     26-27      |          9          |          5           |
|     28-29      |         10          |          5           |
|      30+       |         11          |          6           |

Once a creature is out of breath, it begins **suffocating** and gains 1 [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] at <u>the end of each of its turns</u>. When a creature can breathe again, it removes all levels of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] gained from suffocating.

While **suffocating**, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Silenced|☠️Silenced]].
