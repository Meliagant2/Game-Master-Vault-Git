# ☠️5e - Lightly Wounded☠️

If you would regain HP by any means, the number of HP you regain is halved.

**<u>Ending Lightly Wounded</u>**
**Healed:** You are subjected to an effect that restores your HP.
**Medicine Action:** A creature can spend an Action to make a **DC10 Medicine Check** on themselves or another Creature within 5 ft. _**Success:**_ The Condition ends.
