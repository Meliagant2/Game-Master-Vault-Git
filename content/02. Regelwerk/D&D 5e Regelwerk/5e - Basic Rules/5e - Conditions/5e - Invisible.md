# ☠️5e - Invisible☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You have **ADV** on Attacks against Creatures that can’t see you.
- Creatures that can’t see you have **DisADV** on Attacks against you.
- If you are Invisible when you roll Initiative, you have **ADV** on the roll.

###### Ending Invisible

You stop being invisible if one of the following applies:

- You make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]].
- You deal [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]] to a creature.
- You take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action.
