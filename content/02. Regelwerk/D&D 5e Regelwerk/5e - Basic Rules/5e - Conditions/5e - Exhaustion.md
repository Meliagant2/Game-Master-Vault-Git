# ☠️5e - Exhaustion☠️

Exhaustion is split up into two sub-Conditions: Fatigue and Strife. Both can go from `1 to 5`.  If a feature or an effect mentions **Exhaustion**, it affects both Fatigue and Strife at the same time.

#### [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]]

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You gain a penalty equal to `2 × X` on all <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, <u>CON</u> <u>d20</u> rolls you make.
- Your Speed is reduced by  a number of feet equal to `5 × your Fatigue level`.
- Reaching <u>3 Fatigue</u> gives you an <u>Injury</u>.
- Reaching <u>5 Fatigue</u> gives you a Permanent <u>Injury</u>.

#### [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Strife|☠️Strife]]

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You gain a penalty equal to `2 × X` on all <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, <u>CHA</u> <u>d20</u> rolls you make.
- Reaching <u>3 Strife</u> gives you a <u>Minor Mental Illness</u>.
- Reaching <u>5 Strife</u> gives you a <u>Major Mental Illness</u>.

###### Ending Exhaustion

**<u>Long Rest:</u>** Finishing a **Long Rest** removes `1` Stack of both <u>Fatigue</u> and <u>Strife</u>.
