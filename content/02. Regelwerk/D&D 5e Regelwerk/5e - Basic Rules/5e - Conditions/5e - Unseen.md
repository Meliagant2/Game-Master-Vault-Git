# ☠️5e - Unseen☠️

You are Unseen by a creature while you’re imperceivable to their visual senses, such as when you’re Fully Concealed, you’re Invisible, or they’re Blinded. While Unseen, you’re subjected to the following effects:

- You have **ADV** on Attacks against Creatures that can’t see you.
- Creatures that can’t see you have **DisADV** on Attacks against you.
