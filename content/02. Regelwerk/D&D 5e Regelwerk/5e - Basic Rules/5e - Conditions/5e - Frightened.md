# ☠️5e - Frightened☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You can’t willingly move closer to the source of your fear.
- You have **DisADV** on all <u>Attack Rolls</u> and <u>Skill Checks</u> while the source of your fear is in line of sight.
