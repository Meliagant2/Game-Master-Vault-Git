# ☠️5e - Bleeding☠️

You take `1d6` <u>True</u> damage at the start of each of your turns.

Bleeding can be stacked. A creature can be under the effect of up to `3` stacks of bleeding.

###### Ending Bleeding

**<u>Healed:</u>** When you are subjected to an effect that restores your HP, you remove <u>one</u> stack of **Bleeding** for every `5` points healed. If you are healed by `less than 5` HP, but at least `1`, you still remove <u>one</u> stack of the condition.

**<u>Medicine Action:</u>** A creature can spend an Action to make a **DC10 Medicine Check** on themselves or another Creature within <u>5 feet</u>. _**Success:**_ <u>One</u> stack of this Condition is removed. _**Success (each 5):**_ <u>One</u> more stack of the condition is removed.

**<u>Repeated Save:</u>** At the end of your turn you make a **CON Save** against the **DC of the effect that started the Condition** or, if there isn't any, against the attacker's **Maneuver Save DC**. _**Success:**_ <u>One</u> stack of this Condition is removed. _**Success (each 5):**_ <u>One</u> more stack of the condition is removed.
