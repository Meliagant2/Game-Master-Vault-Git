# ☠️5e - Bleeding☠️

You take `1d6` True damage at the start of each of your turns.

**<u>Ending Bleeding</u>**

**Healed:** You are subjected to an effect that restores your HP.
**Medicine Action:** A creature can spend an Action to make a **DC10 Medicine Check** on themselves or another Creature within <u>5 ft</u>. _**Success:**_ The Condition ends.
**Repeated Save:** At the end of your turn you make a **CON Save** against the DC of the effect that started the Condition or, if there isn't any, against the attacker's Maneuver Save DC.
