# ☠️5e - Terrified☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You must spend your turns trying to move as far away as you can from the source as possible.
- The only **Action** you can take is the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] Action to try to run away, or the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dodge|⚔️Dodge]] Action if you are prevented from moving or there’s nowhere farther to move.
