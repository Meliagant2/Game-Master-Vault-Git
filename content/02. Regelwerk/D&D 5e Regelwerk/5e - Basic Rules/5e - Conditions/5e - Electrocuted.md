# ☠️5e - Electrocuted☠️

**Electrocuted** is a _stackable_ condition. A creature can have a maximum of `5` stacks of **Electrocuted**. Creatures <u>Immune</u> to <u>Lightning</u> damage are also <u>Immune</u> against the **Electrocuted** condition.

You are subjected to the following effects:

- At <u>the start of your turn</u> you take `1d4` <u>Lightning</u> damage.

###### Ending Electrocuted

**<u>End of Turn:</u>** At <u>the end of each of your turns</u>, you lose one stack of **Electrocuted**, as long as you don't stand in water.
