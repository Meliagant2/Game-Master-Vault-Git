# ☠️5e - Shocked☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You cannot take **Reactions**, or, if you have more than one **Reaction** available to you, you lose one of them.
- You have **DISADV** on <u>DEX</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].
