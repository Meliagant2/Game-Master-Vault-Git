# ☠️5e - Stunned☠️

You are subjected to the following effects:

- You’re [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]].
  - You can't move or speak.
  - You can't use **Actions**, **Bonus Actions** or **Reactions**.
- You automatically fail <u>STR</u> and <u>DEX Saves</u>.
- Attacks against you have **ADV**.
