# ☠️5e - Intimidated☠️

While you can _see_ your source of fear, you are subjected to the following effects:

- You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] (**DISADV** on Attack Rolls).
- You are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Impaired|☠️Impaired]] (**DISADV** on Skill Checks).

A creature immune against [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] is also immune against Intimidated.
