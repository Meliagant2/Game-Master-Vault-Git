# 😴5e - Long Rest😴

A Long Rest is a period of extended [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Downtime/5e - Downtime|🧖‍♀️Downtime]]—at least <u>8 hours</u>—available to any creature. During a **Long Rest**, you _sleep_ for at least <u>6 hours</u> and perform no more than <u>2 hours</u> of **light activity**, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch.

During _sleep_, you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unconscious|☠️Unconscious]] condition. After you finish a **Long Rest**, you must wait at least <u>16 hours</u> before starting another one.

**<u>Benefits of the Rest:</u>** To start a **Long Rest**, you must have at least _1 Hit Point_. When you finish the rest, you gain the following benefits:

- **<u>Regain All HP:</u>** You regain all lost _Hit Points_ and all spent _Hit Point Dice_. If your _Hit Point maximum_ was reduced, it returns to normal, unless the feature that reduced them says otherwise.
- **<u>Ability Scores Restored:</u>** If any of your ability scores were reduced, they return to normal.
- **<u>Exhaustion Reduced:</u>** If you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] condition, its level decreases by `1`.
- **<u>Special Feature:</u>** Some features are recharged by a **Long Rest**. If you have such a feature, it recharges in the way specified in its description.

**<u>Interrupting the Rest:</u>** A Long Rest is stopped by the following interruptions:

- Rolling [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]].
- Casting a spell with a _Spell Slot_.
- Taking any damage.
- <u>1 hour</u> of walking or other physical exertion.

If you rested at least <u>1 hour</u> before the interruption you gain the benefits of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]].

You can resume a **Long Rest** immediately after an interruption. If you do so, the rest requires <u>1 additional hour</u> per interruption to finish.
