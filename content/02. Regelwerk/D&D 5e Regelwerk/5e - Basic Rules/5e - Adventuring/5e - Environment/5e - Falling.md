# 🏃‍♀️5e - Falling🏃‍♀️

A falling creature descends <u>500 feet</u> at <u>the start of each of its turns</u>. When a creature **falls**, it takes <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and lands [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]]. The amount of damage the creature takes is calculated as follows:

**<u>The ceature falls less than or equal to 50 ft:</u>** The creature suffers `1d6` damage per 10 ft. it falls.
**<u>The creature falls between 50 and 150 ft:</u>** The creature suffers `2d6` damage per 10 ft. it falls.
**<u>The creature falls between 150 and 300 ft:</u>** The creature suffers `3d6` damage per 10 ft. it falls.
**<u>The creature falls 300 ft. or more:</u>** The creature instantly _dies_.

A creature that falls into water takes <u>half damage</u>, or no damage if it dives with a successful <u>Athletics check</u> (DC equal to the distance it falls divided by 5).
