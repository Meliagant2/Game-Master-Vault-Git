# 🏃‍♀️5e - Fly Speed🏃‍♀️

A **Fly Speed** can be used to travel through the air. While you have a **Fly Speed**, you can stay aloft until you _land_, _fall_, or _die_.

A creature **flying** by natural means (like wings), that takes damage from a creature other than itself, must succeed on a <u>DEX</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] to keep flying. The [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] for that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] is equal to `10` or `1/2 x the damage taken`, whichever is higher. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏔️Falling]].

A creature that gets the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition while **flying**, stops doing so and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏔️Falling]].

A creature that is flying and **hovering** doesn't need to make the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] to keep aloft and also doesn't [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏔️Fall]], when [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].
