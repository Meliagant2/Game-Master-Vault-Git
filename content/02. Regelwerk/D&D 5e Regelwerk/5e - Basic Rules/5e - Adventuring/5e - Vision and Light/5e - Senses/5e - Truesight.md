# 👁️5e - Truesight👁️

Some especially powerful creatures, like particularly old dragons, have truesight. This allows a creature to see in normal or magical [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]], perceive [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] creatures and objects, automatically detect **illusions** and succeed on its <u>saving throw</u> against them, and perceive the original form of a <u>shapechanger</u> or creature that is transformed by magic.

A creature with truesight can also see into the <u>Ethereal Plane</u>.
