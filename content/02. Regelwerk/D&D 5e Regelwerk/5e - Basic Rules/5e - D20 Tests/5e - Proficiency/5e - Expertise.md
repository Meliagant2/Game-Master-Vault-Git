# 🎲5e - Expertise🎲

**Expertise** is a feature that enhances your use of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]]. When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]] in which you have **Expertise**, you add a bonus to the roll equal to your `expertise level`.

### Expertise Level

**<u>Maximum Expertise level:</u>** The maximum _Expertise level_ is `5`, as represented on your _Character Sheet_.

**<u>Expertise Cap:</u>** At any point, the maximum level of **Expertise** levels you can put into a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability modifier]] that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]] is associated with. _For example:_ If you have a <u>+3 STR score</u> and a <u>+2 DEX Score</u>, you can have a maximum of `3` **Expertise** in _Athletics, Breaking and Intimidation_ and a maximum of `2` **Expertise** in each _Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand and Stealth_. A _negative_ modifier in a score is treated the same as a `+0` for the levels of **Expertise** you might have in a Skill.

Some features might allow you to increase your Cap.

**<u>Gaining a Skill Proficiency you already have:</u>** Whenever you gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]], in which you are already _proficient_, you gain one level of **Expertise** instead.

**<u>Gaining Expertise when already Capped:</u>** Whenever you gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]], in which you are already _proficient_, and in which you already have your maximum levels of **Expertise**, you can choose another skill to gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in.
