# 5e - Changelog 1

### Heritages

#### All Heritages

- If a Feature of your Heritage would force a Creature to make a Save (e.g. the Drakari's Breath Weapon), it no longer specifies how the DC for that Feature is calculated and instead uses your Maneuver Save DC or your Spell save DC. Some Features (like the Drakari's Breath Weapon) can use either, some use one of them.

#### Dwarf

- **<u>Toughest of them all</u>** -> **<u>Slow and Steady</u>**: Your movement speed cannot be reduced by difficult terrain (The GM might rule, that in specific instances this Feature doesn't work as written).
  - **Formerly:** Your movement speed cannot be reduced.

### Destinies

#### Devotion Destiny

- **<u>Inspiration Feature: Selfless Aid:</u>** You can now take the Help Action as a Bonus Action as part of the Feature.

#### Fire Destiny

- **Fulfillment Feature: Blazing Soul:** Increased duration for the Ignite Feature, Scaling damage (PB x d4 instead of 2d4).

#### Metamorphosis Destiny

- **<u>Inspiration Feature: Unearthly Diplomacy:</u>** Creatures of a CR lower than your level might by charmed by this Feature for the duration.

### Classes

#### Adept

- **<u>Level 2: Exertion Focus</u>**: The Saving throw now equals your Maneuver Save DC and is no longer based on WIS.

#### Fighter

- **<u>Fighting Style: Great Weapon Fighting</u>**: You no longer reroll 1s or 2s on the damage die, instead you treat 1s and 2s as a 3.
- **<u>Brute Subclass:</u>** Removed, since for some reason 3/6 subclass Features were the exact same -> no real subclass

#### Paladin

- **<u>Divine Lesson: Exemplary</u>**: You now gain an expertise die on all acrobatics and athletics checks, not only to climb, jump, run, and swim.
