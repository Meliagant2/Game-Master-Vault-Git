# 5e - Changelog - V 1

Why did I write everything in english? Because I dislike having 2 languages in one document.

## General Changes

_**<u>THERE ARE MORE CHANGES THAN THIS, BUT THESE ARE THE CHANGES DIRECTLY INFLUENCING YOU, OR THAT ARE GENERALLY GOOD TO KNOW!!!</u>**_

### New Character Sheet

There is a new designed Character sheet to hold all the new information. You can find that on my discord Server or as a PDF in the Signal Group Chat.

### Actions

**<u>Regain Exertion Action:</u>** Instead of taking 1 Minute to regain Exertion, it now takes only an Action.

### Conditions

**<u>Exhaustion:</u>** There might now be effects targeting Exhaustion. In that case, both Fatigue and Strife are affected.
**<u>Fatigue:</u>** Now scales from 1-5 instead of 1-10. 1 level doesn't reduce a d20 roll by 1, but by 2 and for each level of Fatigue, the creatures speed is reduced by 5 feet.
**<u>Strife:</u>** Now scales from 1-5 instead of 1-10. 1 level doesn't reduce a d20 roll by 1, but by 2.
**<u>Grappled:</u>** A grappled creature now has **DISADV** on attacks against creatures other than the grappler.

### Feats

**<u>Feat Categories:</u>** Now categorized into Origin, Class, General and Skill Feats. Each character gains one Class, General and a Skill feat every 2nd level (See below).

### Languages

**<u>Language Mastery:</u>** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Languages|Languages]] now have 3 levels of mastery: Speaking, Reading, Signing. Whenever a character learns a language, they increase that language's mastery by one level, unless stated otherwise.

### Skills and Proficiency

_**<u>Especially with Skill Proficiencies, it might be easiest to rebuild the character from ground up to not make any mistakes.</u>**_

**<u>Skill Categorization:</u>** Skills are now categorized under the ability scores. They can still be used with different ability scores, but this makes it easier to call for checks.
**<u>Expertise Die:</u>** Removed. Instead there is a new system for [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|Expertise]] levels. Simply put: Each point of Expertise equals a +1 in that skill to a max of the associated Ability score or `+5`, whichever is lower.
**<u>Skill Specialties:</u>** Removed. This includes Scent specialty for Perception, Swimming for Athletics, Fey for Arcana etc.
**<u>Animal Handling:</u>** Removed
**<u>Breaking:</u>** New STR Skill.
**<u>Endurance:</u>** New CON Skill.
**<u>Hold Breath:</u>** New CON Skill.
**<u>Intimidation:</u>** Now a STR Skill, but I will still allow using CHA for it instead.
**<u>Medicine:</u>** Now an INT check instead of WIS.
**<u>Occultism:</u>** New INT Knowledge Skill.
**<u>Revelry:</u>** New CON Skill.

### Maneuvers

**<u>Combat Traditions:</u>** Condensed all 14 Combat Traditions (With about 15 maneuvers each) down to 6 core traditions (With 30 maneuvers each). In the future there will be more "special traditions".
**<u>Basic Melee Damage:</u>** Now equals `1d2 + STR` instead of `1 + STR`
**<u>Basic Maneuvers: </u>** Every Basic Maneuver is now a contested Check: Athletics vs Acrobatics or Athletics.
**<u>Maneuver Activation Time:</u>** Each maneuver now has an activation time of "Attack, Action, Bonus Action, Reaction". If a maneuver has an activation time of "Attack", you add it to one attack during your Attack Action. If it is an "Action", you use this maneuver, but can't add any more maneuvers to potential attacks made during this maneuver. The rule still is: Only one Maneuver per attack.
**<u>Reworked and removed Maneuvers:</u>** I reworked and removed lots of maneuvers. I will specify each and every one that affects you in your personal changelogs below.
**<u>Stances:</u>** Rulings about length and maintaining Stances clarified. Stances now require a kind of **concentration** check to keep them active after taking damage.

You can imbue yourself with a primal power called **Rage**, a force that grants you extraordinary might and resilience. You can enter it as a **Bonus Action** if you aren't wearing [[02. - Make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] against an enemy.
- Force an enemy to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].
- Take a **Bonus Action** to _extend_ your **Rage**.

Each time the **Rage** is _extended_, it lasts until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. You can maintain a **Rage** for up to <u>10 minutes</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can enter your **Rage** the number of times shown for your _Barbarian level_ in the <u>Rages column</u> of the <u>Barbarian Features table</u> (**This means you have 2 uses of Rage**). You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
