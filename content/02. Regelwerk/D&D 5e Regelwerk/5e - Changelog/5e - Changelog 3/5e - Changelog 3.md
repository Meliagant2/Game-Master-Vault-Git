# 5e - Changelog 3

## Combat Maneuver Changes

### General Maneuver Changes

**Stances:** Rulings about length and maintaining Stances clarified. Stances now require a kind of concentration check to keep them active after taking damage.
**Regaining Exertion:** Instead of taking 1 Minute and spending a Hit Die to regain Exertion, it now takes only an Action.
**Combat Tradition Rework:** Condensed all 14 Combat Traditions (With about 15 maneuvers each) down to 6 core traditions (With 30 maneuvers each). In the future there will be more "special traditions".
**Reworked and removed Maneuvers:** I reworked and removed lots of maneuvers. I will specify each and every one that affects you in your personal changelogs.
**Zealous Stance:** Renamed to "Reckless Stance"
**Faith within:** Removed
**Stunning Assault:** Removed
**Knockdown Assault:** Duration increased to "end of your next turn"
**Basic Melee Damage:** Now equals `1d2 + STR` instead of `1 + STR`

### Basic Maneuver Changes

**All:** Every Basic Maneuver is now a contested Check: Athletics vs Acrobatics or Athletics.
**Overrun:** Overrun is now always an Attack replacement.

## Spellcasting Changes

### General Spellcasting Changes

**Spell Sources:** Added Occult as a 4th Spell list. Now there are Arcane, Divine, Occult and Primal

### Spells added and removed to/from Spell lists

**Arcane List:** Absorb Elements, Chromatic Orb, Color Spray, Witch Bolt, Magnify Gravity, Hideous Laughter, Concussive Burst, Spellfire Flare, Captivate Undead
**Divine List:** Chill Touch, Cleanse, Friends, Holy Ward, Message, Mind Sliver, Pestilence, Compelled Duel (removed), Find Familiar, Wardaway, Captivate Undead
**Primal List:** Find Familiar, Magnify Gravity, Thorn Armor, Cloak of Shadows

### Removed Spells

**Chaos Bolt:** Integrated into Chromatic Orb
**Control Flames:** Integrated into Elementalism
**Frost Fingers:** Integrated into Cone of Cold
**Glitterbeam:** Starry Wisp is basically the same spell
**Mold Earth:** Integrated into Elementalism
**Shape Water:** Integrated into Elementalism

### Spell Name Changes

**Magic Stone:** Now Magic Fling

## Spell Effect Changes

**Bardcraft:** Different Changes to clarify the spell. Renamed to "Illusory Instrument".
**Barkskin:** Now a Bonus Action instead of an Action.
**Black Tentacles:** Added Upcast option for increased damage.
**Counterspell:** Now a contested spellcasting check to make it more fair.
**Darkvision:** Added Upcast option for multiple targets.
**Death Ward:** Added Upcast option for multiple targets.
**Dimension Door:** Added Upcast option for multiple targets.
**Faerie Fire:** Changed the Area from a 20 foot Cube to a 10 foot radius Sphere.
**Freedom of the Waves:** Added an upcast option for more damage.
**Hunger of Hadar:** Added an upcast option for more damage.
**Lesser Restoration:** Now a Bonus Action instead of an Action.
**Prayer of Healing:** Now gives the benefits of a Short Rest instead of simply healing (which it still does).
**Ray of Enfeeblement:** Added an upcast option for multiple targets.
**Shatter:** Increased Area to 15 ft. radius.
**Silvery Barbs:** Removed Upcast option.
**Slow:** Area changed from 40 ft. Cube to 20 ft. radius sphere.
**Spirit Guardians:** Changed the triggers, when the spell deals damage. Now the caster can move into a creature to deal the damage.

## Spell Level Changes

**Cone of Cold:** Reduced Level from 5 to 1. Added Upcast options.

## Spell School Changes

**Blink:** Transmutation -> Conjuration
**Speak with Dead:** Necromancy -> Divination

## Weapon Changes

### Spellcasting Foci

**Mastery Properties:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Foci]] now act as weapons with properties and mastery properties.

### Weapons

**Weapon Masteries:** Each weapon now has a Mastery Property, which can be used, if you have the Weapon mastery feature (every martial class has that).
