Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]] you make to locate or track the creature.
- When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] against the target, the next creature that makes an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against the Target before <u>the start of your next turn</u>, adds `1d8` to both their [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]].

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as shown in the <u>Ranger Class Table</u> (For you as a **Multiclassed** character, this means "`2`"). You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
