# ⚔️5e - Dash⚔️

When you take the Dash action, you gain extra movement for the current turn. The increase equals your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] after applying any modifiers.

With a Speed of <u>30 feet</u>, for example, you can move up to <u>60 feet</u> on your turn if you Dash. If your Speed of <u>30 feet</u> is reduced to <u>15 feet</u>, you can move up to <u>30 feet</u> this turn if you Dash.

If you have a <u>special speed</u>, such as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]], you can use that speed instead of your Speed when you take this action. You choose which speed to use each time you take it.
