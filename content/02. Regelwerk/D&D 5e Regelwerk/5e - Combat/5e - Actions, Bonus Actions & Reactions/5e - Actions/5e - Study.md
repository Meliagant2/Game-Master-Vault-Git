# ⚔️5e - Study⚔️

When you take the Study action, you make an **Intelligence** check to study your memory, a book, a clue, or another source of knowledge and call to mind an important piece of information about it.

The Areas of Knowledge table suggests which skills are applicable to various areas of knowledge.

###### Areas of Knowledge

| Skill         | Areas                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arcana        | Spells, magic items, eldritch symbols, magical traditions, planes of existence, and certain creatures (Constructs, Monstrosities, and Oozes) |
| Culture       | People and ancient civilizations                                                                                                             |
| Engineering   | Mechanical apparatures, traps, structural integrity of buildings                                                                             |
| History       | Historic events, wars, and certain creatures (Dragons, Giants and Humanoids)                                                                 |
| Investigation | Traps, ciphers, riddles, and gadgetry                                                                                                        |
| Nature        | Terrain, flora, weather, and certain creatures (Beasts, Elementals, Fey, and Plants)                                                         |
| Occultism     | Eldritch symbols, obscure knowledge, dark magic, and certain creatures (Aberrations, and Undead)                                             |
| Religion      | Deities, religious hierarchies and rites, holy symbols, cults, and certain creatures (Celestials, and Fiends)                                |
