# ⚔️5e - Attack⚔️

When you take the Magic action, you cast a <u>Spell</u> that has a casting time of an action or use a <u>feature</u> or <u>magic item</u> that requires a Magic action to be activated.

If you cast a spell that has a casting time of <u>1 minute</u> or longer, you must take the Magic action on each turn of that casting, and you must maintain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] while you do so. If your Concentration is broken, the spell fails, but you don't expend a spell slot.
