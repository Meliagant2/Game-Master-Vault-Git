# ⚔️5e - Tumble⚔️

You can use an **Action** or **Bonus Action** to Tumble through a hostile creature’s space by deftly diving or rolling past. Make an **Acrobatics Check** against the target’s [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Success:**_ You can move through the hostile creature’s space once this turn.

If you are _smaller than the target_, you have **ADV**. If you are _larger than the target_, you have **DISADV** (though if you are _two or more sizes larger than the target_, you can move through the hostile creature’s space without making a check).

You still provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] if you move beyond a creature’s reach.
