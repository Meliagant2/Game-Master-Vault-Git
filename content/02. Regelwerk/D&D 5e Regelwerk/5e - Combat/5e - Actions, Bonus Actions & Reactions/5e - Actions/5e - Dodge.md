# ⚔️5e - Dodge⚔️

If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has **DISADV** if you can see the attacker, and you make <u>DEX Saves</u> with **ADV**.

You lose these benefits if you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition or if your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is 0.
