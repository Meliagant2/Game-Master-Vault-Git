# ⚔️5e - Disengage⚔️

If you take the Disengage action, your movement doesn't provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] for the rest of the current turn.
