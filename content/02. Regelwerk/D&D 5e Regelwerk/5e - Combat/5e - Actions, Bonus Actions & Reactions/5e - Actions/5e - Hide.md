# ⚔️5e - Hide⚔️

When you take the Hide action, you make a **Dexterity (Stealth)** check against the **Passive Perception** of a Creature, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unseen|☠️Unseen]] from. _**Success:**_ You are also [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unheard|☠️Unheard]]. Having both the <u>Unseen</u> and <u>Unheard</u> Conditions gives you the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]] condition against a target.

A Creature can take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Search|⚔️Search]] action to attempt to find you. If the result of that Action is equal to or higher than your Stealth check, you are no longer <u>Hidden</u> from that creature.
