# ⚔️5e - Unarmed Strike⚔️

Instead of using a <u>weapon</u> to make a <u>melee attack</u>, you can use a punch, kick, head-butt, or similar forceful blow. In game terms, this is an Unarmed Strike—a melee attack that involves you using your body to damage, grapple, or shove a target within <u>5 feet</u> of you.

**<u>Attack Roll:</u>** You make an attack roll against the target. Your bonus to the roll equals `your STR + your PB`.

**<u>Damage:</u>** On a hit, the target takes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Basic Melee Damage|💔Basic Melee Damage]].

**<u>Basic Maneuver:</u>** While making a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an <u>Unarmed Strike</u>, you can use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Basic Maneuvers|🤺Basic Maneuvers]]. Unless otherwise noted, using a basic maneuver replaces an attack made on your turn.
