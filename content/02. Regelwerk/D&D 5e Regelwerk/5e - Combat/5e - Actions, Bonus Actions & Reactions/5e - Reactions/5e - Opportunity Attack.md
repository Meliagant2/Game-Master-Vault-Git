# ⚔️5e - Opportunity Attack⚔️

You can make an Opportunity Attack when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its <u>action</u>, its <u>Bonus Action</u>, its <u>Reaction</u>, or one of its <u>speeds</u>. To make the Opportunity Attack, take a **Reaction** to make one melee [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] with a <u>weapon</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach.
