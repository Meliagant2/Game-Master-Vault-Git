# ☄️5e - Magnify Gravity☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft./ 10 ft. Sphere |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

The gravity in a <u>10-foot-radius sphere</u> centered on a point you can see within range increases for a moment. Each creature in the sphere on the turn when you cast the spell makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ A creature takes `2d8` <u>Force</u> damage, and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] until the end of <u>its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

Until the start of your next turn, any object that isn’t being worn or carried in the sphere requires a successful <u>Strength check</u> against your <u>spell save DC</u> to pick up or move.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 1.
