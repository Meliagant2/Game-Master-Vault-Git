# ☄️5e - Feather Fall☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|R (You or a creature you can see within 60 feet of you falls)|
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, M (a small feather or piece of down) |
> |**Duration:**|PT1M |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

Choose up to five falling creatures within range. A falling creature's rate of descent slows to <u>60 feet</u> per round until the spell ends. If a creature lands before the spell ends, the creature takes no damage from the fall, and the spell ends for that creature.
