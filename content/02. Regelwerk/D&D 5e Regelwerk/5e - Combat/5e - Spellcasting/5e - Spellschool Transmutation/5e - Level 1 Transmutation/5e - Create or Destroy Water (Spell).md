# ☄️5e - Create or Destroy Water☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a mix of water and sand) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Primal |

You do one of the following:

**<u>Create Water:</u>** You create up to 35 litres of clean water within range in an open container. Alternatively, the water falls as rain in a <u>30-foot Cube</u> within range, extinguishing exposed flames there.

**<u>Destroy Water:</u>** You destroy up to 35 litres of water in an open container within range. Alternatively, you destroy fog in a <u>30-foot Cube</u> within range.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You create or destroy 35 additional litres of water, or the size of the Cube increases by <u>5 feet</u>, for each spell slot level above 1.
