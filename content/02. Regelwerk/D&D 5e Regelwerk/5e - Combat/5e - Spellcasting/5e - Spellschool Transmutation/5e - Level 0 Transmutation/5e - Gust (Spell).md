# ☄️5e - Gust☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Class List:**| |

You seize the air and compel it to create one of the following effects at a point you can see within range:

- One Medium or smaller creature that you choose makes a **STR Save**.  _**Failure:**_ The Target is pushed up to <u>5 feet</u> away from you. _**Failure (by 5):**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].
- You create a small blast of air capable of moving one object that is neither held nor carried and that weighs no more than 2,5 kg. The object is pushed up to <u>10 feet</u> away from you. It isn’t pushed with enough force to cause damage.
- You create a harmless sensory effect using air, such as causing leaves to rustle, wind to slam shutters shut, or your clothing to ripple in a breeze.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The range you can push a Target, or an Object bot increase by <u>5 ft.</u> when you reach levels <u>5</u> (<u>10 ft.</u> and <u>15 ft.</u>), <u>11</u> (<u>15 ft.</u> and <u>20 ft.</u>), and <u>17</u> (<u>20 ft.</u> and <u>25 ft.</u>). Also, an object pushed by this spell deals extra Bludgeoning damage depending on the distance it traveled, if it hits a Creature, when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`1d6` per <u>10 ft.</u>), <u>11</u> (`2d6` per <u>10 ft.</u>), and <u>17</u> (`3d6` per <u>10 ft.</u>).
