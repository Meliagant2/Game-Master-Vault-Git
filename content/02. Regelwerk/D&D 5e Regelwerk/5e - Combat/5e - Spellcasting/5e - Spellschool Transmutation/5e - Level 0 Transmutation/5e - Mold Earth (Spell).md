# ☄️5e - Mold Earth☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You choose a portion of dirt or stone that you can see within range and that fits within a <u>5-foot cube</u>. You manipulate it in one of the following ways:

- If you target an area of loose earth, you can instantaneously excavate it, move it along the ground, and deposit it up to <u>5 feet</u> away. This movement doesn’t have enough force to cause damage.
- You cause shapes, colors, or both to appear on the dirt or stone, spelling out words, creating images, or shaping patterns. The changes last for <u>1 hour</u>.
- If the dirt or stone you target is on the ground, you cause it to become [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]]. Alternatively, you can cause the ground to become normal terrain if it is already difficult terrain. This change lasts for <u>1 hour</u>.

If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have no more than two of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
