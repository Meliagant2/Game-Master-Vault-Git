Whispering to the spirits of nature, you create one of the following effects within range.

**<u>Weather Sensor:</u>** You create a Tiny, harmless sensory effect that predicts what the weather will be at your location for the next <u>24 hours</u>. The effect might manifest as a golden orb for clear skies, a cloud for rain, falling snowflakes for snow, and so on. This effect persists for <u>1 round</u>.

**<u>Bloom:</u>** You instantly make a flower blossom, a seed pod open, or a leaf bud bloom.

**<u>Sensory Effect:</u>** You create a harmless sensory effect, such as falling leaves, spectral dancing fairies, a gentle breeze, the sound of an animal, or the faint odor of skunk. The effect must fit in a <u>5-foot Cube</u>.

**<u>Fire Play:</u>** You light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a campfire.
