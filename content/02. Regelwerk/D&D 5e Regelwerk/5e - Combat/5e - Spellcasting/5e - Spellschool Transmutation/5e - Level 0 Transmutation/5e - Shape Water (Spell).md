# ☄️5e - Shape Water☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Class List:**| |

You choose an area of water that you can see within range and that fits within a <u>5-foot cube</u>. You manipulate it in one of the following ways:

- You instantaneously move or otherwise change the flow of the water as you direct, up to <u>5 feet</u> in any direction. This movement doesn’t have enough force to cause damage.
- You cause the water to form into simple shapes and animate at your direction. This change lasts for <u>1 hour</u>.
- You change the water’s color or opacity. The water must be changed in the same way throughout. This change lasts for <u>1 hour</u>.
- You freeze the water, provided that there are no creatures in it. The water unfreezes in <u>1 hour</u>.

If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have no more than two of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
