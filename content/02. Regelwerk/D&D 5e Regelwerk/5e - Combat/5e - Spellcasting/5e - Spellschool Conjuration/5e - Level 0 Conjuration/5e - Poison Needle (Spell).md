# ☄️5e - Poison Needle☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You launch a conjured dart coated with lethal poison at a creature you can see within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d10` <u>Poison</u> damage.

If the poison damage reduces the target to <u>0 hit points</u>, the target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stable|☠️Stable]] but [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] for <u>1 hour</u>, even after regaining hit points, and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] while <u>poisoned</u> in this way.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
