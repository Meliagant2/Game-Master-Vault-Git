# ☄️5e - Find Familiar☄️

You gain the service of a familiar, a spirit that takes an animal form, of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Beasts; CR 0/5e - Beasts; CR 0|🐺Beast]] that has a Challenge Rating of 0, that you choose. Appearing in an unoccupied space within range, the familiar has the statistics of the chosen form, though it is a <u>Celestial</u>, <u>Elemental</u>, <u>Fey</u>, or <u>Fiend</u> (your choice) instead of a Beast. Your familiar acts independently of you, but it obeys your commands.

**<u>Telepathic Connection:</u>** While your familiar is within <u>100 feet</u> of you, you can communicate with it telepathically. Additionally, as a **Bonus Action**, you can see through the familiar's eyes and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of any special senses it has.

Finally, when you cast a spell with a range of <u>touch</u>, your familiar can deliver the touch. Your familiar must be within <u>100 feet</u> of you, and it must take a **Reaction** to deliver the touch when you cast the spell.

**<u>Combat:</u>** The familiar is an ally to you and your allies. It acts during your turn. A familiar can't attack, but it can take other actions as normal.

**<u>Disappearance of the Familiar:</u>** When the familiar drops to 0 <u>Hit Points</u>, it disappears. It reappears after you cast this spell again. As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|☄️Magic Action]], you can temporarily dismiss the familiar to a pocket dimension. Alternatively, you can dismiss it forever. As a Magic Action while it is temporarily dismissed, you can cause it to reappear in an unoccupied space within <u>30 feet</u> of you. Whenever the familiar drops to 0 HP or disappears into the pocket dimension, it leaves behind in its space anything it was wearing or carrying.

**<u>One Familiar Only:</u>** You can't have more than one familiar at a time. If you cast this spell while you have a familiar, you instead cause it to adopt a new eligible form.
