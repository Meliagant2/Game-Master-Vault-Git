# ☄️5e - Floating Disk☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A \[R]  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a drop of mercury) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Hour |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Wizard |

This spell creates a circular, horizontal plane of force, <u>1 meter</u> in diameter and <u>2,5 cm</u> thick, that floats 3 feet above the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within range. The disk remains for the duration and can hold up to 250 kg. If more weight is placed on it, the spell ends, and everything on the disk falls to the ground.

The disk is immobile while you are within <u>20 feet</u> of it. If you move more than <u>20 feet</u> away from it, the disk follows you so that it remains within <u>20 feet</u> of you. It can move across uneven terrain, up or down stairs, slopes and the like, but it can't cross an elevation change of <u>10 feet</u> or more. For example, the disk can't move across a <u>10-foot-deep</u> pit, nor could it leave such a pit if it was created at the bottom.

If you move more than <u>100 feet</u> from the disk (typically because it can't move around an obstacle to follow you), the spell ends.
