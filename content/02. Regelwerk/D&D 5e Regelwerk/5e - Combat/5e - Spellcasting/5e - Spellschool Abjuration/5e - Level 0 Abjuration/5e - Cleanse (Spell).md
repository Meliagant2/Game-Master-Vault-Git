Choose `1` of those Conditions. You make an <u>Ability Check</u>, adding your `PB + your Spellcasting Ability modifier` to the roll, against the DC of the Condition. If the Condition doesn't have a Save DC, it is equal to the Save DC of the source of the Condition. If the source doesn't have a Save DC, the GM determines the DC for this check. _**Success:**_ You end the chosen Condition on the Creature.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The number of Conditions this Spell can remove, increases by `1` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2`), <u>11</u> (`3`), and <u>17</u> (`4`).
