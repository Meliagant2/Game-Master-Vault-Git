# ☄️5e - Sanctuary☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|BA  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a small silver mirror) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Artificer |

You ward a creature within range. Until the spell ends, any creature who targets the warded creature with an attack roll or a damaging spell makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature either chooses a new target or loses the attack or spell. This spell doesn't protect the warded creature from areas of effect.

The spell ends if the warded creature makes an attack roll, casts a spell, or deals damage.
