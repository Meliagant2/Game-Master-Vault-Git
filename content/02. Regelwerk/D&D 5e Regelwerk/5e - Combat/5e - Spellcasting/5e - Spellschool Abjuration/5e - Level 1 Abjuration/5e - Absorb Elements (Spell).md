# ☄️5e - Absorb Elements☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |

> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

The spell captures some of the incoming energy, lessening its effect on you and storing it for your next melee attack. You have <u>resistance</u> to the triggering damage type until the <u>start of your next turn</u>. Also, the first time you hit with a <u>melee attack</u> on your next turn, the target takes an extra `1d6` damage of the triggering type, and the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
