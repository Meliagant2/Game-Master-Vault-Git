An imperceptible barrier of magical force protects you. Until the start of your next turn, you have a <u>+3</u> bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from Magic Missile.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell's bonus to AC increases by `1` for every spell slot level above 1st.
