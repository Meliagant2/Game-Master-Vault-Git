You hurl a disorienting magical force toward one creature within range. The target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes 2d4 Force damage and it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Dazed|☠️Dazed]] until the start of <u>your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d4` for each spell slot level above 1.
