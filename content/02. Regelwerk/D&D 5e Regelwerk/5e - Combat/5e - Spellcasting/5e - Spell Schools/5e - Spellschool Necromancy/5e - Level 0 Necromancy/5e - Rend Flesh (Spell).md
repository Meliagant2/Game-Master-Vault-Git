You manipulate the blood beneath the skin of a creature you can see within range. The target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] until the <u>start of its next turn</u>. While poisoned this way, <u>weapon attacks</u> deal an extra `1d4` <u>Necrotic</u> damage to the target.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d4` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d4`), <u>11</u> (`3d4`), and <u>17</u> (`4d4`).
