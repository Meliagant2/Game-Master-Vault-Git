# ☄️5e - Arms of Hadar☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Spell List:**|Warlock |

Invoking Hadar, you cause tendrils to erupt from yourself. Each creature in a <u>10-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ A target takes `3d6` <u>Necrotic</u> damage and can't take **Reactions** until the <u>start of its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
