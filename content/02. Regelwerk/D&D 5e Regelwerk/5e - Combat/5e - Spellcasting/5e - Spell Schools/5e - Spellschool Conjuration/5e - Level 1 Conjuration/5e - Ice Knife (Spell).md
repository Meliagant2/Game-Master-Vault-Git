# ☄️5e - Ice Knife☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a drop of water or a piece of ice) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged, DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You create a shard of ice and fling it at one creature within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `2d10` <u>Piercing</u> damage. Hit or miss, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within <u>5 feet</u> of it make a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `2d6` <u>Cold</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s Cold damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
