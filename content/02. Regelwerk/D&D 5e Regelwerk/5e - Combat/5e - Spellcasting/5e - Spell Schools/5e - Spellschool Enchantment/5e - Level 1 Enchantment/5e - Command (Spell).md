# ☄️5e - Command☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |

> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V |
> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Bard |

You speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature follows the command on its next turn. Choose the command from these options or issue a command other than the one described here:

**<u>Approach:</u>** The target moves toward you by the shortest and most direct route, ending its turn if it moves within <u>5 feet</u> of you.

**<u>Drop:</u>** The target drops whatever it is holding and then ends its turn.

**<u>Flee:</u>** The target spends its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means.

**<u>Grovel:</u>** The target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition and then ends its tn.

**<u>Halt:</u>** On its turn, the target **doesn't move** and takes **no Action** or **Bonus Action**.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
