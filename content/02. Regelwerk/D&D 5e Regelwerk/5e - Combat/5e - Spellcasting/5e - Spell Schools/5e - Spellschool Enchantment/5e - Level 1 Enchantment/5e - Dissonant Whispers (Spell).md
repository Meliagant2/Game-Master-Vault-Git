# ☄️5e - Dissonant Whispers☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Bard |

One creature of your choice that you can see within range hears a discordant melody in its mind. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ It takes `3d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage and must immediately use its **Reaction**, if available, to move as far away from you as it can, using the safest route. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
