# ☄️5e - Disrupting Note☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|BA  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a musical instrument that you are proficient with) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Bard |

You play a discordant note on your instrument aimed at a creature <u>concentrating</u> on a spell or effect within range. The target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature looses <u>concentration</u> on the spell or effect and takes `2d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
