# ☄️5e - Hideous Laughter☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a tart and a feather) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

One creature of your choice that you can see within range makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ It has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] conditions for the duration. During that time, it laughs uncontrollably if it's capable of laughter, and it can't end the <u>Prone</u> condition on itself.

If the Creature takes damage, it repeats the **WIS Save**, and it does so with **ADV**, ending the effect on a success.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
