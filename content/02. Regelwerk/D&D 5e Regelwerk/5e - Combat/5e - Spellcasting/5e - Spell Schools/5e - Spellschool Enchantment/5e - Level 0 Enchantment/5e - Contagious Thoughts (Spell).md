# ☄️5e - Contagious Thoughts☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|10 ft. Emanation |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a handkerchief) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|INT|
> |**Spell List:**|Bard |

You share whatever thought is at the forefront of your mind with all creatures within range. Each creature in a <u>10-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes an **INT Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `1d8` <u>Psychic</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
