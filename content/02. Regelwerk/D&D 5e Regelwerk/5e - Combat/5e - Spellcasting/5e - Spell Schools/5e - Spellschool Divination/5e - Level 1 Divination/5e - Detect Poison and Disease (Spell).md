# ☄️5e - Detect Poison and Disease☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Divination |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A \[R]  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. Emanation |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a yew leaf) |
> |**Duration:**|10 Minutes |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Primal |

For the duration, you sense the location of poisons, poisonous or venomous creatures, and magical contagions within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. You sense the kind of poison, creature, or contagion in each case.

The spell is blocked by <u>30 cm</u> of stone, dirt, or wood; <u>2,5 cm</u> of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
