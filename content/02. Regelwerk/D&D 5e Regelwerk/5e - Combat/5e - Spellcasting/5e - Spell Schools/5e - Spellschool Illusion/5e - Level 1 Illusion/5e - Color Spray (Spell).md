> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Range/Area:**|15 ft. Cone |

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a <u>15-foot Cone</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The cone of this spell increases in size by <u>5 ft.</u>for each spell slot level above 1.
