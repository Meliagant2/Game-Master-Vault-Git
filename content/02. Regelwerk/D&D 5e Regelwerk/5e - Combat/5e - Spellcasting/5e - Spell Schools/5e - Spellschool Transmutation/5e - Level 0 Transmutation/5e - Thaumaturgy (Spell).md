# ☄️5e - Thaumaturgy☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |

> |**Components:**|V |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Blood Hunter |

You manifest a minor wonder within range. You create one of the effects below within range. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its 1-minute effects active at a time.

**<u>Altered Eyes.</u>** You alter the appearance of your eyes for <u>1 minute</u>.

**<u>Booming Voice.</u>** Your voice booms up to three times as loud as normal for <u>1 minute</u>. For the duration, you have **ADV** on <u>Intimidation</u> checks.

**<u>Fire Play.</u>** You cause flames to flicker, brighten, dim, or change color for <u>1 minute</u>.

**<u>Invisible Hand.</u>** You instantaneously cause an unlocked door or window to fly open or slam shut.

**<u>Phantom Sound.</u>** You create an instantaneous sound that originates from a point of your choice within range, such as a rumble of thunder, the cry of a raven, or ominous whispers.

**<u>Tremors.</u>** You cause harmless tremors in the ground for <u>1 minute</u>.
