# ☄️5e - Primal Savagery☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self |
> |**Components:**|S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |

You channel primal magic to cause your teeth or fingernails to sharpen, ready to deliver a corrosive attack. Make a melee spell attack against one creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you. On a hit, the target takes `1d12` <u>Acid</u> damage. After you make the attack, your teeth or fingernails return to normal.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d12`), <u>11</u> (`3d12`), and <u>17</u> (`4d12`).
