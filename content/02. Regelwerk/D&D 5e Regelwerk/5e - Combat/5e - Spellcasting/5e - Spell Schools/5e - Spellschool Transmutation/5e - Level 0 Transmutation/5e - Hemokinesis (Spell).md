# ☄️5e - Hemokinesis☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|S |
> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Blood Hunter |

With a wave of your hand, you seize control of the lifegiving ichor inside a creature you can see within range. The target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target suffers one of the following effects of your choice until the <u>end of its next turn</u>:

- The target suffers a nosebleed or tears of blood start flowing from its eyes. The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]].
- The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] of you, as it feels extreme uneasiness and a sensation of its blood freezing within it.
- One of the target's body parts of your choice starts to convulse. The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Rattled|☠️Rattled]].
