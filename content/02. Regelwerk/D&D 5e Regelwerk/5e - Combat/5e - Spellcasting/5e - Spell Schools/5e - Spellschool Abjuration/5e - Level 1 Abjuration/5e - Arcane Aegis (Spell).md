# ☄️5e - Arcane Aegis☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a statuette of a hedgehog) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Wizard |

When you cast this spell, you are surrounded by a shimmering light that quickly dissipates. You gain `2d10` <u>Temporary Hit Points</u>. If a creature hits you with a <u>melee attack</u> before the spell ends, the creature takes <u>Force</u> damage equal to the number of Temp HP lost as a result of the attack. The spell ends early if you have no Temp HP left.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You gain an additional `2d10` Temp HP for each spell slot level above 1.
