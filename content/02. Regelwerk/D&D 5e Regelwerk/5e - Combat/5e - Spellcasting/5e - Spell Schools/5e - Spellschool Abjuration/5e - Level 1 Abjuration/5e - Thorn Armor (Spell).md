# ☄️5e - Thorn Armor☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Touch |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a rose) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Hour |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |

When you cast this spell, a flexible exoskeleton covered in thorns appears around a willing target within range. The target gains `3d6` <u>Temporary Hit Points</u>. If a creature hits the target with a melee attack roll before the spell ends, that creature takes <u>Piercing</u> damage equal to the number of <u>Temp HP</u> lost as a result of the attack. This spell ends early if the target has no remaining <u>Temp HP</u> granted by this spell.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You gain an additional `2d6` Temp HP for each spell slot level above 1.
