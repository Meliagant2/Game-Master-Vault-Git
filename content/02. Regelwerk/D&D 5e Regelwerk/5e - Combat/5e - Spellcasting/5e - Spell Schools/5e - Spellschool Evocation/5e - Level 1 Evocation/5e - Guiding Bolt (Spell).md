You hurl a bolt of light toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes `4d6` <u>Radiant</u> damage, and it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
