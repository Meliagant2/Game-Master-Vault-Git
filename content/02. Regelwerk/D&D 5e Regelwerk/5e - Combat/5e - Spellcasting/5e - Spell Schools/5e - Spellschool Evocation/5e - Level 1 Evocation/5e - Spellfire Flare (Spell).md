# ☄️5e - Spellfire Flare☄️

> [!wikibox]

You unleash a blast of brilliant fire. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against a target within range; a target gains no benefit from [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Half Cover|🏔️Half Cover]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Three-Quarters Cover|🏔️Three-Quarters Cover]] for this attack roll. On a hit, the target takes `2d10` <u>Radiant</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You create an additional blast for each spell slot level above 1. You can direct the blasts at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each blast.
