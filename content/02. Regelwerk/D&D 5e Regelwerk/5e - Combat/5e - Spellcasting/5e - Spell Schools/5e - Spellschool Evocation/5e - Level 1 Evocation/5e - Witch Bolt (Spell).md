# ☄️5e - Witch Bolt☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a twig struck by lightning) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

A beam of crackling energy lances toward a creature within range, forming a sustained arc of lightning between you and the target. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against it. On a hit, the target takes `2d12` <u>Lightning</u> damage.

On each of your subsequent turns, you can take a **Bonus Action** to deal `1d12` <u>Lightning</u> damage to the target automatically, <u>even if the first attack missed</u>.

The spell ends if the target is ever outside the spell's range or if it has [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Total Cover|🏔️otal Cover]] from you.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s initial damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 1.
