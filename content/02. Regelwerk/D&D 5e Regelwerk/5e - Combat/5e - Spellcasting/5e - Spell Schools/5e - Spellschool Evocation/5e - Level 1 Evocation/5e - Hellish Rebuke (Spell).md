# ☄️5e - Hellish Rebuke☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|R (You take damage from a Creature within range)|

> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|

The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by hellish flames. It makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ It takes `2d10` <u>Fire</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 1.
