# ☄️5e - Concussive Burst☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft./ 20 ft. Sphere |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

A pulse of energy ripples through the air at a point you choose within range, dealing minor damage over a wide area. Each creature within a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on that point makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `2d4` <u>Force</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Deafened|☠️Deafened]] condition until the <u>end of your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d4` for each spell slot level above 1.
