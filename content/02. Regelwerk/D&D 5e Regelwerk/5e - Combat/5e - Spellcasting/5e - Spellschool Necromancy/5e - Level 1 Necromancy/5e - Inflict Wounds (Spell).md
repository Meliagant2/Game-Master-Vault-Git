A creature you touch makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ It takes `2d10` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 1.
