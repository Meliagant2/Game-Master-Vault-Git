# ☄️5e - Captivate Undead☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Necromancy |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a pinch of bone dust) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Hour |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CHA|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Divine,Bard |

You usurp the negative energy animating an Undead creature that you can see within range, attempting to take control of it. The target makes a **CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ For the duration, the target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition (ignoring any <u>Immunity</u> to that condition), and it is Friendly toward you. _**Success:**_ The target has **DISADV** on the next <u>attack roll</u> it makes against you before the <u>start of your next turn</u>. If you or any of your allies damage the target or force it to make a saving throw, the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
