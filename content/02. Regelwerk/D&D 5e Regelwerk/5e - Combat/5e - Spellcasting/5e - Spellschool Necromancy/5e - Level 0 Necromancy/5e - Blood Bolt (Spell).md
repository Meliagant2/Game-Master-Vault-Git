# ☄️5e - Blood Bolt☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Necromancy |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|90 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Blood Hunter |

You hurl a twisting bolt of blood at a creature within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d6` <u>Necrotic</u> damage, and you gain a number of <u>Temporary HP</u> equal to `your spellcasting ability modifier`.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d6`), <u>11</u> (`3d6`), and <u>17</u> (`4d6`).
