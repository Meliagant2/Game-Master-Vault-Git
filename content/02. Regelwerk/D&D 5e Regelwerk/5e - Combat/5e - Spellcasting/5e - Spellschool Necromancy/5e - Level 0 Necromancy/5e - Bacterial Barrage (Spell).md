# ☄️5e - Bacterial Barrage☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Necromancy |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You attack a creature in range with a quick spreading infection that takes advantage of existing conditions. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against a target, on a hit the infection does `1d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage to the target. If the target is suffering from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition or a <u>disease</u>, it instead takes `1d12` <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by one die when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8` or `2d12`), <u>11</u> (`3d8` or `3d12`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8` or `4d12`).
