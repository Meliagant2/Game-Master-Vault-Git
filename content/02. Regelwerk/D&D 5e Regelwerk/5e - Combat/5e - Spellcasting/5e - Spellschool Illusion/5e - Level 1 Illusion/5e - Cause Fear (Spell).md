You awaken the sense of mortality in one creature you can see within range. The target makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ It becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] of you until the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
