You call a group of people to a heightened emotional state, driving them to anger and mistrust. Each Humanoid in a <u>20-foot-radius sphere</u> centered on a point you choose within range makes a **repeated CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ A creature must use its **Reaction** (if available) to make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] whenever another creature (including an ally) moves out of its reach.
