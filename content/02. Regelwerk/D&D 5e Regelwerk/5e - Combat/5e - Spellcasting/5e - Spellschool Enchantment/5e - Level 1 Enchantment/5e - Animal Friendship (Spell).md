# ☄️5e - Animal Friendship☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Enchantment |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a morsel of food) |
> |**Duration:**|24 Hours |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal,Bard |

Target a <u>Beast</u> that you can see within range. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ It is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
