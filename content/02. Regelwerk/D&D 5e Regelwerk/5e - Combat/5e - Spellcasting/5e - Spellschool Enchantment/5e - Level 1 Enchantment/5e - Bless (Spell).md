You bless up to <u>three</u> creatures within range. Whenever a target makes an <u>attack roll</u> or a <u>saving throw</u> before the spell ends, the target adds `1d4` to the attack roll or save.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
