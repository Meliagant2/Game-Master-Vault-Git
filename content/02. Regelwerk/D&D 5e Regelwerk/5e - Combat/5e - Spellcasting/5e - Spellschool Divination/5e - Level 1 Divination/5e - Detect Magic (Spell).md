# ☄️5e - Detect Magic☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Duration:**|PT10M |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Divine,Primal |

For the duration, you sense the presence of magic within <u>30 feet</u> of you. If you sense magic in this way, you can use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears magic, and you learn its school of magic, if any.

The spell is blocked by <u>30 cm</u> of stone, dirt, or wood; <u>2,5 cm</u> of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
