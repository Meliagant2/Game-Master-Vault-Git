# ☄️5e - Chromatic Orb☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|90 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a diamond worth 50+ SP) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

You hurl a 4-inch-diameter sphere of energy at a creature that you can see within range. You choose <u>acid</u>, <u>cold</u>, <u>fire</u>, <u>lightning</u>, <u>poison</u>, or <u>thunder</u> for the type of orb you create, and then make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. If the attack hits, the creature takes `3d10` damage of the type you chose.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 1.
