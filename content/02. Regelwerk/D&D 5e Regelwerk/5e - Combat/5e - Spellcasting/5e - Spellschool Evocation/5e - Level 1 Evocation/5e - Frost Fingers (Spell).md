# ☄️5e - Frost Fingers☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A \[R]  |
> |**Range/Area:**|15 ft. Cone |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

Freezing cold blasts from your fingertips in a <u>15-foot cone</u>. Each creature in that area makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `2d8` <u>Cold</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage.

The cold freezes nonmagical liquids in the area that aren’t being worn or carried.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 1.
