# ☄️5e - Green-Flame Blade☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a weapon with which you have proficiency and that is worth 1+ CP) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Blood Hunter |

You brandish the weapon used in the spell's casting and make a <u>melee attack</u> with it against one creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon attack's normal effects, and you can cause green fire to leap from the target to a different creature of your choice that you can see within <u>5 feet</u> of it. The second creature takes `1d8` <u>Fire</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell's melee attack deals additional Fire damage when you reach levels  <u>5</u> (`1d8`), <u>11</u> (`2d8`), and <u>17</u> (`3d8`). The fire damage to the second creature increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
