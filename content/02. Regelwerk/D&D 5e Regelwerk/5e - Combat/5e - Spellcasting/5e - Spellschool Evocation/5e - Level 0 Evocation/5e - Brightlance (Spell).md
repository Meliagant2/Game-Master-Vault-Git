# ☄️5e - Brightlance☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |

> |**Attack/Save:**|Spell Attack|
> |**Spell List:**|Cleric |

You hurl, slash, or stab crackling radiant energy at a creature or object within range. This energy takes on the shape of a weapon of your choice. If the target is within <u>5 ft.</u> of you, make a <u>melee spell attack</u> against it, otherwise make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against it. This attack roll is made with **DISADV** if the target is further than <u>60 ft.</u> from you. On a hit the target takes `1d4` <u>Radiant</u> damage and `1d4` <u>Lightning</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The radiant and the lightning damage dealt by this spell each increase by `1d4` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d4`), <u>11</u> (`3d4`), and <u>17</u> (`4d4`).
