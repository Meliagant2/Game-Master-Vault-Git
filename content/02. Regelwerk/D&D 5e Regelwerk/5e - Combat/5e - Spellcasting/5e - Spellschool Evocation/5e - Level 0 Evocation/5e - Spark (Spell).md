# ☄️5e - Spark☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

A spark of bright light arcs from you to a creature within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d8` <u>Lightning</u> damage. On a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]], the target is also [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] until the end of its next turn.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The spell creates <u>two</u> sparks at level <u>5</u>, <u>three</u> sparks at level <u>11</u>, and <u>four</u> sparks at level <u>17</u>. You can direct the sparks at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each spark.
