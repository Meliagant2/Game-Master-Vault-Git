> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |

> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You launch a mist of toxic energy at a creature within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d12` <u>Poison</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d12`), <u>11</u> (`3d12`), and <u>17</u> (`4d12`).
