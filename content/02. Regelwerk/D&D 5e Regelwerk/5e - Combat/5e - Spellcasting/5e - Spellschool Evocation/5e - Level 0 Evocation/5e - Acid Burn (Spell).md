# ☄️5e - Acid Burn☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|15 ft. Cone |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Blood Hunter |

You magically produce a spray of acidic formula in a <u>15-foot cone</u> in front of you. All creatures in the cone make a **DEX Save**. _**Failue:**_ The Creature takes `1d6` <u>Acid</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d6`), <u>11</u> (`3d6`), and <u>17</u> (`4d6`).
