# ☄️5e - Booming Blade☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A|
> |**Range/Area:**|Self |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a weapon with which you have proficiency and that is worth 1+ CP) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Round |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Blood Hunter |

You brandish the weapon used in the spell’s casting and make a <u>melee spell attack</u> with it against one creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you. On a hit, the target suffers the weapon attack’s normal effects and then becomes sheathed in booming energy until the start of your next turn. If the target willingly moves <u>5 feet</u> or more before then, the target takes `1d8` <u>thunder</u> damage, and the spell ends.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell's damage increases when you reach certain levels. At <u>5th level</u>, the melee attack deals an extra `1d8` thunder damage to the target on a hit, and the damage the target takes for moving increases to `2d8`. Both damage rolls increase by `1d8` at <u>11th</u> level (`2d8` and `3d8`) and again at <u>17th</u> level (`3d8` and `4d8`).
