# ☄️5e - Sorcerous Burst☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Sorcerer |

You cast sorcerous energy at one creature or object within range. Make a <u>ranged attack roll</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `2d4` damage. If you roll a `4` on a `d4` for this spell, you can roll another `d4`, and add it to the damage.

You choose the damage type each time you cast this spell: <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Poison</u>, <u>Psychic</u>, or <u>Thunder</u>.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d4` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`4d4`), <u>11</u> (`6d4`), and <u>17</u> (`8d4`).
