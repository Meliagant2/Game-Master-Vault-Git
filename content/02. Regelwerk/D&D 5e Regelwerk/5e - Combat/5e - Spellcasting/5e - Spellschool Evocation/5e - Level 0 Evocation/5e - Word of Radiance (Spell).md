# ☄️5e - Word of Radiance☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|5 ft. Emanation |
> |**Components:**|V, M (a holy symbol) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Divine |

You utter a divine word, and burning radiance erupts from you. Each creature of your choice in a <u>5-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `1d10` <u>Radiant</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
