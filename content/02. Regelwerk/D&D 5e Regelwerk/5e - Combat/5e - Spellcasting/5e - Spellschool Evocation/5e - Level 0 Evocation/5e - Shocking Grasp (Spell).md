# ☄️5e - Shocking Grasp☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Touch |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|n |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

Lightning springs from you to a creature that you try to touch. Make a <u>melee spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d10` <u>Lightning</u> damage, and it can't make [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] until the start of its next turn.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
