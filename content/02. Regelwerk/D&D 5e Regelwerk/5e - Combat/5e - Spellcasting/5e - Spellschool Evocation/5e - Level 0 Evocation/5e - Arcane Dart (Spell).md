A single dart of pure magical energy streaks toward a creature within range. Make a <u>ranged spell attack</u> against the target. On a hit, it takes `2d4` <u>Force</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d4` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`4d4`), <u>11</u> (`6d4`), and <u>17</u> (`8d4`).
