You create illusory obstacles such as rocks or spikes in an attempt to confuse up to three creatures you can see within range, affecting their ability to move. Each target makes a **repeated INT Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] and it can't take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] action.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
