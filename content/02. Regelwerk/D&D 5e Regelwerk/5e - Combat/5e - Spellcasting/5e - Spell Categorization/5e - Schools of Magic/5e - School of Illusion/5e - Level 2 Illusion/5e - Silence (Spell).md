For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the Sphere has <u>Immunity</u> to <u>Thunder</u> damage, and creatures have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Deafened|☠️Deafened]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Silenced|☠️Silenced]] conditions while entirely inside it.
