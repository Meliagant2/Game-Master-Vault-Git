# ☄️5e - Distorted Distance☄️

You create an illusory spatial dilation that fills a <u>60-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point within range. For each creature you can see in the Sphere, you can choose one of the following effects for it:

**<u>Dizzying Elongation:</u>** The creature makes an **INT Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10` <u>Psychic</u> damage, and the Sphere is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] for it until <u>the end of its turn</u>.

**<u>Shortened Space:</u>** The creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>20 feet</u> until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.

Until the spell ends, whenever a creature enters the Sphere or ends its turn there, you can choose for it to receive one of these effects. A creature can be affected by this spell only <u>once per turn</u>.
