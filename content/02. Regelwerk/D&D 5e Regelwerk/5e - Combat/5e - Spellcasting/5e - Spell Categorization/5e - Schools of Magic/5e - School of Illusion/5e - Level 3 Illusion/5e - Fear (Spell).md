Each creature in a <u>30-foot Cone</u> makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature drops whatever it is holding and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Terrified|☠️Terrified]] condition for the duration.

If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn't have line of sight to you, the creature makes a **WIS Save**. _**Success:**_ The spell ends on that creature.
