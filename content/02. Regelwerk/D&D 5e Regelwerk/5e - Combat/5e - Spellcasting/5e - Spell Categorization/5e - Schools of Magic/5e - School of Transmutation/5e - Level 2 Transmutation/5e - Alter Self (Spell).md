You alter your physical form. Choose one of the following options. Its effects last for the duration, during which you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to replace the option you chose with a different one.

**<u>Aquatic Adaptation:</u>** You sprout gills and grow webs between your fingers. You can breathe underwater and gain a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]] equal to your Speed.

**<u>Change Appearance:</u>** You alter your appearance. You decide what you look like, including your height, weight, facial features, sound of your voice, hair length, coloration, and other distinguishing characteristics. You can make yourself appear as a member of another species, though none of your statistics change. You can't appear as a creature of a different size, and your basic shape stays the same; if you're bipedal, you can't use this spell to become quadrupedal, for instance. For the duration, you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to change your appearance in this way again.

**<u>Natural Weapons:</u>** You grow claws (<u>Slashing</u>), fangs (<u>Piercing</u>), horns (<u>Piercing</u>), or hooves (<u>Bludgeoning</u>). When you use your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] to deal damage with that new growth, it deals `1d6` damage of the type in parentheses instead of dealing the normal damage for your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]], and you use your _spellcasting ability modifier_ for the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Rolls]] rather than using <u>STR</u>.
