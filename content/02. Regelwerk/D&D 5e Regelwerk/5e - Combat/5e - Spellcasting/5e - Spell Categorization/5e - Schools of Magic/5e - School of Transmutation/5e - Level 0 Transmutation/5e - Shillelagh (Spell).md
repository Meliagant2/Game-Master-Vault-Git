A [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Hammers/5e - Club|🗡️Club]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Quarterstaff|🗡️Quarterstaff]] you are holding is imbued with nature’s power. For the duration, you can use your <u>spellcasting ability</u> instead of <u>STR</u> or <u>DEX</u> for the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Rolls]] of <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attacks]] using that weapon, and the weapon’s <u>damage die</u> becomes a `d8`. If the attack deals damage, it can be <u>Force</u> damage or the weapon’s normal damage type (your choice).

The spell ends early if you cast it again or if you let go of the weapon.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The weapon’s damage die changes when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`d10`), <u>11</u> (`d12`), and <u>17</u> (`2d6`).
