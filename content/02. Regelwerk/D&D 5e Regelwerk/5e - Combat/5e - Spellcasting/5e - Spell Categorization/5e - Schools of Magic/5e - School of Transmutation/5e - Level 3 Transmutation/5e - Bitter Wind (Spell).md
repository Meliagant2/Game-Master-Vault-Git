# ☄️5e - Bitter Wind☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self (90 ft. Line) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a birch wood fan) |
> |**Duration:**|1 round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Cold,Slowed,Fatigue |

You cool down the air around you, forming a freezing breeze, which blasts out from you in a <u>90-foot-long, 15-foot-wide Line</u>. Each creature in the Line makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `5d8` <u>Cold</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. _**Failure (by 10):**_ The creature also gains one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]]. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 3.
