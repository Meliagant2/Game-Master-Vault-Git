A nonmagical weapon you touch becomes a magic weapon. Choose one of the following damage types: <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, or <u>Thunder</u>. For the duration, the weapon has a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] and deals an extra `1d4` damage of the chosen type when it _**hits**_.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** If you use a level 5-6 spell slot, the bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] increases to `+2`, and the extra damage increases to `2d4`. If you use a level 7+ spell slot, the bonus increases to `+3`, and the extra damage increases to `3d4`.
