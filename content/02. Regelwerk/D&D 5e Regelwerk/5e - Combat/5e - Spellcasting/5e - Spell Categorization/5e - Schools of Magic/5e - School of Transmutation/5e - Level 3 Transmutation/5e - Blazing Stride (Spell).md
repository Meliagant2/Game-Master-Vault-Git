The billowing flames of a dragon blast from your feet, granting you explosive speed. For the duration, your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>20 feet</u> and moving doesn't provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]].

When you move within <u>5 feet</u> of a creature or an object that isn't being worn or carried, it takes `1d6` <u>Fire</u> damage from your trail of heat. A creature or object can take this damage only once per turn.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 3. Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is increased by an additional <u>5 feet</u> for each spell slot level above 3.
