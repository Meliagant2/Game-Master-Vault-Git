# ☄️5e - Erupting Earth☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. (15 ft. Sphere) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a piece of obsidian) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Difficult Terrain |

Choose a point you can see on the ground within range. A fountain of churned earth and stone erupts in a <u>15-foot radius Sphere</u> centered on that point. Each creature in that area makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d12` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Additionally, the ground in that area becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] until cleared. Each <u>5-foot-square</u> portion of the area requires at least <u>1 minute</u> to clear by hand.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 3.
