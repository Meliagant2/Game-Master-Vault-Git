# ☄️5e - Buried Alive☄️

You send your enemies to their graves even if they aren't dead yet. A creature that you can see within range that is standing on the ground makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The ground opens and swallows the target, burying it up to <u>10 feet</u> deep. While buried, the target has [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Total Cover|🏔️Total Cover]], has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] conditions, and can't breathe. _**Success:**_ The target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition, and its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is `0` until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.

A buried creature can dig out of the grave by using its **Action** to make a **DC 15** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Athletics|💃Athletics]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]]. _**Success:**_ The creature digs <u>5 feet</u> closer to the surface. _**Success (by 5):**_ The creature reaches the surface. When the creature reaches the surface, it exits the ground with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition.

The target automatically _**succeeds**_ on its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] if it's standing on any thickness of stone or metal, or at least 2 inches of wood.
