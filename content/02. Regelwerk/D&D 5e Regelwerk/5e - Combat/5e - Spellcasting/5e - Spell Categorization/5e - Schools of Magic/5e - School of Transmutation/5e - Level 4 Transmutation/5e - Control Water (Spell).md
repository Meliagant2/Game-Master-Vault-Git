# ☄️5e - Control Water☄️

Until the spell ends, you control any water inside an area you choose that is a <u>Cube</u> up to <u>100 feet</u> on a side, using one of the following effects. As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action on your later turns, you can repeat the same effect or choose a different one.

**<u>Flood:</u>** You cause the water level of all standing water in the area to rise by as much as <u>20 feet</u>. If you choose an area in a large body of water, you instead create a <u>20-foot</u> tall wave that travels from one side of the area to the other and then crashes. Any _Huge or smaller_ [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Tools/5e - Vehicles/5e - Vehicles|🚘Vehicles]] in the wave's path are carried with it to the other side. Any _Huge or smaller_ [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Tools/5e - Vehicles/5e - Vehicles|🚘Vehicles]] struck by the wave have a <u>25 percent</u> chance of capsizing.

The water level remains elevated until the spell ends or you choose a different effect. If this effect produced a wave, the wave repeats on the start of your next turn while the flood effect lasts.

**<u>Part Water:</u>** You part water in the area and create a trench. The trench extends across the spell's area, and the separated water forms a wall to either side. The trench remains until the spell ends or you choose a different effect. The water then slowly fills in the trench over the course of the next round until the normal water level is restored.

**<u>Redirect Flow:</u>** You cause flowing water in the area to move in a direction you choose, even if the water has to flow over obstacles, up walls, or in other unlikely directions. The water in the area moves as you direct it, but once it moves beyond the spell's area, it resumes its flow based on the terrain. The water continues to move in the direction you chose until the spell ends or you choose a different effect.

**<u>Whirlpool:</u>** You cause a whirlpool to form in the center of the area, which must be at least <u>50 feet square and 25 feet deep</u>. The whirlpool lasts until you choose a different effect or the spell ends. The whirlpool is <u>5 feet wide</u> at the base, up to <u>50 feet wide at the top</u>, and <u>25 feet tall</u>. Any creature in the water and within <u>25 feet</u> of the whirlpool is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pulled]] <u>10 feet</u> toward it. When a creature enters the whirlpool for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, it makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d8` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

A creature can swim away from the whirlpool only if it first takes an **Action** to pull away and succeeds on an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Athletics|💃Athletics]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]].
