A willing creature you touch shape-shifts, along with everything it's wearing and carrying, into a misty cloud for the duration. The spell ends on the target if it drops to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] or if it takes a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to end the spell on itself.

While in this form, the target's only method of movement is a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>10 feet</u>, and it can hover. The target can enter and occupy the space of another creature. The target has <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage; it has <u>Immunity</u> to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition; and it has **ADV** on <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, and <u>CON</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]]. The target can pass through narrow openings, but it treats liquids as though they were solid surfaces.

The target can't talk or manipulate objects, and any objects it was carrying or holding can't be dropped, used, or otherwise interacted with. Finally, the target can't [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] or _cast spells_.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3.
