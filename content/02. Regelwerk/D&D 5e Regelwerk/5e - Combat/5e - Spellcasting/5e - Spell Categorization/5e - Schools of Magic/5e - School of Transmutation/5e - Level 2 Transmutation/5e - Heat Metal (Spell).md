# ☄️5e - Heat Metal☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |

> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a piece of iron and a flame) |
> |**Duration:**|PT1M |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Fire,Disarm,Hindered,Impaired |

Choose a manufactured metal object, such as a metal weapon or a suit of Heavy or Medium metal armor, that you can see within range. You cause the object to glow red-hot. Any creature in physical contact with the object takes `2d8` <u>Fire</u> damage when you cast the spell. Until the spell ends, you can take a **Bonus Action** on each of your later turns to deal this damage again if the object is within range.

If a creature is holding or wearing the object and takes the damage from it, the creature makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature drops the object if it can. If it doesn't drop the object, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Impaired|☠️Impaired]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
