# ☄️5e - Stone Shape☄️

> |||

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Divine,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Control |

You touch a stone object of _Medium size or smaller_ or a section of stone no more than <u>5 feet</u> in any dimension and form it into any shape you like. For example, you could shape a large rock into a weapon, statue, or coffer, or you could make a small passage through a wall that is <u>5 feet</u> thick. You could also shape a stone door or its frame to seal the door shut. The object you create can have up to two hinges and a latch, but finer mechanical detail isn't possible.
