# ☄️5e - Catapult☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. |
> |**Components:**|S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

Choose one object weighing <u>0,5</u> to <u>2,5 kg</u> within range that isn’t being worn or carried. The object flies in a straight line up to <u>90 feet</u> in a direction you choose before falling to the ground, stopping early if it impacts against a solid surface. If the object would strike a creature, that creature makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The object strikes the target and stops moving. When the object strikes something, the object and what it strikes each take `2d10` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d10` and the maximum weight of objects that you can target with this spell increases by <u>2,5 kg</u> for each spell slot level above 1.
