You touch one willing creature, and choose <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, or <u>Poison</u>. Until the spell ends, the target can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to exhale a <u>15-foot Cone</u>. Each creature in that area makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` damage of the chosen type. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 2.
