You seize the air and forcefully push a creature within range. A _Medium or smaller_ target makes a **STR Save**.  _**Failure:**_ The Target takes `1d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and is pushed up to <u>10 feet</u> away from you. _**Failure (by 5):**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d6`), <u>11</u> (`3d6`), and <u>17</u> (`4d6`).
