The ground in a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point within range sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] for the duration. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes `2d4` <u>Piercing</u> damage for every <u>5 feet</u> it travels.

The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area when the spell is cast must take a [[02.
