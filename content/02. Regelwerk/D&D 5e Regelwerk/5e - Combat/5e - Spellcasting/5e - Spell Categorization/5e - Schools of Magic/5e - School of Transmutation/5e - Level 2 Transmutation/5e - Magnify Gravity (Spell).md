# ☄️5e - Magnify Gravity☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Transmutation |
> |**Level:**|2 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|90 ft. (10 ft. Cylinder) |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|1 round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

The gravity in a <u>10-foot-radius, 30-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on a point you can see within range increases for a moment. Each creature in the Cylinder on the turn when you cast the spell makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ A creature takes `5d4` <u>Force</u> damage, and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>. A [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Flying]] creature also instantly [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏃‍♀️Falls]] to the ground, taking regular [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏃‍♀️Falling]] damage in the process. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Until <u>the start of your next turn</u>, any object that isn’t being worn or carried in the sphere requires a successful [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Breaking|💃Breaking]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] to pick up or move.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `3d4` for each spell slot level above 2.
