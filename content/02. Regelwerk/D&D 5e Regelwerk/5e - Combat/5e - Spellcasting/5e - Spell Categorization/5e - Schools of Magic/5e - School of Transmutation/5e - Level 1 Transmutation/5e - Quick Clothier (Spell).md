You alter the appearance of a set of nonmagical clothing that isn't being worn by someone else. You determine the color, styling, decoration, and form the clothing takes, though you can't generate clothes worth more than 15 SP. For example, you could transform a tattered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Clothing/5e - Robe|👔Robe]] into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Clothing/5e - Fine Clothes|👔Fine Clothes]], or vice versa. At the end of the spell's duration, the altered clothing returns to its original form.
