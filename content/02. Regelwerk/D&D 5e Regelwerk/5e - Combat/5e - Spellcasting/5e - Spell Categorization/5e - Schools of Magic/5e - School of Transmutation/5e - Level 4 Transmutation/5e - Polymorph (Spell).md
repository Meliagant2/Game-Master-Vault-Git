You attempt to transform a creature that you can see within range into a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Bestiary; Beasts|🐺Beast]]. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target shape-shifts into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Bestiary; Beasts|🐺Beast]] form for the duration. That form can be any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Bestiary; Beasts|🐺Beast]] you choose that has a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Challenge Rating (CR)|🐺Challenge Rating]] equal to or less than the target's (or the target's level if it doesn't have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Challenge Rating (CR)|🐺Challenge Rating]]). The target's game statistics are replaced by the stat block of the chosen Beast, but the target retains its alignment, personality, creature type, [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]].

The target gains a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Bestiary; Beasts|🐺Beast]] form. These [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] vanish if any remain when the spell ends. The spell ends early on the target if it has no [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] left.

The target is limited in the actions it can perform by the anatomy of its new form, and it can't speak or cast spells.

The target's gear melds into the new form. The creature can't use or otherwise benefit from any of that equipment.
