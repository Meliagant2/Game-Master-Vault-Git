You spread your arms wide, allowing yourself to become enveloped by the air around you. Until <u>the end of your next turn</u>, any <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against you is made with **DISADV**.

<u>Melee</u> attackers who successfully _hit_ you must make a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The attacker is flung away from you up to <u>10 feet</u> and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].
