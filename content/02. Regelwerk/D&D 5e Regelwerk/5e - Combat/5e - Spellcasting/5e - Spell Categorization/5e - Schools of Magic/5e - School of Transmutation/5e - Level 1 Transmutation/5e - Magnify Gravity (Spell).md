# ☄️5e - Magnify Gravity☄️

The gravity in a <u>10-foot-radius sphere</u> centered on a point you can see within range increases for a moment. Each creature in the sphere on the turn when you cast the spell makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ A creature takes `1d12` <u>Force</u> damage, and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Until <u>the start of your next turn</u>, any object that isn’t being worn or carried in the sphere requires a successful [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Breaking|💃Breaking]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] to pick up or move.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 1.
