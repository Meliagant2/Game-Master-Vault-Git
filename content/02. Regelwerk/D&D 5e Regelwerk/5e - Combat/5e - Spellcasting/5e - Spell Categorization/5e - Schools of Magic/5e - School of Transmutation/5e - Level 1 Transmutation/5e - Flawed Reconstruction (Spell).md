You stitch together the wounds of a creature you touch, which regains `3d6` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]]. However, the mending is imperfect, and the target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by `1d6`, to a minimum of 1.

The reduction to a creature's _hit point maximum_ is restored when the creature finishes a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]].

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The healing increases by `2d6` and the reduction increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
