Choose an object that you can see within range. The object can be a door, a box, a chest, a set of manacles, a padlock, or another object that contains a mundane or magical means that prevents access.

A target that is held shut by a mundane lock or that is stuck or barred becomes unlocked, unstuck, or unbarred. If the object has multiple locks, only one of them is unlocked.

If the target is held shut by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - Level 2 Abjuration/5e - Arcane Lock (Spell)|☄️Arcane Lock]], that spell is suppressed for <u>10 minutes</u>, during which time the target can be opened and closed.

When you cast the spell, a loud knock, audible up to <u>300 feet</u> away, emanates from the target.
