Choose a creature you can see within range to inflict with a magical poison. The target takes `2d4` <u>Poison</u> damage and makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] for the spell's duration.

While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] in this way, the target makes a **CON Save** at <u>the end of each of its turns</u>. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `2d4` <u>Poison</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The spell ends on that target.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
