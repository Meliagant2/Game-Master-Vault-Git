# ☄️5e - Ice Storm☄️

> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Cold,Difficult Terrain |

Hail falls in a <u>20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on a point within range. Each creature in the Cylinder makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `4d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and `2d10` <u>Cold</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Hailstones turn ground in the Cylinder into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
