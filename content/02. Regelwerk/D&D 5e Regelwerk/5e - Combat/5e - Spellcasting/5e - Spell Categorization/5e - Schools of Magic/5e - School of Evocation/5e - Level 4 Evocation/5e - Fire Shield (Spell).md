Wispy flames wreathe your body for the duration, shedding [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>10-foot radius</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>10 feet</u>.

The flames provide you with a **warm shield** or a **chill shield**, as you choose. The **warm shield** grants you <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Cold</u> damage, and the **chill shield** grants you <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Fire</u> damage.

In addition, whenever a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you hits you with a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], the shield erupts with flame. The attacker takes `2d8` <u>Fire</u> damage from a **warm shield** or `2d8` <u>Cold</u> damage from a **chill shield**.
