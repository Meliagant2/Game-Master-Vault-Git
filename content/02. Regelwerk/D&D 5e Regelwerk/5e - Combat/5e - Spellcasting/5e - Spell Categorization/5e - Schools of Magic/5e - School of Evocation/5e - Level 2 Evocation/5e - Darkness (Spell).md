For the duration, magical [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] spreads from a point within range and fills a <u>15-foot-radius Sphere</u>. [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Darkvision|👁️Darkvision]] can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it.

Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] to fill a <u>15-foot Emanation</u> originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]].

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] created by a _spell_ of _level 2_ or lower, that other spell is dispelled.
