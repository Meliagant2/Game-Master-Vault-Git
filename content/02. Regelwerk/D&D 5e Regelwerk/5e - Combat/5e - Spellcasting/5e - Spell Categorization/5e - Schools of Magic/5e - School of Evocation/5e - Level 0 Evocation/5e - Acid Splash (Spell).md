# ☄️5e - Acid Splash☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft./ 5ft. Sphere |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

You create an acidic bubble and hurl it at a point within range, where it explodes in a <u>5-foot-radius Sphere</u>. Each creature in that sphere makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `1d8` <u>Acid</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
