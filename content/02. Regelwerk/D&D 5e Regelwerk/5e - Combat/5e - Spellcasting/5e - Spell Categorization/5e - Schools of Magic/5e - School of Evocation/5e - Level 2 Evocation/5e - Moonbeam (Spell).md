A silvery beam of pale light shines down in a <u>5-foot-radius, 40-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on a point within range. Until the spell ends, [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] fills the Cylinder, and you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action on later turns to move the Cylinder up to <u>60 feet</u>.

When the Cylinder appears, each creature in it makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Radiant</u> damage, and if the creature is _shape-shifted_ (as a result of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Transmutation/5e - Level 4 Transmutation/5e - Polymorph (Spell)|☄️Polymorph]] spell, for example), it reverts to its true form and can't _shape-shift_ until it leaves the Cylinder. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half as much damage only.

A creature also makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] when the spell's area moves into its space and when it enters the spell's area or ends its turn there. A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only <u>once per turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 2.
