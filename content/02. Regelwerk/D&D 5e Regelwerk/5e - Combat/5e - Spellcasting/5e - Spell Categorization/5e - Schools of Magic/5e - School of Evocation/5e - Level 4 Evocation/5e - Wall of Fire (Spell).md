# ☄️5e - Wall of Fire☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Level:**|4 |

> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a piece of charcoal) |
> |**Duration:**|PT1M |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Fire |

You create a wall of fire on a solid surface within range. You can make the wall up to <u>60 feet long, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick</u>, or a ringed wall up to <u>20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick</u>. The wall is opaque and lasts for the duration.

When the wall appears, each creature in its area makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `5d8` <u>Fire</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

One side of the wall, selected by you when you cast this spell, deals `5d8` <u>Fire</u> damage to each creature that ends its turn within <u>10 feet</u> of that side or inside the wall. A creature takes the same damage when it enters the wall for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there. The other side of the wall deals no damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 4.
