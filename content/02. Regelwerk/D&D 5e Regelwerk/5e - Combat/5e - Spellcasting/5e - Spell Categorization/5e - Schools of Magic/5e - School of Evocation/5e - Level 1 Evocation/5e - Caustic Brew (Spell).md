# ☄️5e - Caustic Brew☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Level:**|1 |

> |**Range/Area:**|Self (30 ft. Line) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a bit of rotten food) |
> |**Duration:**|1 Minute |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |

A stream of acid emanates from you in a <u>30 feet long, 5 feet wide Line</u> in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `1d4` <u>Acid</u> damage and is covered in acid for the spell’s duration or until a creature uses its **Action** to scrape or wash the acid off itself or another creature. A creature covered in the acid takes `1d4` ongoing acid damage at <u>the start of each of its turns</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s initial damage and its ongoing damage bot increases by `1d4` for each spell slot level above 1.
