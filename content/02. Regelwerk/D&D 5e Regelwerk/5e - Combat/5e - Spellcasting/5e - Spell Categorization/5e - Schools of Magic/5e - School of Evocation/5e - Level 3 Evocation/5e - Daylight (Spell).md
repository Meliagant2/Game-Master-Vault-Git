For the duration, sunlight spreads from a point within range and fills a <u>60-foot-radius Sphere</u>. The sunlight's area is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] and sheds [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>60 feet</u>.

Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the sunlight to fill a <u>60-foot Emanation</u> originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the sunlight.

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.
