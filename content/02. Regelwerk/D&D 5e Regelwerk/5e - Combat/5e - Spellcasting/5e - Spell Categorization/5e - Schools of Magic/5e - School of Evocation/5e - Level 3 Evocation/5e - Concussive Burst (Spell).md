# ☄️5e - Concussive Burst☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |

A pulse of sound waves ripples out through the air, forming a <u>100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line</u> from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `3d12` <u>Thunder</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Deafened|☠️Deafened]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 3.
