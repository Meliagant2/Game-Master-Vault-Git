# ☄️5e - Spellfire Storm☄️

You conjure a pillar of spellfire in a <u>20-foot-radius, 20-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on a point within range. The area of the Cylinder is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]], and each creature in the spell's area makes a **CON Save** when it appears, when it enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there (A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn). _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10 + 3d6` <u>Radiant</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

In addition, whenever a creature in the Cylinder casts a spell, that creature makes a **CON Save** . _**Failure:**_ The spell dissipates with no effect, and the **Action**, **Bonus Action**, or **Reaction** used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot, the slot isn't expended.

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it.

**<u>Casting as a Circle Spell:</u>** Casting this as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Circle Magic/5e - Circle Magic|☄️Circle]] spell, in addition to the spell's usual [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Spell Components|☄️Components]], you must provide a special component (a blue star sapphire worth 25,000+ SP), which the spell consumes. The spell's range increases to <u>1,6 km</u>, and it no longer requires [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]]. When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 3+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.

The number of secondary casters determines the spell's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Area of Effect/5e - Area of Effect|💥Area of Effect]] and duration, as shown in the table below. The spell ends early if any caster who participated in this casting contributes to another casting of **Spellfire Storm** as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Circle Magic/5e - Circle Magic|☄️Circle]] spell.

| Secondary Casters | Area of Effect                          | Duration |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------- |
| 1-3               | 40-foot-radius, 40-foot-high Cylinder   | 1 Hour   |
| 4-6               | 60-foot-radius, 60-foot-high Cylinder   | 8 Hours  |
| 7+                | 100-foot-radius, 100-foot-high Cylinder | 24 Hours |

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
