You launch a mote of light at one creature or object within range. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d8` <u>Radiant</u> damage, and until <u>the end of your next turn</u>, it emits [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] in a <u>10-foot Emanation</u> and can’t benefit from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] conditions.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
