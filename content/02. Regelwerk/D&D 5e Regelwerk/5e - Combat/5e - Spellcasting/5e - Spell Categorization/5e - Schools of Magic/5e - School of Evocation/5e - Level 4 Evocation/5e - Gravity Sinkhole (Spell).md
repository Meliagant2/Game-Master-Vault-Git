# ☄️5e - Gravity Sinkhole☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Force,Pulling |

A <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> of crushing force forms at a point you can see within range and tugs at the creatures there. Each creature in the sphere makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `7d6` <u>Force</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pulled]] in a straight line toward the center of the sphere, ending in an unoccupied space as close to the center as possible (even if that space is in the air). _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
