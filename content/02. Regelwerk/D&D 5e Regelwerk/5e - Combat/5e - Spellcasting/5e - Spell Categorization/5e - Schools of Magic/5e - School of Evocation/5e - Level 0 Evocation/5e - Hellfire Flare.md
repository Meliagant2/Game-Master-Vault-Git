# ☄️5e - Hellfire Flare☄️

> |**Spell List:**|Divine |

You create an eruption of smoldering hellfire around a creature you can see within range. The target makes a **CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `1d4` <u>Fire</u> damage plus `1d4` <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>At Higher Levels:</u>** Both of the spell's damage types increase by `1d4` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d4 each`), <u>11</u> (`3d4 each`), and <u>17</u> (`4d4 each`).
