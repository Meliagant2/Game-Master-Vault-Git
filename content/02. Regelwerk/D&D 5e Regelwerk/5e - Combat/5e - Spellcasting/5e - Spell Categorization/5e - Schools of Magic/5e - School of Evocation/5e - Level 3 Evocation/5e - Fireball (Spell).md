# ☄️5e - Fireball☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|150 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Fire |

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on that point makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `6d6` <u>Fire</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Burning|☠️Burning]].

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
