You hurl a mote of fire at a creature or object within range. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d10` <u>Fire</u> damage. A flammable object hit by this spell ignites if it isn't being worn or carried.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
