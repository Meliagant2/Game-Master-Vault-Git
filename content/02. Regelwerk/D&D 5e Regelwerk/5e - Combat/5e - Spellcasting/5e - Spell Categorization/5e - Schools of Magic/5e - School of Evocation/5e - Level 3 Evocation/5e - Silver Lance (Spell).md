# ☄️5e - Silver Lance☄️

> |||

Silver energy bursts out from you in a <u>120-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line</u>. Each creature of your choice in the Line makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `5d6` <u>Piercing</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bleeding|☠️Bleeding]] condition. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
