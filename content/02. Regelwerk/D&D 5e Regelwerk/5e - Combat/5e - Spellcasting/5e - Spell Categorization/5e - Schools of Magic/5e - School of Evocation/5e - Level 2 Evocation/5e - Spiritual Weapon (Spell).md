You create a floating, spectral force that resembles a weapon of your choice and lasts for the duration. The force appears within range in a space of your choice, and you can immediately make one <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against one creature within <u>5 feet</u> of the force. On a _**hit**_, the target takes <u>Force</u> damage equal to `1d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier`.

As a **Bonus Action** on your later turns, you can move the force up to 20 feet and repeat the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of it.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
