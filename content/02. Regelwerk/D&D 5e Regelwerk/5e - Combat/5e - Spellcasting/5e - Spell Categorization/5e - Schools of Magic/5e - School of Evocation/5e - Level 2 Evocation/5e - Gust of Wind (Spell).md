A Line of strong wind <u>60 feet long</u> and <u>10 feet wide</u> blasts from you in a direction you choose for the duration. Each creature in the Line makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Pushed]] <u>15 feet</u> away from you in a direction following the Line. A creature that ends its turn in the Line must make the same [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].

Any creature in the Line must spend <u>2 feet</u> of movement for every <u>1 foot</u> it moves when moving closer to you.

The gust disperses gas or vapor, and it extinguishes candles and similar unprotected flames in the area. It causes protected flames, such as those of lanterns, to dance wildly and has a <u>50 percent</u> chance to extinguish them.

As a **Bonus Action** on your later turns, you can change the direction in which the Line blasts from you.
