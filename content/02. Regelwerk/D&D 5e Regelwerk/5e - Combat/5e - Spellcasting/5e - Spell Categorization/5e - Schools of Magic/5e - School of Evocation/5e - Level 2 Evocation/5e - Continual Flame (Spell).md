A flame springs from an object that you touch. The effect casts [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>20-foot radius</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>20 feet</u>. It looks like a regular flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched.
