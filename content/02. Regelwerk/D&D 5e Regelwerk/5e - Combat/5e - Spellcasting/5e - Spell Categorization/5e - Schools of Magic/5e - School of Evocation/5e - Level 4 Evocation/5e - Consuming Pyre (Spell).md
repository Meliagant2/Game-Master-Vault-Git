# ☄️5e - Consuming Pyre☄️

You cause a creature that you can see within range to burst into consuming, relentless flames. The target makes a **repeated DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `2d8` <u>Fire</u> damage and `2d8` <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (choose when you cast the spell), and the flames linger. While the flames linger, the creature takes the damage again at <u>the start of each of its turns</u>. The target, or another creature within reach of the target, can take an **Action** to allow the target to repeat the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], ending the spell on a success. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only and the spell ends.

A creature killed by the damage from this spell is reduced to ash, along with any nonmagical equipment it is wearing or carrying.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s <u>Fire</u> damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 4.
