# ☄️5e - Chromatic Orb☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|90 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a diamond worth 50+ SP) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult |

You hurl a 4-inch-diameter sphere of energy at a creature that you can see within range. You choose <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Poison</u>, or <u>Thunder</u> for the type of orb you create, and then make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the creature takes `2d8` damage of the chosen type.

If you roll the same number on two or more of the `d8s`, the orb leaps to a different target of your choice within <u>30 feet</u> of the target. Make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack roll]] against the new target, and make a new damage roll. The orb can't leap again unless you cast the spell with a _level 2+_ spell slot.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 1. The orb can leap a maximum number of times equal to the level of the slot expended, and a creature can be targeted only once by each casting of this spell.
