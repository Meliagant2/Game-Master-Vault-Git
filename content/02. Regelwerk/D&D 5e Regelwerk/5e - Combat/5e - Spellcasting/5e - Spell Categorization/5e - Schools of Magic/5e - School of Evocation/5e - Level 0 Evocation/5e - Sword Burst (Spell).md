# ☄️5e - Sword Burst☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult |

You create a momentary circle of spectral blades that sweep around you. Each creature in a <u>5-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `1d8` <u>Force</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
