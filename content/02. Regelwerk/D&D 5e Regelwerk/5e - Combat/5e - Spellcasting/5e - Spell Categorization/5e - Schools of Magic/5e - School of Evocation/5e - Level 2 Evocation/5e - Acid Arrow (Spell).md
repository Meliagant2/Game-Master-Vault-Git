A shimmering green arrow streaks toward a target within range and bursts in a spray of acid. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `6d4` <u>Acid</u> damage and `2d4` <u>Acid</u> damage at <u>the end of its next turn</u>. On a _**miss**_, the arrow splashes the target with acid for half as much of the initial damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage (both initial and later) increases by `2d4` for each spell slot level above 2.
