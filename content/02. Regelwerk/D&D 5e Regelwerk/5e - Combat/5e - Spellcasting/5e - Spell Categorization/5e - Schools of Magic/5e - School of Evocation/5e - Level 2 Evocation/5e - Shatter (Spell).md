# ☄️5e - Shatter☄️

> |||

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Thunder |

A loud noise erupts from a point of your choice within range. Each creature in a <u>15-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered there makes a **CON Save**. A _Construct_ has **DISADV** on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d8` <u>Thunder</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half as much damage only.

A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
