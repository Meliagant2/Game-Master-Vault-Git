# ☄️5e - Lightning Bolt☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

A stroke of lightning forming a <u>100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line</u> blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `6d6` <u>Lightning</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
