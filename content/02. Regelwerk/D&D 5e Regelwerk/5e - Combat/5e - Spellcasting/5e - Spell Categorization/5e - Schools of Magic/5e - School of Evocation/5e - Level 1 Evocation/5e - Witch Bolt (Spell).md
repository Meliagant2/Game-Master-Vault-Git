# ☄️5e - Witch Bolt☄️☄️

A beam of crackling energy lances toward a creature within range, forming a sustained arc of lightning between you and the target. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against it. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `2d12` <u>Lightning</u> damage.

On each of your subsequent turns, you can take a **Bonus Action** to deal `1d12` <u>Lightning</u> damage to the target automatically, <u>even if the first attack missed</u>.

The spell ends if the target is ever outside the spell's range or if it has [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Total Cover|🏔️Total Cover]] from you.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s initial damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 1.
