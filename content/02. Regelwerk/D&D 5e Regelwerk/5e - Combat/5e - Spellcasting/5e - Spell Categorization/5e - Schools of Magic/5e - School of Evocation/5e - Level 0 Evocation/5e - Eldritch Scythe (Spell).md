You lash out with an ephemeral and unearthly blade. Make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d10` <u>Force</u> damage, and you can choose a creature that you can see and that is within <u>5 feet</u> of you. It takes half the damage the first target took.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
