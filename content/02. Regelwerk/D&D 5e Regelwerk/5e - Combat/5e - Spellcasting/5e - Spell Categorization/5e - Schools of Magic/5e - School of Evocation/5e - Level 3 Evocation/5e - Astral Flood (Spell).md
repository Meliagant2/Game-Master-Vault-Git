You channel energy from the Astral Sea to unleash a torrent of magic from you in a <u>30-foot Cone</u>. Each creature in the Cone makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d12 + 2d6` <u>Cold</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (chosen when you cast this spell) and suffers an additional effect determined by your choice of damage type (see below for the effects). _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Cold Damage:</u>** The target has **DISADV** on the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] it makes before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Radiant Damage:</u>** The target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
