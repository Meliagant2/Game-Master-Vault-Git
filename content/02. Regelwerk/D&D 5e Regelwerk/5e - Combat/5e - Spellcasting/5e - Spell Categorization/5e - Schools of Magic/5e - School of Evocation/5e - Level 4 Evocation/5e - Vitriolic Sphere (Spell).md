# ☄️5e - Vitriolic Sphere☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|4 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|150 ft. (25 ft. Sphere) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a drop of bile) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Acid |

You point at a location within range, and a glowing, 1-foot-diameter ball of acid streaks there and explodes in a <u>25-foot-radius Sphere</u>. Each creature in that area makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `12d3` <u>Acid</u> damage and another `4d3` <u>Acid</u> damage at <u>the end of its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the initial damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage (both initial and later) increases by `2d3` for each spell slot level above 4.
