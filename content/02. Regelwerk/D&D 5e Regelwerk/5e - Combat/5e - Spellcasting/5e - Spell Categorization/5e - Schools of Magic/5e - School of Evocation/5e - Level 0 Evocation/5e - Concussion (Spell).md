# ☄️5e - Concussion☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Evocation |
> |**Level:**|0 |

> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|1 round |

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Thunder,Deafened |

With a boom that can be heard <u>100 feet</u> away, you cause a sudden increase in the air pressure surrounding a creature you can see within range. The target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `1d8` <u>Thunder</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Deafened|☠️Deafened]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
