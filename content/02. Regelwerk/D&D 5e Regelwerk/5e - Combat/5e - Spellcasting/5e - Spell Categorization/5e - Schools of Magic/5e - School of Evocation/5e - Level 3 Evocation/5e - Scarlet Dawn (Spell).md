# ☄️5e - Scarlet Dawn☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Divine,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Necrotic,Healing,Blood Magic |

Crimson light shines down in a <u>20-foot-radius, 60-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area that isn't a _Construct_ or _Undead_ makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10 + 2d6` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

_Constructs_ and _Undead_ in the area regain `2d10 + 2d6` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]].

If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` and the level of spell that can be dispelled increases by `1` for each spell slot level above 3.
