# ☄️5e - Cone of Cold☄️

> |**Range/Area:**|15 ft. Cone |

> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Cold,Immobilized |

You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a <u>15-foot Cone</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `1d12` <u>Cold</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

The cold freezes nonmagical liquids in the area that aren't being worn or carried. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 1. The cone's size increases with a spell slot of level 3-4 (<u>30-foot Cone</u>), 5-6 (<u>60-foot Cone</u>), or 7+ (<u>120-foot Cone</u>). When you use a spell slot of _level 3_ to cast this spell, creatures that _**fail**_ the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] are also [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Immobilized|☠️Immobilized]] by ice formations, that bind them to the ground for <u>1 minute</u>. A creature hindered by ice can brake free by taking an **Action**, or by _succeeding_ on a **repeated CON Save** at the end of its turn.
