You hurl <u>two</u> fiery rays. You can hurl them at one target within range or at several. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] for each ray. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `3d6` <u>Fire</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You create one additional ray for each spell slot level above 2.
