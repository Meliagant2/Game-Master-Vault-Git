A <u>120-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line</u> of roaring flame emanates out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line makes a **repeated DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `7d6` <u>Fire</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Burning|☠️Burning]]. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
