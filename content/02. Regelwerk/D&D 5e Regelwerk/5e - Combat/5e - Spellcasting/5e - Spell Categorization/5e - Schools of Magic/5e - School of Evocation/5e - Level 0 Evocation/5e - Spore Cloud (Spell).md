You produce a cloud of virulent spores. Each creature in a <u>5-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `1d10` <u>Poison</u> damage. _**Failure (by 5):**_ The creature is also [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|5e - Poisoned]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
