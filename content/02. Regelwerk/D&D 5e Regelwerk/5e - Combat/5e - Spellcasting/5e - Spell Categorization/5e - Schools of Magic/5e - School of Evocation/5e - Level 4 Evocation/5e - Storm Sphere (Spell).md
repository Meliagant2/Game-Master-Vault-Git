# ☄️5e - Storm Sphere☄️

> |**Components:**|V, S |

> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Lightning,Difficult Terrain |

A <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> of whirling air springs into existence, centered on a point you choose within range. The sphere remains for the spell's duration. Each creature in the sphere when it appears or that ends its turn there makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.

The sphere's space is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]].

Until the spell ends, you can use a **Bonus Action** on each of your turns to cause a bolt of lightning to leap from the center of the sphere toward one creature you choose within <u>60 feet</u> of the center. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]]. You have **ADV** on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] if the target is in the sphere. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `3d10` <u>Lightning</u> damage.

Creatures within <u>30 feet</u> of the sphere have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Perception|💃Perception]] checks made to listen.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 4.
