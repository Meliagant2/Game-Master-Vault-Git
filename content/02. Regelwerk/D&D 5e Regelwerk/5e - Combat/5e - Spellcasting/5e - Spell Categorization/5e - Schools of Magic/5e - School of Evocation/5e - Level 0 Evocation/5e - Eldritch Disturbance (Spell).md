# ☄️5e - Eldritch Disturbance☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

You can strike a creature you can see within <u>60 feet</u> with a reality-distorting curse, blending pain both mental and physical. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `1d10` <u>Force</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
