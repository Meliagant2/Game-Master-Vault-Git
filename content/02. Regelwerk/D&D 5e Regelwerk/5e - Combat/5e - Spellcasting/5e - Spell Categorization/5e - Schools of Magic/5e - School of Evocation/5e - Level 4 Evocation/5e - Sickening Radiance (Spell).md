# ☄️5e - Sickening Radiance☄️

> |**Components:**|V, S |

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Occult |

Dim, greenish light spreads within a <u>30-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point you choose within range. The light spreads around corners, and it lasts until the spell ends.

When a creature moves into the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, that creature makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `5d10` <u>Radiant</u> damage, and it suffers one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] and emits a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim]], greenish light in a <u>5-foot radius</u>. This light makes it impossible for the creature to benefit from being [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]]. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half as much damage only.

The light and any levels of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] caused by this spell go away when the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 4.
