You thrash a creature with a scintillating tendril of arcane force. Make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against a creature within <u>15 feet</u>. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d10` <u>Force</u> damage.

At <u>the end of your turn</u> you gain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the damage you dealt with **Eldritch Whip** this turn, which last until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

When you _**hit**_ a creature with this spell and you have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] that were granted by **Eldritch Whip**, you can lose the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] to gain a bonus to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] equal to `1/2 x the Temp HP you lost`.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** The spell creates <u>two</u> tendrils of arcane force at level <u>5</u>, <u>three</u> tendrils at level <u>11</u>, and <u>four</u> tendrils at level <u>17</u>. You can direct the tendrils at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] for each tendril.
