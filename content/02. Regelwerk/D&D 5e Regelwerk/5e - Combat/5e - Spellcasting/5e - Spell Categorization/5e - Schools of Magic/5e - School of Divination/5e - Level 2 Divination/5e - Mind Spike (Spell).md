# ☄️5e - Mind Spike☄️

> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult |

You drive a spike of psionic energy into the mind of one creature you can _see_ within range. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `4d8` <u>Psychic</u> damage. You also always know the target's location until the spell ends, but only while the two of you are on the same plane of existence. While you have this knowledge, the target can't become [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]] from you, and if it has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] condition, it gains no benefit from that condition against you. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
