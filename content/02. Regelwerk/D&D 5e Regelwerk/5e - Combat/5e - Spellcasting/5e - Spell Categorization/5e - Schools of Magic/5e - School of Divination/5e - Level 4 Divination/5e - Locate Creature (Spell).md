Describe or name a creature that is familiar to you. You sense the direction to the creature's location if that creature is within <u>300 m</u> of you. If the creature is moving, you know the direction of its movement.

The spell can locate a specific creature known to you or the nearest creature of a specific kind (such as a human or a unicorn) if you have seen such a creature up close—within <u>30 feet</u>—at least once. If the creature you described or named is in a different form, such as under the effects of a [[02. This spell can't locate a creature if any thickness of lead blocks a direct path between you and the creature.
