Guided by a flash of magical insight, you make one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] with the weapon used in the spell’s casting. The [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] uses your <u>spellcasting ability</u> for the attack and damage rolls instead of using <u>STR</u> or <u>DEX</u>. If the attack deals damage, it can be <u>Radiant</u> damage or the weapon’s normal damage type (your choice).

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** Whether you choose to deal Radiant damage or the weapon’s normal damage type, the attack deals extra Radiant damage when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`1d6`), <u>11</u> (`2d6`), and <u>17</u> (`3d6`).
