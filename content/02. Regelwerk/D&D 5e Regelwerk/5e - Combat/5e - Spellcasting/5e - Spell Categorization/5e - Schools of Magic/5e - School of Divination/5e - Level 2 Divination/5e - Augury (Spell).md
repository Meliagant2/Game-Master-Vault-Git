# ☄️5e - Augury☄️

> |**Effect:**|Foreknowledge,Ritual |

You receive an omen from an otherworldly entity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next <u>30 minutes</u>. The GM chooses the omen from the Omens table.

|Omen|For Results That Will Be...|
|---|---|
|Weal|Good|
|Woe|Bad|
|Weal and woe|Good and bad|
|Indifference|Neither good nor bad|

The spell doesn't account for circumstances, such as other spells, that might change the results.

If you cast the spell more than once before finishing a **Long Rest**, there is a cumulative <u>25 percent</u> chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.
