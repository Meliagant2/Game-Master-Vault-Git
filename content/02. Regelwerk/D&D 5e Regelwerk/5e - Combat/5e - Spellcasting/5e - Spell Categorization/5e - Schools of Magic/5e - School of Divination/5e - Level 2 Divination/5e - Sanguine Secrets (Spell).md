You draw knowledge through the power of blood about a creature you can see within range. You take `1d4` <u>True</u> damage and you learn the target's _heritage/species_ and _creature type_, its current [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], and any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Conditions|☠️Conditions]] affecting it. You also learn if it has any of the following, and, if so, what they are: <u>Vulnerability</u>, <u>Resistance</u>, or <u>Immunity</u> to damage or conditions. Additionally, the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] you make against the target before <u>the end of your next turn</u> has **ADV**.
