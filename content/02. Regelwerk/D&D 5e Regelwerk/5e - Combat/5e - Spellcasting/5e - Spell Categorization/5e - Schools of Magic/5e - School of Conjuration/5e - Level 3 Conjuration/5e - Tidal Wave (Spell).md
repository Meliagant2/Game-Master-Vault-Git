# ☄️5e - Tidal Wave☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self (100 ft. Line) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a drop of water) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Prone |

You conjure up a wave of water that crashes down in a <u>100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line</u>, starting from you. Each creature in that area makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d12` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]]. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

The water then spreads out across the ground in all directions, extinguishing unprotected flames in its area and within <u>30 feet</u> of it, and then it vanishes.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 3.
