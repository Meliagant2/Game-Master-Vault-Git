# ☄️5e - Conjure Woodland Beings☄️

You conjure nature spirits that flit around you in a <u>10-foot Emanation</u> for the duration. Whenever the Emanation enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, you can force the creature to make a **WIS Save** (A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn). _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `5d8` <u>Force</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

In addition, you can take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Disengage|⚔️Disengage]] action as a **Bonus Action** for the spell's duration.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 4.
