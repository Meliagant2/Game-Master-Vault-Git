# ☄️5e - Flaming Sphere☄️

> |**Concentration:**|x |

> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Fire |

You create a <u>5-foot-diameter sphere</u> of fire in an unoccupied space on the ground within range. It lasts for the duration. Any creature that ends its turn within <u>5 feet</u> of the sphere makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d12` <u>Fire</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

As a **Bonus Action**, you can move the sphere up to <u>30 feet</u>, rolling it along the ground. If you move the sphere into a creature's space, that creature makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the sphere, and the sphere stops moving for the turn.

When you move the sphere, you can direct it over barriers up to <u>5 feet</u> tall and jump it across pits up to <u>10 feet wide</u>. Flammable objects that aren't being worn or carried start [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Burning|☠️Burning]] if touched by the sphere, and it sheds [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>20-foot radius</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>20 feet</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 2.
