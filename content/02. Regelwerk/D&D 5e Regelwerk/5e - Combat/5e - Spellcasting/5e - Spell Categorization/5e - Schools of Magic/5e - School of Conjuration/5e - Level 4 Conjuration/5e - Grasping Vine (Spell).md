# ☄️5e - Grasping Vine☄️

> |**Spell List:**|Primal |

You conjure a vine that sprouts from a surface in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The vine lasts for the duration.

Make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of the vine. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `4d10` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pulled]] up to <u>30 feet</u> toward the vine; if the target is _Huge or smaller_, it has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] condition (**escape DC** equal to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]). The vine can grapple only one creature at a time, and you can cause the vine to release a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] creature (**no Action** required).

As a **Bonus Action** on your later turns, you can repeat the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of the vine.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The number of creatures the vine can grapple increases by one for each spell slot level above 4.
