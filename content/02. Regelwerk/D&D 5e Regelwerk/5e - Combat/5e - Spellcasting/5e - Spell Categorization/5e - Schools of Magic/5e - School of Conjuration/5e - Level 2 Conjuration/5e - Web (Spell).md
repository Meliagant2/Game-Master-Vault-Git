# ☄️5e - Web☄️

You conjure a mass of sticky webbing at a point within range. The webs fill a <u>20-foot radius Sphere</u> there for the duration. The webs are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]], and the area within them is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Lightly Obscured|💡Lightly Obscured]].

If the webs aren't anchored between two solid masses (such as walls or trees) or layered across a floor, wall, or ceiling, the web collapses on itself, and the spell ends at <u>the start of your next turn</u>. Webs layered over a flat surface have a depth of <u>5 feet</u>.

The first time a creature enters the webs on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] condition while in the webs or until it breaks free.

A creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] by the webs can take an **Action** to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Breaking|💃Breaking]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Success:**_ It is no longer [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]].

The webs are flammable. Any <u>5-foot Cube</u> of webs exposed to fire burns away in <u>1 round</u>, dealing `2d4` <u>Fire</u> damage to any creature that starts its turn in the fire.
