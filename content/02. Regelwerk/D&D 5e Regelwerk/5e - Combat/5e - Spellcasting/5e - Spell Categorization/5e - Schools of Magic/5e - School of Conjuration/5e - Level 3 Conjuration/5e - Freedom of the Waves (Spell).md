# ☄️5e - Freedom of the Waves☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. (15 ft. Cylinder) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a strand of wet hair) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Prone,Teleportation |

You conjure a deluge of seawater in a <u>15-foot-radius, 10-foot-tall Cylinder</u> centered on a point within range. This water takes the form of a tidal wave, a whirlpool, a waterspout, or another form of your choice. Each creature in the area makes a **STR Save**. You can choose a number of creatures equal to `your spellcasting modifier (minimum of 1)` to automatically _succeed_ on this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]]. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `3d12`<u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

If you are within the spell's area, as part of the **Action** you use to cast the spell, you can vanish into the deluge and teleport to an unoccupied space that you can see within the spell's area.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 3.
