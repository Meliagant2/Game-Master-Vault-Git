# ☄️5e - Poison Wave☄️

> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Effect:**|Poison,Poisoned |

A wave of poisonous fumes emanates from you in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u>. Creatures within that area make a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `8d6` <u>Poison</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
