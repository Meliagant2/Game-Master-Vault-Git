You hurl a spectral, barbed chain at a creature that you can see within range. The target makes a **repeated STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `2d6` <u>Force</u> damage, is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pulled]] up to <u>30 feet</u> toward you, and is bound by the chain. While bound by the chain, the creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Tethered|☠️Tethered (30 ft.)]] to you, and it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]]. If you are ever more than <u>30 feet</u> away from the target, the spell ends. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 2.
