# ☄️5e - Goodberry☄️☄️

> [!wikibox]

> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |

<u>Five</u> berries appear in your hand and are infused with magic for the duration. A creature can take a **Bonus Action** to eat one berry. Eating a berry restores `1d4` <u>Hit Point</u>.

Uneaten berries disappear when the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The number of berries you conjure increases by `5` for every spell slot level above 1st.
