# ☄️5e - Conjure Constructs☄️

You conjure a group of intangible, orderly spirits that appear as a _Medium_ group of modrons or other _Constructs_ in an unoccupied space you can see within range. The spirits last for the duration. When you cast this spell and as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action on subsequent turns, you can command the spirits to target one creature or object you can see within <u>5 feet</u> of the spirits and create one of the following effects:

**<u>Clockwork Force:</u>** The target makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `4d8` <u>Force</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Orderly Ward:</u>** The target gains [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `2d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier`.

When you _move_ on your turn, you can also move the spirits up to <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] both increase by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 3.
