# ☄️5e - Secret Chest☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|4 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Touch |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a chest, 3 feet x 2 feet x 2 feet, constructed from rare materials worth 5,000+ SP, and a Tiny replica of the chest made from the same materials worth 50+ SP) |
> |**Duration:**|Until Dispelled |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Utility |

You hide a chest and all its contents on the _Ethereal Plane_. You must touch the chest and the miniature replica that serve as Material components for the spell. The chest can contain up to <u>12 cubic feet</u> of nonliving material (<u>3 feet x 2 feet x 2 feet</u> (<u>0,9 m x 0,6 m x 0,6 m</u>)).

While the chest remains on the _Ethereal Plane_, you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action and touch the replica to recall the chest. It appears in an unoccupied space on the ground within <u>5 feet</u> of you. You can send the chest back to the _Ethereal Plane_ by taking a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to touch the chest and the replica.

After <u>60 days</u>, there is a _cumulative_ <u>5 percent</u> chance at <u>the end of each day</u> that the spell ends. The spell also ends if you cast this spell again or if the Tiny replica chest is destroyed. If the spell ends and the larger chest is on the _Ethereal Plane_, the chest remains there for you or someone else to find.
