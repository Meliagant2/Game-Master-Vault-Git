# ☄️5e - Murder of Crows☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Piercing,Blinded |

You summon and unleash a murder of deadly crows. Each creature of your choice in a <u>30-foot Cone</u> originating from you makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature takes `6d6` <u>Piercing</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] condition. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

A Blinded creature **repeats** the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] at <u>the end of each of its turns</u>, ending the effect on itself on a _success_. The effect also ends if the creature regains any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]]. The crows disperse and vanish after they strike.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
