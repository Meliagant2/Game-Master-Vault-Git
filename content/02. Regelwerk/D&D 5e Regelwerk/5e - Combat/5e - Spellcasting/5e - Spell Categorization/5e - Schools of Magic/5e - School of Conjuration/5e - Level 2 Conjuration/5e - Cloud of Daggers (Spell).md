# ☄️5e - Cloud of Daggers☄️

> |||

> |**Duration:**|PT1M |
> |**Concentration:**|x |

> |**Effect:**|Slashing |

You conjure spinning daggers in a <u>5-foot Cube</u> centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area takes `4d4` <u>Slashing</u> damage. A creature also takes this damage if it enters the Cube or ends its turn there or if the Cube moves into its space. A creature takes this damage only <u>once per turn</u>.

On your later turns, you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to teleport the Cube up to <u>30 feet</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The damage increases by `2d4` for each spell slot level above 2.
