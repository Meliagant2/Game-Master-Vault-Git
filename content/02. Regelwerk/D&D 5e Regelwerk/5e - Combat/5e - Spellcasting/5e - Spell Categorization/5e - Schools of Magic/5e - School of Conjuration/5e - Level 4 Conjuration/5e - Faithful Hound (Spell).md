# ☄️5e - Faithful Hound☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|4 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a silver whistle) |
> |**Duration:**|PT8H |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult |
> |**Effect:**|Force |

You conjure a phantom watchdog in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hound remains for the duration or until the two of you are more than <u>300 feet</u> apart from each other.

No one but you can see the hound, and it is intangible and <u>Invulnerable</u>. When a _Small or larger_ creature comes within <u>30 feet</u> of it without first speaking the _password_ that you specify when you cast this spell, the hound starts barking loudly. The hound has [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]] with a range of <u>30 feet</u>.

At <u>the start of each of your turns</u>, the hound attempts to bite one enemy within <u>5 feet</u> of it. That enemy makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `4d8` <u>Force</u> damage.

On your later turns, you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to move the hound up to <u>30 feet</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 4.
