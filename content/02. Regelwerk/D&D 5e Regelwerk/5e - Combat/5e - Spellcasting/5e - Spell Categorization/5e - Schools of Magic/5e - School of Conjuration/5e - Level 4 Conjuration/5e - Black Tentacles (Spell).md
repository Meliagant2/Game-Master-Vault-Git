Squirming, ebony tentacles fill a <u>10-foot-radius Sphere</u> on ground that you can see within range. For the duration, these tentacles turn the ground in that area into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]].

Each creature in that area makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage, and it has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. A creature also makes that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] if it enters the area or ends its turn there. A creature makes that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn.

A [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] creature can take an **Action** to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Breaking|💃Breaking]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Success:**_ The target escapes and is no longer [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]].

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 4.
