# ☄️5e - Ride the Rifts☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|4 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self (60 ft. Line) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a length of copper wire) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Lightning,Thunder,Teleportation |

You transform yourself into a bolt of lightning, creating a <u>5-foot-wide Line</u> between your current space and an unoccupied space within <u>60 feet</u> of you, as you teleport to the chosen space.

Immediately after you disappear, a thunderous boom sounds, and each creature within <u>10 feet</u> of the space you left makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `1d8` <u>Thunder</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only. The thunder can be heard from up to <u>300 feet</u> away.

Each creature in the Line makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d8` <u>Lightning</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s <u>Lightning</u> and <u>Thunder</u> damage increase by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 4. In addition, the maximum length of the line increases by <u>10 feet</u> for each spell slot level above 4.
