# ☄️5e - Call Lightning☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|3 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. (60 ft. Cylinder) |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|PT10M |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Lightning |

A storm cloud appears at a point within range that you can see above yourself. It takes the shape of a <u>Cylinder</u> that is <u>10 feet tall with a 60-foot radius</u>.

When you cast the spell, choose a point you can see under the cloud. A lightning bolt shoots from the cloud to that point. Each creature within <u>5 feet</u> of that point makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10 + 3d3` <u>Lightning</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Until the spell ends, you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to call down lightning in that way again, targeting the same point or a different one.

If you're outdoors in a storm when you cast this spell, the spell gives you control over that storm instead of creating a new one. Under such conditions, the spell's damage increases by `1d10`.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `3d3` for each spell slot level above 3.
