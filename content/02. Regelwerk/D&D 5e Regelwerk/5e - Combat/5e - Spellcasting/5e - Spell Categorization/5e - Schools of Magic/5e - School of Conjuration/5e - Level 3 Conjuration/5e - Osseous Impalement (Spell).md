Massive bone spikes sprout under up to <u>four</u> creatures on the ground of your choice that you can _see_ within range. Each creature makes e a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d10` <u>Piercing</u> damage and is impaled by the spike, gaining the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] condition and be held aloft <u>5 feet</u> in the air for the duration. A creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] in this way takes `1d10` Piercing damage at <u>the start of each of its turns</u>.

A creature can use its **Action** to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Breaking|💃Breaking]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Success:**_ It breakes the bone spike and frees itself, ending the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] condition.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The damage (both initial and ongoing) increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 3.
