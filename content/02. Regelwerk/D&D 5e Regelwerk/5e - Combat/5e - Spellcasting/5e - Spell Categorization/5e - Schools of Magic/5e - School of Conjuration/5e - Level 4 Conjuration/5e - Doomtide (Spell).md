You create a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> of inky fog within range. The fog is magical [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] and lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Evocation/5e - Level 2 Evocation/5e - Gust of Wind (Spell)|☄️Gust of Wind]]) disperses it.

Each creature in the Sphere when it appears, when the Sphere moves into the creature's space, when it enters the Sphere, or when it ends its turn inside the Sphere makes a **WIS Save** (A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn). _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `5d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage and subtracts `1d6` from its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

The Sphere moves <u>10 feet</u> away from you at <u>the start of each of your turns</u>.

**<u>Casting as a Circle Spell:</u>** Casting this as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Circle Magic/5e - Circle Magic|☄️Circle]] spell requires a minimum of <u>five</u> secondary casters. In addition to the spell's usual [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Spell Components|☄️Components]], you must provide a special component (a string of three black pearls from Pandemonium), which the spell consumes. The spell's range increases to <u>1,6 km</u>, and its duration increases to <u>until dispelled</u> (no [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] required). The spell ends early if any caster who participated in this casting contributes to another casting of **Doomtide** as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Circle Magic/5e - Circle Magic|☄️Circle]] spell.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 3+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.
