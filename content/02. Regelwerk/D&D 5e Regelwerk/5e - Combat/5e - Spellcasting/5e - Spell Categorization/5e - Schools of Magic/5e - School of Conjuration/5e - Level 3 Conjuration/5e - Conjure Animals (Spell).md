# ☄️5e - Conjure Animals☄️

> |**Components:**|V, S |

> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Slashing,ADV |

You conjure nature spirits that appear as a Large pack of spectral, intangible animals in an unoccupied space you can see within range. The pack lasts for the duration, and you choose the spirits' animal form, such as wolves, serpents, or birds. It takes the space of a _Large_ creature, but creatures can move through it as if it were [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]].

You have **ADV** on <u>STR</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] while you're within <u>5 feet</u> of the pack, and when you _move_ on your turn, you can also move the pack up to <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see.

Whenever the pack moves within <u>10 feet</u> of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters a space within <u>10 feet</u> of the pack or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a **DEX Save** (A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn). _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d10` <u>Slashing</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 3.
