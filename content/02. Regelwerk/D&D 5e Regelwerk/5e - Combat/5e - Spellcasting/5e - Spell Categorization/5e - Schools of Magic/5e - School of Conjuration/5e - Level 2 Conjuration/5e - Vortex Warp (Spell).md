# ☄️5e - Vortex Warp☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Components:**|V, S |

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Teleportation |

You magically twist space around another creature you can see within range. The target makes a **CON Save** (the target can choose to fail). _**Failure:**_ The target is teleported to an unoccupied space of your choice that you can _see_ within range. The chosen space must be on a surface or in a liquid that can support the target without the target having to squeeze.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The range of the spell increases by <u>30 feet</u> for each slot level above 2.
