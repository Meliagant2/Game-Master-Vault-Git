# ☄️5e - Spirit Guardians☄️

Protective spirits flit around you in a <u>15-foot Emanation</u> for the duration. Their spectral form appears angelic, fiendish or fey (your choice).

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. Any other creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is halved in the Emanation, and whenever the Emanation enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, the creature makes a **WIS Save** (A creature makes this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] only once per turn). _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d8` <u>Radiant</u> damage (angelic or fey form) or `3d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage (fiendish form). _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 3.
