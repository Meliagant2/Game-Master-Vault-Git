# ☄️5e - Confounding Shadows☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|0 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|1 round |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged|
> |**Spell List:**|Occult |
> |**Effect:**|Psychic |

You create an orb of writhing shadows and hurl it at a creature within range. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `1d8` <u>Psychic</u> damage and it is unable to _see_ you until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
