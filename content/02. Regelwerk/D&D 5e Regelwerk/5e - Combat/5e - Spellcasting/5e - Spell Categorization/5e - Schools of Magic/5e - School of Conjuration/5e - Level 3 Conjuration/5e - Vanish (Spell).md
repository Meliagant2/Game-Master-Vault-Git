When you cast this spell, you gain 1 level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Strife|☠️Strife]].

When you cast this spell, you vanish from your current plane of existence and appear in the _Ethereal Plane_, avoiding the triggering damage (The spell has no effect, if you are already on that plane). While on the Ethereal Plane, you can perceive the plane you left, which is cast in shades of gray, but you can't see anything there more than <u>60 feet</u> away. You can affect and be affected only by other creatures on the Ethereal Plane, and creatures on the other plane can't perceive you unless they have a special ability that lets them perceive things on the Ethereal Plane.

At <u>the start of your next turn</u>, you return to an unoccupied space of your choice within <u>30 feet</u> of the space you vanished from. If no unoccupied space is available within that range, you appear in the nearest unoccupied space (chosen at random if more than one space is equally near).
