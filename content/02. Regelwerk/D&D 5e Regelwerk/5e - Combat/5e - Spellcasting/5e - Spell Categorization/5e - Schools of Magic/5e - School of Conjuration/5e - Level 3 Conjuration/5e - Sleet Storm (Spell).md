Until the spell ends, sleet falls in a <u>40-foot-tall, 20-foot-radius Cylinder</u> centered on a point you choose within range. The area is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Heavily Obscured|💡Heavily Obscured]], and exposed flames in the area are doused.

Ground in the Cylinder is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]]. When a creature enters the Cylinder for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] and loses [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]].
