# ☄️5e - Mote of Faith☄️

You manifest a pocket of either the **Heavens** or **Hell** in a <u>15-foot-radius Sphere</u>, centered on a point within range and lasting for the duration.

**<u>Heavens:</u>** You conjure a sphere of radiant [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]], clouds, and unearthly heat. The cloud of heaven is filled with angelic chorus, which can be heard up to <u>30 feet</u> away. No [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]], not even magical [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]], can dim the cloud, the area is considered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Heavily Obscured|💡Heavily Obscured]] and creatures within the area are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]].

The cloud warps space-time, making the cloud's area [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]]. A creature who starts their turn in that area takes `2d6` <u>Radiant</u> damage. A creature who ends their turn in that area makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage as angelic voices overwhelm their mind.

**<u>Hell:</u>** You conjure a sphere of magical [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]], brimstone, and blasting heat. The cloud of hellfire echoes with the cries of damned souls, which can be heard up to <u>30 feet</u> away. No light, even magical light, can illuminate the cloud, and creatures within the area are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]].

The cloud warps the timescape, making the cloud's area [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]]. A creature who starts their turn in that area takes `2d6` <u>Fire</u> damage. A creature who ends their turn in that area makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage as the voices of the damned crowd their mind.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The <u>Fire</u> and <u>Radiant</u> damage, or the <u>Psychic</u> damage (your choice) increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 3.
