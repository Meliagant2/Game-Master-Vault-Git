You conjure spirits from the Elemental Planes that flit around you in a <u>15-foot Emanation</u> for the duration. Until the spell ends, any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] you make deals an extra `2d8` damage when you hit a creature in the Emanation. This damage is <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, or <u>Lightning</u> (your choice when you make the attack).

In addition, the ground in the Emanation is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] for your enemies.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each spell slot levels above 4.
