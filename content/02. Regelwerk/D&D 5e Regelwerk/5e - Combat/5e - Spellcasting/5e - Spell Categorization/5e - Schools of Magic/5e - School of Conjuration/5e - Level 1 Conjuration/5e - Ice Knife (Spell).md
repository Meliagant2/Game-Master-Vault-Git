# ☄️5e - Ice Knife☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Conjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft |
> |**Components:**|S, M (a drop of water or a piece of ice) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Ranged, DEX|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |

You create a shard of ice and fling it at one creature within range. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d10` <u>Piercing</u> damage. _Hit_ or _miss_, the shard then explodes. The target and each creature within <u>5 feet</u> of it make a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Cold</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You create an additional shard for each spell slot level above 1. You can direct the shard at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each shard. If a creature is in the area of more than one ice explosion, it makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the <u>Cold</u> damage only once, but, on a _**failure**_ takes damage for each shard.
