You summon an otherworldly being that appears as a loyal steed in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. This creature uses the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Summons/5e - Otherworldly Steed|🐺Otherworldly Steed]] stat block. If you already have a steed from this spell, the steed is replaced by the new one.

The steed resembles a Large, rideable animal of your choice, such as a horse, a camel, a dire wolf, or an elk. Whenever you cast the spell, choose the steed's creature type—_Celestial_, _Fey_, or _Fiend_—which determines certain traits in the stat block.

**<u>Combat:</u>** The steed is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, it shares your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]] count, and it functions as a controlled mount while you ride it (as defined in the rules on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Mounted Combat/5e - Mounted Combat|🐴Mounted Combat]]). If you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition, the steed takes its turn immediately after yours and acts independently, focusing on protecting you.

**<u>Disappearance of the Steed:</u>** The steed disappears if it drops to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] or if you die. When it disappears, it leaves behind anything it was wearing or carrying. If you cast this spell again, you decide whether you summon the steed that disappeared or a different one.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
