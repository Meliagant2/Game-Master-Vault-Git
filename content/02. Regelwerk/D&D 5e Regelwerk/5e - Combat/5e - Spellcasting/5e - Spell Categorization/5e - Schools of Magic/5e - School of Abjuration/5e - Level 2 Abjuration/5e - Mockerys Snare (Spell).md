You imbue yourself or one willing creature you touch with duplicitous powers, laying a trap to ensnare a foe in spectral bindings. The next time the imbued creature is _hit_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] before the spell ends, the attacking creature makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] until the spell ends. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
