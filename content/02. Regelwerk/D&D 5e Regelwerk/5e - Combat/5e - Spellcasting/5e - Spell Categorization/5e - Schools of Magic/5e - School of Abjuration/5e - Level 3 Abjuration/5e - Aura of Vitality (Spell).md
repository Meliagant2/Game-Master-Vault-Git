An aura radiates from you in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> for the duration. When you create the aura and at <u>the start of each of your turns</u> while it persists, you can restore `2d8` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] to one creature in it.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The healing increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 3.
