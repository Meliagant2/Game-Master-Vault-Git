You touch a willing creature and neutralize its aura. Until the spell ends, the target can't be perceived by _Fey_, _Fiends_, _Celestials_, and _Undead_.

The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], deals [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]], casts a _spell_, or moves within <u>5 feet</u> of one of the creature types.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
