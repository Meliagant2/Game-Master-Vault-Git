A protective magical force surrounds you, manifesting as a spectral magical effect that covers you and your gear. When you cast this spell choose one of the following damage types: <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Necrotic</u>, or <u>Radiant</u>. You gain `5` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] for the duration. If a creature hits you with a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] while you have these [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]], the creature takes <u>5</u> damage of the chosen damage type.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] and damage both increases by `5` for each spell slot level above 1.
