# ☄️5e - Thorn Armor☄️

When you cast this spell, a flexible exoskeleton covered in thorns appears around a willing target within range. The target gains `2d6` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]]. If a creature hits the target with a melee attack roll before the spell ends, that creature takes <u>Piercing</u> damage equal to the number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] lost as a result of the attack. This spell ends early if the target has no remaining [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] granted by this spell.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You gain an additional `2d6` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] for each spell slot level above 1.
