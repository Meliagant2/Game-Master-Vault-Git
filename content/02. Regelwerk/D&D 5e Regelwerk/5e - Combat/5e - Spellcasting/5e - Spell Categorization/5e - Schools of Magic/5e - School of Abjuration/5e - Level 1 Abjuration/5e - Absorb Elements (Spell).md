The spell captures some of the incoming energy, lessening its effect on you and storing it for your next melee attack. You have <u>Resistance</u> to the triggering damage type until the <u>start of your next turn</u>. Also, the first time you hit with a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] on <u>your next turn</u>, the target takes an extra `1d6` damage of the triggering type, and the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 1.
