You fortify the fabric of the planes in a <u>15-foot-radius Sphere</u> you can see within range. Within that area, portals close and can't be opened for the duration. Spells and other effects that allow planar travel or open portals, such as [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 8 Conjuration/5e - Gate (Spell)|☄️Gate]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 7 Conjuration/5e - Plane Shift (Spell)|☄️Plane Shift]], fail if used to enter or leave the area. The Sphere is stationary.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The spell lasts <u>until dispelled</u> if cast with a spell slot of level 6+.
