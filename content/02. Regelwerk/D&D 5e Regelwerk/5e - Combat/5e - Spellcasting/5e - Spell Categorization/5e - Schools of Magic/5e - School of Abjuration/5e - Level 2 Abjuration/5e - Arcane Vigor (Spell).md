You tap into your life force to heal yourself. Roll <u>one</u> or <u>two</u> of your unexpended [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]], and regain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `the roll's total + your spellcasting ability modifier`. Those dice are then expended.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The number of unexpended [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] you can roll increases by <u>one</u> for each spell slot level above 2.
