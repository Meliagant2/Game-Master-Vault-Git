You touch a creature and grant it a measure of protection from death. The first time the target would drop to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] before the spell ends, the target instead drops to `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Point]], and the spell ends.

If the spell is still in effect when the target is subjected to an effect that would kill it instantly without dealing damage, that effect is negated against the target, and the spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
