You touch a creature and speak a promise of no more than 25 words that you vow to keep.

For the duration of the spell, if you knowingly break the promise, the target immediately knows of your breach if it is within <u>1,5 km</u>, and you start [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Burning|☠️Burning]], taking `1` <u>Fire</u> damage at the start of each of your turns instead of the normal burning damage.
