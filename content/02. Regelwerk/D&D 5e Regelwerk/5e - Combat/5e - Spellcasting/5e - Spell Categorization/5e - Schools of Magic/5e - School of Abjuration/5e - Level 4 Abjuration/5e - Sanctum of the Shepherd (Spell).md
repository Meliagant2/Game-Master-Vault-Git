You create a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> of faint, shimmering light centered on a point within range. You and a number of creatures of your choice up to `your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of 1 other creature)` can absorb protective magic from the Sphere. When any of the chosen creatures finish a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]] in the area, the Sphere vanishes, and those creatures gain a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] for the remainder of the duration. Chosen creatures who don't finish a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]] in the Sphere gain no benefit.
