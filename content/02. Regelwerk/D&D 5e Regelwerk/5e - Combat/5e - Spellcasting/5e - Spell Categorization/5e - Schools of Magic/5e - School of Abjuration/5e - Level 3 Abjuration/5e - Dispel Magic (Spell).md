Choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range. Any ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] using your spellcasting ability (**DC 10 plus that spell's level**). _**Success:**_ The spell ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You automatically end a spell on the target if the spell's level is equal to or less than the level of the spell slot you use.
