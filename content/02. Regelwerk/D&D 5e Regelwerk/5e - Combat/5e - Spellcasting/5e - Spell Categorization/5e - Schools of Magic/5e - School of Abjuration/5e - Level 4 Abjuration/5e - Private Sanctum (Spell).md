# ☄️5e - Private Sanctum☄️

> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane |
> |**Effect:**|Control |

You make an area within range magically secure. The area is a <u>Cube</u> that can be as small as <u>5 feet</u> to as large as <u>100 feet</u> on each side. The spell lasts for the duration. When you cast this spell you can designate areas within it, or creatures of your choice, who are not affected by the spell. By casting the spell again while it lasts, you can also add creatures or areas within it, that are unaffected.

When you cast the spell, you decide what sort of security the spell provides, choosing any of the following properties:

- Sound can't pass through the barrier at the edge of the warded area.
- The barrier of the warded area appears dark and foggy, preventing vision (including [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Magical Darkvision|👁️Magical Darkvision]]) through it.
- Sensors created by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - School of Divination|☄️Divination]] spells can't appear inside the protected area or pass through the barrier at its perimeter.
- Creatures in the area can't be targeted by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - School of Divination|☄️Divination]] spells.
- Nothing can teleport into or out of the warded area.
- Planar travel is blocked within the warded area.

Casting this spell on the same spot every day for <u>1 year</u> makes the spell last until dispelled.

<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u> You can increase the size of the <u>Cube</u> by <u>100 feet</u> for each spell slot level above 4.
