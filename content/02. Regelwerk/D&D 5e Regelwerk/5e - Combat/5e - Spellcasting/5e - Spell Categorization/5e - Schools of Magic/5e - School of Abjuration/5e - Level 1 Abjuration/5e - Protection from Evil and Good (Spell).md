# ☄️5e - Protection from Evil and Good☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|1 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|Touch |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (holy water or powdered silver and iron) |
> |**Duration:**|10 Minutes |
> |**Concentration:**|x |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Spell List:**|Divine,Primal,Warlock |

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch is protected against creatures that are <u>Aberrations</u>, <u>Celestials</u>, <u>Elementals</u>, <u>Fey</u>, <u>Fiends</u>, or <u>Undead</u>. The protection grants several benefits:

- Creatures of those types have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against the target.
- The target also can't be <u>possessed</u> by or gain the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] conditions from them. If the target is already <u>possessed</u>, [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] by such a creature, the target has **ADV** on any new [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the relevant effect.
