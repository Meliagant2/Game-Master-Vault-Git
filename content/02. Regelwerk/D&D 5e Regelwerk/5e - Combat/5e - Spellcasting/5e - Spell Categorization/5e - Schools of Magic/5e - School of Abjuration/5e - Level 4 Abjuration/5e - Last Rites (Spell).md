# ☄️5e - Last Rites☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Abjuration |
> |**Level:**|4 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|120 ft. |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a small piece of brass, a rose petal, and a drop of holy water) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Divine |
> |**Effect:**|Radiant |

You target a creature you can see within range with a beam of holy light that shines down upon them. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `7d10` <u>Radiant</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

If you target an _Undead_ or _Fiend_, it makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] with **DISADV**.

If you target a _Celestial_ or _Construct_, it makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] with **ADV**.

If an _Undead_ or _Fiend_ is reduced to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] by this spell, it dies and its body is reduced to ash.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
