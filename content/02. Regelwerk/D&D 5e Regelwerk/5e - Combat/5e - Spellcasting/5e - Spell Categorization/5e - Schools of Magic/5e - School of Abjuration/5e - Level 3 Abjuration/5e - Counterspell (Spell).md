You attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell. You make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] (`d20 + your Spellcasting Ability + the level you cast this spell at`), contested by the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] (`d20 + its Spellcasting Ability + the spell's level`). _**Success:**_ The spell dissipates with no effect, and the **Action**, **Bonus Action**, or **Reaction** used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a _spell slot_, the slot isn't expended.
