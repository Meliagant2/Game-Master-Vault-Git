# ☄️5e - Backlash☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult |
> |**Effect:**|Damage Mitigation,Force |

You ward yourself against destructive energy, reducing the damage taken by `4d6 + your spellcasting ability modifier`.

If the triggering damage was from a creature within range, you can force the creature to make a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `4d6` <u>Force</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The damage reduction and damage from this spell both increase by `1d6` for every spell slot level above 4.
