Up to <u>five</u> creatures of your choice who remain within range for the spell's entire casting gain the benefits of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]] and also regain `2d8` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]]. A creature can't be affected by this spell again until that creature finishes a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The healing increases by `1d8` for each spell slot level above 2.
