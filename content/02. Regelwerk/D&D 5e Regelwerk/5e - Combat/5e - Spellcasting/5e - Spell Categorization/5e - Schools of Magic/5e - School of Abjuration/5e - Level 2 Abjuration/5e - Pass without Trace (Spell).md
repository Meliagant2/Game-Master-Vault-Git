You radiate a concealing aura in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a `+4` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Stealth|💃Stealth]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]] and leave no tracks.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** The bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Stealth|💃Stealth]] increases by `1` for each slot level above 2.
