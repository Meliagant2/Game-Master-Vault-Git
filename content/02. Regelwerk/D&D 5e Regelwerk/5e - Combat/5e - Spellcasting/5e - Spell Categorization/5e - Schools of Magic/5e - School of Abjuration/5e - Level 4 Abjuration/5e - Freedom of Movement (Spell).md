# ☄️5e - Freedom of Movement☄️

> |**Concentration:**|  |

You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] nor cause the target to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]] equal to its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]].

In addition, the target can spend <u>5 feet</u> of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Adventuring Gear/5e - Manacles|🪓Manacles]] or a creature imposing the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] condition on it.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
