# ☄️5e - Bestow Curse☄️

You touch a creature, which makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is _cursed_ for the duration. Until the curse ends, the target suffers one of the following effects of your choice:

- Choose one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability]]. The target has **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] made with that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability]].
- The target has **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against you.
- In combat, the target makes a **WIS Save** at the start of each of its turns. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dodge|⚔️Dodge]] action on that turn.
- If you deal damage to the target with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] or a spell, the target takes an extra `1d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** If you cast this spell using a level 4 spell slot, you can maintain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] on it for up to <u>10 minutes</u>. If you use a level 5+ spell slot, the spell doesn't require [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]], and the duration becomes <u>8 hours</u> (level 5-6 slot) or <u>24 hours</u> (level 7-8 slot). If you use a level 9 spell slot, the spell lasts <u>until dispelled</u>.
