You focus on a creature that you are familiar with that is on the same plane of existence as you. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is _cursed_ for the duration. Once a target fails its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against this spell, you can't target it with a ritual casting of the spell for <u>24 hours</u>.

While _cursed_, the target hears haunting, discordant music and has **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Tests]]. The first time the target _**fails**_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] during a turn, it repeats the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against this spell with neither **ADV** nor **DISADV**. _**Success:**_ The curse ends.
