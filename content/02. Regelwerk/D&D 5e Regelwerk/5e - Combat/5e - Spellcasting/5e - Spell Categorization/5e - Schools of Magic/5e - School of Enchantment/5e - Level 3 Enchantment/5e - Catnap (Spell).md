You make a calming gesture, and up to <u>three</u> willing creatures of your choice that you can see within range have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unconscious|☠️Unconscious]] condition for the spell's duration. The spell ends on a target if it takes damage or another creature takes an **Action** to shake it awake. If a target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unconscious|☠️Unconscious]] condition from this spell for the full duration, that target gains the benefits of finishing a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]], and it can't be affected by this spell again until it finishes a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]].

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 3.
