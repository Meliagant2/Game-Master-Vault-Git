You magically emanate a sense of friendship toward one visible creature within _range_. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] for the duration. The target _succeeds_ automatically if it isn’t a Humanoid, if you’re fighting it, or if you have cast this spell on it within the past <u>24 hours</u>.

The spell ends early if the target takes damage or if you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], deal damage, or force anyone to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]]. When the spell ends, the target knows it was [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] by you.
