One creature that you can see within range makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target's mind is shrouded in the dread of total isolation. For the duration, all other creatures have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] condition toward the target, which the target can't see through—even with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]], or magic such as the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - See Invisibility (Spell)|☄️See Invisibility]] spell—and the target can't perceive other creatures with any of its other senses. The target moves around other creatures, or otherwise rationalizes not moving through spaces occupied by them (such as perceiving a door as closed or as a wall if another creature is standing in the way). The target still feels pain but can't identify the source of any injury caused by another creature.
