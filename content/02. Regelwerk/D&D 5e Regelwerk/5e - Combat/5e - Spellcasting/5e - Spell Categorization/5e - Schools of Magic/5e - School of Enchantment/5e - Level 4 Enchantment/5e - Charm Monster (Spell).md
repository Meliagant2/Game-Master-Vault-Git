# ☄️5e - Charm Monster☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

> |**Components:**|V, S |
> |**Duration:**|PT1H |

> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Charmed |

One creature you can see within range makes a **WIS Save**. It does so with **ADV** if you or your companions are fighting it. _**Failure:**_ It is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] by you until the spell ends or until you or your companions do anything harmful to it. When the spell ends, the creature knows it was charmed by you.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
