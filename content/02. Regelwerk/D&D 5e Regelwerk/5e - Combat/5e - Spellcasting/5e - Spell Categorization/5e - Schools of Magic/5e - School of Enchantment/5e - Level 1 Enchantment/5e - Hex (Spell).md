You place a curse on a creature that you can see within range. Until the spell ends, <u>once per turn</u>, you deal an extra `1d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage to the target when you _hit_ it with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]]. Also, choose one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability Score]] when you cast the spell. The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Impaired|☠️Impaired]] with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skills]] of the chosen [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability Score]].

If the target drops to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] before this spell ends, you can take a **Bonus Action** on a later turn to curse a new creature.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** Your Concentration can last longer with a spell slot of level <u>2</u> (up to 4 hours), <u>3-4</u> (up to 8 hours), or <u>5+</u> (24 hours). Also this spell’s damage increases by `1d8` for each 2 spell slot levels above 1.
