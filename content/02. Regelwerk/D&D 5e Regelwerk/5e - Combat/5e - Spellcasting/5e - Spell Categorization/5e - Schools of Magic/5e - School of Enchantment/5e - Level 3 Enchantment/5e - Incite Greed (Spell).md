When you cast this spell, you present the gem used as the material component and choose any number of creatures within range that can see you. Each target makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] for the duration, or until you or your companions do anything harmful to it. While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] in this way, a creature can do nothing but use its movement to approach you in a safe manner. While an affected creature is within <u>5 feet</u> of you, it cannot move, but simply stares greedily at the gem you present.
