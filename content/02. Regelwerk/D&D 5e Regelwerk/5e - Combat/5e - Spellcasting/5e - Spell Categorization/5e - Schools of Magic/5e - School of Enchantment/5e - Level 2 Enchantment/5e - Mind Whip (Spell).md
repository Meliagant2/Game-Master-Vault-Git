You psychically lash out at one creature you can see within range. The target makes an **INT Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `4d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage and it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Dazed|☠️Dazed]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
