# ☄️5e - Mind Sliver☄️

You drive a disorienting spike of psychic energy into the mind of one creature you can see within range. The target makes an **INT Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target takes `1d8` <u>Psychic</u> damage and subtract `1d4` from the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] it makes before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d8` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8`), <u>11</u> (`3d8`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8`).
