# ☄️5e - Unraveling Whisper☄️

Choose a creature you can see within range and whisper a frightful phrase that only the target can hear. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `2d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] in this way, the creature has **Vulnerability** to your choice of <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, or <u>Slashing</u> damage against the first attack that _hits_ it, before the effect ends.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
