# ☄️5e - Mist of Mourning☄️

> |**Effect:**|Hindered,Slowed,Weakened |

You summon a thin mist that saps joy and vigor in a <u>20-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point within range. Each creature in the mist when it appears, or when it moves into the mist for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there, makes a **CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is filled with deep melancholy until <u>the end of its next turn</u>. An affected creature has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Weakened|☠️Weakened]] conditions.
