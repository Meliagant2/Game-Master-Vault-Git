One creature that you can see within range makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] for the duration. The creature _**succeeds**_ automatically if it isn't Humanoid.

A spectral crown appears on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] target's head, and it must use its **Action** before moving on each of its turns to make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against a creature other than itself that you mentally choose. The target can act normally on its turn if you choose no creature or if no creature is within its reach.

On your later turns, you must take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to maintain control of the target, or the spell ends.
