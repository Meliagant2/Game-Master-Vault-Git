You inflict a creature you can see within range with a magical disease. At <u>the start of each of the target's turns</u>, it makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `1d12` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The target takes half the damage only.

If a target _succeeds_ on three of these [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the spell ends.

A [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - 000 Abjuration Control/abj 2/5e - Lesser Restoration (Spell)|☄️Lesser Restoration]] spell cast on the target ends this spell early.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d12` for each spell slot level above 1.
