You attack a creature in range with a quick spreading infection that takes advantage of existing conditions. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against a target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. If the target is suffering from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition or a <u>disease</u>, it instead takes `1d12` <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by one die when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d8` or `2d12`), <u>11</u> (`3d8` or `3d12`), and <u>17</u> (`4d8` or `4d12`).
