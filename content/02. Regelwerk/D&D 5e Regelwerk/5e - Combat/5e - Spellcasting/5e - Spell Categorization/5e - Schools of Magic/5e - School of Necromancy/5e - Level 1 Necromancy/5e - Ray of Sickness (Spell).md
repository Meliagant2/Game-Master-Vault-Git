A ray of sickening greenish energy lashes out toward a creature within range. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `2d10` <u>Poison</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d10` for each spell slot level above 1.
