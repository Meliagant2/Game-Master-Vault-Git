A beam of enervating energy shoots from you toward a creature within range. The target makes a **repeated CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Weakened|☠️Weakened]]. _**Success:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] on the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] it makes before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
