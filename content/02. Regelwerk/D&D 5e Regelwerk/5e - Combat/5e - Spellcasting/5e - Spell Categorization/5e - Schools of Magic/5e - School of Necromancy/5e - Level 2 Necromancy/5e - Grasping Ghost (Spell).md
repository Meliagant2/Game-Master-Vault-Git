A flock of floating spectral hands appear and grasp a target you can see within range. The target makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `3d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage and ghostly hands [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Immobilized|☠️Immobilize]] the target until <u>the end of your next turn</u>, and immediately move it up to <u>30 feet</u> in a direction of your choice (but not upward). On its turn, the creature can use an **Action** to attempt to escape the hands and end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Immobilized|☠️Immobilized]] condition, by making either an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Acrobatics|💃Acrobatics]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Athletics|💃Athletics]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Success (Ability Check):**_ The [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Immobilized|☠️Immobilized]] condition ends. _**Success (Initial Save):**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
