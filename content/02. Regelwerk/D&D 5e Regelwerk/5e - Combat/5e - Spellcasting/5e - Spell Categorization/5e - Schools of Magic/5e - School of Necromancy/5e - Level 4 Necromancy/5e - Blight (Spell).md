A creature that you can see within range makes a **CON Save**. A _Plant_ creature automatically fails the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]]. _**Failure:**_ The target takes `8d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Alternatively, target a nonmagical plant that isn't a creature, such as a tree or shrub. It doesn't make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]]; it simply withers and dies.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 4.
