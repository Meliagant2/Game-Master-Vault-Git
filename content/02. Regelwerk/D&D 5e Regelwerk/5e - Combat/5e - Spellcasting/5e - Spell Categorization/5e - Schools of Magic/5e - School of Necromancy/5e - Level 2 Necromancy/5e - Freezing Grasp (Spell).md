You touch a creature, absorbing the energy from its body and bolstering your metabolism. Make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against a creature you can reach. On a _**hit**_, the target takes <u>5d6</u> <u>Cold</u> damage, and it can't make [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] until <u>the start of its next turn</u>. In addition, until <u>the end of its next turn</u>, its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is reduced by <u>15 feet</u> and your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is increased by <u>15 feet</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `3d6` for each spell slot level above 2.
