Choose a pile of bones or a corpse of a _Medium or Small Humanoid_ within range. The target becomes an _Undead_ creature: a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Undead/5e - Undead; CR 0,25/5e - Skeleton|🐺Skeleton]] if you chose bones or a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Undead/5e - Undead; CR 0,25/5e - Zombie|🐺Zombie]] if you chose a corpse.

On each of your turns, you can take a **Bonus Action** to mentally command any creature you made with this spell if the creature is within <u>60 feet</u> of you (if you control multiple creatures, you can command any of them at the same time, issuing the same command to each one). You decide what **Action** the creature will take and where it will move on its next turn, or you can issue a general command, such as to guard a chamber or corridor. If you issue no commands, the creature takes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dodge|⚔️Dodge]] action and moves only to avoid harm. Once given an order, the creature continues to follow it until its task is complete.

The creature is under your control for <u>24 hours</u>, after which it stops obeying any command you've given it. To maintain control of the creature for another <u>24 hours</u>, you must cast this spell on the creature again before the current <u>24-hour period</u> ends. This use of the spell reasserts your control over up to four creatures you have animated with this spell rather than animating a new creature.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** You animate or reassert control over <u>two</u> additional _Undead_ creatures for each spell slot level above 3. Each of the creatures must come from a different corpse or pile of bones.
