# ☄️5e - Wither and Bloom☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|
> |**School:**|Necromancy |
> |**Level:**|2 |
> |**Casting Time:**|A  |
> |**Range/Area:**|60 ft. (10 ft. Sphere) |
> |**Components:**|V, S, M (a withered vine twisted into a loop) |
> |**Duration:**|Instantaneous |
> |**Concentration:**|  |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Necrotic,Healing |

You invoke both death and life in a <u>10-foot-radius Sphere</u> centered on a point within range. Nonmagical plants in that area that aren't creatures, such as trees and shrubs, wither and each creature of your choice in that area makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `3d6` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

In addition, one creature of your choice in that area can roll one of its unexpended [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] and regain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `the roll + your spellcasting ability modifier`. That die is then expended.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d6` for each spell slot level above 2. The number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] that can be rolled and expended increases by `1` for each spell slot level above 2. You add your spellcasting modifier to the number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] regained by this spell only once, regardless of the number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] rolled and expended.
