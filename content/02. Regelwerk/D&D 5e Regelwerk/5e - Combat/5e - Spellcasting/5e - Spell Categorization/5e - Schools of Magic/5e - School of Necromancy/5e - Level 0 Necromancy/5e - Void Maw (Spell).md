You reach out into the perilous void to create an orb of hungry shadows. Make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] against the target. On a _**hit**_, the target takes `1d10` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. Inanimate corpses and Tiny or smaller nonmagical objects _hit_ by this spell disintegrate into nothing. If this spell kills a creature, the corpse disintegrates into nothing. A creature reduced to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] by this spell is not consumed until it _fails_ its final [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Save]].

**<u>Cantrip Upgrade:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `1d10` when you reach levels <u>5</u> (`2d10`), <u>11</u> (`3d10`), and <u>17</u> (`4d10`).
