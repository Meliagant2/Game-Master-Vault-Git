# ☄️5e - Festering Blast☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||
> |:-|:-|

> |**Concentration:**|  |

> |**Spell List:**|Arcane,Occult,Primal |
> |**Effect:**|Necrotic,Poisoned |

A <u>60-foot-long, 10-foot-wide Line</u> of miasma blasts from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line makes a **repeated CON Save**. The target takes `4d10` <u>Necrotic</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] for the duration.

A creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] by this spell takes `2d10` <u>Poison</u> damage at <u>the start of each of its turns</u>.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s initial damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 4.
