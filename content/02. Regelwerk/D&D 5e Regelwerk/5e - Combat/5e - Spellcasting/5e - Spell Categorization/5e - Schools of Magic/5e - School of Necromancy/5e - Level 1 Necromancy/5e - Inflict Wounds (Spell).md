# ☄️5e - Inflict Wounds☄️

> [!wikibox]
> |||

A creature you touch makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ It takes `2d8` <u>Necrotic</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage.

**<u>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:</u>** This spell’s damage increases by `2d8` for each spell slot level above 1.
