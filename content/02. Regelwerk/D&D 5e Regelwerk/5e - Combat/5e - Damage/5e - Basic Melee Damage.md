# 💔5e - Basic Melee Damage💔

When you successfully use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Basic Maneuvers|🤺Basic Maneuver]] you also deal <u>Basic Melee Damage</u>. This is equal to `1d2 + your STR`. Basic Melee Damage is <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage, but at the Game Master’s discretion may be <u>Piercing</u> or <u>Slashing</u> damage.
