# 🤺5e - Maneuver Save DC🤺

A maneuver sometimes forces a target to make a <u>Save</u> to resist its effects. The **saving throw DC** is calculated as follows:

**<u>Maneuver DC:</u>** `8 + STR/DEX + Proficiency Bonus`
