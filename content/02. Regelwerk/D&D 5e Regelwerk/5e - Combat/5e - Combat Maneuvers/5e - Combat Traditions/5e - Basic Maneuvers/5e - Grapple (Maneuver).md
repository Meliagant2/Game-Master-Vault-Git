# 🤺5e - Grapple🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Basic |
> |**Degree**|0 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|0 |
> |**Use Time:**|Attack  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Unarmed |
> |**Range/Area:**|5ft. |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Contested|
> |**Effect:**|Grappled |

You must have at least one _free hand_ to attempt a grapple, your target must be within your reach, and the target must be no more than _one size category larger than you_. You make an <u>Athletics</u> check contested by the target's <u>Acrobatics</u> _or_ <u>Athletics</u> check. _**Success:**_ You deal [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Basic Melee Damage|💔Basic Melee Damage]] and the Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] by you.

While grappling a creature, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]].
