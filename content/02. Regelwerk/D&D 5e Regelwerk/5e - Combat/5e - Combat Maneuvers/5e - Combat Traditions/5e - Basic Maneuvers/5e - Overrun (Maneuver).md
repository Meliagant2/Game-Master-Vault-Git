# 🤺5e - Overrun🤺

> [!wikibox]

> |**Effect:**|Movement |

You can use an **Action** _or_ **Bonus Action** to move through a hostile creature’s space by forcing your way past. Make an <u>Athletics</u> check contested by the creature's <u>Athletics</u> check. _**Success:**_ You deal [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Basic Melee Damage|💔Basic Melee Damage]] and can move through the hostile creature’s space once this turn.

The creature can choose to just let you pass instead of rolling. You have **ADV** if you are _larger than the target_, or **DISADV** if you are _smaller_. If you are _two or more sizes smaller_ than the target, you can move through the hostile creature’s space without making a <u>check</u>. You still provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] if you move beyond a creature’s reach.
