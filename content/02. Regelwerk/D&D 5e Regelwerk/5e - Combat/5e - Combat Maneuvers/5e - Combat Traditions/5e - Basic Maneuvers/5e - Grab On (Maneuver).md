While you cannot [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Grapple (Maneuver)|🤺Grapple]] a creature _two or more size categories larger than you_, you can **grab onto it**. By jumping onto its back or climbing up a limb, you can make an <u>Athletics</u> check contested by the target's <u>DEX Save</u>. _**Success:**_ You move into the creature’s space and can move upon its body as if you were [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Crawling|🏃‍♀️Crawling]].

While **grabbing onto** a larger creature, you gain **ADV** on <u>attack rolls</u> against it. The larger creature may be able to attack you as normal, depending on its limbs or attacks. It can also use an **Action** or **replace one of its attacks** on its turn to try to dislodge you, such as shaking you loose or bashing you against a wall, forcing you to make a **STR** or **DEX Save** against its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ You fall [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] into the nearest space adjacent to the creature, taking its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Basic Melee Damage|💔Basic Melee Damage]] in the process.

If the creature chooses to roll over in order to dislodge you, it becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]], but you have **DISADV** on the <u>Save</u> to hang on.
