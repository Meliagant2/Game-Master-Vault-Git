# 🤺5e - Double Tackle🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Comradery |
> |**Degree**|2 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|A  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**| |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Effect:**|Movement,Prone |

Choose an ally you can _see_. Your ally can use their **Reaction** to move up to <u>20 feet</u> to be adjacent to a creature you designate. As long as you move at least <u>20 feet</u> and both you and your ally are adjacent to the creature, it makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

Alternatively, you and your ally may both choose to go [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] to force the creature to _**fail**_ its <u>Save</u>.

You cannot use this maneuver against creatures more than one _size category larger_ than you or your ally.
