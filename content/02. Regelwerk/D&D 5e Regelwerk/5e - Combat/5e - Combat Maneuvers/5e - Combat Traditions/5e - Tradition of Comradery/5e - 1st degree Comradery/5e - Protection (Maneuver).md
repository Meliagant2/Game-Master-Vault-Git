# 🤺5e - Protection🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Comradery |
> |**Degree**|1 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |
> |**Use Time:**|R (A Creature you can see attacks a Target other than you that is within 5 ft. of you)|
> |**Weapon Type:**|Melee/Shield |
> |**Range/Area:**|5 ft. |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Effect:**|DISADV |

You impose **DISADV** on the _triggering Attack Roll_ and all other <u>Attack Rolls</u> against the Target until <u>the start of your next turn</u> as long as you remain within <u>5 feet</u> of the Target.
