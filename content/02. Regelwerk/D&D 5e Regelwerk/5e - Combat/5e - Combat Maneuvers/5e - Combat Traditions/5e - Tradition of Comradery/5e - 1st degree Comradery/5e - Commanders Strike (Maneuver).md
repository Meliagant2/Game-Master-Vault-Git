Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] with this maneuver to direct one of your companions to strike. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]], adding `1d6` to the attack's damage roll on a hit.
