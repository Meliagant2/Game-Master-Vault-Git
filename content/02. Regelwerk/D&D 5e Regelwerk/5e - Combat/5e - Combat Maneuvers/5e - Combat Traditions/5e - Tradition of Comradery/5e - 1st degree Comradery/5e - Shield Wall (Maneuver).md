Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Shields/5e - Shields|🛡️Shield]], you can choose one ally within <u>5 feet</u> that is also wielding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Shields/5e - Shields|🛡️Shield]].
