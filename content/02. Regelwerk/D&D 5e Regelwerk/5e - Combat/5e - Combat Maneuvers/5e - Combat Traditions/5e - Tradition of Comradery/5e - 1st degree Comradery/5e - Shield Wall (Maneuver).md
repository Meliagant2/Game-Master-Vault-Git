# 🤺5e - Shield Wall🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

While you are wielding a _shield_, you can choose one ally within <u>5 feet</u> that is also wielding a _shield_.

Until <u>the start of your next turn</u>, as long as that ally remains within <u>5 feet</u> of you and is able to take **Actions** both you and your ally increase your _AC_ by `2`.
