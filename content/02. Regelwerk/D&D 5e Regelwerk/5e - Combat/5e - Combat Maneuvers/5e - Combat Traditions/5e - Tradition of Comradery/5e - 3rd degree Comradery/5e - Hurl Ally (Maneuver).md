# 🤺5e - Hurl Ally🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Comradery |
> |**Degree**|3 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|BA  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**|5ft. |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee/ DEX|
> |**Effect:**|Movement,Attack,Critical |

Choose a willing ally within <u>5 feet</u>. You attempt to hurl your ally at a creature within <u>20 feet</u>. Make an <u>Athletics</u> check with a **DC**, depending on your allie's _size_. <u>Smaller than you:</u> **Automatic Success**. <u>Your size:</u> **DC20 Athletics**. <u>Larger than you:</u> **Automatic Failure**. _**Success:**_ Your ally lands in a space adjacent to the creature and can use their **Reaction** to make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> against it. On a _**hit**_, the creature makes a **DEX Save** against your allie's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The attack becomes a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]]. If the attack is already a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]], your ally deals maximum damage on that hit.
