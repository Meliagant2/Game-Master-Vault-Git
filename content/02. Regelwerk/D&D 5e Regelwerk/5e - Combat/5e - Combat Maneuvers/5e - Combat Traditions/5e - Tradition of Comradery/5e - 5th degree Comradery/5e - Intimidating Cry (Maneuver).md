# 🤺5e - Intimidating Cry🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Comradery |
> |**Degree**|5 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|BA  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**|30ft. Emanation |
> |**Attack/Save:**|WIS|
> |**Effect:**|Psychic,Intimidated,Frightened |

Each creature of your choice within a <u>30 feet Emanation</u> of you makes a **repeated WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `4d6` <u>Psychic</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] for <u>1 minute</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes _half_ damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Intimidated|☠️Intimidated]] for the duration instead, ending the effect on a successful <u>Save</u>.
