# 🤺5e - United we Stand🤺

> [!wikibox]

Make a single <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against a creature. Any willing ally within _your reach_ can use their **Reaction** to make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> against the same creature. The damage you as a group deal increases with each hit against the creature.

If the creature is hit by the <u>second</u> attack, it takes an extra `1d6` damage.

If the creature is hit by the <u>third</u> attack, it takes an extra `2d6` damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

If the creature is hit by the <u>fourth</u> attack, and every attack after that, it takes an extra `4d6` damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stunned|☠️Stunned]].
