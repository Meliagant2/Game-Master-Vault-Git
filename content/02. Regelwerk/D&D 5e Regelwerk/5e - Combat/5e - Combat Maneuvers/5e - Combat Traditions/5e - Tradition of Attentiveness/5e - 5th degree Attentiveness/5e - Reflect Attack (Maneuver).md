# 🤺5e - Reflect Attack🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Attentiveness |
> |**Degree**|5 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|R (You are hit with a melee attack)|
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |

> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Effect:**|Damage Mitigation |

The _triggering creature_ makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The attack _misses_ you and it is redirected to the attacker. The creature is then subjugated to the damage and effects of its own attack.
