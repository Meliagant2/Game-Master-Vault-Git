On a _**hit**_ the Target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target takes `4d6` <u>Force</u> damage and it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stunned|☠️Stunned]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. _**Failure (by 5):**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] for the duration. _**Success:**_ The Target takes <u>half</u> the damage, is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u> and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against the next <u>Attack roll</u> made against it.
