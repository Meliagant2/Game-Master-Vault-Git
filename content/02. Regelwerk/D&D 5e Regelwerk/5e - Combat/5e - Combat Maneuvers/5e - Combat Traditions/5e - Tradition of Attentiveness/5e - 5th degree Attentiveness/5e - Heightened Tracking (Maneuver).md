# 🤺5e - Heightened Tracking🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Attentiveness |

> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |

> |**Effect:**|Search |

For the next <u>10 minutes</u> you gain the following benefits:

- You immediately know the precise location of every living creature within <u>60 feet</u> that is currently below its _maximum Hit Points_ and do not treat such creatures as [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]].
- You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Search|⚔️Search]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Study|⚔️Study]] actions you make to find creatures, objects, traps, or similar.
- You add `3d6` to any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Search|⚔️Search]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Study|⚔️Study]] action you make to find creatures, objects, traps, or similar.
