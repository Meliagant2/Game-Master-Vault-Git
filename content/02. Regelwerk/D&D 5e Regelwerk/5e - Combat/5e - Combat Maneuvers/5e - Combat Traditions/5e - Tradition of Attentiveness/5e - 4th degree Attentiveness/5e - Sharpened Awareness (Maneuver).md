You make a **Perception Check** against the _triggering attack roll_. _**Success:**_ The attack _misses_ and the attacker is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against you until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. _**Failure:**_ The attacker is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] against you for the next attack it makes against you before <u>the end of its next turn</u>.
