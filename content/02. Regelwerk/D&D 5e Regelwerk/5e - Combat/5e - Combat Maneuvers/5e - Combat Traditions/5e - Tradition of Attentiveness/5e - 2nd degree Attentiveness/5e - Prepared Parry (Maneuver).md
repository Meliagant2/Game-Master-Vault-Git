# 🤺5e - Prepared Parry🤺

> [!wikibox]

> |**Effect:**|AC,Attack |

Choose one creature you can _see_. You gain a bonus to your _AC_ equal to `1d6` against that creature until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

If the creature _misses_ with an attack against you <u>before the start of your next turn</u>, you can make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against it as a **Reaction**.
