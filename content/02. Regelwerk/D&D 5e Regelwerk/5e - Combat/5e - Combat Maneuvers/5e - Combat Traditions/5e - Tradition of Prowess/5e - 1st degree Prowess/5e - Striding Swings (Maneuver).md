When you activate this maneuver, you take the <u>Attack action</u> and make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]], as well as any additional attacks granted by **Extra Attack**. Before or between your attacks, you can move up to <u>15 feet</u>. This movement can be through the space of hostile creatures that are up to _one size category larger than you_, and the spaces of other creatures do not count as [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]].
