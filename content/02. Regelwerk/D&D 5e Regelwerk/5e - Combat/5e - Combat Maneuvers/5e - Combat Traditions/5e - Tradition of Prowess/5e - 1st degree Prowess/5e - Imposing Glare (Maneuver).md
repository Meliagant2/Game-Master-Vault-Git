A creature of your choice, within <u>30 feet</u> you can _see_ and that can _see_ you, makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Intimidated|☠️Intimidated]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. _**Failure (by 5):**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] for the duration instead.
