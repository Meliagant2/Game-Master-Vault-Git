# 🤺5e - Reactive Disarm🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Prowess |
> |**Degree**|4 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|R (A creature misses you with a melee weapon attack)|
> |**Weapon Type:**|Melee |
> |**Range/Area:**|Weapon |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Effect:**|Disarm |

The _triggering creature_ makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target drops one item of your choice it is holding and you knock it <u>10 feet</u> away.

The target makes the <u>Save</u> with **ADV** if it is _larger_ than you or holding the item with _two hands_, and with **DISADV** if it is _smaller_ than you.
