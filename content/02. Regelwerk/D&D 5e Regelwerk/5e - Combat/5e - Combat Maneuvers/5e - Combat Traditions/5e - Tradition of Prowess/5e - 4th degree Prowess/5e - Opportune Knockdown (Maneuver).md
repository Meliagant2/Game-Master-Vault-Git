Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] and you immediately make an additional <u>weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against the next <u>attack roll</u> targeting it before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
