# 🤺5e - Armor Lock🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Degree**|5 |

You can only use this maneuver against an enemy that is wearing _Armor_.

On a _**hit**_ you make a **DEX Save** against the target's _AC_. _**Success:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]].

At <u>the beginning of each of its turns</u>, a creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] by this maneuver makes a **STR Save** (unlike normal, the paralyzed creature doesn’t automatically fail this <u>Save</u>). _**Success:**_ The creature ends the effect on itself.

A creature wearing _Heavy Armor_ has **DISADV** on its <u>Save</u>.
