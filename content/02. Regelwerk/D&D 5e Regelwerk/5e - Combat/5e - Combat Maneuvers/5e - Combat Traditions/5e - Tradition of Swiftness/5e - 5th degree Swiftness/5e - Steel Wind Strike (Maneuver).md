# 🤺5e - Steel Wind Strike🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Swiftness |
> |**Degree**|5 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|BA  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Melee |
> |**Range/Area:**|30 ft. Emanation |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee|
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Piercing,Slashing,Movement |

Each creature of your choice within a <u>30 feet Emanation</u> of you makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `4d6` damage of your weapon's damage type. _**Success:**_ The creature takes _half_ damage only.

You then move to an unoccupied space you can see within <u>5 feet</u> of one of the targets. This movement doesn't provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]].
