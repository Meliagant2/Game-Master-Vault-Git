You fire dozens of pieces of ammunition in a rapid speed. Choose a point within your ranged weapon's reach. Each creature in a <u>40-foot-radius, 20-foot-high Cylinder</u> centered on that point makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `8d6` <u>Piercing</u> damage. _**Success:**_ The creature takes _half_ damage only.
