# 🤺5e - Barrage🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Swiftness |
> |**Degree**|4 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|2 |
> |**Use Time:**|A  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**|Weapon (Cone, max 60 ft.) |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Effect:**|Bludgeoning,Piercing,Slashing,AOE |

The Area of Effect for this maneuver is a <u>Cone</u>. This <u>Cone</u> has a size equal to `your weapon's reach + 10 feet` up to a maximum range of <u>60 feet</u>.

Each creature of your choice within that area makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `6d6` damage of your weapon's damage type. _**Success:**_ The creature takes _half_ damage only.
