On your <u>ranged weapon attack</u>, you completely ignore a target’s **cover**.

Only targets that have total cover from being entirely enclosed by an object or effect (such as inside of a chamber with no openings to shoot through or a sphere from wall of force) have cover against this attack, and you do not have **DISADV** from attacking a target you cannot see.
