When you activate this maneuver, make a <u>weapon attack</u>, as well as any additional attacks granted by **Extra Attack**, against a creature you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]] from. Any attacks you make in this way are made with **ADV** (instead of only the first as normal for surprised creatures) and each deals an additional `1d6` damage.
