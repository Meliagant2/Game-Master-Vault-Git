# 🤺5e - Mistaken Opportunity🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Cunning |
> |**Degree**|3 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|2 |
> |**Use Time:**|R (You miss with a weapon attack)|
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**|Weapon |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Melee/ Ranged|
> |**Effect:**|Attack,ADV |

You redirect your _missed_ attack towards a different target within range. Make a <u>weapon attack</u> using the same weapon you missed with against the new target. This attack is made with **ADV** and on a _**hit**_ it deals extra damage equal to `your PB`.
