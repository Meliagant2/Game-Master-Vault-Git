On a _**hit**_ the Target makes a **repeated CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bleeding|☠️Bleeding]] for <u>1 Minute</u>. _**Success:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Lightly Wounded|☠️Lightly Wounded]] for the duration instead, ending the effect on another successful <u>Save</u>.
