When you activate this maneuver, you take the <u>Attack action</u> and make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]], as well as any additional attacks granted by **Extra Attack**. Move up to <u>40 feet</u> in a straight line. If you hit with a <u>melee weapon attack</u> at the end of your movement, the target makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is either [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] or pushed back <u>10 feet</u> (your choice).
