You hit the ground, sending out a shockwave in a <u>30-foot Cube</u> originating from you. Creatures in the area make a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `8d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]]. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half damage only.
