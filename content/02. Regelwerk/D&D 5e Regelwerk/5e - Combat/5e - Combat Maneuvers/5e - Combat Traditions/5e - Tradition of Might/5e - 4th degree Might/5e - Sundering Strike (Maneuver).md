# 🤺5e - Sundering Strike🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Might |
> |**Degree**|4 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|3 |
> |**Use Time:**|Attack  |
> |**Weapon Type:**|Any |
> |**Range/Area:**|Weapon |
> |**Attack/Save:**|CON|
> |**Effect:**|Vulnerability |

On a _**hit**_ the target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target gains <u>Vulnerability</u> against <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage for the next <u>weapon attack</u> that hits it before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

A creature can be affected by this maneuver only <u>once per turn</u>.
