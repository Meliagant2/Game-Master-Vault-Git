On a _**hit**_ the target makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target gains `1` level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]] for <u>1 minute</u>. _**Failure (by 10):**_ The target gains `2` levels of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]] for <u>1 minute</u> instead.

A target cannot gain more than <u>two</u> levels of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]] from this maneuver.
