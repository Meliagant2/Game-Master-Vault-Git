# 🤺5e - Vengeful Rush🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Might |
> |**Degree**|3 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|2 |
> |**Use Time:**|R (An enemy within 30 ft. made an attack against you)|
> |**Weapon Type:**|Melee |
> |**Range/Area:**|Self/ 5ft. |
> |**Attack/Save:**|STR|
> |**Effect:**|Movement,Pushing |

You move <u>15 feet</u> straight toward the _triggering creature_, without provoking [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]]. If you end your movement within <u>5 feet</u> of that creature, it makes a **STR Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage and you and the target move up to an additional <u>10 feet</u> in the same direction, without provoking [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]]. If the target can’t move the full distance, it takes an additional `1d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage for each <u>5 feet</u> not moved.
