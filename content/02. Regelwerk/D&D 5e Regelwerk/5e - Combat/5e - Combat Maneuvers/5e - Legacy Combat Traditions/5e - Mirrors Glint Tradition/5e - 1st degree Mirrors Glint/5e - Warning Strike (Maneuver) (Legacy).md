# 🤺5e - Warning Strike (Legacy)🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Mirrors Glint |
> |**Degree**|1 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |
> |**Using Time:**|R (A hostile creature that you can see moves out of your reach)|
> |**Range/Area:**|Melee |
> |**Attack/Save:**|Attack|
> |**Effect:**|Movement |

When a hostile creature that you can _see_ moves out of your _reach_, you can use your **Reaction** to make a <u>melee weapon attack</u>. On a _**hit**_ its <u>Speed</u> is reduced to `0` until <u>the start of its next turn</u>.
