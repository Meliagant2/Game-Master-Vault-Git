# 🤺5e - Leading Throw (Legacy)🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Mirrors Glint |
> |**Degree**|1 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |
> |**Using Time:**|R (A creature within your reach misses you with a melee attack)|
> |**Range/Area:**|Melee |
> |**Attack/Save:**|DEX|
> |**Effect:**|Movement |

When a creature within your reach _misses_ you with a <u>melee attack</u>, you can use your **Reaction** to try to throw it. The creature makes a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ It moves <u>15 feet</u> in a straight line in a direction of your choice.
