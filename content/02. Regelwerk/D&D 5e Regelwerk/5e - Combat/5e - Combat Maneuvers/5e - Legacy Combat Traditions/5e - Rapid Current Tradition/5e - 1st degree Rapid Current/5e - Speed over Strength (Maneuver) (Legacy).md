# 🤺5e - Speed over Strength (Legacy)🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Rapid Current |
> |**Degree**|1 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |
> |**Using Time:**|R (A creature attacks you with a melee weapon that has the heavy property, or a Large-sized or larger creature attacks you with a natural weapon)|
> |**Range/Area:**|Melee |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Effect:**|Attack |

When a creature attacks you with a <u>melee weapon</u> that has the <u>heavy</u> property, or a _Large-sized_ or _larger_ creature attacks you with a natural weapon, you can use your **Reaction** to make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> against it, using a weapon that doesn't have the <u>heavy</u> property, before the creature hits you.
