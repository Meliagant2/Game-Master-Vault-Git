# 🤺5e - Parrying Counter (Legacy)🤺

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Tradition**|Rapid Current |
> |**Degree**|2 |
> |**Exertion Cost**|1 |
> |**Using Time:**|R (At the end of your turn)|
> |**Range/Area:**|Self |
> |**Attack/Save:**||
> |**Effect:**|AC |

At the end of your turn, you can use your **Reaction** to gain an <u>expertise die</u> to your _AC_ against one creature you can see within reach. This increase to your AC only applies to the chosen creature and ends at <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

Every time the creature _misses_ with an attack against you before the start of your next turn, you can make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> against it (**no Reaction** required).
