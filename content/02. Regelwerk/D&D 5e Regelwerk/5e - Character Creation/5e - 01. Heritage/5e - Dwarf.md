Most theories suggest that dwarves were the offspring of humans who made a contract with a higher being. Might it be one of the gods, an elemental being, or the planet itself, no one can be sure. Whatever theory is correct, it is certainly true that dwarves are sturdier than most other mortals.

Dwarves can carve themselves a niche almost anywhere — their keen sight and their endurance allow them to create and thrive where others dare not tread. Caves become halls, mountains become castles. As their hammers fall, as their kettles boil, unspeakable beauties are unleashed upon their world.

Although they are often strong and muscular, dwarves are not tall. Their broad, compact frames make them hardy and stout. They can be twice as heavy as a human a few feet taller. Their skin ranges from fair and pale to dark and brown, even gray, with a healthy bronze color being especially common. Under the correct light, dwarven eyes seem like polished black, brown, blue, or green gemstones. Dwarven hair is most often black, but red, brown, and blonde are not uncommon colors. Their beards grow full and long—sometimes it’s hard to say where a dwarf’s hair ends and beard begins. This is true even for dwarven women, a trait that is most confusing to other mortals.

## Dwarf Traits

### Basic Heritage Traits

**<u>Age:</u>** Dwarves age as fast as humans, but most cultures only consider them adults at 50. They have extremely long lives; some dwarves live to be over 250 years old and their average life span is 200 years.\
**<u>Size:</u>** _Medium_ (4-5 feet / 120cm - 150cm).
**<u>Speed:</u>** <u>30 feet</u>.

### Basic Dwarf Traits

**<u>Darkvision:</u>** You have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Darkvision|👁️Darkvision]] with a range of <u>120 feet</u>.
**<u>Dwarven Resilience:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Poison</u> Damage. You also have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] you make to avoid or end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition.
**<u>Dwarven Toughness:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Point]] maximum increases by `1`, and it increases by `1` again whenever you gain a level.
**<u>Stonecunning:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Tremorsense|👁️Tremorsense]] with a range of <u>60 feet</u> for <u>10 minutes</u>. You must be on a stone surface or touching such a surface to use this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Tremorsense|👁️Tremorsense]]. The stone can be natural or worked. <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Expanded Dwarf Traits

In addition to the traits found in your **Basic Traits**, select <u>one</u> of the following gifts:

#### Runner

**<u>Endurance:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] you make to avoid or end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Fatigue|☠️Fatigue]] condition. You remove one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] whenever you finish a **Short Rest**.
**<u>Speed Increase:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>5 feet</u>.

#### Toughest of them all

**<u>Great Health:</u>** As a **Bonus Action,** you gain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|❤️Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `1d10 + your level`. These [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|❤️Temporary Hit Points]] last for <u>1 minute</u>. <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
**<u>Slow and Steady:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] cannot be reduced by [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] (The GM might rule, that in specific instances this feature doesn't work as written, for example when the **Difficult Terrain** is caused by magical illusions).

### Dwarven Paragon

When you reach level 10, you are an exemplar of the Dwarves, and you gain one of the following features:

#### Fury of the Earth

As an **Action**, you can strike the ground with a melee weapon you’re proficient with. The ground in a <u>30-foot radius Emanation</u> becomes [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]]. Each creature on the ground in the area makes a **DEX Save** (DC = your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]], or your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]], whichever is higher). _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

A creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲concentrating]] on a spell makes a **CON Save (DC 10)**. _**Failure:**_ The [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] is broken.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

#### Unbreakable

When you _succeed_ on a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Save]], you can expend <u>one</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]] to regain `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Point]] as if your check result was a `natural 20`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.

#### Unstoppable

You can take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] action as a **Bonus Action** and you regain `1` additional [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]] when you take a **Long Rest** (But no more than your maximum).
