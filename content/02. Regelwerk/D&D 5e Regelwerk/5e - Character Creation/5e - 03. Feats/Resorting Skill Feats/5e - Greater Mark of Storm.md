# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Storm🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Acrobatics</u> and with <u>Navigator's Tools</u>.

**<u>Improved Storm:</u>** When you use the **Storm Magic** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Storm|🥇Mark of Storm]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 2 Evocation/5e - Gust of Wind (Spell)|☄️Gust of Wind]] without a spell slot, you also gain a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>60 feet</u> for the duration of the spell.
