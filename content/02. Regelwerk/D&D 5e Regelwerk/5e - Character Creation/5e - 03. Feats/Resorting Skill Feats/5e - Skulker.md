# 🥇5e - Skulker🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Blindsight:</u>** You have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]] with a range of <u>10 feet</u>.

**<u>Fog of War:</u>** You exploit the distractions of battle, gaining **ADV** on any <u>Stealth</u> check you make as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Hide|⚔️Hide]] action during combat.

**<u>Sniper:</u>** If you make an attack roll while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]] and the roll misses, making the attack roll doesn't reveal your location.
