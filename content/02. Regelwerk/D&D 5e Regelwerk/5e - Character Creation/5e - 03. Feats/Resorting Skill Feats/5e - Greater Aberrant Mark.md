# 🥇5e - Greater Aberrant Mark🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Aberrant Spellmark |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Fortitude:</u>** When you use the **Aberrant Fortitude** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Aberrant Spellmark|🥇Aberrant Spellmark]] feat, you can roll `1d6` instead of `1d4`. You also now regain your use of Aberrant Fortitude whenever you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.

**<u>Mark of Inspiration:</u>** When you cast a cantrip, you can roll one or two of your unexpended _Hit Point Dice_. You gain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `the number rolled + your CON`, and one creature of your choice within <u>30 feet</u> of you (not including you) takes <u>Force</u> damage equal to `the number rolled`. Those dice are then expended.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
