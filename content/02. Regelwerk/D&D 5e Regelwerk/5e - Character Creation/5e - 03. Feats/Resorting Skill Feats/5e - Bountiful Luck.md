# 🥇5e - Bountiful Luck🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When an ally you can see (Which includes yourself) within <u>30 feet</u> of you rolls a `1` on the `d20` for any <u>D20 Test</u>, you can use your **Reaction** to let the ally reroll the die. The ally must use the new roll.
