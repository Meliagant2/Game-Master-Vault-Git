# 🥇5e - Eye for Detail🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Insight Proficiency |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You notice patterns others cannot. As an **Action**, you can study a creature you have watched speak for at least <u>1 minute</u>. You can ask the Game Master one of these questions:

- Is the creature hiding any strong emotions?
- Does the creature have a secret agenda?
- Did the creature lie in the last minute?
- Is the creature hiding their true identity, heritage and/or culture?

The Game Master must give you a truthful answer.
