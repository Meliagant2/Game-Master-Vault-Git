# 🥇5e - Face in the Crowd🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Quick Fingers:</u>** You gain <u>Sleight of Hand</u> proficiency. If you are already proficient in <u>Sleight of Hand</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Hidden in the crowd:</u>** If you enter or start your turn in a crowd of creatures with which you share a creature type, you are considered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unseen|☠️Unseen]] to creatures that are not part of the crowd and gain **ADV** on <u>Stealth checks</u> to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Hide|⚔️Hide]] in the crowd. You must stay within the crowd to retain this benefit, and it may end early if you take any actions that would seem out of line with the crowd (such as bursting into song, casting a showy spell, or not participating in the crowd’s ritualistic chanting), as determined by the GM. If the crowd is targeted by an effect that requires a <u>Save</u>, you may choose to fail it to remain hidden.
