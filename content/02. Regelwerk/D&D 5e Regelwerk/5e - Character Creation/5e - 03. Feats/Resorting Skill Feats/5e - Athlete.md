# 🥇5e - Athlete🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Climb Speed:</u>** You gain a <u>Climb speed</u> equal to your Speed

**<u>Hop Up:</u>** When you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]], you can right yourself with only <u>5 feet</u> of movement.

**<u>Jumping:</u>** You can make a running <u>Long</u> or <u>High</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Jumping|🏃‍♀️Jump]] after moving only <u>5 feet</u>. You also have **ADV** on <u>ability checks</u> to jump.
