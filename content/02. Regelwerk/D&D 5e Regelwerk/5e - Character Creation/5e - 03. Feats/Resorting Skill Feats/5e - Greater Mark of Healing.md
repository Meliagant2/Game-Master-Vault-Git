# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Healing🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Healing |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Medicine</u> and in using the <u>Herbalism Kit</u>.

**<u>Improved Healing:</u>** You can now use the **Healing Touch** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Healing|🥇Mark of Healing]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 1 Abjuration/5e - Cure Wounds (Spell)|☄️Cure Wounds]] without using a spell slot a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**. Additionally, when you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 1 Abjuration/5e - Cure Wounds (Spell)|☄️Cure Wounds]] and roll dice to determine the number of _Hit Points_ restored, you can treat any `1` or `2` on a roll as a `3`.
