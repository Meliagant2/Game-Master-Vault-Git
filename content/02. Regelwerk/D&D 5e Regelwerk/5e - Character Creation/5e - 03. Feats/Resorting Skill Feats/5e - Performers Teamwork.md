# 🥇5e - Performers Teamwork🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Travelling Performer |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Withering Wordplay:</u>** When you take the[[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Help|⚔️Help]] action to assist an ally's <u>attack roll</u> against an enemy, that enemy also has **DISADV** on the first <u>Save</u> it makes before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Inspiring Willpower:</u>** If you _succeed_ on a <u>Save</u> to end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] condition on yourself, you can choose one ally you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself that has the same condition. That condition immediately ends for that ally.
