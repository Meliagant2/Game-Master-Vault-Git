# 🥇5e - Chef🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cook's Utensils:</u>** You gain proficiency with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Tools/5e - Artisan_s Tools/5e - Cooks Utensils|🛠️Cook's Utensils]]. If you are already proficient with <u>Cook's Utensils</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Replenishing Meal:</u>** As part of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]], you can cook special food, provided you have <u>ingredients</u> and <u>cook’s utensils</u> on hand. You can prepare enough of this food for a number of creatures equal to `4 + PB`. At the end of the <u>short rest</u>, any creature who eats the food and spends one or more <u>Hit Dice</u> to regain <u>hit points</u> regains an extra `1d8` <u>hit points</u>.

**<u>Bolstering Treats:</u>** With <u>1 hour</u> of work or when you finish a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]]. you can cook a number of treats equal to `your proficiency bonus`. These special treats last <u>8 hours</u> after being made. A creature can use a **Bonus Action** to eat one of those treats to gain a number of <u>temporary hit points</u> equal to `your proficiency bonus + your CON/WIS (whichever is higher)`.
