You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Light activity Watch:</u>** When taking a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]], add your proficiency bonus to the number of hours you can spend in <u>light activity</u>.

**<u>Strenuous Activity Watch:</u>** One hour of that time can be spent in <u>strenuous activity</u> near the rest site (such as patrolling, fighting, or other adventuring activities) without interrupting your rest.
