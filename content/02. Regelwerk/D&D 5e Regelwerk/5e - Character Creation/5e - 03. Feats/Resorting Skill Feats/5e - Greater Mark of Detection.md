# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Detection🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Detection |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Investigation</u> and <u>Insight</u>.

**<u>Shared Detection:</u>** When you use the **Magical Detection** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Detection|🥇Mark of Detection]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - See Invisibility (Spell)|☄️See Invisibility]] without a spell slot, you can choose one creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. That creature also gains the benefits of the spell for its duration.
