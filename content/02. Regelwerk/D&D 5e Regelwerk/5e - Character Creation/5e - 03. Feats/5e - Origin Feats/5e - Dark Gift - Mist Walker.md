# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Mist Walker🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You know how to slip through the Mists' grasp, but this freedom comes at a price: If you remain in one area for too long, the Mists find you and drain your life force. You gain the following features:

**<u>Domain Traveler:</u>** When you enter _the Mists_ intent on reaching a specific domain, you are treated as if you possess a _Mist talisman_ keyed to that domain. To use this feature, you must know the name of the domain you have chosen as your destination, but you don't need to have previously visited that land. This trait doesn't allow you to bypass domain borders closed by a Darklord's will.

**<u>Mist Walk:</u>** When you take damage or _fail_ a <u>Save</u> to avoid or end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]] condition, you can take a **Reaction** and teleport up to <u>15 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see. You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Poisoned Roots:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, the world around you in a <u>15 km radius</u> becomes a siphon that leeches away at your vitality. Whenever you finish a **Short Rest** in that area, make a **DC 13 + PB CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ You get no benefits from finishing that rest.
