# 🥇5e - Cruel🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain a number of **Cruelty Dice** equal to `your PB`. your **Cruelty Dice** are `d6s`. You can roll only one **Cruelty Die** per turn, and a **Cruelty Die** is spent when you roll it. You can roll a **Cruelty Die** under any of the following circumstances:

- **<u>Damage:</u>** When you deal damage to a creature, spend one **Cruelty Die** to deal extra damage to the creature equal to the roll.
- **<u>Critical Hit Points:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]], spend one **Cruelty Die** to gain _Temporary Hit Points_ equal to `PB x the die`.
- **<u>Intimidation:</u>** When you make an <u>Intimidation</u> check, spend one **Cruelty Die** and add the roll to your check.

**<u>Regaining Uses:</u>** You regain all spent **Cruelty Dice** when you finish a **Long Rest**.
