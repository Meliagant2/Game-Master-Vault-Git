# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Living Shadow🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

The shadow you cast is animate and ever-present—sometimes it even acts according to its own will. You gain the following features:

**<u>Grasping Shadow:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 0 Conjuration/5e - Mage Hand (Spell)|☄️Mage Hand]] spell and can cast it without spell components. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Lengthened Strike:</u>** When you make a <u>melee attack roll</u> as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] on your turn, you can increase your _reach_ for that attack by <u>10 feet</u>, as your shadow stretches to aid you. You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Ominous Will:</u>** Immediately after you make a <u>D20 test</u> and roll a `1` on the `d20`, your shadow attempts to exert its will. Make a **DC 13 + PB WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ You have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u>, at which point you must roll on the <u>Shadow's Will table</u> to determine what you do during that turn.

##### Shadow's Will

| 1d8 | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | You don't take an **action** or a **Bonus ACtion**, and you use all your movement to move. Roll `1d4` for the direction: <u>1</u>, north; <u>2</u>, east; <u>3</u>, south; or <u>4</u>, west.           |
| 2-6 | You don't move or take a **Bonus Action**, and you take the **Attack Action** to make one <u>melee attack</u> against a random creature within reach. If none are within reach, you take **no action**. |
| 7-8 | You have the <u>☠️Prone</u> condition, and your turn ends.                                                                                                                                              |
