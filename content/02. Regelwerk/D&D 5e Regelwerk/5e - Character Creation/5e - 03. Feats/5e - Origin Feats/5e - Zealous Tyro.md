# 🥇5e - Zealous Tyro🥇

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Stand as One:</u>** When an ally within <u>5 feet</u> of you is subjected to an effect that would [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pull]] it, you can take a **Reaction** to prevent that ally from being pushed or pulled. To receive this benefit, the ally can't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.

**<u>Vigilant:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Ready|⚔️Ready]] action, the next <u>attack roll</u> made against you has **DISADV** before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

> [!info] Feat "Upgrades"
> Taking this Feat allows you, to take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - General Feats/5e - Orders Resilience|🥇Orders Resilience]] Feat Later
