You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Adroit Escape:</u>** You have **ADV** on checks to escape from nonmagical <u>restraints</u> or the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] condition.

**<u>Vitality Ward:</u>** When you take <u>Necrotic</u> damage, you can take a **Reaction** to mitigate the damage. Roll a number of `d6s equal to your Proficiency Bonus`, and add them together. Reduce the <u>Necrotic</u> damage you take by this total.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
