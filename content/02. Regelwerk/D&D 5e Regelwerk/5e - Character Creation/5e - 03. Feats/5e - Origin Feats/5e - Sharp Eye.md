When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Search|⚔️Search]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Study|⚔️Study]] action, you can give yourself **ADV** on any <u>ability check</u> made as part of that action.

You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your Proficiency Bonus</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]]. If the check <u>fails</u>, the use of this feature isn't expended.
