# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Second Skin🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

There is another side of you that most people never see: a beast, a terrifying avenger, or a walking nightmare. You gain the following features:

**<u>Alternate Form:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 2 Transmutation/5e - Alter Self (Spell)|☄️Alter Self]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot or spell components, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 2 Transmutation/5e - Alter Self (Spell)|☄️Alter Self]] without a spell slot using this feature, it doesn't require [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]].

**<u>Involuntary Change:</u>** Certain circumstances can involuntarily trigger your transformation. When you select this feat, roll on the <u>Change Catalyst table</u> to determine what triggers your change.

After you experience the catalyst, at <u>the start of your next turn</u>, make a **DC 13 + PB CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ You immediately use **Alternate Form** to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 2 Transmutation/5e - Alter Self (Spell)|☄️Alter Self]] without a spell slot. If you've already expended the use of that feature, you instead have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stunned|☠️Stunned]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
