# 🥇5e - Mark of Handling🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special, No other Spellmark |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Wild Intuition:</u>**  You gain an <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Animal Handling</u> and <u>Nature</u>.

**<u>Primal Connection:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Animal Friendship (Spell)|☄️Animal Friendship]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] spells prepared. You can cast each spell <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast these spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Monstrous Connections:</u>** When you reach character _level 3_, you can target a <u>Monstrosity</u> when you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Animal Friendship (Spell)|☄️Animal Friendship]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] if the creature's <u>INT modifier</u> is `-3` or lower.

**<u>Spells of the Mark:</u>** If you have a Spell List, the following spells are added to your Spell list:
**Spell level 1:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Beast Bond (Spell)|☄️Beast Bond]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Command (Spell)|☄️Command]]
**Spell level 2:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - Beast Sense (Spell)|☄️Beast Sense]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 2 Enchantment/5e - Calm Emotions (Spell)|☄️Calm Emotions]]
**Spell level 3:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 3 Abjuration/5e - Beacon of Hope (Spell)|☄️Beacon of Hope]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 3 Conjuration/5e - Conjure Animals (Spell)|☄️Conjure Animals]]
**Spell level 4:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 4 Abjuration/5e - Aura of Life (Spell)|☄️Aura of Life]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 4 Enchantment/5e - Dominate Beast (Spell)|☄️Dominate Beast]]
**Spell level 5:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 5 Transmutation/5e - Awaken (Spell)|☄️Awaken]]
