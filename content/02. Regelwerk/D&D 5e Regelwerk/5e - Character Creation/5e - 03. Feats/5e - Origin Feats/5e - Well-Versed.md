You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Lore Proficiency:</u>** You gain proficiency in one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Tools/5e - Musical Instruments/5e - Musical Instruments|🛠️Musical Instrument]] and one of the following skills: <u>Arcana</u>, <u>History</u>, <u>Nature</u>, or <u>Religion</u>.

**<u>Useful Knowledge:</u>** When you make an Intelligence (<u>Arcana</u>, <u>History</u>, <u>Nature</u>, or <u>Religion</u>) <u>Check</u>, you can give yourself **ADV** on the roll.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can do this a number of times equal to <u>your Proficiency Bonus</u> and regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
