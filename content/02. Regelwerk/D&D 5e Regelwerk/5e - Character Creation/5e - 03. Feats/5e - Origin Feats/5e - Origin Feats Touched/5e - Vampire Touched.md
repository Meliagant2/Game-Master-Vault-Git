Choose one level 1 spell from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Spellschool Enchantment|☄️Enchantment]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Spellschool Illusion|☄️Illusion]] school of magic. You always have that spell and the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 2 Transmutation/5e - Spider Climb (Spell)|☄️Spider Climb]] spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot.

<u>Once</u> you cast either spell in this way, you can't cast that spell in this way again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).
