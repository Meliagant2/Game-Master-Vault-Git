# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Symbiotic Being🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

A second being resides within your body, offering knowledge and assistance while furthering its own agenda. You gain the following features:

**<u>Entwined Existence:</u>** The symbiote can't be targeted. If you die, so does your symbiote. If you are returned to life, your symbiote also revives.

**<u>Second Mind:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in one of the following _Skills_, or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_: [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Arcana|💃Arcana]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Deception|💃Deception]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - History|💃History]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Intimidation|💃Intimidation]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Insight|💃Insight]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Investigation|💃Investigation]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Nature|💃Nature]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Occultism|💃Occultism]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Religion|💃Religion]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Perception|💃Perception]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Persuasion|💃Persuasion]].

You also gain a **mastery level** in <u>one</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Languages|🗣️Language]] of your choice.

**<u>Sustained Symbiosis:</u>** When you _fail_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can take a **Reaction** and expend one of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]]. Roll the die and add the number rolled to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], potentially turning the failure into a _success_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Symbiotic Agenda:</u>** Immediately after you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] and roll a `1` on the `d20`, your symbiote attempts to assert control. Make a **DC 13 + PB CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ You have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition for <u>1d12 hours</u>. While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]], you must try to follow the symbiote's commands and further its goals, as determined by the GM. Whenever you take damage, you can repeat this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], ending the effect on a _success_.

At the GM's discretion, you might make this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] whenever you act contrary to the symbiote's agenda.
