# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Aberrant Anatomy🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Exposure to alien horrors has warped your physical form in supernatural ways. You gain the following features:

**<u>Breathless:</u>** You can hold your breath for <u>1 hour</u>.

**<u>Extrasensory Perception:</u>** You have proficiency in the <u>Perception</u> skill, if you lack it. You also gain an <u>expertise die</u> in that skill. In addition, you have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]] with a range of <u>15 feet</u>.

**<u>Warping Flesh:</u>** Immediately after you make a <u>D20 test</u> and roll a `1` on the `d20`, the aberrant influence infecting your form flares, wrenching control of your flesh. Make a **DC 13 + PB CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ You have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stunned|☠️Stunned]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
