You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Mount Up:</u>** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Mounted Combat/5e - Mounted Combat|🐴Mounting]] or <u>dismounting</u> costs you only <u>5 feet</u> of movement.

**<u>Precise Strike:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you make a <u>weapon attack</u> roll against a creature, you can cause the attack roll to have **ADV**. If the attack hits, you roll a `d8` and add the number rolled as a bonus to the attack’s <u>damage roll</u>.

You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your proficiency bonus</u>, but a use is expended only if the attack hits. You regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.
