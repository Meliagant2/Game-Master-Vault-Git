# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Symbiotic Being🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

A second being resides within your body, offering knowledge and assistance while furthering its own agenda. You gain the following features:

**<u>Entwined Existence:</u>** The symbiote can't be targeted. If you die, so does your symbiote. If you are returned to life, your symbiote also revives.

**<u>Second Mind:</u>** You gain proficiency in one of the following skills: <u>Arcana</u>, <u>Deception</u>, <u>History</u>, <u>Intimidation</u>, <u>Insight</u>, <u>Investigation</u>, <u>Nature</u>, <u>Religion</u>, <u>Perception</u>, or <u>Persuasion</u>.

You also know one additional [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Languages|🗣️Language]] of your choice.

**<u>Sustained Symbiosis:</u>** When you _fail_ a <u>Save</u>, you can take a **Reaction** and expend one of your _Hit Dice_. Roll the die and add the number rolled to the <u>Save</u>, potentially turning the failure into a _success_.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Symbiotic Agenda:</u>** Immediately after you make a <u>D20 test</u> and roll a `1` on the `d20`, your symbiote attempts to assert control. Make a **DC 13 + PB CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ You have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition for <u>1d12 hours</u>. While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]], you must try to follow the symbiote's commands and further its goals, as determined by the GM. Whenever you take damage, you can repeat this <u>Save</u>, ending the effect on a _success_.

At the GM's discretion, you might make this <u>Save</u> whenever you act contrary to the symbiote's agenda.
