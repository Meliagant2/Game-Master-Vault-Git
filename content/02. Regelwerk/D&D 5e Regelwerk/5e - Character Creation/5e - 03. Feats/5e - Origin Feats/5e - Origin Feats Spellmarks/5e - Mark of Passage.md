# 🥇5e - Mark of Passage🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Courier's Speed:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>5 feet</u>.

**<u>Intuitive Motion:</u>** You gain an <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Acrobatics</u> and <u>Athletics</u>. 

**<u>Magical Passage:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**Spell level 3:** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 3 Transmutation/5e - Blink (Spell)|☄️Blink]], [[02.
