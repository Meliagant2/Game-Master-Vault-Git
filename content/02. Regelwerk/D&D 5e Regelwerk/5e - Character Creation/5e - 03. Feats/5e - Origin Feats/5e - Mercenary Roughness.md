You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Exploit Opening:</u>** When you roll damage for an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]], you can roll the damage dice <u>twice</u> and use either roll against the target.

**<u>Family First:</u>** If you have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]] when you roll [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]], you can expend it to give yourself and your allies **ADV** on that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]] roll.

> [!info] Feat "Upgrades"
> Taking this Feat allows you, to take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - General Feats/5e - Mercenary Tactics|🥇Mercenary Tactics]] Feat Later
