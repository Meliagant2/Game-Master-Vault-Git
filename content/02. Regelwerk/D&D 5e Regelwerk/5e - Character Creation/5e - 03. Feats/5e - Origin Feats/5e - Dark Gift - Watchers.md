# 🥇5e - Dark Gift - Watchers🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Origin |
> |**Level Requirement**|0 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Special |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Something unnatural is always watching you, taking the form of scurrying vermin and other eerie creatures. You gain the following features:

**<u>Borrowed Eyes:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - Beast Sense (Spell)|☄️Beast Sense]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] spells prepared. You can cast each spell without a spell slot, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Heightened Suspicion:</u>** You gain an <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Insight</u>, <u>Medicine</u>, <u>Perception</u>, and <u>Survival</u>.

**<u>Incessant Watchers:</u>** You have **DISADV** on <u>Saves</u> made against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 5 Conjuration/5e - Scrying (Spell)|☄️Scrying]] spell.

In addition, immediately after you make a <u>D20 test</u> and roll a `1` on the `d20`, paranoia threatens to overwhelm you. Make a **repeated DC 13 + PB WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ You have **DISADV** on <u>D20 Tests</u> for <u>1 minute</u>.
