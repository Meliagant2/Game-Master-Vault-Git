# 🥇5e - Boon of Fluid Forms🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shapechanger:</u>** You can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] to shape-shift into a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Beasts/5e - Bestiary; Beasts|🐺Beast]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Humanoids/5e - Bestiary; Humanoids|🐺Humanoid]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Monstrosities/5e - Bestiary; Monstrosities|🐺Monstrosity]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Challenge Rating (CR)|🐺Challenge Rating]] no higher than `10`. When you shape-shift, you gain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to the _Hit Points_ of the form. The shape-shifting effect lasts for <u>1 hour</u>, and it ends early if you have no [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] left or if you take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] to return to your true form.

Your game statistics are replaced by the _stat block_ of the chosen form, but you retain your _creature type_; _personality_; _Intelligence_, _Wisdom_, and _Charisma_ scores; _Hit Points_; _Hit Dice_; _proficiencies_; and _ability to communicate_. If you have the **Spellcasting** feature, you retain it too. Upon shape-shifting, you determine whether your equipment drops to the ground or changes in size and shape to fit the new form while you're in it.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Hardy Transformation:</u>** When you gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] when you shape-shift, increase that number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] by `20`.
