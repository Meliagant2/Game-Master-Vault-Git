# 🥇5e - Boon of the Furious Storm🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Eye of the Storm:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Lightning</u> and <u>Thunder</u> damage. While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]], you have <u>Immunity</u> to <u>Lightning</u> and <u>Thunder</u> damage.

**<u>Storm's Strength:</u>** Creatures have **DISADV** on <u>Saves</u> against your _spells_ that deal <u>Lightning</u> or <u>Thunder</u> damage.
