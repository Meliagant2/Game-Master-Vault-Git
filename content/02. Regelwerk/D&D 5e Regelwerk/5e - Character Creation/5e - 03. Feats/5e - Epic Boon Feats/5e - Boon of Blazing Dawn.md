# 🥇5e - Boon of Blazing Dawn🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Beloved of the Sun:</u>** You have <u>Immunity</u> to <u>Radiant</u> damage.

**<u>Blazing Strike:</u>** When you _hit_ a creature with a weapon attack, the damage can be <u>Radiant</u> or the weapon's normal damage type (your choice).

**<u>Burst of Sunlight:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you _**hit**_ a creature with an attack that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can emit [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>30-foot radius</u> from yourself and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>30 feet</u> until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. This light is _sunlight_.
