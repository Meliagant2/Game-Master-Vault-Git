# 🥇5e - Boon of Revelry🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Inspire Dance:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 6 Enchantment/5e - Irresistable Dance (Spell)|☄️Irresistable Dance]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you cast the spell, it requires _no spell components_, and taking damage doesn't break your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] on it.

**<u>Sing Out:</u>** While a creature that _failed_ its <u>Save</u> against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 6 Enchantment/5e - Irresistable Dance (Spell)|☄️Irresistable Dance]] has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition from that spell, it can't cast spells with _Verbal_ components, and it sings delightful nonsense if it can sing.
