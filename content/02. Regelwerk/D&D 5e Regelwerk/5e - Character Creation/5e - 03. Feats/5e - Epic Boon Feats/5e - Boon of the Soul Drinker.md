# 🥇5e - Boon of the Soul Drinker🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Grave Resistance:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Cold</u> damage and <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>Siphon Life:</u>** When an enemy within <u>120 feet</u> of you is reduced to _0 Hit Points_, you can take a **Reaction** to regain `50` _Hit Points_.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
