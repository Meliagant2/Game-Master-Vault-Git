# 🥇5e - Boon of Terror🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fearless:</u>** You have <u>Immunity</u> to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition.

**<u>Flee, Fools!:</u>** When a creature with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition starts its turn within <u>60 feet</u> of you, you can take a **Reaction** to stoke its terror, provided you can _see_ the creature and it isn't behind [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Total Cover|🏔️Total Cover]]. If you do so, the creature makes a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Terrified|☠️Terrified]] until <u>the end of its current turn</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.

**<u>Intimidating:</u>** You gain Proficiency in the <u>Intimidation</u> skill if you don't already have it. You also gain `5` <u>expertise dice</u> in <u>Intimidation</u>.
