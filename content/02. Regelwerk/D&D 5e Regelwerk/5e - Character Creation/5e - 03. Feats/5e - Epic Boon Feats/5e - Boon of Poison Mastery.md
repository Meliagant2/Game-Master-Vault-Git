# 🥇5e - Boon of Poison Mastery🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Antitoxic:</u>** You have <u>Immunity</u> to Poison damage and the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition.

**<u>Perfect Poisoner:</u>** Once per turn, when you roll dice to determine Poison damage a creature takes from your attack, spell, or feature, you can instead use the highest number possible for each die.
