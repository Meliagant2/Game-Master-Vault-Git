You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cunning Speaker:</u>** You don't have **DISADV** on ability checks to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Influence|⚔️Influence]] hostile creatures.

**<u>Gifted Interpreter:</u>** You understand the literal meaning of any language you hear or see signed, and you can understand the literal meaning of any written language you see.

**<u>Mental Communication:</u>** You gain telepathy with a range of <u>120 feet</u>.
