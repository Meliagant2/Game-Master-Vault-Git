You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Flying:</u>** You have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>40 feet</u> and can hover.

**<u>Graceful Fall:</u>** If you fall more than <u>5 feet</u>, your rate of descent slows to <u>60 feet</u> per round until you land. You are immune to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Falling|🏃‍♀️Falling]] damage.
