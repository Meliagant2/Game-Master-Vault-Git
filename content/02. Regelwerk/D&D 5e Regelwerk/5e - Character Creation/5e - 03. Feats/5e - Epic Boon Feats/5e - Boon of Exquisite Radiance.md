# 🥇5e - Boon of Exquisite Radiance🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Eternal Rest:</u>** Creatures you reduce to _0 Hit Points_ can't become <u>Undead</u>.

**<u>Powerful Radiance:</u>** When you make a damage roll that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can instead use the highest number possible for each damage die. <u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
