# 🥇5e - Boon of Bloodshed🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Killer's Fortune:</u>** When an enemy you can see is reduced to _0 Hit Points_, you gain **ADV** on the next <u>attack roll</u> you make before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Power from Pain:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you make an <u>attack roll</u> while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]], you can deal extra damage to the target equal to `your PB`. The extra damage's type is the same as the attack's type.
