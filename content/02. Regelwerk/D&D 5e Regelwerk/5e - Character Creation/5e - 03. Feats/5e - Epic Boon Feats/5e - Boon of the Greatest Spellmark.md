Choose a _level 8_ or lower spell from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Occult/5e - Spell Source Occult|☄️Occult]] spell source or a spell from the list below (The list includes spellmark suggestions if you'd like to associate a spell with one of the marks). You can cast the chosen spell <u>once</u> without a _spell slot_ or _spell components_ as a _level 8_ spell, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**. You can also cast this spell using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).
