# 🥇5e - Boon of Misty Escape🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Gaseous Form:</u>** If you drop to _0 Hit Points_ but aren't killed outright, you can instead drop to _1 Hit Point_ and cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 3 Transmutation/5e - Gaseous Form (Spell)|☄️Gaseous Form]] without expending a spell slot (**no action** required). When you cast this spell this way, you can target only yourself, your Fly Speed is 20 feet, and you regain _10 Hit Points_ at <u>the start of each of your turns</u> for the spell's duration.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
