You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Augmented Health:</u>** When you gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]], increase the number of _Temp HP_ you gain by `5`.

**<u>Superior Recuperation:</u>** When you spend one or more _Hit Point Dice_ to regain _Hit Points_, you can instead use the _highest_ number possible for each die.
