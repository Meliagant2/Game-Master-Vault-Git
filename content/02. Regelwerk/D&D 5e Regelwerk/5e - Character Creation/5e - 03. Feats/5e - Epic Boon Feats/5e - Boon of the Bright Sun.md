# 🥇5e - Boon of the Bright Sun🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Epic Boon |
> |**Level Requirement**|20 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Daylight Presence:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you radiate a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] that is _sunlight_. If any of the Emanation's area overlaps with an area of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]] created by a spell, that spell is dispelled. The Emanation lasts until you dismiss it (no action required), die, or have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.

**<u>Fortifying Light:</u>** When your **Daylight Presence** is active, at <u>the start of each of your turns</u>, you and allies you can see in your Daylight Presence gain `10` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]].
