# 🥇5e - Crusher🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Push:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you hit a creature with an attack that deals <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage, you can move it <u>5 feet</u> to an unoccupied space, provided the target is no more than one size larger than you.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to a creature, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.
