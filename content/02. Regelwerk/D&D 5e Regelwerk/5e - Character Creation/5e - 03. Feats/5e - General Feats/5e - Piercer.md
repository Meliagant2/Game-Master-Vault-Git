# 🥇5e - Piercer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Prerequisite**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Puncture:</u>** When you hit a creature with an attack that deals <u>Piercing</u> damage, you can treat any `1` or `2` on a damage die as a `3`

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Piercing</u> damage to a creature, you can roll <u>one additional damage die</u> when determining the extra <u>Piercing</u> damage the target takes.
