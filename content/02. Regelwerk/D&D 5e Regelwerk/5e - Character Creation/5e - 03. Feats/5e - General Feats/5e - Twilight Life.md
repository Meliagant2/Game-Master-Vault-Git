You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Deathless:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]].

**<u>Resistance:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> and <u>Radiant</u> damage.

**<u>Twilight Focus:</u>** If you cast a spell that deals <u>Necrotic</u> damage, you can choose to have it inflict <u>Radiant</u> damage instead. If you cast a spell that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can choose to have it inflict <u>Necrotic</u> damage instead.
