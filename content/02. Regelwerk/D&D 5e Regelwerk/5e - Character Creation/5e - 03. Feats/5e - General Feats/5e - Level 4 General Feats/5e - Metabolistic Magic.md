# 🥇5e - Metabolistic Magic🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Arcane Skill:</u>** When you _fail_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]], you can expend a _spell slot_ to gain a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals `2 + the level of the spell slot`.

**<u>Vital Fuel:</u>** When you finish a **Short Rest**, you can expend a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] up to <u>your PB</u> to recover expended _spell slots_. Choose _spell slots_ to recover that have a combined level equal to no more than the number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] expended.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
