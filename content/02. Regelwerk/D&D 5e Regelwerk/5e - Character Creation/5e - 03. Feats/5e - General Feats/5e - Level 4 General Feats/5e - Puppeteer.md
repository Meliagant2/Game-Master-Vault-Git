# 🥇5e - Puppeteer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Poppets |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Curse Magic:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Hex (Spell)|☄️Hex]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Hex (Spell)|☄️Hex]], the affected creature subtracts `1d4` from its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.

**<u>Voodoo Magic:</u>** While using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Poppets/5e - Poppets|🎩Poppet]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], you can use a **Bonus Action** to create an invisible, magical connection between your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Poppets/5e - Poppets|🎩Poppet]] and a creature you can _see_ within <u>60 feet</u> for <u>8 hours</u>. While this connection lasts, you can target the creature with _spells_, even if you can't _see_ it, or it is behind [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Total Cover|🏔️Total Cover]], as long as the creature is within _range_ of the spell you are casting.
