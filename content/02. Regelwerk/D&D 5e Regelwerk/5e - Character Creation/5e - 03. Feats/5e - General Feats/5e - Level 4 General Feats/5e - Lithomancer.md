# 🥇5e - Lithomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Crystals |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Crystal Color Magic:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 1 Illusion/5e - Color Spray (Spell)|☄️Color Spray]] spell preoapred. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 1 Illusion/5e - Color Spray (Spell)|☄️Color Spray]], the spell's area is a <u>15-feet Emanation</u>, centered on you, instead of a <u>15-foot Cone</u>.

**<u>Crystal Mage:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, you can imbue a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Crystals/5e - Crystals|🎩Crystal]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] on your person with magic. While holding the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Crystals/5e - Crystals|🎩Crystal]], you gain <u>one</u> of the following benefits, which you choose, when you finish your **Long Rest**:

- You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Magical Darkvision|👁️Magical Darkvision]] with a range of <u>60 feet</u>.
- Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>20 feet</u>.
- You gain a bonus to <u>CON Saves</u> that you make to maintain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] equal to your **Spellcasting Ability modifier**.

If you imbue another [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Crystals/5e - Crystals|🎩Crystal]] with magic, using this feature, the previous enchantment ceases to function.
