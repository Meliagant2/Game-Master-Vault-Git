# 🥇5e - Minstrel🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Instruments |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Disrupting Performance:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Disrupting Note (Spell)|☄️Disrupting Note]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Disrupting Note (Spell)|☄️Disrupting Note]], the target of the _spell_ also gains the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Impaired|☠️Impaired]] condition on a _**failed**_ [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].

**<u>Musical Magic:</u>** While using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Musical Instruments/5e - Musical Instruments|🎩Musical Instrument]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], when you cast a _spell_ with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]] components, you can increase your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]] for that casting of the spell by `2`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
