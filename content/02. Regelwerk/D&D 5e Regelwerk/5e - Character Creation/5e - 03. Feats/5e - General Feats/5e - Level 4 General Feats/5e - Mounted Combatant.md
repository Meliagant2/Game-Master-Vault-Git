# 🥇5e - Mounted Combatant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +2, or DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Mounted Strike:</u>** While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Mounted Combat/5e - Mounted Combat|🐴mounted]] you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against any unmounted creature within <u>5 feet</u> of your mount that is at least _one size smaller_ than the mount.

**<u>Leap Aside:</u>** If your mount is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a **DEX Save** to take only _half_ damage, it instead takes _no damage_ if it _**succeeds**_ on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] and only _half damage_ if it _**fails**_. For your mount to gain this benefit, you must be riding it, and neither of you can have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.

**<u>Veer:</u>** While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Mounted Combat/5e - Mounted Combat|🐴mounted]], you can force an attack that _hits_ your mount to _hit_ you instead if you don't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.
