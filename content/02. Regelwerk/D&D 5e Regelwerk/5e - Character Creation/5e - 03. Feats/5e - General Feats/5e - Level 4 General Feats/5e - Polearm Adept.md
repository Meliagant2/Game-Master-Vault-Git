# 🥇5e - Polearm Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +2, or DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Reactive Strike:</u>** While you are holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Polearms|🗡️Polearm]] weapon, you can use your **Reaction** to make one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] against a creature that enters the _Reach_ you have with that Weapon.
