# 🥇5e - Polearm Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +2, or DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Reactive Strike:</u>** While you are holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Polearms|🗡️Polearm]] weapon, you can use your **Reaction** to make one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] against a creature that enters the _Reach_ you have with that Weapon.

**<u>Strike the Charge:</u>** <u>Once per round</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] you made with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Polearms|🗡️Polearm]] weapon, you can deal extra damage equal to `your PB` to the creature, as long as either you or the target have moved <u>15 feet</u> during this turn, prior to making the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]].
