You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Advanced Shillelagh:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Shillelagh (Spell)|☄️Shillelagh]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Shillelagh (Spell)|☄️Shillelagh]], your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Staffs/5e - Staff|🎩Staff]] you used in the _spell's_ casting gains the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Reach (Property)|🗡️Reach (10ft.)]] property.

**<u>Explosive Casting:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, while using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Staffs/5e - Staff|🎩Staff]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], if all targets of a _spell_ you cast are more than half the spell's range from you, one creature of your choice that you _**hit**_ with a <u>spell</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] or that _**fails**_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the _spell_ has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition.
