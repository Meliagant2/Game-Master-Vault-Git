# 🥇5e - Poison Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

**<u>Venomous Touch:</u>** When you deal<u>Poison</u> damage, you can cause <u>one</u> creature taking the damage to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] in this way, <u>once per turn</u>, when the creature takes damage, it takes an additional `1d4` <u>Poison</u> damage.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
