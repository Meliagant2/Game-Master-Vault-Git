# 🥇5e - Spellfire Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fueled Spellfire:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when a spell you cast deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can expend up to <u>two</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]], roll them, and add the total rolled to one <u>damage roll</u> of the spell.

**<u>Searing Spellfire:</u>** When you make a <u>damage roll</u> that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Radiant</u> damage.
