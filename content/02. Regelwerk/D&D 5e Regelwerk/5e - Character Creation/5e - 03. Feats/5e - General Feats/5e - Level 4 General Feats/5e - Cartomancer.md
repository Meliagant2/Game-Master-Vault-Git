# 🥇5e - Cartomancer🥇

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Spellcasting Focus:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Card Decks/5e - Card Decks|🎩Card Decks]].

**<u>Card Tricks:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Prestidigitation (Spell)|☄️Prestidigitation]] cantrip and can use it to create illusions that duplicate the effects of stage magic. When you use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Prestidigitation (Spell)|☄️Prestidigitation]] in this way, you can conceal the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]] components of the spell as ordinary conversation and card handling.

**<u>Hidden Ace:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, you can choose one spell from your class's spell list and imbue that spell into a card. The chosen spell must have a casting time of <u>1 Action</u>, and it must be a level for which you have spell slots. The card remains imbued with this spell for <u>8 hours</u>. While the card is imbued with the spell, you can use a **Bonus Action** to flourish the card and cast the spell within. The card then immediately loses its magic.
