# 🥇5e - Archer Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Long Shot:</u>** When you make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Bows/5e - Bows|🗡️Bow]], you gain a bonus to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], depending on how far away the target is. For every <u>20 feet</u> between you and the target, you gain a `+1` bonus to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], up to a maximum of `+3`.

**<u>Speed Increase:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>10 feet</u>.
