# 🥇5e - Orbmancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Orbs |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fated Guidance:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 0 Divination/5e - Guidance (Spell)|☄️Guidance]] cantrip. For you it has a _range_ of <u>30 feet</u>. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Prescience:</u>** When you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**, roll `1d6` and record the result. When you cast a spell using an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Orbs/5e - Orbs|🎩Orb]] as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], you can _add_ the result of this prescience roll to your <u>spell</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], or _reduce_ one target's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] by that amount. You must choose to do so before the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] is rolled.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**. Unused **Prescience** rolls are lost when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
