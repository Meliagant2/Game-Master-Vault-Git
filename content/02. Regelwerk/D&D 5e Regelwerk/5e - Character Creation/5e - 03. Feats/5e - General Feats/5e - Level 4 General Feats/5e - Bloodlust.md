# 🥇5e - Bloodlust🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CON +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Powerful Recovery.</u>** When you roll a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]] to regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], you can treat any roll of `1` or `2` as a `3`.

**<u>Sanguine Feast.</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you hit a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]] creature that isn't a _Construct_ or _Undead_ with an attack roll, you can expend <u>one</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]], roll it, and regain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `the number rolled + your CON`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
