# 🥇5e - Shield Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Shield Proficiency |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Interpose Shield:</u>** If you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a **DEX Save** to take only _half_ damage, you can use your **Reaction** to take _no damage_ if you _**succeed**_ on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] and are wielding a _Shield_, interposing your shield between yourself and the source of the effect.

**<u>Perfect Dodge:</u>** When you have a _Shield_ in your hand, you may apply its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] bonus to <u>DEX</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] you make.
