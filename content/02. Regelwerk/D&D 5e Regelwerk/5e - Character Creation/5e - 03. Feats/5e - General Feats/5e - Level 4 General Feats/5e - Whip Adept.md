You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Parrying Immunity:</u>** When you [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]], you ignore the target's bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]], it gains from a _Shield_ it is holding, the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Tradition of Prowess/5e - 1st degree Prowess/5e - Parry (Maneuver)|🤺Parry]] maneuver, or the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 1 Abjuration/5e - Shield (Spell)|☄️Shield]] spell.

**<u>Pull:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you _**hit**_ with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]], you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pull]] the target up to <u>10 feet</u> towards you, as long as it is at most _one size larger_ than you.
