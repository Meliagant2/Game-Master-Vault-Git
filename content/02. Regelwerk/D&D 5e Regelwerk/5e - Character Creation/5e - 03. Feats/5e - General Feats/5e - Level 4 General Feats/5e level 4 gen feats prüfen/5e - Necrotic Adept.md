# 🥇5e - Necrotic Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

**<u>Siphon Life Force:</u>** When you deal <u>Necrotic</u> damage, you can cause one creature taking that damage to gain the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] condition for <u>1 minute</u>. A creature can be affected by this feat only <u>once</u> at the same time.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
