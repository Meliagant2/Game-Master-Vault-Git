# 🥇5e - Twilight Mage🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Resistance:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> and <u>Radiant</u> damage.

**<u>Twilight Focus:</u>** If you cast a _spell_ that deals <u>Necrotic</u> damage, you can choose to have it inflict <u>Radiant</u> damage instead. If you cast a spell that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can choose to have it inflict <u>Necrotic</u> damage instead.
