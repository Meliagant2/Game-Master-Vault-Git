# 🥇5e - Charger🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +2, or DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Dash:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] Action, your Speed increases by <u>10 feet</u> for that Action.

**<u>Charge Attack:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, if you move at least <u>10 feet</u> in a straight line immediately before hitting with an attack as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] Action on your turn, choose one of the following effects:

- Gain a `+1d8` bonus to the attack’s <u>damage roll</u>.
- [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] the target up to <u>10 feet</u>, provided the target you want to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] is no more than _one Size larger_ than you.
