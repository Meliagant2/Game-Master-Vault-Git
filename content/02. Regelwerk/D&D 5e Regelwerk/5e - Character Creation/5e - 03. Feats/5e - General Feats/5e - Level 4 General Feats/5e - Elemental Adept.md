# 🥇5e - Elemental Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**|x |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Piercing Energy:</u>** Choose one of the following Damage Types: <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Poison</u> or <u>Thunder</u>. Your Spells and Attacks ignore <u>Resistance</u> to damage of the chosen type.

**<u>Energy Mastery:</u>** When you roll damage for an Attack or a Spell you cast that deals damage of that type, you can treat any `1` on a damage die as a `2`.
