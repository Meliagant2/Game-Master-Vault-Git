# 🥇5e - War Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Concentration:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>CON Saves</u> that you make to maintain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]].

**<u>Somatic Components:</u>** You can perform the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] components of spells even when you have _Weapons_ or a _Shield_ in one or both hands.
