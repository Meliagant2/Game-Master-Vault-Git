# 🥇5e - Rod Expert🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Rods |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shake the Earth:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 1 Transmutation/5e - Earth Tremor (Spell)|☄️Earth Tremor]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 1 Transmutation/5e - Earth Tremor (Spell)|☄️Earth Tremor]], creatures make the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the _spell_ with **DISADV**.

**<u>Unshakeable Casting:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, while using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Rods/5e - Rod|🎩Rod]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], if all targets of a _spell_ you cast are within <u>30 feet</u> of you, one creature of your choice that you _**hit**_ with a <u>spell</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] or that _**fails**_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against the _spell_ has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] condition until <u>the start of its next turn</u>.
