# 🥇5e - Incensomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Spellcasting Focus:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Censers/5e - Censers|🎩Censers]].

**<u>Cloying Incense:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Fog Cloud (Spell)|☄️Fog Cloud]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Fog Cloud (Spell)|☄️Fog Cloud]], nonmagical flames in the spell's Sphere are extinguished, and creatures other than you and your allies have their [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] reduced by <u>5 feet</u> while in the spell's Sphere.

**<u>Lingering Scent:</u>** When a _spell_ with a duration of at least <u>1 minute</u>, that you cast, ends, the spell's effect continues until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. This effect doesn't work, if the _spell_ ended, because a creature _succeeded_ on a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against it's effects.
