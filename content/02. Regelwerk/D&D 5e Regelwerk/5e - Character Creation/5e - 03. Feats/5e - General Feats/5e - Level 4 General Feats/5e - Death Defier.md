# 🥇5e - Death Defier🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CON +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cling to Life:</u>** You add your <u>CON</u> (minimum of 1) to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]].

**<u>Enduring Vitality:</u>** If an effect would kill you outright without [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]] or would reduce you directly to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] without dealing damage, you are instead reduced to a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `your level`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
