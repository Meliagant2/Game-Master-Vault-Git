# 🥇5e - Magic Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Magic Initiate Feat |
> |**Repeatable**|x |

Depending on the Spell source you chose for the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Origin Feats garantiert/5e - Magic Initiate|🥇Magic Initiate]] Feat, you gain the following benefits. If you took the <u>Magic Initiate</u> Feat more than once, you choose one of the following Features, for which you chose a Spell source with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Origin Feats garantiert/5e - Magic Initiate|🥇Magic Initiate]] Feat:

---

##### Arcane Spell Source

**<u>Ambitious Magic:</u>** You learn one <u>2nd-level</u> spell of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Arcane/5e - Spell Source Arcane|☄️Arcane]] Spell list. You can cast this feat's _2nd-level spell_ without a spell slot, and you must finish a **long rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast this spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this spell (whichever is highest).

**<u>Life Channel:</u>** You can channel your life force into the power of your magic. When a creature you can _see_ within <u>60 feet</u> of you fails a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against a spell that deals damage that you cast, you can expend a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] equal to `the level of the spell`. Roll the expended [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] and add them together. The damage that the creature takes increases by an amount equal to that total.

---

##### Divine Spell Source

**<u>Protective Magic:</u>** You learn one <u>2nd-level</u> spell of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Divine/5e - Spell Source Divine|☄️Divine]] Spell list. You can cast this feat's _2nd-level spell_ without a spell slot, and you must finish a **long rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast this spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this spell (whichever is highest).

**<u>Protective Ward:</u>** When you or a creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you takes damage, you can use your **Reaction** to expend a _spell slot_ and weave protective magic around the target. Roll a number of `d6s` equal to `the level of the spell slot` expended and reduce the damage the target takes by `the total rolled on those dice + your spellcasting ability modifier`.

---

##### Occult Spell Source

**<u>Mysterious Magic:</u>** You learn one <u>2nd-level</u> spell of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Occult/5e - Spell Source Occult|☄️Occult]] Spell list. You can cast this feat's _2nd-level spell_ without a spell slot, and you must finish a **long rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast this spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this spell (whichever is highest).

**<u>Foul Arcana:</u>** You can tap into a brief glimpse of the void behind the universe to empower a spell, at the cost of overwhelming your mind with a horrifying, cosmic insight. When you cast a spell, you can give that _spell's_ [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell's Save DC]] a `+2` bonus but you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u>. You can do so a number of times equal to <u>your proficiency bonus</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

##### Primal Spell Source

**<u>Insightful Magic:</u>** You learn one <u>2nd-level</u> spell of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Primal/5e - Spell Source Primal|☄️Primal]] Spell list. You can cast this feat's _2nd-level spell_ without a spell slot, and you must finish a **long rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast this spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this spell (whichever is highest).

**<u>Magical Balance:</u>** When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] and roll a `9` or lower on the `d20`, you can balance fate and treat the roll as a `10`. You can balance fate in this way a number of times equal to <u>your proficiency bonus</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.
