# 🥇5e - Thunder Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

**<u>Wrath of the Storm:</u>** When you deal <u>Thunder</u> damage, you can cause <u>one</u> creature taking the damage to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
