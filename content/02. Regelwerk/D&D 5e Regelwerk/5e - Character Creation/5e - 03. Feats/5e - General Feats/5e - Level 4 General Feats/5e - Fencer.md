You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Blade Form:</u>** When you _**miss**_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Swords|🗡️Sword]], you can use your **Reaction** to make another [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] with that weapon against the same target.

**<u>Multi-Faceted:</u>** When you [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Swords/5e - Swords|🗡️Sword]], you can deal your choice of <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, or <u>Slashing</u> damage.
