You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Mangling Slice:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] you made with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Axes/5e - Axes|🗡️Axe]], you can give the creature the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Lightly Wounded|☠️Lightly Wounded]] condition until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.

**<u>Savage Momentum</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can spend _half_ your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]], to gain **ADV** on the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] you make with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Axes/5e - Axes|🗡️Axe]].
