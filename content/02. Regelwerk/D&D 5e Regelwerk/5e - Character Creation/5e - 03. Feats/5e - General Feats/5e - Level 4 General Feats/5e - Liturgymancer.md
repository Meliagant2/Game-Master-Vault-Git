# 🥇5e - Liturgymancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Holy Symbols |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Bright Flame:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Sacred Flame (Spell)|☄️Sacred Flame]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest). You can also cast this cantrip as a **Bonus Action** a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Divine Help:</u>** While using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Holy Symbols/5e - Holy Symbols|🎩Holy Symbol]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], when you cast a _spell_ on one of your allies, that ally gains a `1d4` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
