You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Quickly Stabilized:</u>** When you are successfully [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stable|☠️stabilized]] while _dying_, you regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to your <u>CON</u> modifier (minimum of `1`).

**<u>Healing Flesh:</u>** Whenever you regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] as a result of a _spell_, _potion_, _class feature_, or another _feat_, you regain additional [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to your <u>CON</u> modifier (minimum of `1`).
