# 🥇5e - Hammerer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +2, or DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Armor Breaker:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Hammers/5e - Hammers|🗡️Hammer]], you can reduce the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] by `1` until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. If the _triggering_ [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] was a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]], the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] is reduced by `2` for the duration instead.

**<u>Jump Attack:</u>** If you are holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Hammers/5e - Hammers|🗡️Hammer]], if a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you moves out of your weapon's _reach_ (either willingly or by being pushed or pulled), or it stands up from [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]], you can make a **Jump Attack** against it as a **Reaction**. You move up to <u>10 feet</u> towards the creature and make a single <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] using your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Hammers/5e - Hammers|🗡️Hammer]], if the target is within range. On a _**hit**_ the target takes additional damage equal to `your PB`.
