# 🥇5e - Wandslinger🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +2, WIS +2, or CHA +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Spellcasting Focus:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Wands/5e - Wands|🎩Wands]].

**<u>Basic Magic:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Arcane Dart (Spell)|☄️Arcane Dart]] cantrip.

**<u>Core Wand:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, you can choose one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Wands/5e - Wands|🎩Wand]] on your person to be your **Core Wand**. You can only have <u>one</u> **Core Wand** at a time. This **Core Wand** can have a number of **Core Charges** equal to `your PB`. Whenever you finish a **Long Rest**, the number of **Core Charges** in the **Core Wand** resets to `1`. Whenever you expend a _spell slot_ to cast a _spell_ of _1st level_ or higher while holding your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Wands/5e - Wands|🎩Wand]], it gains up to a number of **Core Charges** equal to `1/2 x the spell's level`. You can expend these **Core Charges** in the following ways:

- When you make a <u>spell</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] or a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can expend up to `3` **Core Charges** to gain a bonus to the roll equal to the number of **Core Charges** spent. You can wait until after you roll the d20, but must decide before the GM says whether the roll _succeeds_ or _fails_.
- Whenever you are _hit_ by an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can expend any number of **Core Charges** as a **Reaction** to gain a bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] against that attack equal to the number of **Core Charges** spent.
