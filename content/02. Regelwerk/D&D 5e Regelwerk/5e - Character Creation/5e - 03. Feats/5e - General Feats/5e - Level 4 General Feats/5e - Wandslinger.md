# 🥇5e - Wandslinger🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting Focus Proficiency with Wands |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Basic Magic:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Arcane Dart (Spell)|☄️Arcane Dart]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Doubled Spell:</u>** While using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Wands/5e - Wands|🎩Wand]] as your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]], when you cast a _cantrip_ that targets only <u>one</u> creature and doesn't have a _range_ of _self_, you can target a <u>second</u> creature in range with the same _cantrip_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
