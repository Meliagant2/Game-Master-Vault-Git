# 🥇5e - Gunner🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +2 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Critical Shot:</u>** If you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]] you don't roll your additional damage dice for the crit, but deal maximum damage with them instead.

**<u>Trick Shot:</u>** When you _see_ a missile from a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] enter into the _normal range_ of a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]] you are holding, you can use your **Reaction** to deflect it. You make a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] using the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]] you are holding against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] of the missile you are targeting, which is equal to the `attack roll of that missile`. _**Hit:**_ The missile is deflected and _misses_ its target.

> [!info] Special Projectiles
> At the GM’s discretion, certain ranged weapon attacks (like a giant’s thrown rock) may be too heavy to be deflected by Trick Shot.
