# 🥇5e - Crossbow Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Additional Mastery:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Crossbows/5e - Crossbows|🗡️Crossbows]] all gain the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Graze Mastery Property|🗡️Graze]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties|🗡️Weapon Mastery Property]] in addition to their normal [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties|🗡️Mastery Property]].

**<u>Piercing Bolts:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with a <u>ranged</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] using a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Crossbows/5e - Crossbows|🗡️Crossbow]], you can hit additional creatures in a line directly behind your target. You make a separate [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] for each additional target, starting with the closest one to your original target. The **Piercing Bolt** stops, once you _**miss**_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]].
