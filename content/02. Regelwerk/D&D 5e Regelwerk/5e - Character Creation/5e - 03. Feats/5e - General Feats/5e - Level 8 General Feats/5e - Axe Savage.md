# 🥇5e - Axe Savage🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Brutal Lacerate:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] you made with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Axes/5e - Axes|🗡️Axe]] and force the target to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against a _condition_, the target makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] with **DISADV**.

**<u>Wild Swing:</u>** Before you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Axes/5e - Axes|🗡️Axe]], you can choose to take a `-5` penalty to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]]. If the attack _hits_, you add `+10` to the attack's damage.
