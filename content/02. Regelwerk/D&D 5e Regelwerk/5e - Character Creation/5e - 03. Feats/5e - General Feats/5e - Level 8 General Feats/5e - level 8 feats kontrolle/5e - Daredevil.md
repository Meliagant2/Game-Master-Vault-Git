# 🥇5e - Daredevil🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CON +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Last Stand:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>attack rolls</u> while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]].

**<u>Increased Critical:</u>** Your <u>attack rolls</u> can score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] on a roll of `19 or 20` on the `d20` while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]]. If you already have a feature that increases your critical hit range, you increase it by `1` instead (maximum `17-20`).
