# 🥇5e - Deadeye🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Dead Aim:</u>** When you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition, you don't have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attacks]] you make with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Crossbows/5e - Crossbows|🗡️Crossbows]], or a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearms]], and you gain a `+2` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] with those weapons while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

**<u>Point Blank:</u>** When you _**hit**_ a creature that is within <u>5 feet</u> of you with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] you made with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Crossbows/5e - Crossbows|🗡️Crossbow]], or a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]], you gain a `+2` bonus to the damage roll.
