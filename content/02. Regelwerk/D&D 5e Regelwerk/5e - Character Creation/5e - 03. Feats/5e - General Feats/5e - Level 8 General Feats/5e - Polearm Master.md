# 🥇5e - Polearm Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3, or DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Pole Rush:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Polearms|🗡️Polearms]], if you move at least <u>15 feet</u> before taking the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action.

**<u>Pole Strike:</u>** Immediately after you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action and attack with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Polearms/5e - Polearms|🗡️Polearm]], you can use a **Bonus Action** to make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] with the opposite end of the Weapon. The weapon’s damage die for this attack is a `d4`, and it deals <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.
