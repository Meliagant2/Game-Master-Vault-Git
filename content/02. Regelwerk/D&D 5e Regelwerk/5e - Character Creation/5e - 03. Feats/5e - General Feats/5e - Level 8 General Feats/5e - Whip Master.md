# 🥇5e - Whip Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Bind Creature:</u>** When you _**hit**_ a creature with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]], you can attempt to bind the creature to you. It makes a **STR Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] by you and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Tethered|☠️Tethered]] with a length of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]]. While you bind a creature in such a way, you can't [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]], that is currently binding the creature.

**<u>Disarm:</u>** When you force a creature to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against being _disarmed_, that creature makes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] with **DISADV**, as long as you use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Whips/5e - Whips|🗡️Whip]] to force this [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].
