# 🥇5e - Pack Fighting🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3, or DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Inspiring Strike:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] against a creature, you can choose an ally within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself who can see or hear you and who lacks [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]]. That ally gains [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]].

**<u>Team Fighting:</u>** When you make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against a creature, you gain **ADV** on the attack roll, if at least one of your allies is within <u>5 feet</u> of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.
