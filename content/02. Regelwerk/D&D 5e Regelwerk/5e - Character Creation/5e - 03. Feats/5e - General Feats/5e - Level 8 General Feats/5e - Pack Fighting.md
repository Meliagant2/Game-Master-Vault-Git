# 🥇5e - Pack Fighting🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3, or DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against a creature, you gain **ADV** on the attack roll, if at least one of your allies is within <u>5 feet</u> of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.
