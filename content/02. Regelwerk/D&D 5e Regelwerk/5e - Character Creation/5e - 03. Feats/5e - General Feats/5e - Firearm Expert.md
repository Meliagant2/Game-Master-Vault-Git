# 🥇5e - Firearm Expert🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Critical Shot:</u>** If you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]] you don't roll your additional damage dice for the crit, but deal maximum damage with them instead.

**<u>Deadeye:</u>** Attacking at _long range_ or within <u>5 feet</u> of an enemy doesn't impose **DISADV** on your attack rolls with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]].

**<u>Trick Shot:</u>** Immediately after a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you moves, you can take a **Reaction** to make a <u>ranged weapon attack</u> with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Firearms/5e - Firearms|🗡️Firearm]] against that creature.
