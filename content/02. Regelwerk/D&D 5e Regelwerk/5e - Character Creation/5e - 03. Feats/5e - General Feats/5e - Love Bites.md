# 🥇5e - Love Bites🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Immediately after you damage a creature with a <u>Melee weapon</u> or an <u>Unarmed Strike</u>, you can take a **Bonus Action** to give the target the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition until the <u>start of your next turn</u> or until you or your allies damage it.

<u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
