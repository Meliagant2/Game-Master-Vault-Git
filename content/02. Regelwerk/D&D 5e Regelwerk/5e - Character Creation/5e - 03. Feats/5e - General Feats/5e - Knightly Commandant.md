# 🥇5e - Knightly Commandant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Squire Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Encourage Ally:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you bolster one ally you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u>. The ally gains [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `2d6 + STR/DEX/CON (whatever is highest)`. You can take this **Bonus Action** a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Last Stand:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>attack rolls</u> while [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]].
