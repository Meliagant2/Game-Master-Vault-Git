# 🥇5e - Cloying Mists🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Arise, Fog:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Fog Cloud (Spell)|☄️Fog Cloud]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot, and you must finish a **Long Rest** before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast it using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

**<u>Grasping Mist:</u>** Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Fog Cloud (Spell)|☄️Fog Cloud]], nonmagical flames in the spell's Sphere are extinguished, and creatures other than you and your allies have their Speed reduced by <u>5 feet</u> while in the spell's Sphere.
