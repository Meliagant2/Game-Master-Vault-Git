# 🥇5e - War Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcaster Class |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Concentration:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>CON Saves</u> that you make to maintain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Saves/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]].

**<u>Reactive Spell:</u>** When a creature provokes an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] from you by leaving your reach, you can take a **Reaction** to cast a spell at the creature rather than making an Opportunity Attack. The spell must have a casting time of one <u>action</u> and must target only that creature.

**<u>Somatic Components:</u>** You can perform the Somatic components of spells even when you have weapons or a Shield in one or both hands.
