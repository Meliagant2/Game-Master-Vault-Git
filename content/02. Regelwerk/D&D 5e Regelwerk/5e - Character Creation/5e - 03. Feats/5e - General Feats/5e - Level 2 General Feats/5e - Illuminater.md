# 🥇5e - Illuminater🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1, WIS +1, or CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Radiant Strike:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Radiant</u> damage to a creature, you can temporarily negate the creature's defenses. The creature subtracts `1d4` from the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] it makes before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage to a creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
