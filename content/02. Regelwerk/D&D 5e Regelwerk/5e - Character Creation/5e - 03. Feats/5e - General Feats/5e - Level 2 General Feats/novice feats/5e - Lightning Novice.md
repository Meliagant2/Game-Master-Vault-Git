You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cantrip:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Shocking Grasp (Spell)|☄️Shocking Grasp]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Lightning Strike:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Lightning</u> damage to a creature, you can temporarily weaken its reflexes. The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] against the next attack against it before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
