# 🥇5e - Light Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1, WIS +1, or CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Eyes of the Sun:</u>** You and allies within <u>10 feet</u> of you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] made to avoid or end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] condition.

**<u>Faerie Fire:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 1 Evocation/5e - Faerie Fire (Spell)|☄️Faerie Fire]] spell. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 1 Evocation/5e - Faerie Fire (Spell)|☄️Faerie Fire]] without a spell slot using this benefit, taking damage can't break your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] on the spell.
