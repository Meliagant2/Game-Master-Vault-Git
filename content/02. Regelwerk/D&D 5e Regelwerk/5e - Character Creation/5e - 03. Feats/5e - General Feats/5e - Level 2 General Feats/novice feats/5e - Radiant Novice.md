> [!wikibox]

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cantrip:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Flare (Spell)|☄️Flare]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Radiant Center:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Radiant</u> damage to a creature, you can direct the target's attention to you. The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Taunted|☠️Taunted]] by you until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.
