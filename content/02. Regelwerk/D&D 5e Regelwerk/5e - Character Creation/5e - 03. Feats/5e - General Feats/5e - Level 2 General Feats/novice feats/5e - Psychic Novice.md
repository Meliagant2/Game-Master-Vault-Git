You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cantrip:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 0 Enchantment/5e - Mind Sliver (Spell)|☄️Mind Sliver]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Slow Mind:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Psychic</u> damage to a creature, you can muddle its mind. The creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is reduced by <u>10 feet</u> until <u>the end of its next turn</u>.
