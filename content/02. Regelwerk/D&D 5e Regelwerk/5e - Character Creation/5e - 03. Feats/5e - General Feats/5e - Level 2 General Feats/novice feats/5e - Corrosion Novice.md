# 🥇5e - Corrosion Novice🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1, WIS +1, or CHA +1 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cantrip:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Acid Splash (Spell)|☄️Acid Splash]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Corrosion:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Acid</u> damage to a creature, you can temporarily melt its armor. The creature subtracts `2` from its _AC_ until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
