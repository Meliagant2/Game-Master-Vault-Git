# 🥇5e - Shield Warrior🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Shield Proficiency |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shields as Weapons:</u>** You can use a _Shield_ as a _Martial melee weapon_. On a _**hit**_, your _Shield_ deals `1d8` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage. The _Shield_ has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Push Mastery Property|🗡️Push Mastery Property]].

**<u>Perfect Dodge:</u>** When you have a _Shield_ in your hand, you may apply its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] bonus to <u>DEX</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] you make.
