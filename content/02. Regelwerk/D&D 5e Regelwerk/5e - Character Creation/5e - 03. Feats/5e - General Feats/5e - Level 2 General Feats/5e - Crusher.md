# 🥇5e - Crusher🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1, or DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Area Crush:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you deal <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to a creature, you can turn the area within a <u>5-foot Emanation</u> from the creature into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] until the <u>start of your next turn</u>, if the ground is made of a material that allows this.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to a creature, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exposed|☠️Exposed]] until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.
