# 🥇5e - Legionary🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1, or DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Stand as One:</u>** When an ally within <u>5 feet</u> of you is subjected to an effect that would [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pull]] it, you can take a **Reaction** to prevent that ally from being [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Pushed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pulled]]. To receive this benefit, the ally can't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.

**<u>Vigilant:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Ready|⚔️Ready]] action, the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] made against you has **DISADV** before <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
