# 🥇5e - Dual Wielder🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1, or DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Enhanced Dual Wielding:</u>** You can use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Two-Weapon Fighting|⚔️Two-Weapon Fighting]] even when the <u>one-handed melee weapons</u> you are wielding don't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Light (Property)|🗡️Light]] property.

**<u>Quick Draw:</u>** You can draw or stow <u>two</u> weapons that lack the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Two-Handed (Property)|🗡️Two-Handed]] property when you would normally be able to draw or stow only <u>one</u>.
