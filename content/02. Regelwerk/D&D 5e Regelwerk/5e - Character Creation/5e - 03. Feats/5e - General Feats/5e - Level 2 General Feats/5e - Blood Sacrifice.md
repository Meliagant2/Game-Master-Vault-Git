# 🥇5e - Blood Sacrifice🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CON +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

As a **Bonus Action**, you can choose to take `1d6` damage to gain a **Blood Boon**. Your boon lasts for <u>1 hour</u> or until expended. You can expend this **Blood Boon** to gain one of the following benefits:

- **<u>Ability Check:</u>** When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]], add `1d6` to the total of the roll.
- **<u>Attack:</u>** When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], add `1d6` to the total of the roll.
- **<u>Siphon Strength:</u>** When you cause a creature to make a <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, or <u>CON</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], roll `1d4` and reduce the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] by the amount rolled.
