# 🥇5e - Grappler🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Attack Advantage:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>Attack Rolls</u> against a creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] by you.

**<u>Punch and Grab:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] on your turn, you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grapple]] the target as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] Action, without needing to take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Grapple (Maneuver)|🤺Grapple]] maneuver.
