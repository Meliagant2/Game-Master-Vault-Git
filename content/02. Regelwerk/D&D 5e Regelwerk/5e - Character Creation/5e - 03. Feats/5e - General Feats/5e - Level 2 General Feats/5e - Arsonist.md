# 🥇5e - Arsonist🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1, WIS +1, or CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Hurtful Burn:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Fire</u> damage to a creature, you can reduce the damage it deals with the next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] it makes before <u>the start of your next turn</u> by `1d4`.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Slashing</u> damage to a creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Burning|☠️Burning]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
