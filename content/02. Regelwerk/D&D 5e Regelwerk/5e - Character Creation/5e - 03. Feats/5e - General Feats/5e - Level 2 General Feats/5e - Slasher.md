# 🥇5e - Slasher🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |

> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1, or DEX +1 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Hamstring:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Slashing</u> damage to a creature, you can reduce the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] of that creature by <u>10 feet</u> until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Slashing</u> damage to a creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
