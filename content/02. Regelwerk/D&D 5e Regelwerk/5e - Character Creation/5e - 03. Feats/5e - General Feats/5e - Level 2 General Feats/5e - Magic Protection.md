# 🥇5e - Magic Protection🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CON +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Magic Absorption:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you take damage from a _spell_ or _magical effect_, you reduce the total damage taken by `1d4`. You can't use this benefit if you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition.

**<u>Resistance:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 0 Abjuration/5e - Resistance (Spell)|☄️Resistance]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).
