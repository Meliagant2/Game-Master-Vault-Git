# 🥇5e - Melee Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1, WIS +1, or CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Casting in Melee:</u>** Being within <u>5 feet</u> of an enemy doesn’t impose **DISADV** on your ranged Attack Rolls with spells.

**<u>Reactive Spell:</u>** When a creature provokes an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] from you by leaving your reach, you can take a **Reaction** to cast a _spell_ at the creature rather than making an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]]. The _spell_ must have a casting time of one **Action** and must target only that creature.
