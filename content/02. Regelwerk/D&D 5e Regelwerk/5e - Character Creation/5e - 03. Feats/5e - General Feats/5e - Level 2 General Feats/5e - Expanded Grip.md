# 🥇5e - Expanded Grip🥇

> [!wikibox]

> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1, or DEX +1 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>One-Handed Grip:</u>** When you use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Versatile (Property)|🗡️Versatile]] weapon with one hand, the weapon deals the damage in parentheses when used to make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]].

**<u>Zealous Grasp:</u>** If an effect forces you to drop what you are holding, you can make a **DC 15** <u>STR</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] to maintain your grip. When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] to maintain your grip, you have **ADV** on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].
