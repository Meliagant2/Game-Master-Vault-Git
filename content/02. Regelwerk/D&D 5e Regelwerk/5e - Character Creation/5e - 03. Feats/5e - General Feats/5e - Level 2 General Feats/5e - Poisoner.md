# 🥇5e - Poisoner🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +1, or INT +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Potent Poison:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you deal <u>Poison</u> damage to a creature, the creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Impaired|☠️Impaired]] until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Poison</u> damage to a creature, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.
