# 🥇5e - Inspiring Leader🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

At the end of a **Short** or **Long Rest**, you can give an inspiring performance: a speech, a song, or a dance. When you do so, choose up to <u>eight</u> friendly creatures (which can include yourself) within <u>30 feet</u> of you who witness the performance. The chosen creatures each gain <u>Temporary Hit Points</u> equal to `your Level + WIS/CHA (whichever is higher)`.
