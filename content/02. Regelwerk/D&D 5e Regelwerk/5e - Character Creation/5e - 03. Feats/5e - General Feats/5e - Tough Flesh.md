# 🥇5e - Tough Flesh🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Immediately after you take <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, or <u>Slashing</u> damage, you can take a **Reaction** to gain <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.

<u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
