# 🥇5e - Spellfire Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellfire Spark Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fueled Spellfire:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when a spell you cast deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can expend up to two _Hit Dice_, roll them, and add the total rolled to one <u>damage roll</u> of the spell.

**<u>Searing Spellfire:</u>** When you make a <u>damage roll</u> that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Radiant</u> damage.
