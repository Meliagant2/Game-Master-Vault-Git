# 🥇5e - Slasher🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Hamstring:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you hit a creature with an attack that deals <u>Slashing</u> damage, you can reduce the <u>Speed</u> of that creature by <u>10 feet</u> until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Enhanced Critical:</u>** When you score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] that deals <u>Slashing</u> damage to a creature, it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] until the start of your next turn.
