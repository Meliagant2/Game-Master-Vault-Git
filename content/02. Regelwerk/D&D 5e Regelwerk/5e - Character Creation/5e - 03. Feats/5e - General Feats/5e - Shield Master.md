# 🥇5e - Shield Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Shield Training |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shield Bash:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, if you attack a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] Action and hit with a <u>Melee Weapon</u>, you can immediately bash the target with your Shield if it’s equipped, forcing the target to make a **STR Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] or push it <u>5 feet</u> away.

**<u>Interpose Shield:</u>** If you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a <u>DEX Save</u> to take only half damage, you can use your **Reaction** to take no damage if you _**succeed**_ on the <u>Save</u> and are wielding a Shield, interposing your shield between yourself and the source of the effect.

**<u>Perfect Dodge:</u>** When you have a shield in your hand, you may apply its <u>AC</u> bonus to <u>DEX Saves</u> made against effects that target you.
