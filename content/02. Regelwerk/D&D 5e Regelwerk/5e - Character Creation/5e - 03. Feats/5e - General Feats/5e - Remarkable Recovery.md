# 🥇5e - Remarkable Recovery🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Prerequisite**|Durable Feat |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Quickly Stabilized:</u>** When you are successfully [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stable|☠️stabilized]] while dying, you regain _Hit Points_ equal to your <u>CON</u> modifier (minimum of `1`).

**<u>Healing Flesh:</u>** Whenever you regain _Hit Points_ as a result of a spell, potion, class feature, or another feat (not this feat), you regain additional _Hit Points_ equal to your <u>CON</u> modifier (minimum of `1`).
