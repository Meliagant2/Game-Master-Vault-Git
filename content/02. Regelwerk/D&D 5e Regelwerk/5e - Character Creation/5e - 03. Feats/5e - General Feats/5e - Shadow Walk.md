> [!wikibox]
> | | |

> |**Prerequisite**|Stealth Proficiency |

When you are in [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]], as a **Bonus Action** you can teleport up to <u>60 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in **Dim Light** or **Darkness**. You have **ADV** on <u>Stealth</u> checks until the beginning of your next turn.
