You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cantrip:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Ray of Frost (Spell)|☄️Ray of Frost]] cantrip. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spell (whichever is highest).

**<u>Frostbite:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you _**hit**_ a creature with an attack roll and deal <u>Cold</u> damage, you can temporarily negate the creature's defenses. The creature subtracts `1d4` from the next <u>Save</u> it makes before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
