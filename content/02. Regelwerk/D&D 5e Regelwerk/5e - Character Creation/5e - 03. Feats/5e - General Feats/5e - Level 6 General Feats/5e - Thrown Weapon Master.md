Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attacks]] using <u>Simple weapons</u> that have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Thrown (Property)|🗡️Thrown]] property as a **Bonus Action**.

**<u>Returning:</u>** Weapon with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Thrown (Property)|🗡️Thrown]] property that you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Returning Mastery Property|🗡️Returning]] mastery property.
