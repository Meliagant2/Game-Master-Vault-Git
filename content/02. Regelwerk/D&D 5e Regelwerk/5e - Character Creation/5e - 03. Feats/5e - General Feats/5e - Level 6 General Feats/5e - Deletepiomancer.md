# 🥇5e - Deletepiomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Venomous Touch:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal<u>Poison</u> damage, you can cause <u>one</u> creature taking the damage to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. While [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] in this way, <u>once per turn</u>, when the creature takes damage, it takes an additional `1d4` <u>Poison</u> damage.

**<u>Potent Poison:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Poison</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Poison</u> damage. You are also able to apply the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition to targets that are normally <u>Immune</u> against it.
