**<u>Siphon Life Force:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Necrotic</u> damage to a creature other than yourself, you can regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `1/2 x the Necrotic damage dealt`.

**<u>Slow Necrosis:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Necrotic</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> damage.
