# 🥇5e - Pyromancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Burning Flame:</u>** Whenever you deal <u>Fire</u> damage and roll the highest number on any damage die, you can roll that damage die again and add it to the damage, rolling again if it is the highest number, and so on. You can add a maximum number of dice to the damage roll equal to `your PB`.

**<u>Flare Damage:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Fire</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Fire</u> damage.
