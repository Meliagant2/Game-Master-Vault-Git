# 🥇5e - Geomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Push:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to a creature, you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] it <u>15 feet</u> to an unoccupied space, provided the target is no more than _one size larger_ than you.

**<u>Crushing Blow:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.
