# 🥇5e - Rapid Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Preemptive Casting:</u>** When you roll [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]], you can use your **Reaction** to cast a _spell_. The _spell_ must have a _casting time_ of <u>1 Action</u> and target only you or your allies.

**<u>Immediate Response:</u>** When you cast a _spell_ as a **Reaction**, that spell doesn't expend a _spell slot_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
