# 🥇5e - Corrosion Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

**<u>Acid Surface:</u>** When you deal <u>Acid</u> damage, you can create a <u>10-foot Radius</u> pool of acid centered on the target. The area is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] and creatures within the area subtract `2` from their _AC_. The pool remains for <u>1 minute</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
