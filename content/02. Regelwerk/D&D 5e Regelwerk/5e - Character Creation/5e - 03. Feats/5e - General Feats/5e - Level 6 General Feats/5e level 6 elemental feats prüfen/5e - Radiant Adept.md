# 🥇5e - Radiant Adept🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

**<u>Radiant Rebuke:</u>** When you deal <u>Radiant</u> damage, you can cause one _Huge or smaller_ creature taking the damage to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
