# 🥇5e - Photomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fueled Light:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Radiant</u> damage to a creature, you can expend up to <u>two</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]], roll them, and add the total rolled to one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] of the _triggering_ damage.

**<u>Searing Light:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Radiant</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Radiant</u> damage.
