# 🥇5e - Cryomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Tomb of Ice:</u>** When you cast a spell that deals <u>Cold</u> damage, you can gain a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `1d6 + the spell's level`. If a creature hits you with a <u>melee attack</u> while you have these [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]], the creature takes `2d4` <u>Cold</u> damage.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
