# 🥇5e - Legatus🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3, or DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Family First:</u>** If you have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]] when you roll [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]], you can expend it to give yourself and your allies **ADV** on that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]] roll.

**<u>Reassert:</u>** When an enemy you can see deals damage to an ally of yours that is within <u>5 feet</u> of you, you have **ADV** on your next <u>attack roll</u> against that enemy before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
