# 🥇5e - Flex Caster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Upcast:</u>** When you cast a _spell_, that can be cast with a _higher-level spell slot_ for more powerful effects, you can expend any <u>one</u> additional _spell slot_ to increase the spell's effective _level by 1_, to a maximum of level `9`.

**<u>Downcast:</u>** When you cast a spell using a _higher-level spell slot_, you choose to recycle the excess magical energy instead of improving the spell. The spell is cast at its base level and you regain an expended _level 1_ spell slot.
