# 🥇5e - Shield Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Shield Warrior Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shield Bash:</u>** If you attack a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action and hit with a <u>melee weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]], you can immediately bash the target with your Shield if it’s equipped. As a **Bonus Action** you make a <u>melee weapon attack</u> with your _Shield_. On a _**hit**_ the target makes a **STR Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The Target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] or you [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] it <u>5 feet</u> away.

**<u>Interpose Shield:</u>** If you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a **DEX Save** to take only _half_ damage, you can use your **Reaction** to take _no damage_ if you _**succeed**_ on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] and are wielding a _Shield_, interposing your shield between yourself and the source of the effect.
