# 🥇5e - Sentinel🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +3, or DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Guardian:</u>** Immediately after a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you takes the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Disengage|⚔️Disengage]] action or _hits_ a target other than you with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] against that creature.

**<u>Halt:</u>** When you hit a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]], the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] becomes `0` for <u>the rest of the current turn</u>.
