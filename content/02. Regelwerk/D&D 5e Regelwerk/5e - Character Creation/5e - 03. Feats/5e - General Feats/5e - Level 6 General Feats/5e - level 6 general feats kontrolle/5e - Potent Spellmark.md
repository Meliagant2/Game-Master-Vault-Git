# 🥇5e - Potent Spellmark🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Any Spellmark Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Spellmark Preparation:</u>** You always have the spells on your **Spells of the Mark list** (if any) prepared.

**<u>Spellmark Spellcasting:</u>** You have one extra _spell slot_ to cast the spells granted by your **Spellmark** feat. The spell slot's level is `1/2 × your Level`, to a maximum of _level 5_. You regain the expended slot when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**. You can use this spell slot to cast only a spell you have prepared because of your **Spellmark** feat or the **Spellmark Preparation** benefit of this feat.
