# 🥇5e - Aeromancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Laceration:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Slashing</u> damage, you can inflict <u>one</u> stack of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bleeding|☠️Bleeding]] to a creature taking the damage.

**<u>Deep Cuts:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Slashing</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Slashing</u> damage.
