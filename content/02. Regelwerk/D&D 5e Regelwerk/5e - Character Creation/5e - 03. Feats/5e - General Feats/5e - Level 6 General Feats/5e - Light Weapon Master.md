# 🥇5e - Light Weapon Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|DEX +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Expert Wielder:</u>** Increase the size of the <u>damage die</u> for every [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Light (Property)|🗡️Light]] weapon you are holding. (`d4` -> `d6`; `d6` -> `d8`; `d8` -> `d10`; `d10` -> `d12`; `d12` -> `2d6`)

**<u>Enhanced Defense:</u>** You gain a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] while you are wielding a separate <u>melee weapon</u> in each hand.
