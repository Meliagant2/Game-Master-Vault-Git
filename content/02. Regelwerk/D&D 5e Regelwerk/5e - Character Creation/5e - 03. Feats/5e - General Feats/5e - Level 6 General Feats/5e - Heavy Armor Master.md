# 🥇5e - Heavy Armor Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Heavy Armor Training |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you're hit by an attack while you're wearing Heavy armor, any damage dealt to you by that attack is reduced by an amount equal to `your Proficiency Bonus`.
