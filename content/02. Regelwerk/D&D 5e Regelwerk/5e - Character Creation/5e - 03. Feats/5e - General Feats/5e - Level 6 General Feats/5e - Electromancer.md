# 🥇5e - Electromancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

**<u>Flyby Shock:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Lightning</u> damage, you gain a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>40 feet</u> until <u>the end of the current turn</u>. While utilizing that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] against you are made with **DISADV**.

**<u>Piercing Lightning:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Lightning</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Lightning</u> damage.
