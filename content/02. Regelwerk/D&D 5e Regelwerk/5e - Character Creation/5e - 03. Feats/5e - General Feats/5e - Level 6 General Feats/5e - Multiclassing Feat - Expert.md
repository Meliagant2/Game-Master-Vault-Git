# 🥇5e - Multiclassing Feat - Expert🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**|x |

You gain **one** of the following from a Class that you have at least 1 Class Feature from:

**<u>Level 5 Feature:</u>** You gain **one** level 5 Class Feature from that Class, that you don't already have.

**<u>Class Feat:</u>** You gain a level 2, or level 4 [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Class Feats/5e - Class Feats|🥇Class Feat]] from that Class, that you don't already have.

**<u>Subclass Archetype:</u>** You gain the level 3 Subclass Feature(s) and Flavor Feature of one of its Subclasses.
