# 🥇5e - Anoiyomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Guard Self:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Piercing</u> damage to a creature, you can give yourself a bonus to <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, and <u>WIS</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]], equal to `1/5 x the Piercing damage dealt (minimum of +1; maximum of +5)` until <u>the start of your next turn</u> .

**<u>Pierce Steel:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Piercing</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Piercing</u> damage.
