# 🥇5e - Ormymancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +3, WIS +3, or CHA +3 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

**<u>Sustain Energy:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you _miss_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] that would deal <u>Force</u> damage, or when a creature _succeeds_ on a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against an effect that would deal <u>Force</u> damage, some of the energy returns to you as an orbiting sphere. You can have a maximum of <u>one</u> orbiting sphere at a time and it vanishes after <u>1 minute</u>. While you have an orbiting sphere, you can absorb its energy to do the following:

- **<u>Energy Blast:</u>** When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] for an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] that would deal <u>Force</u> damage, you can consume the orbiting sphere to gain **ADV** on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]].
- **<u>Interception:</u>** When an attacker that you can _see hits_ you with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can take a **Reaction** to consume the orbiting sphere to halve the attack's damage against you.
- **<u>Speed of Light:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can consume the orbiting sphere to teleport <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can _see_.

**<u>Unstoppable Force:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Force</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Force</u> damage. You are also able to apply the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition to targets that are normally <u>Immune</u> against it.
