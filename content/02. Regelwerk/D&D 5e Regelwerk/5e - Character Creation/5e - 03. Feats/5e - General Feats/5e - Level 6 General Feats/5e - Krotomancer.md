**<u>Thundering Echo:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u> when you deal <u>Thunder</u> damage, you can inflict <u>one</u> stack of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Reverbration|☠️Reverbration]] to a creature taking the damage. While that creature has a stack of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Reverbration|☠️Reverbration]] applied by this feat, it has **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] against the effects of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Reverbration|☠️Reverbration]].

**<u>Unresistable Storm:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage Roll|💔Damage Roll]] that deals <u>Thunder</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Thunder</u> damage.
