# 🥇5e - Mage Slayer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Concentration Breaker:</u>** When you damage a creature that is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Saves/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentrating]], it has **DISADV** on the <u>Save</u> it makes to maintain <u>Concentration</u>.

**<u>Guarded Mind:</u>** If you fail an <u>INT</u>, a <u>WIS</u>, or a <u>CHA</u> Save, you can cause yourself to succeed instead. <u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
