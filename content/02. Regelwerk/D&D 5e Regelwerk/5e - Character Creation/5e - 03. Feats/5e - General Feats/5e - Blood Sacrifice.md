# 🥇5e - Blood Sacrifice🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

As a **Bonus Action**, you can choose to take `1d6` damage to gain a **Blood Boon**. Your boon lasts for <u>1 hour</u> or until expended. You can expend this **Blood Boon** to gain one of the following benefits:

- **<u>Attack:</u>** When you make an <u>attack roll</u>, add `1d6` to the total of the roll.
- **<u>Damage:</u>** When you hit with an <u>attack</u>, you deal an additional `2d6` <u>Necrotic</u> damage.
- **<u>Siphon Strength:</u>** When you cause a creature to make a <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, or <u>CON</u> <u>Save</u>, roll `1d4` and reduce their Save by the amount rolled.
