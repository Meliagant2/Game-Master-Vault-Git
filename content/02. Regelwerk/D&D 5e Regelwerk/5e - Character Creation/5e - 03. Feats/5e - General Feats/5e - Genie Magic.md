As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]], you can cast a _level 1_ spell from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Primal/5e - Spell Source Primal|☄️Primal]] spell source that has a casting time of an **Action**. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you reach _level 11_, the spell you cast with this feat is cast as though using a _level 2_ spell slot.

When you reach _level 17_, the spell is cast as though using a _level 3_ spell slot.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
