# 🥇5e - Dual Wielder🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR or DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Enhanced Dual Wielding:</u>** You can use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Two-Weapon Fighting|⚔️Two-Weapon Fighting]] even when the one-handed melee weapons you are wielding don't have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Light (Property)|🗡️Light]] property.

**<u>Quick Draw:</u>** You can draw or stow two weapons that lack the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Two-Handed (Property)|🗡️Two-Handed]] property when you would normally be able to draw or stow only one.

**<u>Enhanced Defense:</u>** You gain a `+1` bonus to <u>Armor Class</u> while you are wielding a separate melee weapon in each hand.
