# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Finding🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Finding |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Perception</u> and <u>Survival</u>, increasing the dice in size.

**<u>Improved Finding:</u>** When you use the **Finder's Magic** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Finding|🥇Mark of Finding]] to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 1 Evocation/5e - Faerie Fire (Spell)|☄️Faerie Fire]] without a spell slot, the range of the spell is doubled, and you can modify the spell so that the target make their <u>Save</u> against the spell with **DISADV**.
