# 🥇5e - Death Defier🥇

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cling to Life:</u>** You add your <u>CON</u> (minimum of 1) to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Saves/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]].

**<u>Enduring Vitality:</u>** If an effect would kill you outright without [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Saves/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]] or would reduce you directly to _0 Hit Points_ without dealing damage, you are instead reduced to a number of Hit Points equal to `your level`.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
