# 🥇5e - Metabolistic Magic🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcasting |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Arcane Skill:</u>** When you _fail_ a <u>D20 Test</u>, you can expend a _spell slot_ to gain a bonus to the roll, potentially causing it to succeed. The bonus equals `2 + the level of the spell slot`.

**<u>Vital Fuel:</u>** When you finish a **Short Rest**, you can expend a number of _Hit Dice_ up to <u>your PB</u> to recover expended spell slots. Choose spell slots to recover that have a combined level equal to no more than the number of _Hit Dice_ expended. <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
