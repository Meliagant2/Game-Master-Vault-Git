# 🥇5e - Medium Armor Master🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Medium Armor Training |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Silent Steps:</u>** Wearing medium armor doesn't impose **DISADV** on your <u>Stealth</u> checks.

**<u>Dexterous Wearer:</u>** When you wear medium armor, you can add `3`, rather than `2`, to your <u>AC</u> if you have a <u>DEX</u> of <u>+3</u> or higher.
