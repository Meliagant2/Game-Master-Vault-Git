# 🥇5e - Grappler🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR or DEX +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Attack Advantage:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>Attack Rolls</u> against a creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] by you.

**<u>Fast Grappler:</u>** You aren’t [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Slowed|☠️Slowed]] when you move a creature Grappled by you, provided the creature is your Size or smaller.

**<u>Punch and Grab:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you hit a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] Action on your turn, you can deal damage to the target and also <u>grapple</u> it, without needing to take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Grapple (Maneuver)|🤺Grapple]] Basic Maneuver.
