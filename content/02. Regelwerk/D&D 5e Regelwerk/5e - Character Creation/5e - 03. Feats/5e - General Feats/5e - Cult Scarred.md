# 🥇5e - Cult Scarred🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cult Initiate |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Damage Resistance:</u>** When you gain this feat, choose <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Necrotic</u>, or <u>Poison</u>. You have <u>Resistance</u> to the chosen damage type.

**<u>Fearsome Power:</u>** When you deal damage to a creature as part of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] on your turn, you can use the **Terror** benefit of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Cult Initiate|🥇Cult Initiate]] feat as a **Bonus Action** this turn.
