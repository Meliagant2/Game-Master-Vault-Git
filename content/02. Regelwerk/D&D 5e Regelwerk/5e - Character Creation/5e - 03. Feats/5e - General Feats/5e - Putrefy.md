# 🥇5e - Putrefy🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you make a damage roll that deals <u>Necrotic</u> damage, you can cause one creature taking that damage to have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition until the <u>start of your next turn</u>.

<u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
