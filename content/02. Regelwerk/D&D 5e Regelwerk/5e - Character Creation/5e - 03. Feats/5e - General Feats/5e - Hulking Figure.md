# 🥇5e - Hulking Figure🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|STR +1 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Brutal:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, you can deal an extra `1d4` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to a target you hit with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]].

**<u>Intimidating:</u>** When you make an <u>Intimidation</u>, <u>Performance</u>, or <u>Persuasion</u> check, you can also add your <u>STR</u> to the roll.

**<u>Powerful:</u>** You count as one size _larger_ (to a maximum size of Large) when determining your carrying capacity.
