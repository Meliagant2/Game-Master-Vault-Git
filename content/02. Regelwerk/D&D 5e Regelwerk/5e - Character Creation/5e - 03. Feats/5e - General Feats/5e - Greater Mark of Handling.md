# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Handling🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Handling |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Animal Handling</u> and <u>Nature</u>, increasing the dice in size.

**<u>Improved Handling:</u>** While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Movement and Position/5e - Mounted Combat/5e - Mounted Combat|🐴Mounted]], immediately after you _hit_ a target within <u>5 feet</u> of your mount with a <u>melee attack roll</u>, your mount can take a **Reaction** to move up to its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] or take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action to make <u>one</u> attack only (your choice).

**<u>Subdue Animal:</u>** As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]], you exert command over one <u>Beast</u> or <u>Monstrosity</u> you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. The target makes a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
