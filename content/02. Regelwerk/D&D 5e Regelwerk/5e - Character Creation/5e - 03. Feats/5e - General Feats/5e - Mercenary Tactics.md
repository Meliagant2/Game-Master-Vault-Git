# 🥇5e - Mercenary Tactics🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mercenary Roughness |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Retaliate:</u>** Immediately after a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you _hits_ you with a <u>melee attack</u>, you can make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]] against that creature.

**<u>Versatile Merc:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, choose a skill in which you have proficiency. You gain an <u>expertise die</u> in that skill until you finish your next **Long Rest**.
