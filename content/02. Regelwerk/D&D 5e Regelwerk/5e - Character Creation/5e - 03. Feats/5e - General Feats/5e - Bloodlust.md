# 🥇5e - Bloodlust🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Powerful Recovery.</u>** When you roll a <u>Hit Point Die</u> to regain <u>Hit Points</u>, you can treat any roll of `1` or `2` as a `3`.

**<u>Sanguine Feast.</u>** Once per turn when you hit a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]] creature that isn't a <u>Construct</u> or <u>Undead</u> with an attack roll, you can expend a <u>Hit Point Die</u>, roll it, and regain a number of <u>Hit Points</u> equal to `the number rolled + your CON`.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your <u>Proficiency Bonus</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
