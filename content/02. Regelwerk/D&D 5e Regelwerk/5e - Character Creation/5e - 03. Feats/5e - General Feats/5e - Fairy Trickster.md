# 🥇5e - Fairy Trickster🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|General |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Faerie Trod Trotter:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Disengage|⚔️Disengage]] action on your turn, [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]] doesn't cost you extra movement for the rest of that turn.

**<u>Flustering Strike:</u>** When you _**hit**_ a creature with an <u>attack roll</u>, you can attempt to fluster the target. The target makes a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]] or your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target has **DISADV** on <u>Saves</u> until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
