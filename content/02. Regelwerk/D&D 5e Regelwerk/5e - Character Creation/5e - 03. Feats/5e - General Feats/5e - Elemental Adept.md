# 🥇5e - Elemental Adept🥇

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Piercing Energy:</u>** Choose one of the following Damage Types: <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, or <u>Thunder</u>. Your Spells and Attacks ignore <u>Resistance</u> to damage of the chosen type.

**<u>Energy Mastery:</u>** When you roll damage for an Attack or a Spell you cast that deals damage of that type, you can treat any `1` on a damage die as a `2`.
