You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Dodgy:</u>** While you aren't wearing _Armor_ or wielding a _Shield_, you can take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dodge|⚔️Dodge]] action as a **Bonus Action**.

**<u>Slippery:</u>** While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Restrained|☠️Restrained]], your attacks don't have **DISADV** and attacks against you don't have **ADV**.
