# 🥇5e - Amphibious Combatant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Aquatic Expert:</u>** You can spend `1` <u>Exertion</u> to reroll a failed <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, or <u>CON</u> <u>Check</u> related to aquatic activity.

**<u>Hold Breath:</u>** You add your proficiency bonus to the number of minutes you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Suffocating|☠️hold your breath]], and can spend `1` <u>Exertion</u> to hold your breath for an additional minutes equal to `your proficiency bonus`.

**<u>Swimming Speed:</u>** You gain a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]] equal to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]].
