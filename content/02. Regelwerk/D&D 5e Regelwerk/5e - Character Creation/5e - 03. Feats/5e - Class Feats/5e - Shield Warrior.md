# 🥇5e - Shield Warrior🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Shields as Weapons</u>** You can use a _Shield_ as a <u>martial melee weapon</u>. On a _**hit**_, your _Shield_ deals `1d8` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage. The _Shield_ has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery/5e - Push Mastery Property|🗡️Push Mastery Property]]

**<u>Wielding only the Shield:</u>** If you are wielding a _Shield_ and nothing else, you gain a `+1` bonus to your shield attack <u>damage rolls</u> and to your _AC_.
