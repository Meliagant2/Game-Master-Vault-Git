# 🥇5e - Action Surge (Legacy)🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You can push yourself past your limits, if only for a moment. <u>Once during your turn</u>, you can take one additional **Action**, except the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]].

<u>Once</u> you do so, you must finish a **short** or **long rest** before you can use this feature again.
