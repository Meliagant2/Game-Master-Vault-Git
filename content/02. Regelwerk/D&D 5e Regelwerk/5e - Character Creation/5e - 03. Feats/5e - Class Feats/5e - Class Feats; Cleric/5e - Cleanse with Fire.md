You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Lesser Cleanse:</u>** You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - Level 2 Abjuration/5e - Lesser Restoration (Spell)|☄️Lesser Restoration]] on a willing creature without expending spell slots, but the target takes `2d6` <u>Fire</u> damage. This damage ignores <u>Resistance</u> and <u>Immunity</u>.

**<u>Greater Cleanse:</u>** You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - 000 Abjuration Control/5e - Greater Restoration (Spell)|☄️Greater Restoration]] on a willing creature without expending spell slots and without [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] components, but the target takes `5d6` <u>Fire</u> damage. This damage ignores <u>Resistance</u> and <u>Immunity</u>.
