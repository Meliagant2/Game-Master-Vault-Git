# 🥇5e - Eyes Unveiled🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Your sight is unhindered, and you can see in the deepest shadows. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Darkvision:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Darkvision|👁️Darkvision]] with a range of <u>120 feet</u>. If you already have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Darkvision|👁️Darkvision]], the range of it increases by <u>120 feet</u> instead.

**<u>Pierce Illusion:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] against [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Illusion/5e - School of Illusion|☄️Illusion]] spells and on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Checks]] to determine whether something is an illusion.
