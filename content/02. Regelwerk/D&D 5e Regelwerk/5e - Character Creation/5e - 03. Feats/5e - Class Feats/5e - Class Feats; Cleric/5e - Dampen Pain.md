You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Dampen Pain:</u>** As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action you can choose a number of creatures within <u>30 feet</u> equal to `your WIS`. Those creatures gain <u>Resistance</u> against the damage types of your **Divine Damage** feature for <u>1 hour</u>.
