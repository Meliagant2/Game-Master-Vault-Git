# 🥇5e - Emboldening Bond🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You can forge an empowering bond among people. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Channel Divinity:</u>** As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action, you present your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] and expend a use of your **Channel Divinity** to choose a number of willing creatures within <u>30 feet</u> of you (this can include yourself) equal to `your WIS`. You create a magical bond among them for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you use this feature again. <u>Once per turn</u>, while any bonded creature is within <u>30 feet</u> of another, the creature can roll a `d4` and add the number rolled to any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] it makes.
