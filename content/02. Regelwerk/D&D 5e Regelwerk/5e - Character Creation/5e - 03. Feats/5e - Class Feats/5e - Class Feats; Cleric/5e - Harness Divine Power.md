# 🥇5e - Harness Divine Power🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Restore Spell Slot:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you utter a prayer, and regain <u>one</u> expended spell slot, the level of which can be no higher than `half your Cleric level (rounded up)`, and the slot cannot be level 6 or higher.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
