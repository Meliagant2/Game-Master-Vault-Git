# 🥇5e - Blessed Acolyte🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Guidance:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 0 Divination/5e - Guidance (Spell)|☄️Guidance]] cantrip.

**<u>Bless:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Bless (Spell)|☄️Bless]] spell prepared. You can cast it a number of times without a spell slot equal to `your WIS`, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
