# 🥇5e - Guided Strike🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Channel Divinity:</u>** When you or a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of you _misses_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you can expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** and give that roll a `+10` bonus, potentially causing it to _hit_. When you use this feature to benefit another creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you must take a **Reaction** to do so.
