# 🥇5e - Grave Keeper🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Studying funeral rites and tending the dead's rest has made you a conduit between the worlds of the living and the dead. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in one of the following _Skills_, or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_: [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Occultism|💃Occultism]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Religion|💃Religion]].

**<u>Last Rites:</u>** You can enact a <u>10-minute</u> ritual to prepare the corpse of a _Medium or smaller_ creature, expending at least 10 SP worth of incense, oils, linen wraps, or similar trappings. The prepared corpse can't become _Undead_.
