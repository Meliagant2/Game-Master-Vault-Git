# 🥇5e - Touch of Death🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You can destroy another creature’s life force by touch. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Necrotic Touch:</u>** When you _**hit**_ a creature with a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** to deal extra <u>Necrotic</u> damage to the target (**no Action** required). The damage equals `5 + your Cleric Level`.
