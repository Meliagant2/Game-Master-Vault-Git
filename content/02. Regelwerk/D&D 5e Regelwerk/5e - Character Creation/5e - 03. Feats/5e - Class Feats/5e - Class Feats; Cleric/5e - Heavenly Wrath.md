# 🥇5e - Heavenly Wrath🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Rebuke Attacker:</u>** When a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of you that you can see _hits_ you with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can take a **Reaction** to cause the creature to make a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes damage of one of your **Divine Damage types** (your choice) equal to `1d8 + your Cleric Level`. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
