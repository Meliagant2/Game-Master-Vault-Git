# 🥇5e - Water Walk🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept, Adept Speed |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the ability to move along vertical surfaces on your turn without falling during the move. You can use a **Bonus Action** and spend `1` <u>Exertion</u> to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 3 Transmutation/5e - Water Walk (Spell)|☄️Water Walk]] on yourself, without needing to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Saves/5e - Concentration|🎲concentrate]] on it. The effect lasts for <u>1 Minute</u>.

The casting of this feat's spell does **not** count as a magical effect. It is considered to be a _Maneuver_ effect instead.
