# 🥇5e - Share Focus🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept |
> |**Repeatable**| |

As an **Action**, you can share your spiritual fortitude with others. Spend `3` <u>Exertion</u> and choose a number of creatures you can _see_ within 10 ft. of you, up to a number equal to `your WIS`. For <u>1 hour</u>, you and the chosen creatures add your **Martial Arts die** to the <u>first</u> <u>Save</u> they make on each of their turns.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
