You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Brutal Weaponry:</u>** You can wield a weapon that has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Two-Handed (Property)|🗡️Two-Handed]] property in _one_ hand, provided your other hand isn't also wielding a weapon that has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Two-Handed (Property)|🗡️Two-Handed]] property or holding a _Shield_.

**<u>Killing Streak:</u>** You can use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Two-Weapon Fighting|⚔️Two-Weapon Fighting]], while holding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Two-Handed (Property)|🗡️Two-Handed]] weapon and a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Light (Property)|🗡️Light]] weapon.
