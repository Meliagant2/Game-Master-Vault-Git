# 🥇5e - Dispelling Smite🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|10 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Supernal Smite:</u>** When you use your **Paladin's Smite**, you can deal <u>Force</u> damage with it, instead of its normal damage type. When you do so, if the target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentrating]] on a spell, that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] is broken.

In addition, each ongoing spell of level 3 or lower on the target ends. For each ongoing spell of level 4 or higher on the target, make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] using your CHA (**DC 10 plus that spell's level**). _**Success:**_ The spell ends.

If you have more than one feature that adds an effect to your **Paladin's Smite**, you can only use <u>one</u> of them per instance of smite.
