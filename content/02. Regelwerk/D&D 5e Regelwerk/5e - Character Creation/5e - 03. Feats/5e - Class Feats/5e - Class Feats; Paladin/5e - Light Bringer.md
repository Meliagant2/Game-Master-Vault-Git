# 🥇5e - Light Bringer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Sacred Magic:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Light (Spell)|☄️Light]] spell and can cast it without Material components.

**<u>Solar Luminance:</u>** When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Light (Spell)|☄️Light]], you can have the light from the spell be _sunlight_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
