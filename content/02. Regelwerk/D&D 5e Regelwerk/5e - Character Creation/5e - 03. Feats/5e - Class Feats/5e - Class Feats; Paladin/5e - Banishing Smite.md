# 🥇5e - Banishing Smite🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|10 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Banishing Smite:</u>** When you use your **Paladin's Smite**, you can deal <u>Force</u> damage with it, instead of its normal damage type. When you do so, if the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] reduced the target to `50` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] or fewer, the target makes a **CHA Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target is transported to a harmless demiplane for the duration. While there, the target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition. When the spell ends, the target reappears in the space it left or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied.

If you have more than one feature that adds an effect to your **Paladin's Smite**, you can only use <u>one</u> of them per instance of smite.
