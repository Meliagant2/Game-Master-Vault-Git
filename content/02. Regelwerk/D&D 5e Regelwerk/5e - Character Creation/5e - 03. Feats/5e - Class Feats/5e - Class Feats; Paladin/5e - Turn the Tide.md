# 🥇5e - Turn the Dide🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Channel Oath:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to bolster injured creatures within <u>30 feet</u> of you. Each creature of your choice that can _hear_ you within range regains [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `2d6 + your CHA (minimum of 1)`.
