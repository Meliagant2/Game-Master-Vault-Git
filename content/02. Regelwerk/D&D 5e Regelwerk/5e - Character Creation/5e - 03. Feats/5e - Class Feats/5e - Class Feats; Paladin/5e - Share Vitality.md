# 🥇5e - Share Vitality🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Channel Oath:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** and take an amount of <u>Necrotic</u> damage up to `your current number of Hit Points`. This <u>Necrotic</u> damage cannot be reduced in any way. Up to <u>four</u> creatures you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of you regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `1/2 x the Necrotic damage taken`.
