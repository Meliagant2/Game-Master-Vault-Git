# 🥇5e - Shifting Combatant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Domino Strike:</u>** When you [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] a creature into a space occupied by a Large or smaller creature, using a _Maneuver_ or a weapon's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Push Mastery Property|🗡️Push]] mastery property to do so, you can force the creatures to collide. Each creature makes a **DEX Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.

**<u>Fearless Leap:</u>** When you make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Jumping|🏃‍♀️Long Jump]], moving at least <u>10 feet</u> immediately before the jump, and land in a space within <u>5 feet</u> of <u>two</u> or more enemies, <u>attack rolls</u> made against you have **DISADV** until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.
