# 🥇5e - Flash Recall🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Cleric, Druid, Paladin |
> |**Repeatable**| |

As a **Bonus Action**, you prepare a spell of <u>1st level</u> or higher from your class spell list. This spell must be of a level for which you have spell slots, and it replaces another spell of an equal or higher level that you had previously prepared.

<u>Once</u> you use this feat to recall a spell, you can’t do so again until you complete a **short** or **long rest**.
