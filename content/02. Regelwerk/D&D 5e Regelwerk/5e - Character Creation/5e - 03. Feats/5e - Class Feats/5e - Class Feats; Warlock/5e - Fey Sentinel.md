# 🥇5e - Fey Sentinel🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, Fey Pact Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fading Target:</u>** When an enemy _misses_ you with an attack roll, you can take a **Reaction** to gain the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u> or until immediately after you make an <u>attack roll</u>, deal damage, or cast a spell. <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Fey Shift:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] action, you can forgo the extra movement to teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within a distance equal to half your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] instead.

**<u>Nature's Roots:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Entangle (Spell)|☄️Entangle]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you reach character _level 5_, you also always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 3 Transmutation/5e - Plant Growth (Spell)|☄️Plant Growth]] spell prepared and can cast it the same way.
