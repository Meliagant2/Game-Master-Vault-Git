You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Infernal Resistance:</u>** You gain <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Fire</u> or <u>Poison</u> damage.

**<u>Infernal Sight:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Magical Darkvision|👁️Magical Darkvision]] with a range of <u>30 feet</u>.

**<u>Silver-Tongued:</u>** You gain proficiency in the <u>Deception</u> skill, or if you are already proficient in <u>Deception</u>, an <u>expertise die</u> in that skill.
