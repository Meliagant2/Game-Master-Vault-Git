**<u>Repeatable:</u>** You can gain this Feat more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Pulling:</u>** Choose one of your known _Warlock cantrips_ that requires an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]]. When you _**hit**_ a _Large or smaller_ creature with that cantrip, you can [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pulling|🏃‍♀️Pull]] the creature up to <u>10 feet</u> straight towards you.
