# 🥇5e - Gift of the Depths🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Swimmer:</u>** You can breathe underwater, and you have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]] equal to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]]. If you already have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]], you increase that by <u>10 feet</u> instead.

**<u>Water Breathing:</u>** You can also cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Transmutation/5e - Level 3 Transmutation/5e - Water Breathing (Spell)|☄️Water Breathing]] <u>once</u> without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a **Long Rest**.
