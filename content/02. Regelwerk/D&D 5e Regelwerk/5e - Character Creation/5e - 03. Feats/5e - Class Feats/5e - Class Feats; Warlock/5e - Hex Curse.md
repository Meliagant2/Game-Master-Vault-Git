# 🥇5e - Hex Curse🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, a Spell that curses |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

When you place [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Spell Tags/5e - Curses|☄️Curse]] on creatures, you can choose <u>one</u> of those creatures. Until the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Spell Tags/5e - Curses|☄️Curse]] ends, you gain the following benefits:

- **<u>Critical Bonus:</u>** Any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] you make against the target scores a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] on a roll of `19 or 20` on the `d20`. If you already have a feature that increases your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] range, you increase it by `1` instead (maximum `17-20`).
- **<u>Damage Bonus:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, you gain bonus to a damage roll against the target equal to `your Spellcasting Ability modifier`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
