**<u>Repeatable:</u>** You can gain this Feat more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Damage Increase:</u>** Choose one of your known _Warlock cantrips_ that deals damage. You can add `your Spellcasting Ability modifier` to that spell's damage rolls.
