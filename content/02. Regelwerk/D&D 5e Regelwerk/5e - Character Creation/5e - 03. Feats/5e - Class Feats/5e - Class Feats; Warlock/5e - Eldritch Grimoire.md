# 🥇5e - Eldritch Grimoire🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, Pact of the Tome |
> |**Repeatable**| |

A new page appears in your **Book of Shadows**. When you recover physical evidence from a creature (a drop of its blood, a strand of its hair, a feather, tooth or claw, etc.), you can offer it to your book of shadows in a special ritual that takes <u>10 minutes</u> to perform. The ritual consumes the offered physical evidence as the name of the creature from which it came is inscribed upon the page in red ink, which can contain a number of names equal to `your Spellcasting Ability (minimum of 1)`. After this ritual, as long as you have your **Book of Shadows** with you, you have **ADV** on any <u>INT</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Checks]] you make related to the creature.

Additionally, you can spend <u>1 minute</u> examining the page to learn one of the following facts about the creature:

- Its creature type
- Any names or aliases it uses
- Its current distance and direction from you (provided it is on the same plane of existence)
- Any <u>Vulnerability</u> or flaw it has
- Whether or not it has all of its [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]]
- Whether or not it is suffering from a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Conditions|☠️Condition]], a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Spell Tags/5e - Curses|☄️Curse]], or a similar effect, as well as the nature of that effect

As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action, you can erase a name on the page by touching it. A name also vanishes, if the creature dies.
