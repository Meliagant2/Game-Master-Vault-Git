# 🥇5e - Madenning Hex🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, a Spell that curses |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Damage the Cursed:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you cause a psychic disturbance around a target [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Spell Tags/5e - Curses|☄️Cursed]] by you, that you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of you. When you do so, you deal <u>Psychic</u> damage to the cursed target and each creature of your choice that you can _see_ within <u>5 feet</u> of it, equal to `your Spellcasting Ability modifier`.
