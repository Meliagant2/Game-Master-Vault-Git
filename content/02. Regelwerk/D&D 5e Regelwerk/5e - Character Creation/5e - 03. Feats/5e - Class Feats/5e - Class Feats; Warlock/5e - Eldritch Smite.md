# 🥇5e - Eldritch Smite🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, Pact of the Blade |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Smite:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with your **Pact Weapon**, you can expend a spell slot to deal extra <u>Force</u> damage to the target, equal to `1d8 + 1d8 per level of the spell slot`, and you can give the target the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] condition if it is _Huge or smaller_.
