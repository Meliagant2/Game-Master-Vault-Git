# 🥇5e - Fey Tormentor🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock, Fey Pact Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Faerie Time Warp:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, choose up to <u>three</u> creatures you can see within <u>60 feet</u> of yourself. Each target makes a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] is reduced by <u>10 feet</u>, and it takes a `-2` penalty to _AC_ until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Hag's Hex:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Hex (Spell)|☄️Hex]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

When you reach character _level 5_, you also have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 3 Enchantment/5e - Bestow Curse (Spell)|☄️Bestow Curse]] spell prepared and can cast it in the same way.
