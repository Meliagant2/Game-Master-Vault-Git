**<u>Repeatable:</u>** You can gain this Feat more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Damage Increase:</u>** Choose one of your known _Warlock cantrips_ that deals damage and has a range of <u>10+ feet</u>. When you cast that spell, its range increases by a number of feet equal to `30 x your Warlock level`.
