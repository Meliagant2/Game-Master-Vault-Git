You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Mobility:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>10 feet</u>.

**<u>Strike and Fade:</u>** <u>Once on each of your turns</u>, you can deal extra damage equal to `your PB` to a creature you hit with an attack that deals <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, or <u>Slashing</u> damage, as long as you have moved <u>15 feet</u> prior to making the attack.
