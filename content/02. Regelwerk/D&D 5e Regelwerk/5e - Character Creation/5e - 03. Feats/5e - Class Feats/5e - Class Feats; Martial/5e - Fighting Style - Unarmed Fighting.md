# 🥇5e - Fighting Style - Unarmed Fighting🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|1 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Martial Class |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Unarmed Damage:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strikes]] can deal <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage equal to `1d6 + STR` on a hit, instead of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Basic Melee Damage|💔Basic Melee Damage]]. If you aren’t wielding any _Weapons_ or a _Shield_ when you make the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], the `d6` becomes a `d8`.

**<u>Crushing Grapple:</u>** At <u>the start of each of your turns</u>, you can deal `1d4` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage to one creature [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Grappled|☠️Grappled]] by you.
