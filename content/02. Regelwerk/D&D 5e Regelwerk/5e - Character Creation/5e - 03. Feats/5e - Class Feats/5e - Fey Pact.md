# 🥇5e - Fey Pact🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|1 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Warlock |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Fey Bond:</u>** You know <u>Sylvan</u>. If you already know Sylvan when you select this feat, you instead learn one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Languages|🗣️Language]] of your choice. You also gain Proficiency in <u>Nature</u>, or an <u>expertise die</u> in the skill, if you are already proficient.

**<u>Fey Cantrips:</u>** You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Druidcraft (Spell)|☄️Druidcraft]] cantrip and learn one other cantrip of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Sources/5e - Spell Source Primal/5e - Spell Source Primal|☄️Primal]] spell source. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Honeyed Words:</u>** When you _fail_ a <u>Deception</u> or <u>Persuasion</u> check, you can reroll the check, and you must use the new roll. <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

> [!info] Feat "Upgrades"
> Taking this Feat allows you, to take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Class Feats/5e - Fey Sentinel|🥇Fey Sentinel]] and/or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Class Feats/5e - Fey Tormentor|🥇Fey Tormentor]] Feats Later
