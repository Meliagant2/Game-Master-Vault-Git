# 🥇5e - Flurry🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

<u>Once on each of your turns</u> when you make an <u>weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] and have **ADV** on the roll, you can forgo **ADV** on that attack roll. After resolving that attack, you can then make another attack with the same <u>weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] against a different creature. The new target must be within <u>5 feet</u> of the first target and within the weapon's reach.
