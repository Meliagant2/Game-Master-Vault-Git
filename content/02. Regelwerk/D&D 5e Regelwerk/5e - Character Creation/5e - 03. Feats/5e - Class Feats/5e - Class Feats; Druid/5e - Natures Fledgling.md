# 🥇5e - Nature's Fledgling🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Druid |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Nature's Protection:</u>** You are constantly under the effect of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 2 Abjuration/5e - Barkskin (Spell)|☄️Barkskin]] spell.

**<u>Speak with Animals:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] spell prepared. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this spell (whichever is highest). You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]]. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have. When you cast this spell as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Ritual Casting|☄️Ritual]], its duration is <u>8 hours</u>.
