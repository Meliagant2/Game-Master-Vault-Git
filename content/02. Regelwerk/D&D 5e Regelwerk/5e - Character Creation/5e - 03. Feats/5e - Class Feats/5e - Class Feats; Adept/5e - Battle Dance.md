# 🥇5e - Battle Dance🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Dancer:</u>** You gain <u>Performance</u> proficiency. If you are already proficient in <u>Performance</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Battle Dances:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can spend `2` <u>Exertion</u> to start dancing for <u>1 minute</u>. You can only have one **Dance** active at the same time and you choose the dance out of the following options:

- **<u>Swift Dance:</u>** Your _Speed_ increases by <u>20 feet</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] against you are made with **DISADV**.
- **<u>Warding Dance:</u>** Your _AC_ increases by `+2` and you gain a `+1` bonus to <u>Saves</u>.
