# 🥇5e - Battle Dance🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Performance|💃Performance]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Battle Dances:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can spend `2` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Exertion|🤺Exertion]] to start dancing for <u>1 minute</u>. You can only have one **Dance** active at the same time and you choose the dance out of the following options:

- **<u>Swift Dance:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>20 feet</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]] against you are made with **DISADV**.
- **<u>Warding Dance:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] increases by `+2` and you gain a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].
