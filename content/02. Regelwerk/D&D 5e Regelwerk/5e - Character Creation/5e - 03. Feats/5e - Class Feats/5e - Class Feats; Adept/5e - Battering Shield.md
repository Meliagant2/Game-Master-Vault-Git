# 🥇5e - Battering Shield🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|10 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Your weapon attacks blend together so aggressively that your assault acts almost as a shield. When you hit a creature with <u>two</u> or more <u>melee attacks</u> in the same round, you gain a `+2` bonus to _AC_ against it until <u>the beginning of your next turn</u>.
