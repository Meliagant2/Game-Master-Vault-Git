# 🥇5e - Share Focus🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept |
> |**Repeatable**| |

As an **Action**, you can share your spiritual fortitude with others. Spend `3` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Exertion|🤺Exertion]] and choose a number of creatures you can _see_ within <u>10 feet</u> of you, up to a number equal to `your WIS`. For <u>1 hour</u>, you and the chosen creatures add your **Martial Arts die** to the first [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] they make on each of their turns.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
