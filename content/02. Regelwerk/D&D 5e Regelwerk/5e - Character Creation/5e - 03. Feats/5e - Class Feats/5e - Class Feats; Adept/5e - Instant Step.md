# 🥇5e - Instant Step🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|10 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept, Adept Speed |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You can move so fast you seem to teleport. You can use an **Action** and spend `4` <u>Exertion</u> to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 4 Conjuration/5e - Dimension Door (Spell)|☄️Dimension Door]]. In addition to the Spell effects, you become [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] until the beginning of your next turn.

The casting of this feat's spell does **not** count as a magical effect. It is considered to be a _Maneuver_ effect instead.
