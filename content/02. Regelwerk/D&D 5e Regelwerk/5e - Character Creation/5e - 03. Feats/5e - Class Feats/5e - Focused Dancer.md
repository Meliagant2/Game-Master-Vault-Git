# 🥇5e - Focused Dancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|10 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Adept, Battle Dance or Mystical Dance |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Dancer:</u>** You gain an <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Performance</u>.

**<u>Dancing Maneuver:</u>** When you use a **Bonus Action** to activate a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Maneuvers|🤺Combat Maneuver]], you can spend `2` <u>Exertion</u> (in addition to the normal cost of the **Dance**) to start one of your **Dances** as part of the same **Bonus Action**.

**<u>Last Dance:</u>** When the duration of one of your **Dances** ends, you can spend `2` <u>Exertion</u> to extend the duration for <u>3 turns</u> (**no action** required).

In addition, you can spend `3` <u>Exertion</u> (in addition to the normal cost of the **Dance**) to activate two **Dances** using the same **Bonus Action**.
