# 🥇5e - Bloodied Resilience🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Bloodied Reaction.</u>** While you're [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]] and take any damage, you can spend one of your <u>Hit Dice</u> as a **Reaction** and reduce the damage you take by `the amount rolled + CON`. You can only spend one <u>Hit Die</u> at a time in this way, each time you are damaged.

**<u>Bloodied Saves.</u>** If you succeed on a **STR** or **CON Save** that would normally cause you to take _half_ damage on a _**success**_, you take _no damage_ instead.
