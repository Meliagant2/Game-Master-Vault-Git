# 🥇5e - Bloodied Resilience🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Bloodied Reaction:</u>** While you're [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]] and take any damage, you can spend one of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] as a **Reaction** and reduce the damage you take by `the amount rolled + CON`. You can only spend one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]] at a time in this way, each time you are damaged.
