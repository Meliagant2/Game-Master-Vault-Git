# 🥇5e - Extreme Leap🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dash|⚔️Dash]] action and make a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Jumping|🏃‍♀️Long Jump]] after moving no less than <u>20 feet</u>, your jump distance is <u>doubled</u> for the turn. You may spend `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Exertion|🤺Exertion]] to <u>triple</u> your <u>jump distance</u> for the turn instead, and may move the full distance of your jump even if it exceeds your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]].
