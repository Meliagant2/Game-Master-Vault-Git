# 🥇5e - Combat Readiness🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

At <u>the start of your first turn in combat</u>, you gain <u>one</u> of the following benefits:

**<u>Advance:</u>** You move up to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]]. You have **ADV** on the next <u>weapon attack</u>, or the next <u>Acrobatics</u>, or <u>Athletics Check</u> you make until <u>the end of the combat</u>.

**<u>Fortify:</u>** You gain the benefits of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Dodge|⚔️Dodge]] action and have **ADV** on the next <u>Save</u> you make until <u>the end of the combat</u>.
