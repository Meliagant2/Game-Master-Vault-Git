You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Honed Instincts:</u>** When you _**fail**_ an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]], you can roll `1d6` and add the number rolled to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]], potentially turning it into a _success_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you use one of this feat's benefits, you can't use that benefit again until you roll **Initiative** or finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
