# 🥇5e - Bloody Resolve🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you take damage, you gain a `+1` bonus to <u>Saves</u> until <u>the end of your next turn</u>. While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]], the bonus to <u>Saves</u> increases to `+2`, and you also gain a `+1` bonus to <u>attack</u> and <u>damage rolls</u> until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.
