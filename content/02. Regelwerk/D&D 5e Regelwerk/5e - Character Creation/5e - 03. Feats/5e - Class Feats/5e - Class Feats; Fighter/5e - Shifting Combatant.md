You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Domino Strike:</u>** When you [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Pushing|🏃‍♀️Push]] a creature into a space occupied by a _Large or smaller_ creature, using a _Maneuver_ or a weapon's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Mastery Properties/5e - Push Mastery Property|🗡️Push]] mastery property to do so, you can force the creatures to collide. Each creature makes a **DEX Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d6` <u>Bludgeoning</u> damage.
