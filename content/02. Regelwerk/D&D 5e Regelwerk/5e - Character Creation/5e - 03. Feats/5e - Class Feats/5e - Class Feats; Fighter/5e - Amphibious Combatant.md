# 🥇5e - Amphibious Combatant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Aquatic Expert:</u>** You can spend `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Exertion|🤺Exertion]] to reroll a _failed_ <u>STR</u>, <u>DEX</u>, or <u>CON</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] related to aquatic activity.

**<u>Hold Breath:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CON Skills/5e - Hold Breath|💃Hold Breath]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_, and can spend `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Exertion|🤺Exertion]] to hold your breath for an additional minutes equal to `your PB`.

**<u>Swim Speed:</u>** You gain a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]] equal to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]]. If you already have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Swim Speed|🏃‍♀️Swim Speed]], you increase that by <u>10 feet</u> instead.
