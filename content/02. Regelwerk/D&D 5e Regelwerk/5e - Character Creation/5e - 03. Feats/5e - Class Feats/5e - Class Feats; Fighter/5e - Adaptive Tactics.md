# 🥇5e - Adaptive Tactics🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|6 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you roll for [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]], and at <u>the end of each of your turns</u>, you gain a `d8` **Tactical Die** if you don’t already have one. You can spend a **Tactical Die** to gain one of the following Tactics:

**<u>Assault:</u>** When you make a <u>weapon attack</u>, you can add the die to the Attack’s result.

**<u>Deflect:</u>** When you are Attacked, you can subtract the die from the Attack’s result.
