# 🥇5e - Great Performer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Additional Performances:</u>** You gain <u>one</u> additional use for both your **Bardic Inspiration** and **Bardic Performance** features.

**<u>Better Performance:</u>** Your Bardic Performance changes in the following ways:

- **<u>Battle Ballad:</u>** The die increases to `1d8`.
- **<u>Clarity:</u>** The die increases to `1d12`.
- **<u>Emotional:</u>** Creatures gain **ADV** on <u>Saves</u> against all of the listed conditions.
- **<u>Fast Tempo:</u>** The Speed now increases by <u>20 feet</u>.
