You can use soothing music or oration to help revitalize your wounded allies during a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]]. If you or any friendly creatures who can hear your performance regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] by spending [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] at the end of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]], each of those creatures regain extra [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `one roll of your Bardic Inspiration die` (This doesn't expend a use of **Bardic Inspiration**).
