# 🥇5e - Inspiring Inspiration🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Heroic Success:</u>** When a creature, that has one of your **Bardic Inspiration dice**, uses that die and _**succeeds**_ on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]], that creature adds `1d6` to their next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] they make before a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Horrible Failure:</u>** When a creature, that has one of your **Bardic Inspiration dice**, uses that die and _**fails**_ on the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]], that creature adds `2d6` to their next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] they make before a **Long Rest**.
