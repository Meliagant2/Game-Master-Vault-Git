# 🥇5e - Dual Masterpiece🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard, Visual Specialty |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you cast a bard spell with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] components, that you have already cast on your last turn, its spell level is increased by `1` for this casting only.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to `your CHA`, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short Rest**.
