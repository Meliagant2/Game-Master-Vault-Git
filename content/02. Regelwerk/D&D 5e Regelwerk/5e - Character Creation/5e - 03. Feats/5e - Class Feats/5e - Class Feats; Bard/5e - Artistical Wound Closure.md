# 🥇5e - Artistical Wound Closure🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you or an ally within <u>5 feet</u> of you receives healing of any kind, you may use your **Reaction** to expend one use of your **Bardic Inspiration** and provide extra healing equal to `2 x your Bardic die + CHA`.
