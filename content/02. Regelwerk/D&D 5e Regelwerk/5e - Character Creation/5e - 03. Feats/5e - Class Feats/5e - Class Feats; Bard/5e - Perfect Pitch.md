# 🥇5e - Perfect Pitch🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard, Wind Instrument Specialty |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When using a wind instrument as a spell focus, you can impose **DISADV** on the <u>Save</u> of one of your spells against one target of the spell.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to `your CHA`, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short Rest**.
