# 🥇5e - Marching Song🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

After finishing a **Short** or **Long Rest**, you can increase the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] of a number of creatures equal to `PB + your CHA` by <u>5 feet</u> for the next <u>8 Hours</u>. During that time you and every affected creature travels <u>2 kilometres per hour</u> faster than normal (7 km/h instead of 5 km/h). Your party cannot use [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Stealth|💃Stealth]] while traveling in this manner.
