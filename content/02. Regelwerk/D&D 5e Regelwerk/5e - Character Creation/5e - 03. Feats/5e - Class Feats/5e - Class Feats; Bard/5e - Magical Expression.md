# 🥇5e - Magical Expression🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You learn to express your art in a unique manner, granting you the ability to alter how you cast Spells. Choose the manner of your expression: **Visual** or **Auditory**.

**<u>Visual:</u>** Through acrobatics, dancing, juggling, painting, drawing, or miming, you can ignore the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]] Components of a Spell you cast, but you must provide a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] Component instead.

**<u>Auditory:</u>** Through singing, playing music, poetry, comedy, or storytelling, you can ignore the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]] Components of a Spell you cast, but you must provide a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]] Component instead.
