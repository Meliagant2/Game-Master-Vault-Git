# 🥇5e - Pluck the Heartstrings🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

Each creature that has a **Bardic Inspiration die** from you can use it for one of the following effects.

**<u>Defense:</u>** When the creature is reduced to <u>0 Hit Points</u> and not killed outright, the creature can roll the **Bardic Inspiration die** to be reduced to a number of Hit Points rolled on the **Bardic Inspiration die** instead.

**<u>Offense:</u>** Immediately after the creature hits a target with an attack roll, the creature can roll the **Bardic Inspiration die** and add the number rolled as extra <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage dealt by the attack.
