# 🥇5e - Magical Inspiration🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

If a creature has a **Bardic Inspiration die** from you and casts a spell that <u>restores hit points</u> or <u>deals damage</u>, the creature can roll that die and choose a target affected by the spell. Add the number rolled as a bonus to the hit points regained or the damage dealt. The Bardic Inspiration die is then lost.
