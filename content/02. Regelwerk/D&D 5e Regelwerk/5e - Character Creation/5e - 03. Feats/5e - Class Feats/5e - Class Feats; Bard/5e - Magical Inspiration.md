# 🥇5e - Magical Inspiration🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

If a creature has a **Bardic Inspiration die** from you and casts a spell that restores [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] or deals [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]], the creature can roll that die and choose a target affected by the spell. Add the number rolled as a bonus to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] regained or the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]] dealt. The **Bardic Inspiration die** is then lost.
