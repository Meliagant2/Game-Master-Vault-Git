# 🥇5e - Marching Song🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

After finishing a **Short** or **Long Rest**, you can increase the Speed of a number of creatures equal to `PB + your CHA` by <u>5 feet</u> for the next <u>8 Hours</u>. During that time you and every affected creature travels <u>2 kilometres per hour</u> faster than normal (7 km/h instead of 5 km/h). Your party cannot use <u>Stealth</u> while traveling in this manner.
