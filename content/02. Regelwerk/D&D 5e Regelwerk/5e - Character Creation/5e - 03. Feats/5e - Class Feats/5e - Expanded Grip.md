# 🥇5e - Expanded Grip🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>One-Handed Grip:</u>** When you use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Weapons/5e - Weapon Properties/5e - Versatile (Property)|🗡️Versatile]] weapon with one hand, the weapon deals the damage in parentheses when used to make a <u>melee attack</u>.

**<u>Zealous Grasp:</u>** If an effect forces you to drop what you are holding, you can make a **DC15 STR Save**. _**Success:**_ You don't drop what you are holding. When an effect forces you to make a <u>Save</u> to maintain your grip, you have **ADV** on the <u>Save</u>.
