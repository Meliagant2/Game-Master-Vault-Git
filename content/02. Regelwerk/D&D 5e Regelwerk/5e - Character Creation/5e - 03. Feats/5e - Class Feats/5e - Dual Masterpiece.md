# 🥇5e - Dual Masterpiece🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|8 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Bard, Visual Specialty |
> |**Repeatable**| |

When you cast a bard spell with Somatic components, that you have already cast on your last turn, its spell level is increased by `1` for this casting only.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to `your CHA`, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short Rest**.
