# 🥇5e - Familiar Keeper🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Wizard |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Find Familiar:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Find Familiar (Spell)|☄️Find Familiar]] spell prepared. Additionally, you can cast it as a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic Action]] without expending a spell slot or Material components. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest). <u>Once</u> you cast the spell using this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Expanded Options:</u>** When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Schools/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Find Familiar (Spell)|☄️Find Familiar]], you can choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Fiends/5e - Fiends; CR 1/5e - Imp|🐺Imp]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Dragons/5e - Dragons; CR 0,25/5e - Pseudodragon|🐺Pseudodragon]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Fiends/5e - Fiends; CR 1/5e - Quasit|🐺Quasit]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Celestials/5e - Celestials; CR 1/5e - Sphinx of Wonder|🐺Sphinx of Wonder]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Monster Rules/5e - Bestiary/5e - Creatures by Type/5e - Bestiary; Fey/5e - Fey; CR 0,25/5e - Sprite|🐺Sprite]]

**<u>Familiar Distraction:</u>** When a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of your familiar makes an <u>attack roll</u>, you can take a **Reaction** to command your familiar to give the attack **DISADV**.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
