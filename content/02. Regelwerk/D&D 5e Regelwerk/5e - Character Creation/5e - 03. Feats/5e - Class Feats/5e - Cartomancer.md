# 🥇5e - Cartomancer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Spellcaster Class |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Spellcasting Focus:</u>** You can use a card deck as your spellcasting focus.

**<u>Card Tricks:</u>** You learn the[[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Prestidigitation (Spell)|☄️Prestidigitation]] cantrip and can use it to create illusions that duplicate the effects of stage magic. When you use <u>Prestidigitation</u> in this way, you can conceal the <u>verbal</u> and <u>somatic</u> components of the spell as ordinary conversation and card handling.

**<u>Hidden Ace:</u>** When you finish a **Long Rest**, you can choose one spell from your class's spell list and imbue that spell into a card. The chosen spell must have a casting time of <u>1 action</u>, and it must be a level for which you have spell slots. The card remains imbued with this spell for <u>8 hours</u>. While the card is imbued with the spell, you can use a **Bonus Action** to flourish the card and cast the spell within. The card then immediately loses its magic.
