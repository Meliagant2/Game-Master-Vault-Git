# 🥇5e - Tactical Combatant🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Class |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Fighter |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Buffering Strike:</u>** When you hit a creature with a <u>weapon attack</u>, you can gain _Temporary Hit Points_ equal to the total number rolled on the weapon's damage dice.

**<u>Honed Instincts:</u>** When you fail an <u>ability check</u>, you can roll `1d6` and add the number rolled to the ability check, potentially turning it into a success.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** <u>Once</u> you use one of this feat's benefits, you can't use that benefit again until you roll **Initiative** or finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
