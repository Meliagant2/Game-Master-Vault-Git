You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Insight</u> and <u>Perception</u>.

**<u>Improved Sentinel:</u>** When you use the **Vigilant Guardian** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Sentinel|🥇Mark of Sentinel]] feat, you can also make one <u>weapon attack</u> or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] as part of that same **Reaction**.
