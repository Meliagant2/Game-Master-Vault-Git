You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Culture|💃Culture]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Fortune from the Many:</u>** If you fail a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]], you can take a **Reaction** to gain a bonus to the roll equal to the number of allies you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of you (maximum bonus of `+3`).

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
