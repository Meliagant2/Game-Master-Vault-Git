# 🥇5e - Face in the Crowd🥇

> | | |

> |**Prerequisite**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Sleight of Hand|💃Sleight of Hand]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Hidden in the crowd:</u>** If you enter or start your turn in a crowd of creatures with which you share a _creature type_, you are considered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Unseen|☠️Unseen]] to creatures that are not part of the crowd and gain **ADV** on any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] you make to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Hide|⚔️Hide]] in the crowd. You must stay within the crowd to retain this benefit, and it may end early if you take any **Actions** that would seem out of line with the crowd (such as bursting into song, casting a showy spell, or not participating in the crowd’s ritualistic chanting), as determined by the GM. If the crowd is targeted by an effect that requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you may choose to _fail_ it to remain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hidden|☠️Hidden]].
