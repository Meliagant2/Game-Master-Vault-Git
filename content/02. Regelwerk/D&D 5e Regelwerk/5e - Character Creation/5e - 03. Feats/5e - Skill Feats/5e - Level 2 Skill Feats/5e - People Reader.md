# 🥇5e - People Reader🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|WIS +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Insight|💃Insight]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Pattern Recognition:</u>** You notice patterns others cannot. As an **Action**, you can study a creature you have watched _speak_ for at least <u>1 minute</u>. Make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Insight|💃Insight]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] against the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Deception|🎲Deception]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]]. _**Success:**_ You can ask the Game Master <u>one</u> of the following questions, or think of a similar one, if the GM allows you to. The GM must give you a truthful answer, but a creature shielded magically against mind reading or similar effects is unaffected by this feature. _**Success (each 5):**_ You can ask <u>one</u> additional question.

- Is the creature hiding any strong emotions?
- Does the creature have a secret agenda?
- Did the creature lie in the last minute?
- Is the creature hiding their true identity, heritage and/or culture?

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
