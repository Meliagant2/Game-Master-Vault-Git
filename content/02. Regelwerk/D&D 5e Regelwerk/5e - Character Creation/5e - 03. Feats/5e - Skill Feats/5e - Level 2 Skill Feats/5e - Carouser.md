You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CON Skills/5e - Revelry|💃Revelry]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Alcohol Tolerance:</u>** The amount of alcoholic drinks you have to consume, before needing to make [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CON Skills/5e - Revelry|💃Revelry]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]] against becoming [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Drunk|☠️Drunk]], is increased by `1`. Also, each effect on the **Drunkeness Level Table**, that would apply **DISADV** to any of your rolls, you instead gain a penalty on those rolls equal to `your Drunkeness level`. Lastly you never suffer the effects of _level 6_ **Drunkeness**.
