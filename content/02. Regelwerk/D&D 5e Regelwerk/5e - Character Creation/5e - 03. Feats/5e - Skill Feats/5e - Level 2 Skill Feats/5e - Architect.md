# 🥇5e - Architect🥇

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Engineering|💃Engineering]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Calculate Statics:</u>** As a **Bonus Action** you identify the weight-bearing wall or pillars of a structure. Make a **DC15** [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Engineering|💃Engineering]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]]. _**Success:**_ You deal _double damage_ against it with your next <u>weapon attack</u>. Alternatively, you can _double_ the hit points a structure regains from the next **Action** you or an ally take to repair it. _**Failure:**_ The **DC** to analyze this structure again increases by `5`.
