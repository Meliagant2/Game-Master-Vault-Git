You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Engineering|💃Engineering]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Precise Calculations:</u>** When another creature you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> makes an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]], you can take a **Reaction** to either _add_ or _substract_ `1d4` to or from the creature's <u>Check</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
