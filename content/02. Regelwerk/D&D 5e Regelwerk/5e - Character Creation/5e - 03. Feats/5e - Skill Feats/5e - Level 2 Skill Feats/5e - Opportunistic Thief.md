You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Sleight of Hand|💃Sleight of Hand]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Quick Concealment:</u>** Whenever you successfully use your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Sleight of Hand|💃Sleight of Hand]] outside of combat to steal an object, you may immediately conceal it flawlessly on your person, or put another object you possess in its place.
