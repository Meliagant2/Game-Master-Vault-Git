# 🥇5e - Linguist🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**|x |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Language Proficiency:</u>** You gain <u>six</u> **Language Mastery Levels** to put into [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Languages|🗣️Languages]] of your choice.

**<u>Multilingual Persuasion:</u>** Whenever you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Influence|⚔️Influence]] action, while talking to a creature in their native language, you gain a bonus to any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Check]] you make against that creature equal to `2 + your Language Mastery Level in that language`.
