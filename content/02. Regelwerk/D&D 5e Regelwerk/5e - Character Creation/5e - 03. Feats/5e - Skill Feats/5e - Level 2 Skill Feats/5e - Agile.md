# 🥇5e - Agile🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|

> |**Level Requirement**|2 |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Acrobatics|💃Acrobatics]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Incredible Agility:</u>** Your reflexes and agility allow you to move with a burst of speed. When you move on your turn, you can _double_ your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] until <u>the end of the turn</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you use this trait, you can't use it again until you move <u>0 feet</u> on one of your turns.
