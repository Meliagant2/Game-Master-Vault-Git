# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Passage🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Passage |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Acrobatics</u> and <u>Athletics</u>. 

**<u>Improved Passage:</u>** When you use the **Magical Passage** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Passage|🥇Mark of Passage]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] without a spell slot, you can also choose up to two willing creatures you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself before you teleport. Each target can then take a **Reaction** to also teleport up to <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space it can see.
