# 🥇5e - Light Bringer🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Sacred Magic:</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Light (Spell)|☄️Light]] spell and can cast it without Material components. If you already know that cantrip, you learn a different [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Lists/5e - Spell List Divine/5e - Spell List Divine|☄️Divine]] cantrip of your choice. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

**<u>Solar Luminance:</u>** When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Evocation/5e - Level 0 Evocation/5e - Light (Spell)|☄️Light]], you can have the light from the spell be <u>sunlight</u>. <u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

**<u>Sun's Healing:</u>** As a **Bonus Action** while within sunlight, you can expend one of your <u>Hit Point Dice</u>, roll the die, and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll.

<u>Once</u> you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.
