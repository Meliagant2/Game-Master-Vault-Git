# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Hospitality🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Hospitality |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Persuasion</u>, <u>Brewer's Supplies</u> and <u>Cook's Utensils</u>.

**<u>Improved Hospitality:</u>** When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 1 Transmutation/5e - Purify Food and Drink (Spell)|☄️Purify Food and Drink]], you can modify the spell so that instead of its normal effect, each creature of your choice within <u>30 feet</u> of you is refreshed. Each affected creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] level is reduced by `1`, and the creature gains [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `your PB + your INT/WIS/CHA (whichever is highest)`. <u>Once</u> you modify the spell with this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
