# 🥇5e - Poisoner🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Potent Poison:</u>** When you make a damage roll that deals <u>Poison</u> damage, it ignores <u>Resistance</u> to Poison damage.

**<u>Brew Poison:</u>** You gain proficiency with the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Tools/5e - Misc Tools/5e - Poisoners Kit|🛠️Poisoners Kit]], or an <u>expertise die</u>, if you are already proficient. With <u>1 hour</u> of work using such a kit, you can create a number of <u>poison doses</u> equal to `your PB`. As a **Bonus Action**, you can apply a poison dose to a weapon or piece of ammunition. Once applied, the poison retains its potency for <u>1 minute</u> or until you deal damage with the poisoned item, whichever is shorter. When a creature takes damage from the poisoned item, that creature makes a **CON Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The Target takes `2d8` <u>Poison</u> damage and has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Poisoned|☠️Poisoned]] condition until the <u>end of your next turn</u>. _**Success:**_ The Creature takes half the damage only.
