# 🥇5e - Religious Training🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Religion Proficiency:</u>** You gain <u>Religion</u> proficiency. If you are already proficient in <u>Religion</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Religious Information:</u>** When you are in contact with a holy relic, religious artifact, or any kind of similar object or structure, you can spend either `2` <u>Exertion</u> or a <u>1st-level</u> or higher Spell Slot to learn something about it. You receive a correct answer from the Game Master for a question about the object that can be answered with a _yes_ or _no_.
