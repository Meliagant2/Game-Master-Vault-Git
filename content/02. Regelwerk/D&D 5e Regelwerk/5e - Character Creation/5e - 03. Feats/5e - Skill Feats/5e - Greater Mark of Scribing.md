# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Scribing🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Scribing |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in <u>History</u> and with <u>Calligrapher's Supplies</u>.

**<u>Inspired Scribing:</u>** When you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Comprehend Languages (Spell)|☄️Comprehend Languages]], you can modify the spell to encompass up to <u>three</u> willing creatures you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. Each chosen creature also gains the benefits of the spell for the duration. In addition, for the duration of the spell, you and the chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with each other while within <u>2 km</u> of each other. <u>Once</u> you modify the spell with this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a **Long Rest**.
