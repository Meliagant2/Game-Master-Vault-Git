You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Arcana</u> and all <u>Artisan's Tools</u>.

**<u>Improved Making:</u>** When you use the **Spellsmith** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Making|🥇Mark of Making]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 2 Transmutation/5e - Magic Weapon (Spell)|☄️Magic Weapon]] without a spell slot, you cast the spell as its _level 3_ version.
