# 🥇5e - Showmanship🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Expert Performer.</u>** You gain <u>Performance</u> proficiency. If you are already proficient in <u>Performance</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Taunt.</u>** You can take a **Bonus Action** to mock a creature within <u>15 feet</u> of yourself. If the target can _hear_ you (though it need not understand you), it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] on its next <u>attack roll</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
