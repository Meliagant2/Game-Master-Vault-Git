# 🥇5e - Keen Mind🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|INT +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Lore Knowledge:</u>** Choose one of the following skills: <u>Arcana</u>, <u>Culture</u>, <u>Engineering</u>, <u>History</u>, <u>Investigation</u>, <u>Nature</u>, or <u>Religion</u>. If you lack proficiency in the chosen skill, you gain proficiency in it, and if you already have proficiency in it, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> in it.

**<u>Quick Study:</u>** You can take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Study|⚔️Study]] action as a **Bonus Action**.
