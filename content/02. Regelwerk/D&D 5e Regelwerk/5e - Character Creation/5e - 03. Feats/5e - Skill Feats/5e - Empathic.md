You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Reading like a book:</u>** You gain <u>Insight</u> proficiency. If you are already proficient in <u>Insight</u>, you gain an <u>expertise die</u> instead.

**<u>Social Solidity:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Influence|⚔️Influence]] action, you treat a roll of `7` or lower on the `d20` as an `8`.
