# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Shadow🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in both <u>Performance</u> and <u>Stealth</u>.

**<u>Improved Shadow:</u>** When you use the **Shape Shadows** benefit of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Mark of Shadow|🥇Mark of Shadow]] feat to cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 2 Illusion/5e - Invisibility (Spell)|☄️Invisibility]] without a spell slot, you cast the spell as its _level 3_ version.
