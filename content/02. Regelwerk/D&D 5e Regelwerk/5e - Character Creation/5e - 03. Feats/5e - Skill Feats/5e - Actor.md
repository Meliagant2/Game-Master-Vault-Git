# 🥇5e - Actor🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|CHA +1 |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Impersonation:</u>** While you're disguised as a real or fictional person, you have **ADV** on <u>Deception</u> or <u>Performance</u> checks to convince others that you are that person.

**<u>Mimicry:</u>** You can mimic the sounds of other creatures, including speech. A creature that hears the mimicry makes a contested **Insight Check** against your **Deception** or **Performance Check** (determined by the GM). _**Failure:**_ The Creature doesn't determine the effect is faked.
