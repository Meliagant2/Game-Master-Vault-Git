# 🥇5e - Dungeon Delver🥇

> [!wikibox]

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Sensing the unknown:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Search|⚔️Search]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Study|⚔️Study]] action to detect secret doors or traps, you have **ADV** on that check.

**<u>Used to traps:</u>** You have **ADV** on <u>Saves</u> made to avoid or resist traps. You also have <u>Resistance</u> to the damage dealt by traps.

**<u>Running from danger:</u>** Traveling at a fast pace doesn't impose the normal `-5` penalty on your **passive Perception** score.
