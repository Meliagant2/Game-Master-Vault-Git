# 🥇5e - Medic🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|2 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Healer Origin Feat |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Great Physician:</u>** When you use the **Physician** Feature of your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 03. Feats/5e - Origin Feats/5e - Healer|🥇Healer]] Feat, instead of expending a <u>Hit Point Die</u>, your Target regains a number of <u>Hit Points</u> equal to `1d6 + your PB + your level`.

**<u>Rest Patch up:</u>** During a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]], you can clean and bind the wounds of a number of <u>Beasts</u> and <u>Humanoids</u> equal to `your PB + your INT/WIS (whichever is higher)`. Expend a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Adventuring Gear/5e - Healers Kit|🪓Healers Kit]] use and make a **DC15 Medicine** check. _**Success:**_ If a creature spends a <u>Hit Die</u> during this rest, that creature can forgo the roll and instead regain the <u>maximum</u> number of hit points the die can restore. A creature can do so only once per rest, regardless of how many Hit Dice it spends.
