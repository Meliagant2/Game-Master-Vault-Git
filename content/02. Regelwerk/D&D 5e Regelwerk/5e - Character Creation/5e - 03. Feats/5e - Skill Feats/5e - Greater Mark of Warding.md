# 🥇5e - Greater Mark of Warding🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**|Mark of Warding |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Improved Intuition:</u>** You gain another <u>expertise die</u> in <u>Investigation</u> and with <u>Thieves' Tools</u>.

**<u>Improved Warding:</u>** When a creature makes an attack roll against you or a creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself, you can take a **Reaction** to impose **DISADV** on that roll. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
