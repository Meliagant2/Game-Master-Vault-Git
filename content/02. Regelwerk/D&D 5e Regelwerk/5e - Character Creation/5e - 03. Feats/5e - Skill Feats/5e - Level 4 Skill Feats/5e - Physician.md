# 🥇5e - Physician🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - INT Skills/5e - Medicine|💃Medicine]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Consistent Healing:</u>** Whenever you roll a die to determine the number of _Hit Points_ you restore to a creature, you can treat any `1` or `2` on a die as a `3`.

**<u>Healing Touch:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Abjuration/5e - Level 1 Abjuration/5e - Cure Wounds (Spell)|☄️Cure Wounds]] spell prepared. You can cast it <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).

**<u>Rest Patch up:</u>** During a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Short Rest|😴Short Rest]], you can clean and bind the wounds of up to <u>6</u> creatures. Expend a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Adventuring Gear/5e - Healers Kit|🪓Healers Kit]] use and make a **DC15 Medicine** check for each creature. _**Success:**_ If a creature spends any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Dice]] during this rest, it regains additional _Hit Points_ equal to `your Medicine Check bonus` for every [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Dice|💖Hit Die]] it spends.
