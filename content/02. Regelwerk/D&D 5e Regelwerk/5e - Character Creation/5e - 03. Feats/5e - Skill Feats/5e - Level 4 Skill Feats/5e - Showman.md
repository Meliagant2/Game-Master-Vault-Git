You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Performance|💃Performance]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Taunt.</u>** You can take a **Bonus Action** to mock a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. If the target can _hear_ you (though it need not understand you), it is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Hindered|☠️Hindered]] on its next <u>attack roll</u>.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your PB</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.
