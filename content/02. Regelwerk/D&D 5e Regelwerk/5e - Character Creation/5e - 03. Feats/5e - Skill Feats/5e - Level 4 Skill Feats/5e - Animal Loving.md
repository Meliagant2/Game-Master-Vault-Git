# 🥇5e - Animal Loving🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Nature|💃Nature]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Friend to Animals:</u>** You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Checks]] when taking the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Influence|⚔️Influence]] action with _Beasts_.

**<u>Two Hearts, One Mind:</u>** You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Speak with Animals (Spell)|☄️Speak with Animals]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Divination/5e - Level 2 Divination/5e - Beast Sense (Spell)|☄️Beast Sense]] spells prepared. You can cast each of them <u>once</u> without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**. When you cast one of the spells without a spell slot using this feature, it doesn't require [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]]. You can also cast the spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (whichever is highest).
