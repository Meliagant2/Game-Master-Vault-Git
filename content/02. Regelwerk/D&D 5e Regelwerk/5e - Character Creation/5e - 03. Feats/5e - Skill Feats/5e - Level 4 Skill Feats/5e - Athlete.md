You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Athletics|💃Athletics]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Jumping|🏃‍♀️Jump]] after moving only <u>5 feet</u>. You also have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Checks]] to _jump_.
