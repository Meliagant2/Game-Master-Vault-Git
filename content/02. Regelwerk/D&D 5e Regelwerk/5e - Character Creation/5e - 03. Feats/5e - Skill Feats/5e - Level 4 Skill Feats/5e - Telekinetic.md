# 🥇5e - Telekinetic🥇

> [!wikibox]
> | | |
> |---|---|
> |**Category**|Skill |
> |**Level Requirement**|4 |
> |**Prerequisite**| |
> |**Repeatable**| |

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Skill Proficiency:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]] in <u>one</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]] of your choice, or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

**<u>Minor Telekinesis.</u>** You learn the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Conjuration/5e - Level 0 Conjuration/5e - Mage Hand (Spell)|☄️Mage Hand]] spell. You can cast it without <u>Verbal</u> or <u>Somatic</u> components, you can make the spectral hand [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]], and its range and the distance it can be away from you both increase by <u>30 feet</u> when you cast it. The spell's spellcasting ability is <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> (whichever is highest).

**<u>Telekinetic Shove.</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can telekinetically shove one creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. When you do so, the target makes a **STR Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Save DC|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The Target is moved <u>5 feet</u> toward or away from you.
