# 🦸‍♀️(Legacy) 5e - Oath of the Crown🦸‍♀️

The Oath of the Crown is sworn to the ideals of civilization, be it the spirit of a nation, fealty to a sovereign, or service to a deity of law and rulership. The paladins who swear this oath dedicate themselves to serving society and, in particular, the just laws that hold society together. These paladins are the watchful guardians on the walls, standing against the chaotic tides of barbarism that threaten to tear down all that civilization has built, and are commonly known as guardians, exemplars, or sentinels. Often, paladins who swear this oath are members of an order of knighthood in service to a nation or a sovereign, and undergo their oath as part of their admission to the order’s ranks.

> [!lore] TENETS OF THE CROWN
> The tenets of the Oath of the Crown are often set by the sovereign to which their oath is sworn, but generally emphasize the following tenets.
> **<u>Law:</u>** The law is paramount. It is the mortar that holds the stones of civilization together, and it must be respected.
> **<u>Loyalty:</u>** Your word is your bond. Without loyalty, oaths and laws are meaningless.
> **<u>Courage:</u>** You must be willing to do what needs to be done for the sake of order, even in the face of overwhelming odds. If you don’t act, then who will?
> **<u>Responsibility:</u>** You must deal with the consequences of your actions, and you are responsible for fulfilling your duties and obligations.

### Level 3: Champion Challenge

As a **Bonus Action**, you use expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** and issue a challenge that compels other creatures to do battle with you. Each creature of your choice that you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you must make a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature can’t willingly move more than <u>30 feet</u> away from you. This effect ends on the creature if you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] or die or if the creature is more than <u>30 feet</u> away from you.

### Level 3: Turn the Tide

As a **Bonus Action**, you can bolster injured creatures with your **Channel Divinity**. Each creature of your choice that can hear you within <u>30 feet</u> of you regains <u>hit points</u> equal to `(PB + CHA) x 1d6`.

### Level 6: Divine Allegiance

When a creature within <u>10 feet</u> of you takes damage, you can use your **Reaction** to magically substitute your own health for that of the target creature, causing that creature not to take the damage. Instead, you take the damage. This damage to you can’t be reduced or prevented in any way.

### Level 9: Spirit of a Warrior

You trained to perfection. When you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]] at the end of your turn, you regain <u>hit points</u> equal to `1/2 x your paladin level + CHA`.

### Level 13: Unyielding Spirit

You have **ADV** on <u>Saves</u> to avoid becoming [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|☠️Paralyzed]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Stunned|☠️Stunned]].

You also gain proficiency in <u>INT saving throws</u>.

### Level 17: Exalted Champion

Your presence on the field of battle is an inspiration to those dedicated to your cause. As a **Bonus Action**, you can magically gain the following benefits for <u>1 hour</u>:

- You have <u>resistance</u> to <u>bludgeoning</u>, <u>piercing</u>, and <u>slashing</u> damage.
- You and your allies have **ADV** on <u>death saving throws</u> while within <u>30 feet</u> of you.
- You have **ADV** on <u>WIS Saves</u>, as do your allies within <u>30 feet</u> of you.

<u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a <u>5th-level spell slot</u> (**no action** required).
