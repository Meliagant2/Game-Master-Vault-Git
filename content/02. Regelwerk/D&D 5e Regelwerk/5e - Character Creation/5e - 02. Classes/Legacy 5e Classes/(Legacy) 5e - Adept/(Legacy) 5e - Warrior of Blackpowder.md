# 🦸‍♀️(Legacy) 5e - Warrior of Blackpowder🦸‍♀️

This order’s teachings requires them to seek out battle to master their visceral mortal fear, and thus free themselves from the burden of any fears.

Adepts of the Way of the Blackpowder can dodge and even catch bullets, curve their shots around barriers, and perform other supernatural feats with firearms.

Due to their devotion to confronting fear of the unknown, nearly every student undertakes a journey into foreign lands, and tales of wandering elvish gunslingers are popular. A few such adepts have shared their enlightened mastery.

### Level 3: Gun Vidya

You gain proficiency in all <u>firearms</u>. They count as adept weapons for you. If you have the **Stunning Strike** ability, you can use it through firearms attacks. You also gain the following benefits:

- Being within <u>5 feet</u> of a hostile creature doesn’t impose **DISADV** on your ranged attack rolls with firearms.
- You ignore the <u>loading</u> property for firearms.
- You are proficient with <u>Tinker's tools</u>, and can craft firearms and firearm enhancements.

### Level 6: One with the Gun

When you miss with an attack roll you make for one firearm weapon attack, you can spend <u>1 to 3 Exertion</u> to increase your attack roll by <u>2</u> for each of these <u>Exertion</u> you spend, potentially turning the miss into a hit.

Additionally, when you hit a target with a firearm, you can spend <u>1 exertion</u> to cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target equal to your <u>Martial Arts die</u>. You can use this feature only once on each of your turns.

### Level 9: The Art of the Draw

You can almost always shoot first. If you have a holstered pistol, whenever a creature you’re aware of starts their turn you can spend <u>1 exertion</u> to draw and make one **Attack** with that holstered firearm against that creature. This attack deals extra damage equal to your <u>Martial Arts die</u>.

When you use this ability, you cannot spend an extra exertion to use the ability **One with the Gun** to add your Martial Arts die to this damage twice.

You can use this ability even if you haven’t acted in combat yet. After you use this ability, you cannot use it again until you complete a **short** or **long rest**.

### Level 13: Quickdraw

You add your <u>WIS</u> to your <u>initiative</u>. You can also stow a firearm, then draw another firearm as a single object interaction on your turn.

### Level 17: Mystic Bullets

While you are wielding a firearm, as an **Action**, you can fire a single bullet that curves towards your enemies, even those behind barriers and those you are unaware of. As long as the bullet can trace a path to an enemy, the path is unblocked by barriers more substantial than a window, and the path remains exclusively within <u>60 feet of you</u>, the bullet can reach the enemy. Each enemy the bullet can reach must make a **DEX Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Target takes <u>10d6</u> force damage. _**Success:**_ The Target takes half damage.

After you use this ability, you cannot use it again until you complete a **short** or **long rest**.
