# 🦸‍♀️(Legacy) 5e - Warrior of Pugulism🦸‍♀️

Most adepts find intellectual and spiritual knowledge an important part of their studies—that’s not the case for pugulists. These adepts are only concerned about fighting and winning, which not only makes them tougher than their peers but also more versatile and unpredictable.

### Level 3: Unorthodox Arsenal

Everything can be a weapon in your hands. You are proficient with <u>improvised weapons</u>. When you hit a target with an improvised weapon, you can spend <u>1 exertion</u> to deal additional damage equal to your <u>Martial Arts die</u> or use a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Traditions/5e - Basic Maneuvers/5e - Basic Maneuvers|🤺Basic Maneuver]] against the same target.

### Level 6: No Sell

When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your **Reaction** to spend <u>1 exertion</u> and reduce the impact. After damage is determined (including any resistances you might have), roll your <u>Martial Arts die</u>. Reduce the damage suffered by that amount.

### Level 9: Unpredictable Style

The unpredictability of your combat style bleeds over to all aspects of your life. You can approach problems from unforeseen angles, using somewhat brutal solutions that get the job done swifty (if messily). When you make any <u>ability check</u> that could use a <u>skill</u> or <u>tool</u> you are not proficient with, you can roll your <u>Martial Arts die</u> and add it to that check. On a success, you can’t use this feature with the same ability until you finish a **short** or **long rest**. On a failure, you fall [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]] and roll your <u>Martial Arts die</u>, taking damage equal to that amount.

### Level 13: Brutish Force

Whenever you make a **Save**, you add one <u>expertise die</u> to that roll. If applying this bonus to a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Save]], increasing the total to <u>20</u> or higher, you gain the benefits of rolling a <u>20 </u>on the <u>d20</u>.

### Level 17: Unrelenting

When you are dropped to 0 hit points, roll your <u>Martial Arts die</u>. You recover that many <u>hit points</u> and lose that much <u>exertion</u>.

When your exertion pool reaches 0, roll your <u>Martial Arts die</u>. You recover that much <u>exertion</u> and lose that many <u>hit points. </u>

Once you have gained either <u>exertion</u> points or <u>hit points</u> from this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **short** or **long rest**.
