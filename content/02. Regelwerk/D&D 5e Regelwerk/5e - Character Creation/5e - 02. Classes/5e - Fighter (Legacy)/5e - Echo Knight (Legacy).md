# 🦸‍♀️5e - Echo Knight🦸‍♀️

A mysterious and feared frontline warrior, the Echo Knight has mastered the art of using magic powers to summon the fading shades of unrealized timelines to aid them in battle. Surrounded by echoes of their own might, they charge into the fray as a cycling swarm of shadows and strikes.

### Level 3: Manifest Echo

You can use a **Bonus Action** to magically manifest an echo of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within <u>15 feet</u> of you. This echo is a magical, translucent, gray image of you. The Echo shares your Initiative in combat, but it cannot act on its own. You can telepathically command it to move on your turn, but if you end your turn and your Shade is more than <u>30 feet</u> away from you it is instantly destroyed.

Your Echo lasts until it is destroyed, or you use your **Bonus Action** to dismiss it, or you conjure another Echo.

<p><strong><em>Shade Strike:</em></strong> Whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, you can choose for any number of your attacks to originate from your Shade instead of you. These attacks deal damage as if you had attacked the target, and can use any maneuvers you know, as normal.</p>

### Level 3: Unleash Incarnation

You can heighten your echo’s fury. Whenever you take the **Attack action**, you can make one additional melee attack from the echo’s position.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your <u>CON modifier</u> (a minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**. If you have no uses left, you can expend `2 exertion` to use this feature again.

### Level 6: Echo Avatar

You can temporarily transfer your consciousness to your echo. As an **Action**, you can see through your echo’s eyes and hear through its ears. During this time, you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Deafened|☠️Deafened]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]]. You can sustain this effect for up to <u>10 minutes</u>, and you can end it at any time (requires no action). While your echo is being used in this way, it can be up to <u>1,000 feet</u> away from you without being destroyed.

### Level 9: Shadow martyr

You can make your echo throw itself in front of an attack directed at another creature that you can see. Before the attack roll is made, you can use your **Reaction** to teleport the echo to an unoccupied space within <u>5 feet</u> of the targeted creature. The attack roll that triggered the reaction is instead made against your echo.

<u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **short** or **long rest**.

### Level 13: Reclaim Potential

You’ve learned to absorb the fleeting magic of your echo. When an echo of yours is destroyed by taking damage, you can gain a number of <u>temporary hit points</u> equal to `1d10 + CON`, provided you don’t already have temporary hit points.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your <u>CON modifier</u> (a minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

### Level 17: Legion of One

You can use a **Bonus Action** to create two echoes with your **Manifest Echo** feature, and these echoes can coexist. If you try to create a third echo, the previous two echoes are destroyed. Anything you can do from one echo’s position can be done from the other’s instead.

In addition, when you roll <u>initiative</u>, you regain one use of your **Unleash Incarnation** feature.
