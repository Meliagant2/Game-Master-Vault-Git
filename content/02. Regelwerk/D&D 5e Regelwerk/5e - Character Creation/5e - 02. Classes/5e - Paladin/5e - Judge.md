# 🦸‍♀️5e - Judge🦸‍♀️

Judges are sworn to the ideals of civilization, be it the spirit of a nation, fealty to a sovereign, or service to a deity of law and rulership. The paladins who swear this oath dedicate themselves to serving society and, in particular, the just laws that hold society together. These paladins are the watchful guardians on the walls, standing against the chaotic tides of barbarism that threaten to tear down all that civilization has built, and are commonly known as guardians, exemplars, or sentinels. Often, paladins who swear this oath are members of an order of knighthood in service to a nation or a sovereign, and undergo their oath as part of their admission to the order’s ranks.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Guided Strike:</u>** When you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] and _miss_, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to gain a `+10` bonus to the roll, potentially turning it into a _hit_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of Shielding

Choose one of the following damage types: <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Poison</u>, <u>Psychic</u>, or <u>Thunder</u>. You and your allies have <u>Resistance</u> to that damage type while in your **Sacred Aura**.

At <u>the start of each of your turns</u>, you can change the damage type affected by this feature to one of the other listed options (**no Action** required).

### Level 11: Unstoppable Judgment

You do not tolerate those who would stand in the way of you. <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a _Large or smaller_ creature with a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], you can force them to make a **STR Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Prone|☠️Prone]].

### Level 15: Rebuke

When you are _hit_ by an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you can take a **Reaction** to halve the attack's damage against yourself (round down) and force the attacker to make a **DEX Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The attacker takes damage equal to `2d10 + your CHA` of one of the following types (your choice): <u>Acid</u>, <u>Cold</u>, <u>Fire</u>, <u>Lightning</u>, <u>Poison</u>, <u>Psychic</u>, or <u>Thunder</u>. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your CHA</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 19: Noble Judge

As a **Bonus Action**, you gain the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required):

**<u>Flight:</u>** You have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>60 feet</u>, and can hover.

**<u>Restore Balance:</u>** When you or an ally in your **Sacred Aura** _fails_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]], you can take a **Reaction** to make the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] _succeed_ instead.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
