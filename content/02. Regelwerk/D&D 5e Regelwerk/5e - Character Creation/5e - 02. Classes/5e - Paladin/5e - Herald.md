# 🦸‍♀️5e - Herald🦸‍♀️

Heralds follow their ideals of justice and order. These Paladins meet the archetype of the knight in shining armor. They hold themselves to the highest standards of conduct, and some—for better or worse—hold the rest of the world to the same standards.

Many who swear this oath are devoted to gods of law and good and use their gods' tenets as the measure of personal devotion. Others hold angels as their ideals and incorporate images of angelic wings into their helmets or coats of arms.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Sacred Weapon:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to imbue one <u>Melee</u> weapon that you are holding with positive energy. For <u>10 minutes</u> or until you use this feature again, you add `your CHA` to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] you make with that weapon (minimum bonus of `+1`), and each time you _**hit**_ with it, you cause it to deal its normal damage type or <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (your Choice).

The weapon also emits [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>20-foot radius</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] <u>20 feet</u> beyond that.

You can end this effect early (**no Action** required). This effect also ends if you aren't carrying the weapon.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of Devotion

You and your allies have <u>Immunity</u> to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition while in your **Sacred Aura**. If a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] ally enters the aura, that condition has no effect on that ally while there.

### Level 11: Sundering Strike

<u>Once per turn</u>, when you deal <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage to a creature, that creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Heavily Wounded|☠️Heavily Wounded]] until <u>the beginning of your next turn</u>.

### Level 15: Smite of Protection

Your magical smite now radiates protective energy. Whenever you use **Paladin's Smite**, you and your allies have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Cover/5e - Half Cover|🏔️Half Cover]] while in your **Sacred Aura**. The aura has this benefit until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

### Level 19: Holy Nimbus

As a **Bonus Action**, you can imbue your **Sacred Aura** with holy power, granting the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).

**<u>Holy Ward:</u>** You have **ADV** on any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] you are forced to make by a _Celestial_, _Fiend_, or _Undead_.

**<u>Radiant Damage:</u>** Whenever an enemy starts its turn in the aura, that creature takes <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (your Choice) equal to `your CHA + your PB`.

**<u>Sunlight:</u>** The aura is filled with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] that is _sunlight_.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
