# 🦸‍♀️5e - Blackguard🦸‍♀️

Blackguards are Paladins who indulge their own ambitions. They wander the wilds as lost warriors, often becoming bandits and mercenaries. Some quest for purpose, seeking to renew their Oath, while others fall into despair. Blackguards often adorn their armor and weapons with dread accoutrements or are marked by eerie phenomena.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Frightening Presence:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to choose a creature you can see within <u>10 feet</u> of you. The target makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition for <u>1 minute</u>. The condition ends early if the target takes damage or if you use this **Bonus Action** again.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of Terror

A creature that is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] by you has a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] of `0`, while within your **Sacred Aura** and it takes <u>Psychic</u> damage equal to `1/2 x your Paladin level`, if it starts its turn there.

### Level 11: Fell Smite

When you use your **Paladin's Smite** and deal damage to a creature that has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition, you can end that condition on the creature and give it the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Paralyzed|5e - Paralyzed]] condition for <u>1 minute</u>. To end the condition, the target makes a **repeated WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] at <u>the end of each of its turns</u>. The effect also ends if the creature takes any damage.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your CHA</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 19: Invincible Blackguard

As a **Bonus Action**, you gain the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).:

**<u>Critical Threshold:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attacks]] and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strikes]] score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] on a roll of `19 or 20` on the `d20`. If you already have a feature that increases your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] range, you increase it by `1` instead (maximum `17-20`).

**<u>Extra Attack:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action on your turn, you can make one additional [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] as part of that **Action**.

**<u>Resistance:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
