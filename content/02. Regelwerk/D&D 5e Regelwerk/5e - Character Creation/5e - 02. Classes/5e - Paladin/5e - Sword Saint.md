# 🦸‍♀️5e - Sword Saint🦸‍♀️

Sword Saints steel their combat prowess on a daily basis. They are often swown to punishing those who have committed grievously evil acts. When armies slaughter helpless villagers, when a tyrant defies the will of the gods, when a thieves' guild grows too violent, when a dragon rampages through the countryside—at times like these, Sword Saints arise and attempt to set right what has gone wrong.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Vow of Enmity:</u>** When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to utter a **Vow of Enmity** against a creature you can _see_ within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. You have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against the creature for <u>1 minute</u> or until you use this feature again.

If the creature drops to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] before the vow ends, you can transfer the vow to a different creature within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself (**no Action** required).

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of Alacrity

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Speed Increase:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>10 feet</u>.

**<u>Quicken Allies:</u>** Whenever an ally enters your **Sacred Aura** for the first time on a turn or starts their turn there, the ally's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] increases by <u>10 feet</u> until <u>the end of their next turn</u>.

### Level 11: Relentless Avenger

Your supernatural focus helps you close off a foe's retreat. When you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]], you can reduce the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] to `0` until <u>the end of the current turn</u>. You can then move up to half your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] as part of the same **Reaction**. This movement doesn't provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attack]].

### Level 15: Soul of Vengeance

Immediately after a creature under the effect of your **Vow of Enmity** _hits_ or _misses_ with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you can take a **Reaction** to make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] against that creature if it's within range.

### Level 19: Sword Angel

As a **Bonus Action**, you gain the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).

**<u>Flight:</u>** You sprout spectral wings on your back, have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] of <u>60 feet</u>, and can hover.

**<u>Frightful Aura:</u>** Whenever an enemy starts its turn in your **Sacred Aura**, that creature makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition for <u>1 minute</u> or until it takes any damage. [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] creature have **ADV**.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
