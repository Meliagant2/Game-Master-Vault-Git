# 🦸‍♀️5e - Crusader🦸‍♀️

Crusaders travel the world in search for their true purpose and to protect the realm from forces beyond. Crusaders hone their minds, spirits, and bodies to be the ultimate weapons against such threats.

### Level 3: Crusader's Experience (Flavor)

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Perception|💃Perception]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - WIS Skills/5e - Survival|💃Survival]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Crusader's Ward:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Oath** to choose a number of creatures you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you, up to a number equal to `your CHA (minimum of 1)`. For <u>1 minute</u>, you and the chosen creatures have **ADV** on <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, and <u>CHA</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of the Sentinel

You and your allies gain a bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Initiative|🎲Initiative]] rolls equal to `your CHA` while in your **Sacred Aura**.

### Level 11: Inspiring Smite

Immediately after you use **Paladin's Smite**, you can distribute [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] to creatures of your choice within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself, which can include you. The total number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equals `2d10 + your Paladin level`, divided among the chosen creatures however you like.

### Level 15: Vigilant Rebuke

You’ve learned how to chastise anyone who dares wield beguilements against you and your wards. Whenever you or a creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you _succeeds_ on an <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, or <u>CHA</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can use your **Reaction** to deal `3d8 + CHA` <u>Force</u> damage to the creature that forced the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]].

### Level 19: Mortal Bulwark

As a **Bonus Action**, you manifest a spark of divine power in defense of the mortal realms and gain the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).

**<u>Saving Throw Reroll:</u>** If you _fail_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can take a **Reaction** to reroll it. You must use this new roll.

**<u>Truesight:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]] with a range of <u>120 feet</u>.

**<u>Unerring Strike:</u>** <u>Once on each of your turns</u> when you make a <u>melee</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] or an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Unarmed Strike|⚔️Unarmed Strike]] and _miss_, you can cause that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] to _hit_ instead.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
