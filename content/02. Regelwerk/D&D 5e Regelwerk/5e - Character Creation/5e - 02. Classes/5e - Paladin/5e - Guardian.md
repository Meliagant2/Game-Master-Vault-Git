# 🦸‍♀️5e - Guardian🦸‍♀️

Guardians are sworn to protect the common folk and guards against rampaging monsters. Those who embrace this oath range from itinerant Paladins who single-handedly defend towns on the borderlands to elite groups of knights tasked with protecting the cardinals of a religious order.

### Level 3: Guardian Training (Flavor)

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - STR Skills/5e - Athletics|💃Athletics]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CON Skills/5e - Endurance|💃Endurance]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Champion Challenge:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you use expend one use of your **Channel Oath** and issue a challenge that compels other creatures to do battle with you. Each creature of your choice that you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of you makes a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Taunted|☠️Taunted]] condition and can’t willingly move more than <u>30 feet</u> away from you for <u>1 minute</u>. This effect ends on the creature if you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] or die or if the creature is more than <u>30 feet</u> away from you.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of the Guardian

You can shield others from harm at the cost of your own health. When a creature within your **Sacred Aura** takes damage, you can magically take that damage, instead of that creature taking it (**no Action** required). This feature doesn't transfer any other effects that might accompany the damage, and this damage can't be reduced in any way.

### Level 11: Protective Spirit

While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Bloodied|☠️Bloodied]], as long as you are not [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]], you regain `1/2 x your Paladin level + CHA` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]], at <u>the start of each of your turns</u>.

### Level 15: Glorious Defense

You can turn defense into a sudden strike. When you or another creature you can see within <u>10 feet</u> of you is _hit_ by an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you can take a **Reaction** to grant a bonus to the target's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] against that [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]], potentially causing it to _miss_. The bonus equals `your CHA (minimum of +1)`. If the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack]] _misses_, you can make one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon Attack]] against the attacker as part of this **Reaction** if the attacker is within your weapon's range.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your CHA</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 19: Exalted Champion

As a **Bonus Action**, your presence on the field of battle is an inspiration to those dedicated to your cause and you gain the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).

**<u>Mental Strength:</u>** While in your **Sacred Aura**, you and your allies have **ADV** on <u>WIS</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].

**<u>Unkillable:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage.

**<u>Scorn Death:</u>** While in your **Sacred Aura**, you and your allies have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Death Save|🎲Death Saves]].

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
