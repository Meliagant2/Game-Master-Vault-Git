# 🦸‍♀️5e - Spellknight🦸‍♀️

Spellknights believe in maintaining the balance of power between the ordinary and the extraordinary, including themselves. These paladins are resourceful and quick to remember their own mortality and privilege, often choosing to pursue mundane solutions before magical ones. Despite their caution, these wardens of balance are exemplary arcanists who can feel the weave of magic in the world as keenly as some of the finest sorcerers.

### Level 3: Channel Oath

You can channel divine energy to fuel magical effects. Other Paladin features might give additional **Channel Oath** effect options. Each time you use **Channel Oath**, you choose which effect from this class to create.

If a **Channel Oath** effect requires a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Difficulty Class/5e - Difficulty Class|🎲DC]] equals the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]] from this class's Spellcasting feature.

**<u>Absorb Magic:</u>** You can refute certain spells and use them to nourish yourself. When you _see_ a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of you casting a spell, you can take a **Reaction** and expend a use of your **Channel Oath** to interrupt it. You make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] (`d20 + your CHA + 1/3 x your Paladin level`), contested by the creature's [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Ability Check]] (`d20 + its Spellcasting Ability + the spell's level`). _**Success:**_ The spell dissipates with no effect, and the **Action**, **Bonus Action**, or **Reaction** used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a _spell slot_, the slot isn't expended. You also gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `your CHA + 5 x the dissipated spell's level`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times as stated in the <u>Paladin Class Table</u>. You regain `1` expended use when you finish a **Short Rest**, and you regain `all` expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Aura of Warding

Ancient magic lies so heavily upon you that it forms an eldritch ward; you and your allies have <u>Resistance</u> to damage from spells while in your **Sacred Aura**.

### Level 11: Spellslinger

When you take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Attack Action|⚔️Attack]] action on your turn, you can replace one of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attacks]] with a casting of one of your cantrips that has a casting time of an **Action**.

### Level 15: Armored Focus

While you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentrating]] on a spell, your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Concentration/5e - Concentration|🎲Concentration]] can't be broken as a result of taking damage.

### Level 19: Arcane Conduit

As a **Bonus Action**, you can imbue your **Sacred Aura** with crackling magic, granting the benefits below for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them (**no Action** required).

**<u>Diminish Defiance:</u>** Enemies in the aura have **DISADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] against your spells and **Channel Oath** options.

**<u>Magic Resistance:</u>** While in your **Sacred Aura**, you and your allies have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] against spells and magical effects.

**<u>Swift Spells:</u>** Whenever you cast a spell that has a casting time of an **Action**, you can cast it using a **Bonus Action** instead.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a level 5 spell slot (**no Action** required).
