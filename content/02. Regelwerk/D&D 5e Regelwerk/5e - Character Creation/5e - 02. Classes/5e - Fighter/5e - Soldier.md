# 🦸‍♀️5e - Soldier🦸‍♀️

Soldiers are battlefield hardened Fighters, who earn their living by fighting in wars. They could be mercenaries, men-at-arms, or unlucky peasants, who were drafted into an army.

### Level 3: Fluid Traditions (Flavor)

You gain proficiency in one **Combat Tradition** of your choice, that is available to Fighters at 1st level. When you gain this feature you can choose one of the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Maneuvers|🤺Combat Maneuvers]] you know and replace it with another maneuver of the same degree or lower from your newly chosen combat tradition.

### Level 3: Advanced Technique

Your ability to learn and utilize martial techniques exceeds most other warriors. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Heightened Exertion:</u>** Your total number of <u>Exertion</u> increases by `2`.

**<u>Potent Maneuvers:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Maneuver Rules/5e - Maneuver Save DC|🤺Maneuver Save DC]] increases by `1`.

### Level 3: Fighting Spirit

Your intensity in battle can shield you and help you strike true. As a **Bonus Action** on your turn, you can give yourself **ADV** on <u>weapon attack rolls</u> until the end of the current turn. When you do so, you also gain _Temporary Hit Points_ equal to `your Fighter Level`.

**<u>Number of Uses:</u>** You can use this feature <u>three</u> times. You regain <u>one</u> expended use when you finish a **Short Rest** and you regain <u>all</u> expended uses of it when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Consistent Skill

You are able to fight at your peak capability for longer than most other warriors. When you use your **Second Wind** to _regain HP_, you regain `2` <u>Exertion</u>.

### Level 11: Master of Forms

Your technical skill with the weapons of war has grown, allowing you to learn exotic techniques from a variety of disciplines. Choose `2` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Maneuvers|🤺Combat Maneuvers]] from any Combat Tradition. Those maneuvers must be from a degree you can use. You learn both of those maneuvers and they don't count against your maximum number of maneuvers known.

### Level 15: Master of Maneuvers

You can draw on your techniques as a reflex in times of great need. <u>Once per turn</u> you can use one of your **Maneuvers** without expending Exertion.

### Level 19: Combat Superiority

Your mastery with the weapons of war is near-supernatural. Once per turn when you use a **maneuver**, you can expend `3 exertion` to use another maneuver on the attack. You still need to spend the exertion for that maneuver.
