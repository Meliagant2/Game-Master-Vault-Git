# 🦸‍♀️5e - Dungoneer🦸‍♀️

A dungeoneer is at home in underground lairs, winding caves, and labyrinthian passages. They have adapted to the harsh conditions of dungeon environments and turned them to their advantage. Whether battling in cramped rooms shrouded in darkness, smashing down locked doors, dodging traps, or enduring natural hazards, experienced dungeoneers thrive underneath the earth, fighting on equal footing with the denizens they encounter there.

### Level 3: Close-Quarters Fighting

You have adapted to the confined conditions found in dungeons, and you fight best with a wall at your back. Choose one of the following benefits:

**<u>Back to the Wall:</u>** While within <u>5 feet</u> of a solid wall, you add your proficiency bonus to <u>damage rolls</u> with <u>weapon attacks</u>.

**<u>Tunnel Guardian:</u>** While within <u>5 feet</u> of a solid wall, you gain `+2` <u>AC</u>.

### Level 3: Dungoneering Traditions

You gain proficiency in one of the following combat traditions of your choice: [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Adamant Mountain Tradition/5e - Adamant Mountain Tradition|🤺Adamant Mountain]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Razors Edge Tradition/5e - Razors Edge Tradition|🤺Razors Edge]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Tempered Iron Tradition/5e - Tempered Iron Tradition|🤺Tempered Iron]]. When you gain this feature you can choose one of the maneuvers you know and replace it with another maneuver of the same degree or lower from your newly chosen combat tradition.

### Level 6: Dungeon Crawler

Your experience exploring dungeons has made you a veteran of the depths, able to smash your way through common obstacles with ease or endure whatever hazards you encounter. Choose one of the following options:

**<u>Aggressive Delving:</u>**

- You make <u>DEX</u> <u>saving throws</u> to avoid or resist traps with **ADV**.
- You have **ADV** on <u>ability checks</u> to break objects such as locked or jammed doors and locked chests, and your attacks deal double damage to objects and constructs.

**<u>Defensive Delving:</u>**

- You make <u>CON</u> <u>saving throws</u> to avoid or resist traps with **ADV**.
- You can hold your breath for a minimum of `5 minutes + your CON` before checking for suffocation or drowning.
- You have <u>resistance</u> to <u>falling damage</u>.

### Level 9: Shadow Sense

You are at home in pitch darkness and fight even more effectively without light.

- You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]] with a radius of <u>10 feet</u>.
- You gain an <u>expertise die</u> to attack rolls while in [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Darkness (Illumination)|💡Darkness]].

### Level 13: Close-Quarters Veteran

You have mastered the art of fighting in cramped environments both offensively and defensively.

- If you selected **Back to the Wall** as your **Close-Quarters Fighting** feature, your damage bonus increases to `2 + your proficiency bonus`.
- If you selected **Tunnel Guardian** as your **Close-Quarters Fighting** feature, your bonus to AC increases to `+3`.
- You gain the **Close-Quarters Fighting** feature you did not select at level 3.

### Level 17: Dauntless Delver

You have become better at withstanding common attacks you encounter when exploring the depths, shrugging off blows that would severely harm other adventurers. When you take damage, you can use your **Reaction** to gain <u>Resistance</u> to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, as well as to acid, necrotic, and poison damage, until the start of your next turn. You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your proficiency bonus</u>, regaining all uses when you finish a **long rest**.
