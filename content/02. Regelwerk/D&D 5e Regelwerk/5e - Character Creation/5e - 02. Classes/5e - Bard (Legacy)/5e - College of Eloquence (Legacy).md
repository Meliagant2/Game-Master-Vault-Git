# 🦸‍♀️5e - College of Eloquence🦸‍♀️

Adherents of the College of Eloquence master the art of oratory. Persuasion is regarded as a high art, and a well-reasoned, well-spoken argument often proves more persuasive than facts. These bards wield a blend of logic and theatrical wordplay, winning over skeptics and detractors with logical arguments and plucking at heartstrings to appeal to the emotions of audiences.

### Level 3: Silver Tongue

You are a master at saying the right thing at the right time. When you make a <u>Persuasion</u> or <u>Deception check</u>, you can treat a <u>d20</u> roll of <u>9</u> or lower as a <u>10</u>.

### Level 3: Unsettling Words

You can spin words laced with magic that unsettle a creature and cause it to doubt itself. As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Bardic Inspiration** and choose one creature you can see within <u>60 feet</u> of you. Roll the <u>Bardic Inspiration die</u>. The creature must subtract the number rolled from the next <u>saving throw</u> it makes before the start of your next turn.

### Level 6: Universal Speech

You have gained the ability to make your speech intelligible to any creature. As an **Action**, choose one or more creatures within <u>60 feet</u> of you, up to a number equal to your <u>CHA modifier</u> (minimum of one creature). The chosen creatures can magically understand you, regardless of the language you speak, for <u>1 hour</u>.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**, unless you expend a spell slot to use it again.

### Level 9: Unfailing Inspiration

Your inspiring words are so persuasive that others feel driven to succeed. When a creature adds one of your <u>Bardic Inspiration dice</u> to its <u>D20 Test</u> and the roll fails, the creature can keep the <u>Bardic Inspiration die</u>.

### Level 13: Infectious Inspiration

When you successfully inspire someone, the power of your eloquence can now spread to someone else. When a creature within <u>60 feet</u> of you adds one of your <u>Bardic Inspiration dice</u> to its <u>D20 Test</u> and the roll succeeds, you can use your **Reaction** to encourage a different creature (other than yourself) that can hear you within <u>60 feet</u> of you, giving it a <u>Bardic Inspiration die</u> without expending any of your **Bardic Inspiration** uses.

You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your <u>CHA modifier</u> (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

### Level 17: Unfaltering Words

Your words are spoken with clearity and sincerity. As an **Action** you can enter a state of perfect self for <u>1 hour</u>. During this time you can treat every <u>Charisma ability check</u> you make you roll as a <u>15</u> on the die instead. Additionally, no matter what you say, magic that would determine if you are telling the truth indicates that you are being truthful.
