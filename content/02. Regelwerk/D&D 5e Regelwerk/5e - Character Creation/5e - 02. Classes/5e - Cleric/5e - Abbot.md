# 🦸‍♀️5e - Abbot🦸‍♀️

An abbot is a cleric, who tries to understand the signs of their belief. They tend to old texts, symbols and knowledge, while they themselves might want to hide this knowledge from the uninitiated.

### Level 3: Secret Knowledge (Flavor)

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - History|💃History]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Occultism|💃Occultism]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Knowledge Skills/5e - Religion|💃Religion]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

### Level 3: Secret Rituals

You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - Level 1 Divination/5e - Comprehend Languages (Spell)|☄️Comprehend Languages]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 1 Conjuration/5e - Find Familiar (Spell)|☄️Find Familiar]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Illusion/5e - Level 1 Illusion/5e - Illusory Script (Spell)|☄️Illusory Script]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Spell Tags/5e - Ritual Casting|☄️Ritual]] spells prepared and they don't count against your maximum number of spells.

### Level 3: Invoke Illusory Helper

As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** to create a perfect visual illusion of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. The illusion is intangible and doesn't occupy its space. It lasts for <u>1 minute</u>, but it ends early if you dismiss it (**no Action** required) or have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition. The illusion is animated and mimics your expressions and gestures. While it persists, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cast Spells:</u>** You can cast spells as though you were in the illusion's space, but you must use your own senses.

**<u>Distract:</u>** When both you and your illusion are within <u>5 feet</u> of a creature that can _see_ the illusion, you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against that creature, given how distracting the illusion is to the target.

**<u>Move:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can move the illusion up to <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see that is within <u>120</u> feet of yourself.

**<u>Teleportation:</u>** Whenever you take the **Bonus Action** to create or move the illusion, you can teleport, swapping places with the illusion.

### Level 7: Veil Heresy

If you cast an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Illusion/5e - School of Illusion|☄️Illusion]] spell using a spell slot, you can change the spell’s casting time to a **Bonus Action** for this casting, provided the spell’s casting time is normally an **Action**.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS (minimum of once)</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 11: Guarded Mind

If you _**fail**_ an <u>INT</u>, a <u>WIS</u>, or a <u>CHA</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can cause yourself to _**succeed**_ instead.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.

### Level 15: See the Truth

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]] with a range of <u>30 feet</u>. If you already have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]], the range of it increases by <u>30 feet</u> instead.

### Level 19: Improved Illusion

The illusion of your **Invoke Illusory Helper** has grown more powerful in the following ways:
**<u>Farther Duplicate:</u>** When you create your Duplicate, you can teleport up to <u>60 feet</u> rather than <u>30 feet</u>.
**<u>Shared Distraction:</u>** When you and your allies make [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of the illusion, the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] have **ADV**.
**<u>Healing Illusion:</u>** When the illusion ends, you or a creature of your choice within <u>5 feet</u> of it regains a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `your Cleric level`.
