# 🦸‍♀️5e - Prophet🦸‍♀️

Prophets sometimes receive visions directly from their deity and receive fleeting omens of the future. They share impossible knowledge with their allies and prophesize their enemies' doom. Some prophets consider the future preordained, while others understand the multiverse as a place of infinite possibility.

### Level 3: Mind Magic (Flavor)

As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action you choose one spell from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Divination/5e - School of Divination|☄️Divination]] school you have prepared, and that doesn't deal any damage. As part of that **Action**, you cast that spell without expending a spell slot or needing [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] components.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 3: Restore Balance

When a creature you can _see_ within <u>60 feet</u> of yourself is about to roll a <u>d20</u> with **ADV** or **DISADV**, you can take a **Reaction** to prevent the roll from being affected by **ADV** and **DISADV**.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 3: Plucking at Threads

As a **Bonus Action**, you present your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] and expend a use of your **Channel Divinity** to  alter the weaves of Fate around you. Allies within <u>30 feet</u> of you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] rolls and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

### Level 7: Unfettered Mind

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Telepathy:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Languages/5e - Rare Languages/5e - Telepathy|🗣️Telepathy]] out to <u>60 feet</u>. When you use this telepathy, you can simultaneously contact a number of creatures equal to `your WIS (minimum of one)`.

**<u>Shielded Mind:</u>** You gain proficiency in <u>INT</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]]. If you already have this proficiency, you instead gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] proficiency with one [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Character Creation/5e - 00. Ability Scores/5e - 00. Ability Scores|⚙️Ability]] in which you lack it.

### Level 11: Balance

Immediately after you deal damage with a spell you cast with a spell slot on your turn, you and any allies within <u>30 feet</u> of you regain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `the spell’s level + your WIS (minimum of 1)`.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 15: Tip the Scale

If you of a creature within <u>30 feet</u> of you _fails_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can choose the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] to be _successful_ instead (**no Action** required).

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of this feature by expending a level 6+ spell slot (**no Action** required).

### Level 19: Divine Foreknowledge

As a **Bonus Action**, you magically expand your mind to the future. For <u>1 hour</u>, you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Tests]].

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Long Rest**. You can also restore your use of this feature by expending a level 6+ spell slot (**no Action** required).
