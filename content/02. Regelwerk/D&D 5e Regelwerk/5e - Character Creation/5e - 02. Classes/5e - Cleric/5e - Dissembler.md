# 🦸‍♀️5e - Dissembler🦸‍♀️

Dissemblers hide their true religious feelings or identity under a false appearance. This might be because they fear persecution, feel unsure about their belief, or are scared to be judged by allies and friends for their faith. Dissemblers aren't less devout than other clerics, but some religious orders might look at them in disdain for hiding who they are.

### Level 3: Religious Secrecy (Flavor)

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Deception|💃Deception]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Sleight of Hand|💃Sleight of Hand]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Stealth|💃Stealth]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Proficiency|🎲Proficiency]], or one level of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Proficiency/5e - Expertise|🎲Expertise]], if you are already _proficient_.

### Level 3: Blessing of Secrecy

As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action, you can choose yourself or a willing creature within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself to have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - CHA Skills/5e - Deception|💃Deception]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Sleight of Hand|💃Sleight of Hand]], and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - DEX Skills/5e - Stealth|💃Stealth]] [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Ability Check/5e - Ability Check|🎲Checks]]. This blessing lasts until you finish a **Long Rest** or you use this feature again.

### Level 3: Invoke Duplicity

As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** to create a perfect visual illusion of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. The illusion is intangible and doesn't occupy its space. It lasts for <u>1 minute</u>, but it ends early if you dismiss it (**no Action** required) or have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition. The illusion is animated and mimics your expressions and gestures. While it persists, you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Cast Spells:</u>** You can cast spells as though you were in the illusion's space, but you must use your own senses.

**<u>Distract:</u>** When both you and your illusion are within <u>5 feet</u> of a creature that can _see_ the illusion, you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against that creature, given how distracting the illusion is to the target. Creatures with [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]] are unaffected by your illusion.

**<u>Move:</u>** As a **Bonus Action**, you can move the illusion up to <u>30 feet</u> to an unoccupied space you can see that is within <u>120</u> feet of yourself.

### Level 7: Veiled Heresy

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Transposition:</u>** Whenever you take the **Bonus Action** to create or move the illusion of you **Invoke Duplicity**, you can teleport, swapping places with the illusion.

**<u>Trickster's Magic:</u>** If you cast an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Illusion/5e - School of Illusion|☄️Illusion]] spell using a spell slot, you can change the spell’s casting time to a **Bonus Action** for this casting, provided the spell’s casting time is normally an **Action**.

### Level 11: Guarded Mind

If you _**fail**_ an <u>INT</u>, a <u>WIS</u>, or a <u>CHA</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]], you can cause yourself to _**succeed**_ instead.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS (minimum of once)</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 15: See the Truth

You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]] with a range of <u>30 feet</u>. If you already have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Truesight|👁️Truesight]], the range of it increases by <u>30 feet</u> instead.

### Level 19: Improved Duplicity

The illusion of your **Invoke Duplicity** has grown more powerful in the following ways:

**<u>Shared Distraction:</u>** When you and your allies make [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] against a creature within <u>5 feet</u> of the illusion, the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Rolls]] have **ADV**.

**<u>Healing Illusion:</u>** When the illusion ends, you or a creature of your choice within <u>5 feet</u> of it regains a number of [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] equal to `your Cleric level`.
