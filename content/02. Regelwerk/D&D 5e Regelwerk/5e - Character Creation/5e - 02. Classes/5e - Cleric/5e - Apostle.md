# 🦸‍♀️5e - Apostle🦸‍♀️

Apostles are devout Clerics who believe themselves to follow a divine purpose in the world. They follow their doctrine with zealous fervour.

### Level 3: Following the Doctrine (Flavor)

You have <u>Immunity</u> against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition.

### Level 3: Armor of Zeal

While you aren’t wearing [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Armor/5e - Armor|⛑️Armor]] or wielding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Shields/5e - Shields|🛡️Shield]], you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Unarmored Defense:</u>** Your base [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] equals `10 + DEX + WIS`.

**<u>Strong Mind:</u>** You gain a `1d4` bonus to <u>INT</u>, <u>WIS</u>, and <u>CHA</u> [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].

### Level 3: Radiant Rebuke

As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action, you present your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] and expend a use of your **Channel Divinity** to emit a flash of divine magic in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> originating from yourself. Each creature of your choice in that area makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10 + your Cleric level` damage of one of your **Divine Damage types** (your choice). _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

### Level 7: Eyes of Devotion

Your eyesight cannot be dimmed by heretics. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Blindsight:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]] with a range of <u>30 feet</u>. If you already have [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Senses/5e - Blindsight|👁️Blindsight]], the range of it increases by <u>15 feet</u> instead.

**<u>Shared Devotion:</u>** Allies within <u>10 feet</u> of you have **ADV** on [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]] made to avoid or end the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blinded]] condition.

### Level 11: Destroy Influence

Immediately after you cast a spell with a spell slot that restores [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] to a creature or ends a condition on a creature, you can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Abjuration/5e - Level 3 Abjuration/5e - Dispel Magic (Spell)|☄️Dispel Magic]] as part of that **Action**, **Bonus Action**, or **Reaction**, and without expending a spell slot.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**. You also restore your use of it by expending one use of **Channel Divinity** (**no Action** required).

### Level 15: Clerical Precision

If you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] against a creature and _miss_, you have **ADV** on your next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] against that creature before <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

### Level 19: Avatar of Conviction

You gain <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage.
