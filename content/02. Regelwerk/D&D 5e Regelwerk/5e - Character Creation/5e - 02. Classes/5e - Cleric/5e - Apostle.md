# 🦸‍♀️5e - Apostle🦸‍♀️

Apostles are devout Clerics who believe themselves to follow a divine purpose in the world. They follow their doctrine with zealous fervour.

### Level 3: Following the Doctrine (Flavor)

You are <u>Immune</u> against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition.

### Level 3: Armor of Zeal

While you aren’t wearing [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Armor/5e - Armor|⛑️Armor]] or wielding a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Shields/5e - Shields|🛡️Shield]], you gain the following benefits:

**<u>Unarmored Defense:</u>** Your base [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Armor Class|🛡️AC]] equals `10 + DEX + WIS`.

**<u>Save Bonus:</u>** You gain a `+1` bonus to [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Saves]].

### Level 3: Radiant Rebuke

As a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action, you present your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Equipment/5e - Spellcasting Focus/5e - Spellcasting Focus|🎩Spellcasting Focus]] and expend a use of your **Channel Divinity** to emit a flash of divine magic in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> originating from yourself. Each creature of your choice in that area makes a **CON Save**. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10 + your Cleric level` <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (your choice). _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

### Level 7: Divine Judgment

If you cast an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Evocation/5e - School of Evocation|☄️Evocation]] spell using a spell slot, you can change the spell’s casting time to a **Bonus Action** for this casting, provided the spell’s casting time is normally an **Action**.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can use this feature a number of times equal to <u>your WIS (minimum of once)</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 11: Rightful Fury

Whenever you deal damage to a creature, or when a creature _**fails**_ a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Saving Throw/5e - Saving Throw|🎲Save]] against one of your spells of features, that creature takes <u>Necrotic</u> or <u>Radiant</u> damage (your choice) equal to `your WIS`. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

### Level 15: Clerical Precision

Whenever you _**miss**_ with a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]], you gain **ADV** on your next [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]].

### Level 19: Avatar of Conviction

You gain <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage.
