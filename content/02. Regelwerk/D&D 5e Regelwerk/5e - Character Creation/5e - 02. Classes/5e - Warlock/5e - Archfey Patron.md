# 🦸‍♀️5e - Archfey Patron🦸‍♀️

Your patron is a lord or lady of the fey, a creature of legend who holds secrets that were forgotten before the mortal races were born. This being’s motivations are often inscrutable, and sometimes whimsical, and might involve a striving for greater magical power or the settling of age-old grudges.

### Level 3: Dream Walker (Flavor)

While sleeping, you are lucid and can manipulate what appears in your dreams. Additionally, while you’re in contact with a sleeping creature you can take a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action to try to witness the creature's dream. The target makes an **INT Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ You can witness their dream. You choose if the creature notices that you’re watching their dream or not. _**Success:**_ You can’t use this Feature again on that creature until it wakes up.

### Level 3: Steps of the Fey

You always have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] spell prepared. You can cast it a number of times without a spell slot equal to `your Spellcasting Ability modifier (minimum of once)`, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a **Long Rest**.

Whenever you cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]], you can choose one of the following additional effects:

- **<u>Refreshing Step:</u>**  Immediately after you teleport, you or one creature you can see within <u>10 feet</u> of yourself gains `1d10` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]].
- **<u>Taunting Step:</u>** Creatures within <u>5 feet</u> of the space you left make a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Taunted|☠️Taunted]] by you until <u>the start of your next turn</u>.

### Level 7: Misty Escape

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Reactive Misty Step:</u>** You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] as a **Reaction** in response to taking damage.

**<u>Stronger Steps of the Fey:</u>** The following effects are now among your **Steps of the Fey** options:

- **<u>Disappearing Step:</u>** You have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Invisible|☠️Invisible]] condition until <u>the start of your next turn</u> or until immediately after you make an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], deal [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Damage/5e - Damage|💔Damage]], cast a spell, or take the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Actions/5e - Magic Action|⚔️Magic]] action.
- **<u>Dreadful Step:</u>** Creatures within <u>5 feet</u> of the space you left or the space you appear in (your choice) **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes `2d10` <u>Psychic</u> damage.

### Level 11: Rested Guile

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Charmed Immunity:</u>** You have <u>Immunity</u> against the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Charmed|☠️Charmed]] condition.

**<u>Trance:</u>** You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a **Long Rest** in <u>4 hours</u> if you spend those hours in a trancelike meditation, during which you retain consciousness, but still dream.

### Level 15: Bewitching Magic

Your patron grants you the ability to weave your magic with teleportation. Immediately after you cast an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Enchantment/5e - School of Enchantment|☄️Enchantment]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Illusion/5e - School of Illusion|☄️Illusion]] spell using an **Action** and a spell slot, you can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] as part of the same **Action** and without expending a spell slot.

### Level 19: Hidden Paths

You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Feystep:</u>** You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] without a spell slot an unlimited amount of times per day.

**<u>Greater Feystep:</u>** You can always choose <u>two</u> options for your **Steps of the Fey**. In addition, the following effects are now among your **Steps of the Fey** options:

- **<u>Distant Step:</u>** Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - Level 2 Conjuration/5e - Misty Step (Spell)|☄️Misty Step]] has a range of <u>60 feet</u>.
- **<u>Dual Step:</u>** When you teleport, you can also teleport one willing creature, that is within <u>5 feet</u> of you, and it teleports to a space within <u>5 feet</u> of your destination space.
