# 🦸‍♀️5e - Undead Patron🦸‍♀️

You've made a pact with a creature that defies the cycle of life and death: a powerful lich, a vampire, or another entity of undeath. Having once been mortal, these ancient patrons know firsthand the paths of ambition and the routes past the doors of death. They eagerly share this profane knowledge and other secrets with those who work their will among the living.

### Level 3: Undead Endurance (Flavor)

You don't gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] levels from dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation. In addition, you don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

### Level 3: Undead Spells

The magic of your patron ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a _Warlock level_ specified in the <u>Undead Spells table</u>, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

### Level 3: Form of Dread

As a **Bonus Action**, you transform into an avatar of your patron's dreadful power, gaining the benefits below for <u>1 minute</u>, until you have the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Incapacitated|☠️Incapacitated]] condition, or until you end the form (**no Action** required).

**<u>Facsimile of Life:</u>** You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Temporary Hit Points|💖Temporary Hit Points]] equal to `1d10 + your Warlock level`.

**<u>Fearless Form:</u>** You have <u>Immunity</u> to the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition. If you are [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] when you transform, the condition immediately ends for you.

**<u>Frightful Avatar:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]], you can force it to make a **WIS Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The target has the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] condition until <u>the end of your next turn</u>.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ You can transform a number of times equal to <u>your Spellcasting Ability modifier</u>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **Long Rest**.

### Level 7: Grave Touched

Your patron's powers have a profound effect on your body and magic, granting you the following benefits:

**<u>Arcane Necrosis:</u>** <u>Necrotic</u> damage from your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attacks]], _Warlock spells_, and _Warlock features_ ignores <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> damage. <u>Once per turn</u> when you cast a spell that deals damage, you can change that spell's damage type to <u>Necrotic</u>.

**<u>Dreaded Necrosis:</u>** <u>Once per turn</u>, when you _**hit**_ a creature with an [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Attack Roll|🎲Attack Roll]] and deal <u>Necrotic</u> damage while using your **Form of Dread**, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the <u>Necrotic</u> damage the target takes.

### Level 11: Necrotic Husk

Your connection to undeath saturates your body. You gain the following benefits:

**<u>Necrotic Resilience:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> damage. While using your **Form of Dread**, you have <u>Immunity</u> to <u>Necrotic</u> damage.

**<u>Unholy Resuscitation:</u>** If you drop to `0` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] and don't die outright, you can cause your body to erupt with deathly energy. Each creature of your choice in a <u>30-foot Emanation</u> originating from you makes a **CON Save** against your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Save|☄️Spell Save DC]]. _**Failure:**_ The creature takes <u>Necrotic</u> damage equal to `2d10 + your Spellcasting Ability modifier`. _**Success:**_ The creature takes half the damage only.

Your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Hit Points/5e - Hit Points|💖Hit Points]] then change to `2 x your Warlock level`, and you gain `1` [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Exhaustion|☠️Exhaustion]] level.

_**<u>Number of Uses:</u>**_ <u>Once</u> you've used this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a **Short** or **Long Rest**.

### Level 15: Superior Dread

Your **Form of Dread** improves, granting you the following benefits while you are using it:

**<u>Dread Resistance:</u>** You have <u>Resistance</u> to <u>Bludgeoning</u>, <u>Piercing</u>, and <u>Slashing</u> damage.

**<u>Ghostly Flight:</u>** You have a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Fly Speed|🏃‍♀️Fly Speed]] equal to your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Movement/5e - Speed/5e - Speed|🏃‍♀️Speed]] and can hover. You can move through creatures and objects as if they were [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Environment/5e - Difficult Terrain|🏔️Difficult Terrain]], but you take `1d10` <u>Force</u> damage if you end your turn inside a creature or an object.

**<u>Profane Casting:</u>** Whenever you cast a <u>Warlock spell</u> from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Conjuration/5e - School of Conjuration|☄️Conjuration]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Categorization/5e - Schools of Magic/5e - School of Necromancy/5e - School of Necromancy|☄️Necromancy]] school, you cast it without any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Verbal Components|☄️Verbal]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Somatic Components|☄️Somatic]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] components, except [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Components/5e - Material Components|☄️Material]] components that are costly or consumed.
