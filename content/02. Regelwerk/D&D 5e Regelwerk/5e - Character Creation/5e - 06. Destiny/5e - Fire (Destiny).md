# 🔮5e - Fire🔮

Fire can be put to work, but it has no goal in mind, no plans for the future beyond spreading and burning bright. Fire is impulsive, destructive, and beautiful. Those with a destiny of fire tend to be rabble rousers and instigators, brash and bold. They take up causes as burning beacons, only to abandon them once passions have cooled. They are beings of passion and wrath, and they often love and care deeply while leaving nought but ashes in their wakes.

**<u>Source of Inspiration: Passion:</u>** You draw inspiration from ignited passions in yourself and others. You gain [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]] whenever you passionately adopt a new love, cause, or interest, and whenever you rally others to do the same. Devote yourself to a new cause, become passionately enraged at something new, rally a large number of NPCs to fight, fall deeply in love.
**<u>Inspiration Feature: Ignite:</u>** Your passions ignite and your current obsession possesses you. You may spend your [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Inspiration|✨Inspiration]] as a **Reaction** to ignite a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Maneuvers|🤺Combat Tradition]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellcasting|☄️School of Magic]] of your choice for a number of rounds equal to `your PB`. Any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - D20 Tests|🎲D20 Test]] made in an ignited [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Skills/5e - Skills|💃Skill]], [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Combat Maneuvers/5e - Combat Maneuvers|🤺Combat Tradition]], or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellcasting|☄️School of Magic]] gains a `1d8` bonus for the duration. You can end this effect early (**no Action** required).

##### Fulfilling Your Destiny

You fulfill your destiny of **Fire** when you let it all burn for the sake of someone or something you’re passionate about.

Save someone you love at the cost of vast destruction, destabilize a nation or the world, unleash a great power you have no control over.

**<u>Fulfillment Feature: Blazing Soul:</u>** You are the heart of the inferno, a cackling spark that sets the world ablaze.

- You have <u>Immunity</u> to <u>Fire</u> damage.
- When you activate your **Ignite** feature, you can choose to literally cloak yourself in flame for the same duration. During that time you gain the following abilities:
  - The duration of the Feature is increased to `2 x your PB`.
  - Unattended objects within <u>5 feet</u> of you catch fire.
  - You shed [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Bright Light|💡Bright Light]] in a <u>20-foot radius</u> and [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Vision and Light/5e - Illumination/5e - Dim Light|💡Dim Light]] for an additional <u>20 feet</u>
  - Any [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - D20 Tests/5e - Attack Roll/5e - Weapon Attack|🎲Weapon]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell Rules/5e - Spell Effects/5e - Spell Attack Roll|☄️Spell Attack]] you make during this time deals an additional amount of <u>Fire</u> damage equal to a number of `d4s` equal to `your PB`.
