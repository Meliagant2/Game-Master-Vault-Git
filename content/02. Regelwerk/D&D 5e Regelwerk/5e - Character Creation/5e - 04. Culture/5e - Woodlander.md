# 🎎5e - Woodlander🎎

In vast, foreboding forests and jungles filled with monsters and magic, the villages of woodlanders are a rare and wondrous sight. These unique settlements are known for their harmony with nature — typical dwellings are tree houses with the mightiest branches forming living walkways, walls, and alcoves. The grandest of these woodlander villages have many houses in a single tree and elegant walkways connecting each home.

Woodlander communities are often fairly isolated. They raise their own food, train their own warriors, and produce their own crafts. Plenty of these villages are insular and xenophobic, but many more are hospitable and generous to travelers — with some guests even invited to stay permanently. Their social orientation is communalistic and egalitarian.

Although woodlander laws and hierarchy do exist, they prefer to resolve conflicts through consensus. For complex conflicts they use large circle meetings where anyone can speak.

Other important cultural values for woodlanders are curiosity and exploration. Isolated though woodlander villages may be, it is not uncommon to see a woodlander troubadour, craftmaker, or sightseer on a quest to learn something new.

Characters raised in the wood elf culture share a variety of traits in common with one another:

**Languages:** You can speak, read, write, and sign <u>Common</u> and <u>Sylvan</u>.
**Fleet of Foot:** Your Speed increases by <u>5 feet</u>.
**Nature’s Ally:** You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Enchantment/5e - Level 1 Enchantment/5e - Animal Friendship (Spell)|☄️Animal Friendship]] without material components once per long rest. Your spellcasting ability for this spell is INT, WIS, or CHA (whichever is highest).
**Nature’s Touch:** Choose one of the following:

- **Way with Animals:** You gain proficiency in <u>Animal Handling</u> and with <u>land vehicles</u>.
- **Way with Plants:** You gain proficiency in <u>Nature</u> and with <u>herbalism kits</u>.
- **Treeborne Scout:** You gain a <u>climb speed</u> equal to your Speed.

**Woodland Weapon Training:** You are proficient with <u>longbows</u> and <u>shortswords</u>.
