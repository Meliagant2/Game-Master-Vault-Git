# 🎎5e - Dragonbound🎎

Dragonbound clans, villages or cities live under the direct rule of a dragon; in the case of dragonborn characters, that dragon will usually be their progenitor dragon. Dragons ruling over mortals are called _Warden dragons_ and they view the people they rule over as their hoard.

Life in a dragonbound culture is always a life of community in a way. Depending on the warden dragon, life in these communities can be overly jovial, brutal and bloody, or just a normal life with a dragon in the center of everything. Not everyone, who is part of a warden dragon's hoard was raised in a dragonbound culture, but they might join and become part of it.

Warden dragons can be tyrannical rulers, who train their hoard to be fierce warriors, ready to fight everyone outside of their hoard, others might rule in unison with mortal leaders of a country. Some are even leaders of wizard schools, teaching the arcane ways of magic.

Warden dragon often have unique looks. For example Magi warden dragons, who teach magic to their hoard look like reptilian owls, while Archon dragons resemble winged lions.

Characters raised in the dragonbound culture share a variety of traits in common with one another:

**Languages:** You can speak, read, write, and sign <u>Common</u> and <u>Draconic</u>.
**Draconic Diplomacy:** You’ve been welltrained in the sometimes difficult art of draconic etiquette and protocols. You gain an <u>expertise die</u> on Charisma checks made to influence dragon creatures.
**Dragonbound Teachings:** You know one <u>cantrip</u> of your choice from the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell List Arcane/5e - Spell List Arcane|☄️Arcane]] or [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spell List Divine/5e - Spell List Divine|☄️Divine]] spell lists. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip is INT, WIS, or CHA (whichever is highest).
**Progenitor’s Boon:** Members of dragonbound clans overseen by their progenitors usually enjoy the benefits of their progenitor’s boon. Choose one of the following:

- **Chromatic Dragon’s Boon:** Chromatic dragons raise their warriors to shock and terrify their enemies. You can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 3 Illusion/5e - Fear (Spell)|☄️Fear]] without the need for material components once per [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Adventuring/5e - Resting/5e - Long Rest|😴Long Rest]]. Until you reach <u>5th level</u>, the area of this casting of the spell is limited to a <u>15-foot cone</u>. Your spellcasting ability for this spell is CHA.
- **Essence Dragon’s Boon:** Essence Dragons ensure that their guardians can easily commune with the land and the spirits within. You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Druidcraft (Spell)|☄️Druidcraft]] cantrip. In addition, your diplomatic understanding extends towards the creatures of the land and the spirits within it. You gain an <u>expertise die</u> on Charisma checks made to influence beast
- **Gem Dragon’s Boon:** Gem dragons ensure their agents’ missions go undetected by bestowing upon them subterfuge magic. You know the [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Transmutation/5e - Level 0 Transmutation/5e - Message (Spell)|☄️Message]] cantrip. Once you reach <u>3rd level</u>, you can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 1 Illusion/5e - Illusory Script (Spell)|☄️Illusory Script]] once per long rest. At <u>5th level</u>, you can cast [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Spellcasting/5e - Spellschool Illusion/5e - Level 2 Illusion/5e - Invisibility (Spell)|☄️Invisibility]] once per long rest. You don’t need material components for these spells, and when casting them your spellcasting ability is INT or CHA (whichever is highest).
- **Metallic Dragon’s Boon:** Metallic dragons let their pupils study vast repositories of knowledge. Choose a skill from among <u>Arcana</u>, <u>History</u>, <u>Medicine</u>, <u>Nature</u>, or <u>Religion</u>. You have proficiency in the chosen skill and you gain an <u>expertise die</u> on ability checks you make using it.
