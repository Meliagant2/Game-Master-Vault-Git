# 5e Speed

## Speed modification

Your Strength score alters your walking speed. The following table shows you the changes.

|Score|Walking Speed Increase|
|:-:|:-:|
|<0|-5|
|0-1|+0|
|2|+5|
|3-4|+10|
|5|+15|
|6-7|+20|
|8|+25|
|9-10|+30|
