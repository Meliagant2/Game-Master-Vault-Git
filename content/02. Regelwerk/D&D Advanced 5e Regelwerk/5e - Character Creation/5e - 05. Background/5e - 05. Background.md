# ⛏️5e - Backgrounds⛏️

What did you do before you took up your adventuring career? Were you a mercenary? A weaver? A street performer? Although you no longer follow your old trade, your background provides you with ability score bonuses, skills, and other benefits that will serve you in your coming adventures.

If your background provides a skill or tool proficiency you already have from another source, you can instead gain a different proficiency of the same type.

Also be sure to inform your Game Master of your connection. Your past may not be done with you yet!

<div class="description">
<h2>Making Backgrounds</h2>
<p>If you want to design your own background, it should include the following:</p>
<p>- +1 to two ability scores of your choice.</p>
<p>- Proficiency in two skills.</p>
<p>- Proficiency in two tools, two languages, or one of each.</p>
<p>- One connection: an acquaintance, ally, or enemy from your past.</p>
<p>- One memento: an item of sentimental value worth less than 30 silver.</p>
<p>- A background feature describing your relationship with the world. Your background feature typically provides a source of information, shelter, or financial support in a certain lifestyle.</p>
</div>
