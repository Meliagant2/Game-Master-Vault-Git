# 🦸‍♀️5e - Oath of Vengeance🦸‍♀️

The Oath of Vengeance is a solemn commitment to punish those who have committed a grievous sin. When evil forces slaughter helpless villagers, when an entire people turns against the will of the gods, when a thieves’ guild grows too violent and powerful, when a dragon rampages through the countryside — at times like these, paladins arise and swear an Oath of Vengeance to set right that which has gone wrong. To these paladins — sometimes called avengers or dark knights — their own purity is not as important as delivering justice.

> [!lore] TENETS OF VENGEANCE
> The tenets of the Oath of Vengeance vary by paladin, but all the tenets revolve around punishing wrongdoers by any means necessary. Paladins who uphold these tenets are willing to sacrifice even their own righteousness to mete out justice upon those who do evil. The core principles of the tenets are brutally simple.
> **<u>Fight the Greater Evil:</u>** Faced with a choice of fighting my sworn foes or combating a lesser evil, I choose the greater evil.
> **<u>No Mercy for the Wicked:</u>** Ordinary foes might win my mercy, but my sworn enemies do not.
> **<u>By Any Means Necessary:</u>** My qualms can’t get in the way of exterminating my foes.
> **<u>Restitution:</u>** If my foes wreak ruin on the world, it is because I failed to stop them. I must help those harmed by their misdeeds.

### Level 3: Vow of Enmity

As a **Bonus Action**, you can expend one use of your **Channel Divinity** to utter a vow of enmity against a creature you can see within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself. You gain **ADV** on attack rolls against the creature for <u>1 minute</u>.

If the creature drops to <u>0 Hit Points</u> before the vow ends, you can transfer the vow to a different creature within <u>30 feet</u> of yourself (**no action** required).

### Level 6: Relentless Avenger

Your supernatural focus helps you close off a foe’s retreat. When you hit a creature with an Attack as part of a reaction, you can reduce the creature’s <u>Speed</u> to 0, and you can then move up to half your Speed as part of the same Reaction.

This movement doesn’t provoke [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Combat/5e - Actions, Bonus Actions & Reactions/5e - Reactions/5e - Opportunity Attack|⚔️Opportunity Attacks]].

### Level 9: Divine Justice

You gain the following benefits against an enemy under the effect of your **Vow of Enmity** Feature:

- Your <u>Weapon attacks</u> deal extra damage against that target equal to `your CHA`.
- You score a [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Checks & Saves/5e - Checks & Saves Outcomes/5e - Critical Success|🎲Critical Hit]] against that creature on a roll of <u>19 or 20</u>.
- The Creature has **DISADV** on <u>Saves</u> against your Paladin Spells.

### Level 13: Soul of Vengeance

Immediately after a creature under the effect of your **Vow of Enmity** hits or misses with an attack, you can use your **Reaction** to make a <u>melee attack</u> against that creature if it’s within range.

### Level 17: Avenging Angel

As a **Bonus Action**, you can imbue your **Sacred Aura** with holy power, granting the following benefits for <u>10 minutes</u> or until you end them as a **Bonus Action**:

**<u>Flight:</u>** You sprout spectral wings on your back. You have a <u>Fly Speed</u> of <u>60 ft.</u> and can Hover.
**<u>Frightful Aura:</u>** Whenever an enemy starts its turn in the **aura**, that creature must make a **WIS Save**. _**Failure:**_ The Creature is [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Frightened|☠️Frightened]] for <u>1 minute</u> or until it takes any damage. Attack rolls against the Frightened creature have **ADV**.

<u>Once</u> you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**. You can also restore your use of it by expending a <u>5th-level spell slot</u> (**no action** required).
