# 🎲5e - Advantage & Disadvantage🎲

Sometimes a circumstance grants **Advantage (ADV)** or **Disadvantage (DISADV)** on an **ability check**, a **saving throw**, or an **attack roll**. When that happens, you roll two <u>D20</u> instead of one. For **advantage**, you use the higher of the two rolls. For **disadvantage**, you use the lower of the two rolls. When you have both advantage and disadvantage (regardless of from how many sources), they cancel each other out and you roll normally.
