# 💡5e - Heavily Obscured💡

An area that is **Heavily Obscured** may be a shoreline cloaked in dense fog, a jungle full of thick foliage and hanging vines, or darkness (magical or otherwise), that obstructs vision completely. A creature in this area is considered [[02. Regelwerk/D&D Advanced 5e Regelwerk/5e - Basic Rules/5e - Conditions/5e - Blinded|☠️Blind]].
